§1 — Orientation: go through the physics, not around it

S9 settled how MSE-free internal-current information actually flows on MAST. The D2 TORAX-EnKF mechanism finding: held-out MSE pitch is recovered by the current-diffusion forward model + σ(Te) closure time-history — assimilating magnetics barely moves the interior (analysis ≈ forecast). The supervised oracle then closed the other door: direct obs→pitch regression gains nothing from time-history (0.190 ≈ 0.188) and no multimodal feature set breaks the ~0.41 rad near-axis floor. S12's fast-channel sweep confirmed it again. The information enters through the physics model; therefore the highest-leverage ML is to improve the physics model's weakest parts — its closures — not to replace it.

This is the second of the two user-directed (2026-06-10) world-model builds for the idle 4×H200 reservation, and it is the project's core principle made concrete: extract reduced-order nonlinear dynamics, boundary conditions, closures and assumptions from raw data; fold hard physics priors — resistivity cannot be negative, current diffuses on a slow resistive timescale, force balance holds — into high-resolution multi-modal data. It is also the natural home for the open survey followup (neural-kf-architecture-survey / f-nkfs-prototype-class2): the "learned components inside a principled filter" class, built on the strong baseline as that survey prescribed.

§2 — Thesis and pre-registered win condition

Thesis. The reduced 1-D poloidal-flux-diffusion model is structurally right (parabolic PDE, Ip boundary drive) but its closures are assumptions: Sauter neoclassical η∥(Te, ne, Zeff) with an assumed flat Zeff, an assumed bootstrap fraction, assumed sources. Parameterize exactly those pieces as small neural corrections with the physics hard-wired — η∥ = Sauter(Te, ne) · softplus-positive learned factor gθ(ρ, features); an effective Zeff(ρ, t) bounded to [1, 6]; an optional bootstrap multiplier — and train them end-to-end through a differentiable solver, MSE-free, on losses the data actually observes: magnetics innovation through the EFIT-free GS forward operator, total-Ip consistency, and (when S10 lands it) the Thomson GS-p′ pressure observation. Amortize gθ across the 5,672-shot training split — the generalization the per-shot EKF cannot have, and a workload that batches perfectly onto 4×H200. Validate on held-out MSE via the locked D1 harness.

Pre-registered win condition (all on the locked D1 harness, 112 held-out MSE shots, shot = independent unit, bootstrap CIs; the frozen sequential-EKF v1 bar from sequential-current-da-v1 is the comparator):

  • W1 — beat the frozen v1 bar on sightline pitch RMSE with CI clear of zero, at the same conformal calibration (cov90 ∈ [0.88, 0.92] via the v1 split-conformal layer, which stays frozen — calibration was solved in S10 and is not re-opened here).
  • W2 — proper scores: CRPS/NLL on beam-on slices no worse than the v1 bar; transient windows reported separately.
  • W3 — closure attribution: the learned-closure filter beats its own Sauter-only ablation (identical solver, gθ ≡ 1) — otherwise the networks earned nothing and the verdict says so.
  • Near-axis (clean test, no claim). Near-axis interior RMSE is reported against the ~0.41 rad wall. The oracle says that wall is information-limited from these channels, so no interior win is promised; the one real interior lever remains the S10 GS-p′ pressure observation, which this framework composes with (D4). A clean negative is a result.
  • W4 — the extracted closure is itself a deliverable. Maps of the learned η-correction and effective Zeff(ρ, t) across the corpus, compared against the Sauter assumption, with a sparse (SINDy-style) summary of what the data says the closure depends on. This is the "extraction of closures and assumptions from raw data" output, win or lose on W1.

§3 — Why this is not the thing the oracle refuted

The oracle refuted direct readout: regressing pitch from (any) instantaneous or windowed observation features. It did not — and could not — test closure learning, because closure learning changes the forward model that the oracle's arms all lacked. Three concrete differences:

  • The carrier is the PDE, not the features. D2 proved the recovery skill lives in the ψ-diffusion forward + σ(Te) history. The learned closure refines that exact carrier; observations enter the same way they did in the winning mechanism (drive + weak correction), not as a regression input.
  • Hard priors bound the hypothesis class. Positivity of η, the parabolic diffusion structure, Ip boundary condition, [1,6] Zeff bounds — the network cannot invent interior structure the physics forbids; it can only re-shade assumptions the physics left free. The 5–6 DOF localization lesson (S10 lock) is inherited: data corrects only what it can see.
  • Amortization is new information use. The per-shot EKF fits each shot alone; gθ shares statistical strength across 5,672 shots — systematic closure errors (e.g. Zeff trends with density/heating) are learnable across the corpus even where a single shot under-determines them.

The honest ceiling: the oracle's ~0.19 rad sightline frontier was measured with MSE supervision; an MSE-free method has no licence to beat it, and the realistic W1 target is closing the gap between the v1 EKF bar and that frontier with honest calibration. That is a publishable, decision-relevant result for ITER startup either way.

§4 — Formulation

The state and solver follow the locked S10 substrate decisions verbatim: ψ(ρ) is the filtered primitive (filtered-state lock), the reduced banded-Jacobian implicit scheme is primary with TORAX as per-shot cross-check only (da-substrate lock), and j, q and the pitch readout derive post-hoc through the shared pitch_from_current_profile contract so numbers are method-matched with every prior bar. The training-time observation losses reuse gs/operator.py (the validated EFIT-free Green's-function operator) and robust_sensor_scale for the magnetics noise model.

§6 — Compute plan and GPU safety (binding)

The workload is thousands of independent 1-D PDE rollouts (state dim ~50–100, ~10³–10⁴ steps) plus tiny networks — embarrassingly batchable. A vmapped implicit solver at batch 512–2048 shots/GPU keeps 4×H200 busy; a full training run is hours, not days, so the iteration loop (D2→D4) is genuinely fast-return. CPU smoke tests precede any GPU submission.

Every sbatch clones the hardened header verbatim (AGENTS.md §2a, §2a-cancel, §2a-cancel-time): STOP-FILE checked at every optimiser-step boundary, --signal=B:USR1@600 trap, AMBIX_SOFT_TIME_LIMIT, --time as 2–4× ceiling only, SIGTERM handler with <5 s clean exit, model/solver state checkpointed for lossless resume. Never scancel. In-process single long-lived process per GPU (§2b); no subprocess-per-shot.

§7 — Risks and honesty

  • Identifiability. η-correction, Zeff and sources can trade against each other. Mitigations: minimal closure set first (decision closure-set), strong smoothness/sparsity priors, the Sauter-only ablation as the attribution control (W3), and the S10 localization philosophy — corrections regularized toward 1 where data is silent.
  • Gradients through a stiff solver. Implicit scheme + adjoint, gradient clipping, curriculum from short windows to full shots. D0's gradcheck is the gate.
  • Fitting magnetics noise. Innovation whitening with the measured robust_sensor_scale noise model; eddy-current systematics flagged from Stage-2 carried as a known confounder.
  • Sequencing on S10. W1 needs the frozen v1 bar from sequential-current-da-v1 (pilot 0.124 / cov90 0.962 on 5 shots; full manifest pending). D0–D2 proceed in parallel; D4 scoring waits for the bar to freeze. The near-axis lever stays with the S10 pressure experiment; this plan composes with it, it does not replace it.
  • No interior overclaim. Three sprints of negatives say the ~0.41 rad near-axis wall is information, not model capacity. This plan's claims live on the sightline metric, calibration, and the extracted closures.
