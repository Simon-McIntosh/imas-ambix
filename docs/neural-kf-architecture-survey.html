§1 — The two-class verdict

This is a survey now, build later document. The S9 oracle established the governing fact: for MSE-free internal-current recovery on MAST the binding constraint is interior information, not ML architecture (time-history adds nothing, 0.190 ≈ 0.188; near-axis interior model-failure-floored at ~0.41 rad for magnetics and all multimodal channels). Every neural Kalman-filter family is therefore judged on two separate questions, which most prior framings conflate:

  • Class-1 question: does it add interior information? The answer is uniformly NO — no learned filter can manufacture interior current information that the diagnostics do not carry. This is an information bound, not an architecture limitation.
  • Class-2 question: does it fix a named weak-EnKF symptom? Here some architectures genuinely help — but the two symptoms worth fixing (calibration; amortised multimodal fusion) are both gated behind a strong baseline and richer diagnostics, and the most valuable one (calibration) is already solved by split-conformal in sequential-current-da-v1.

The table's left column is the whole point: it is uniformly NO. No architecture choice on this target buys interior information. That is why this sprint builds a physics baseline and chains diagnostics first, and defers all ML.

§2 — New primaries (cross-reference, do not duplicate)

The general state-space method catalogue lives in probabilistic-state-space-methods and is not duplicated here. This section records only the primaries that anchor the verdicts above:

  • KalmanNet — Revach et al., learned Kalman gain for partially-known dynamics (arXiv:2107.10043). The learned-gain corrector path.
  • Recursive KalmanNet — recursive-covariance KalmanNet variant (arXiv:2506.11639). The later calibration alternative.
  • LAE-EnKF — linear(ised)-autoencoder latent + EnKF: a Koopman-style linear latent in which the EnKF analysis is exact, the most principled Class-2 fusion candidate.
  • KalMamba — Mamba/SSM-backbone Kalman filtering (arXiv:2406.15131). The sequence-model Class-2 representative — deprioritised by the oracle's time-history-null result.
  • Lusch continuous-spectrum failure — Lusch et al., deep learning of Koopman embeddings and the continuous-spectrum obstruction (arXiv:1712.09707). The caveat that bounds naive Koopman embeddings.
  • SINDy-AE — Champion et al., data-driven discovery of coordinates and governing equations (arXiv:1904.02107). The interpretable-latent-dynamics representative.

§3 — Where ML earns its keep, later

Two Class-2 roles survive scrutiny, both gated:

  • Recursive KalmanNet as a calibration alternative. It is a later option, not a second cov90 problem: split-conformal in sequential-current-da-v1 §6 already delivers a frozen, distribution-free coverage guarantee. RKN-style learned covariance is only worth considering if there is a regime where the conformal interval is needlessly wide, and only after the strong baseline exists.
  • KalmanNet as a learned-gain TORAX-bias corrector. The forward model (reduced diffusion / TORAX) is mis-specified relative to the true plasma; a learned Kalman gain can correct the systematic forecast bias the reduced model leaves. This is principled exactly where the dynamics are partially known — the KalmanNet thesis — and is the highest-value learned-gain role on this problem.

§4 — Defer the sequence models

Transformer / S4 / Mamba / KalMamba sequence backbones are deferred. The oracle's result is dispositive for this target: time-history adds nothing (0.190 ≈ 0.188), so the sequence-modelling capacity that makes these architectures attractive elsewhere has no signal to capture here. They are not on the build path for current recovery and should not be revisited unless a future target is shown to be genuinely dynamics-limited.

§5 — Honest framing: RKN is the wrong successor, Koopman + learned-gain are right

The recurrent RKN is directionally the WRONG successor — and this is true independent of the information ceiling. Koopman-linear-latent + learned-gain filters are more principled than the RKN on their own merits: a linear latent admits an exact EnKF analysis and a tractable covariance, and a learned gain corrects a known-but-mis-specified forward model where the structure is genuinely partially known. The recurrent RKN bundles dynamics, gain, and uncertainty into one opaque recurrence that is harder to ground in the GS physics and harder to calibrate. Frame doc (c) as "the right architecture for WHEN richer data exists," not "deferred because architecture didn't matter." The oracle showed architecture is not today's binding constraint — it did not vindicate the RKN. When richer diagnostics arrive (the pressure lever lands, or a future machine brings polarimetry), the successor to prototype is a Koopman-LAE-EnKF or a learned-gain KalmanNet, not a deeper RKN.

Is any neural filter built this sprint?

LOCKED (S10, 2026-06-02): no ML build this sprint. The oracle proved the binding constraint is interior information, not ML architecture; the Class-1 column is uniformly NO. This sprint shores up the strong physics baseline and chains diagnostics; ML is deferred to build on that stronger baseline + richer info.

Which architecture is the principled successor when ML is eventually built?

LOCKED (S10, 2026-06-02): Koopman-linear-latent + learned-gain are the principled successors, MORE principled than the recurrent RKN INDEPENDENT of the information ceiling (exact EnKF analysis in a linear latent; learned-gain corrects a partially-known forward model). The recurrent RKN is directionally the WRONG successor — it bundles dynamics/gain/uncertainty opaquely and is harder to ground in GS physics and to calibrate. The oracle did NOT vindicate the RKN.

Under what conditions does ML earn its keep on this problem?

LOCKED (S10, 2026-06-02): ML earns its keep ONLY on Class-2 roles (calibration + amortised multimodal fusion), and ONLY once richer diagnostics exist. It cannot add interior information (Class-1 uniformly NO). Note: cov90 calibration is already solved by split-conformal in sequential-current-da-v1 §6 — RKN-style learned covariance is NOT a re-opening of that problem.
