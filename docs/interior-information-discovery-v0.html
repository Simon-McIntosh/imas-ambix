§1 — Thesis: is the information absent, or just thrown away?

The S9 oracle established a hard result: a supervised probe that sees raw magnetics plus all multimodal channels cannot recover the near-axis internal current better than ~0.41 rad pitch RMSE, and time-history adds nothing (0.190 ≈ 0.188 on the locked metric). The verdict was that the binding constraint on MSE-free current recovery is interior information, not ML architecture or dynamics. That conclusion re-shaped the project (the S10 baseline track is deliberately CPU-only and physics-first).

But the oracle fed magnetics the way EFIT does: as scalar, time-averaged, integral signals. That is precisely the representation a least-squares equilibrium solver can digest — and precisely the representation that destroys the high-frequency structure carrying interior information. The plasma broadcasts its internal current profile through MHD activity: tearing modes localise to rational surfaces (q = 2, 3/2), the sawtooth inversion radius marks q = 1, Alfvén eigenmodes pin q-min, and the toroidal/poloidal mode numbers of these instabilities are written across the fast pickup-coil array. None of that survives the integral, decimated form EFIT consumes.

Hypothesis (falsifiable). Interior-current information is present in fast/exotic MAST channels but is annihilated by the traditional least-squares representation. Served as time-frequency / cross-coil-mode representations — hand-crafted and GPU-learned — these channels recover internal-current observables the scalar-magnetics oracle cannot, breaking the ~0.41 rad floor. A clean negative is also a result: it would upgrade "we lack interior information" from "in the representation we tried" to "in the data at all", which is itself decision-grade for the north-star.

This is deliberately the class of question historically skipped because it is intractable in a least-squares sense: a mixed-rate inverse problem spanning xma at 5 kHz, xsx at 100–500 kHz, and hundreds of channels over thousands of shots. It is exactly the kind of representation audit the H200 reservation was meant to answer.

§3 — Measurement: oracle bar + physics probes (both)

The study produces a single comparable verdict and a mechanistic explanation.

  1. Primary — extend the S9 oracle. Reuse the built statespace/mse_eval.py + oracle_probe.py + the frozen 112-shot cross-family / cross-shot split (mse_split.py) and held-out MSE q-truth. Add each representation arm as a new feature set and score on the identical locked metric: near-axis interior pitch RMSE, with split-conformal coverage held in [0.88, 0.92]. The bar is the established ~0.41 rad floor (mag + all multimodal). Because the metric and split are unchanged, the result is directly comparable to S9 by construction.
  2. Secondary — physics localisation probes. Independent of the oracle: derive the toroidal mode number n(t) (and m where the array allows) from xma cross-coil phase; the sawtooth inversion radius from xsx; density-fluctuation structure from aoe. Check the inferred rational-surface radii r(q = 1), r(q = 2) against EFIT/MSE (eval-only boundary). This answers where any recovered information comes from, and guards against the oracle winning for a spurious reason.

Scope locks carried from S9 (do not relitigate): observables / on-axis only — full continuous q(ψ) is OUT; EFIT is an eval-only boundary / sanity check, never an input; raw signals only (no efm/esm/xdc).

§4 — Representations: hand-crafted vs learned

The core of the "too-hard-for-least-squares" thesis is the head-to-head: feed the same target the same data through two representation families and ask whether the GPU finds more than the physics-motivated transform.

  • Arm A — hand-crafted, interpretable. Per-coil STFT spectrograms; SVD/Fourier cross-coil decomposition into toroidal-mode-number spectra (the physically meaningful magnetics representation); Morlet wavelet scalograms for xsx; k-ω spectra for aoe; line-shape moments for act. Fast to debug, physics-anchored, and the honest baseline the learned arm must beat.
  • Arm B — learned, GPU-resident. 1D-CNN / wavelet-scattering / raw-waveform transformer encoders trained on the H200s directly from the native waveforms (5 kHz xma; 100–500 kHz xsx), with the cross-coil structure exposed as a channel axis. Maximal "let the model find it"; this is where the reservation would be spent if the crafted rung found an interior-reaching signal.

Verdict question: does Arm B beat Arm A on the locked metric? If yes, the GPU is extracting structure the physics transform misses. If the two tie at the floor, the information is genuinely absent from these channels (strong negative). If both break the floor, interior information was in the representation all along — the headline win.

§ Decisions

Which exotic/fast sources does v0 include?

Locked to the broad panel (user, 2026-06-04). xma (5 kHz) and xsx (100–500 kHz) are the high-availability fast magnetics+SXR core: xma 91% / xsx hcam 78% of the 112 held-out MSE shots. aoe (reflectometry, 19%) and act (CXRS, 95%) extend the panel on smaller usable-N cohorts. xma Mirnov coils (ccbv_01..40) confirmed included (2026-06-04, advisor + user). These are AC dBθ/dt coils measuring MHD fluctuations — physically distinct from the quasi-static equilibrium B_pol probes + flux loops that set the 0.41 rad floor. Mode-structure information (cross-coil coherence → n/m mode numbers → rational-surface radii) is rate-robust at 5 kHz: the 5 kHz limit costs crash microdynamics, not spatial mode information. xsx at 500 kHz is the primary fast-crash arm; xma ccbv is the spatial-mode arm. The oracle (not an a-priori argument) decides what beats 0.41. D1 arm design: ccbv decomposed cross-coil (n-spectrum / mode amplitudes / coherence), NOT flat per-channel regression. ama n=2/n=odd at 500 kHz tracked separately so oracle gains are attributable. fl_cc flux loops treated as equilibrium-adjacent (separate from ccbv). Note: act high-availability (95%); aoe is the smaller-N arm (19%).

How is "interior information present?" measured?

Both (user, 2026-06-04). Primary verdict from the locked mse_eval metric vs the ~0.41 floor (directly comparable to S9); physics probes explain where any recovered information comes from and guard against a spurious oracle win.

How is fast data served to the GPU?

Compare both on the same target/metric (user, 2026-06-04). Arm A (STFT + cross-coil mode decomposition / wavelet / k-ω / line-shape) vs Arm B (learned 1D-CNN / wavelet-scattering / transformer on raw waveforms). Directly tests the 'too-hard-for-least-squares' thesis. CRITICAL FINDING (2026-06-05, advisor): the D1/D2 oracle runs used MAGNITUDE-ONLY features (|rfft|, detrended RMS). Mode-number and rational-surface information lives in CROSS-CHANNEL PHASE (m/n = phase wraps around the sensor array; a rotating NTM is a phase progression across chords at one frequency). Magnitude-only features are structurally blind to this, independent of downstream model capacity. D3-phase closeout (commit ceb01ed): the phase-preserving crafted rung stayed null on the locked mse_eval metric. A_mag = 0.1879 rad, E_mag_sxr_phase = 0.1889 (paired CI crosses 0), E_mag_mirnov_phase = 0.1923, and the combined phase arm = 0.1938. The only real carrier remained bolometer and it stayed off-interior (carrier_reaches_interior_reliable = false), so Arm B / H200 is not justified for S12.

D0 I/O strategy for the 5 kHz / ~660 MB-per-shot fast magnetics?

On-the-fly chosen over precompute for two reasons: (1) D1 compares crafted (STFT/wavelet/k-ω) vs learned (1D-CNN/wavelet-scattering/transformer) arms on the same raw data — precomputing locks in one representation before the arm comparison; (2) I/O is tractable at the D1 study scale: xma 51 key channels = 274 ms/shot (28 MB compact), xsx 36 ch = 93 ms/shot (43 MB), aoe 5 bands = 25 ms/shot, act = 13 ms/shot; total ~405 ms/shot single-threaded GPFS. For 112 held-out MSE shots ~45 s/epoch is acceptable. Rate correction (measured 2026-06-04): xma actual rate is 5 kHz (200 µs spacing), NOT 660 kHz as the plan title implied — plan text counted array length 660 000 as a rate. The 5 kHz Nyquist (2.5 kHz) still resolves low-n MHD modes; the genuinely fast signal for sub-ms MHD is xsx at 100–500 kHz. Precomputing all STFT scales for the 3473-shot MSE cohort would require ~10 GB; on-the-fly avoids that pipeline before the representation arm decision.

Which held-out MSE observables are the regression target?

Locked for like-for-like comparison against the established S9 / ~0.41 floor, per user direction on 2026-06-05. Rational-surface radii remain probe-only extensions: surface them in physics probes or secondary reporting if phase-preserving evidence suggests real signal, but do not change the primary regression target for D3-phase or the main study verdict.
