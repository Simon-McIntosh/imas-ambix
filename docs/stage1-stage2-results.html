Stage-1 + Stage-2 Results — Completed Work

An illustrated, honest record of the work completed before the 2026-05-31 pivot. It captures what the latent state-space engine (Stage-1) and the Grad–Shafranov prior (Stage-2) actually delivered — wins, losses, and the corrected mislabels — and the durable assets carried into mse-free-current-recovery-v0. Every figure below is plotted directly from the committed JSON artifacts under imas_ambix/{statespace,gs}/artifacts/; the source artifact + plotted values are recorded in each figure's generation manifest.

§1 — The honest arc

Two stages were completed. Both produced calibration-honest results in which a learned or physics component lost the headline proper-score to a trivial baseline — yet both produced a durable asset and a sharp insight that, taken together, define the next move.

  • Stage-1 — a Recurrent-Kalman-Network latent state-space engine that forecasts a whole held-out diagnostic family (Dα) from raw magnetics + interferometer. It is well-calibrated and avoids confident-catastrophic transient misses, but a persistence-of-nowcast wins aggregate CRPS because the Dα target is ~96.8 % quiescent. Dynamics did not pay on a near-stationary target.
  • Stage-2 — a self-applied Grad–Shafranov soft prior. The force-balance residual is a real signal but is beaten by a trivial |dB/dt| detector; grounding the latent in magnetics neither enriched the dynamics nor recovered the internal current (near-vacuum ratio 0.98). This was the decisive insight: magnetics + boundary cannot constrain the internal current profile — and MSE is unusable from level-1 as a dense input. But Stage-2 delivered a validated, EFIT-free GS forward operator.
  • The pivot (mse-free-current-recovery-v0) turns these into the ITER-relevant thesis: recover the current profile without MSE, from raw multi-modal data, beating a classical EnKF — on a genuinely dynamic target where the dynamics should finally pay.

§2 — Stage-1: the latent state-space engine

§2.1 — Forecasting: the headline loss, and the real win

On identical dense ELM windows, the engine's autonomous latent rollout loses forecast CRPS to a persistence-of-nowcast at every horizon — bulk h=1 0.0861 vs 0.0651, h=20 0.1277 vs 0.0879. The mechanism: a near-stationary target rewards a sharp frozen nowcast, and the engine's predictive σ is over-wide on the quiescent bulk.

The genuine win is on the safety-relevant axis: long-horizon transient NLL. When an ELM lands at t+h, the static nowcast is confidently catastrophic (NLL +13.2 at h=20); the engine's calibrated belief bounds the loss (stays in [−0.87, −0.16]), winning at h≥5.

§2.2 — Calibration & out-of-distribution honesty

Filtering is well-calibrated — conformal 90 % coverage 0.913, inside the [0.88, 0.92] gate — and the reliability curves track the diagonal both in- and out-of-distribution (ECE 0.067 / 0.047).

Under regime shift the engine widens honestly: coverage degrades gracefully (0.944→0.755, non-collapse) with ~3.3× σ-inflation. But — the corrected finding — the engine's dynamics-native OOD signal (filter-innovation AUROC 0.748) does not beat a static deep-ensemble's disagreement on the same data (0.810). The "0.81 AUROC" reported as an engine win in earlier records was the static ensemble's number, mislabelled.

§2.3 — Refinement progression & latent structure

Across v0 (Gaussian) → v1 (+drift-reg) → v2 (+Student-t) the refinements improved monotonically (the Student-t head sharpened the transient-NLL win and narrowed the CRPS gap) but never flipped the headline verdict; the static comparator was unchanged throughout (CRPS h=1 = 0.0651 in all three). The learned 16-D latent lives on a ~3-D manifold (participation ratio ≈ 1.76, 90 %-rank 3).

§3 — Stage-2: the Grad–Shafranov prior

§3.1 — The force-balance residual frontier

The GS residual instrument has genuine resolution — the pooled quiescent residual sits well above the trivial floor (0.02) and below the starved ceiling (0.9) across the profile-DOF × λ grid — and the operating point (order-1, λ=0, median ≈ 0.40) was chosen by a min-DOF sanity rule, not by tuning to the detection metric.

§3.2 — Q1: the residual is beaten by a trivial baseline

The pre-committed Q1 gate FAILS: the GS force-balance-departure residual detects transients (AUROC ≈ 0.68) but is beaten by a trivial |dB/dt|/B-variance baseline on both labels (ELM 0.68 vs 0.76, margin −0.079; disruption 0.67 vs 0.92, margin −0.252). The negative is principled: a global axisymmetric residual is poorly matched to edge-localised ELMs, and disruptions are dB/dt current-quench events.

§3.3 — Grounding: the decisive insight

Grounding the latent in raw magnetics preserved Dα skill (filtering CRPS −6.4 %, within the single-seed/thread floor) but did not enrich the dynamics (discovery gap 0.78 % vs the ungrounded 1.28 % — not widened) and, decisively, the current inferred from the latent did not vanish in near-vacuum (ratio 0.98 vs the per-slice solver's 0.03). This is the project's central insight, and an expected physics result rather than a bug: magnetics + boundary fundamentally under-determine the internal current profile — the latent was fed only magnetics + density, so it cannot see inside the plasma.

§3.4 — The MSE data wall

The proposed fix — feed the latent an internal-current diagnostic (MSE) — ran into a presence-vs-content wall. MSE (ams) is present in 4,882 shots, but the time-resolved pitch-angle array (gamma) is ~99 % NaN (per-element finite fraction ≈ 0.86 %, valid only during beam-on), and a large fraction of shots carry no gamma at all. The MSE→internal-current half — precisely the term targeting the under-determination — is unrealizable from level-1 as a dense input.

§4 — What carries forward

The completed work hands the pivot three durable assets and one re-framing:

  • A validated, EFIT-free GS forward operator (gs/operator.py) — a geometry-only differentiable observation operator (vacuum round-trip, on-axis Bz to 1e-6, FD-vs-analytic to 1e-3) already wired as the magnetics-consistency head. This is exactly the forward model a learned multi-modal filter wraps, and that a classical EnKF measurement update needs.
  • The latent SSM + calibration stack — engine, filter/RTS-smoother, split-conformal calibration, the leakage-audit and cross-family hold-out machinery — all target-agnostic and reusable.
  • The MSE wall, re-cast — MSE is too sparse to be a dense input, but it is a raw diagnostic (not a banned solver output), and slice-dense during beam-on. So it becomes the validation key: train MSE-free, validate against held-out MSE.
  • The under-determination insight — magnetics alone cannot constrain the interior; closure requires time-history through a forward dynamics model + a Te-derived conductivity, plus multi-modal channels. That is the design brief for the pivot.
