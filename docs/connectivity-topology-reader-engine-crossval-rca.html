Research findings (RCA) for connectivity-topology-reader §6b, correcting the §6b findings doc. Trigger: the §6b census cross-validation reported the validated four-pass engine degrading 3–10× corpus-wide with the single-null classes "inverted below the bare seed" — surprising, because the same chain had just beaten persistence CI-clear on a leakage-audited 112-shot held-out MSE split while approaching the classical EnKF that consumes a measured Te channel the engine is denied. The working hypothesis was an implementation bug introduced into the reconstruction spine between the two evaluations. This RCA ran the decisive reproduction test, audited every code change and both harnesses, fixed what was broken, and re-scored the census.

TL;DR

1 — The reproduction test

The engine's binding validation is the held-out MSE gate: scripts/heldout_mse_gate_eval.py on the locked, leakage-audited 112-shot split (mse_heldout_split_v0.json, shots 21978–30203), frozen η, MSE never in any fit path. It was re-run twice with identical configuration: once on the current tree, once in a git worktree pinned at the gate's landing commit (3c4fedf, 2026-07-19) — i.e. the exact code state that produced the archived artifact — on the same partition class, split into quarter-cohorts to fit the debug wall clock.

Two per-slice A/B probes sharpen this to the census's worst offenders: shots 27893 and 29496 (sn-lower, late campaigns, 33–45 cm axis excursions) were run through the chain on both trees and compared slice-by-slice — axis and current-centroid values are byte-identical on every slice. A fable-tier audit of the full diff between the two states (761 changed lines in gs_solve.py, the temperature-smoothed kernel re-point, the boundary-binding unification, the basin/profile rename's serialized keys, the campaign grid cache) found every change on the default chain path either verified-neutral or genuinely opt-in with defaults intact. The spine did not regress.

2 — Defect 1 (dominant): the boundary metric measured axis placement, not boundary shape

The census scorer rendered EFIT's LCFS polygon to ray radii about the engine's own read axis (topology_engine_crossval.py, scoring loop). The validated 1.5–2.1 cm flat-top envelope — the provenance of the 3 cm tolerance — measures each boundary about its own axis (geometry_target_pushout radii vs the referee's resample_lcfs_radii about EFIT's axis), a translation-insensitive shape comparison. A ray fan cast from an origin displaced by Δ reads a perfectly-shaped boundary with radii distorted by ~|Δ·cos θ|, i.e. a median of ≈0.7·|Δ| over the fan — so the engine's documented axis error was charged to the boundary column.

The fixed harness scores both frames per slice: shape_dmed_cm (own-axis, gates quality — the quantity the 3 cm tolerance belongs to) and radii_dmed_cm (engine-frame placement, reported), with axis distance its own column rather than a contaminant.

The published table's bare column was read from the earlier §6 bare-chain artifact — 45 scored sn-lower slices, roughly half early-campaign, aggregated over all phases — and compared against the full engine's flat-top median on an 874-row draw that is 64% late-campaign. The headline "sn-lower: engine 18.5 cm worse than bare seed 7.9 cm" compared different shots, different slices, different phases and different campaign mixes under a metric dominated by Defect 1.

4 — Defect 3: class confounded with campaign era

Splitting the same rows at shot 23000: every class degrades ~2.5–3× beyond it, and the per-class draws mix the bands very differently (the sn-lower draw is ~3/4 late-band at flat-top; connected-dn ~2/5). The published per-class ranking was substantially a campaign-composition ranking. Provenance matters here: the spine's boundary-fidelity cohort (the 1.5 cm envelope) tops out at shot 18559, so the late band was never boundary-validated — while the MSE-gate cohort (21978–30203, 67 shots > 23000) validated the internal profile through pitch there and passed. The fixed aggregates report the bands separately.

5 — Defect 4: "flat-top" meant t ≥ 0.2 s

The census phase bin calls every slice with t ≥ 0.2 s flat-top; the validated envelope's flat-top statistic is the per-shot peak-|Ip| slice. On slow-ramp shots the census bin therefore contains still-ramping slices, inflating "flat-top" medians relative to the validated definition. Retained (with the band split making it visible) rather than changed, so the census keeps its denser time cover — but the two definitions must not be compared as if they were one.

6 — Framing conflation: which 1.5 cm was the engine ever supposed to match?

The coupled chain (coupled_solve_chain) runs without the D2 spine's disc-annulus soft anchor (weight 10, recorded as load-bearing in the frozen-spine notes) and without the outboard reseed lever (reseed_axis_r_max=None vs the spine's 1.4) — in the gate AND in the census, unchanged since landing. Its position authority is the σ=2 cm current-centroid tether + warm start + diffusion prior alone. The 1.499 cm flat-top LCFS in closure_spine_frozen.json belongs to the full D2 closure harness with those priors; the chain was validated on held-out pitch. Comparing chain boundary output against the D2 envelope conflates two configs — the corrected expectation for the chain is "clears the 3 cm bar where its axis is right", which is what the re-scored data shows.

11 — Addendum (2026-07-22): the production D2 spine, census-scored

Issue #5 above left the load-bearing question open: the census had only ever scored the gate chain (centroid pin + ψ-diffusion prior — no disc anchor, no reseed), while production training labels come from the frozen D2 spine (disc soft-anchor w=10, non-negative ladder np=nf=3, passive_k=8 sidecar, reseed 1.4). A fourth census arm now runs the exact label-factory configuration per slice (factory warm-start in time order, chain-arm payload budget for a maximal paired cover; harness commits 4a104e6/3de6b8e): 300 stratified shots, 6,250 scored slices, paired per-slice against both the gate chain and the bare disc seed.

Routing — with a deliberate interpretation firewall. The formal corpus-wide gate is holds=False (sn-lower shape over tolerance; diverted-class agreement <0.80). The measured facts are clean: the anchored spine holds boundary shape corpus-wide in the validated band and beats the chain on shape AND axis on this draw. What the facts do not yet license is retiring the pin/prior: the ψ-diffusion prior and the passive currents carry genuine physics, and the pin's target is a current centroid derived from the same magnetics being questioned — if the late-campaign sensor layer is degraded, miswired, or physically moved, the pin faithfully propagates that corrupted datum with weight (σ=2 cm) while the unpinned spine merely averages over it. "Pin worse than spine" and "pin amplifies a data-layer bias" are observationally identical on this census. Separating known physics from implementation/calibration error needs a per-era instrument characterisation first: (a) per-sensor vacuum-shot scorecard across the corpus — score each magnetics channel against the others on coil-only intervals per campaign era, detecting gain drift, sign flips, rewiring (a channel whose response matches a different catalogued position's Green's column), and position changes (f-ctr-15, extends the 48-shot single-era vacuum_coil_response_audit); (b) axis placement remains the spine's interior deficit → interior-closure-hardening §2; (c) the late-band collapse → f-ctr-13/f-ctr-15 jointly. Verdict block: production_spine in topology_full_engine_verdicts-v0.json.

Resolution (f-ctr-15, 2026-07-22): the per-era vacuum-shot magnetics scorecard (findings) resolves the confound to PARTIAL — YES with a mechanism. On coil-only intervals (no EFIT, no inversion), 16 (M7) and 21 (M8) of 65 vacuum-scored channels drift >10 % from their M5 gain, concentrated as a coherent −30 to −50 % drop at the P4/P5 flux loops (fl_p4l/p5l/p5u, post-fit corr 0.83–0.91) — exactly the in-vessel RMP-coil bay. The late-band magnetics therefore carry a real model/data-layer systematic (the unmodelled 12→18 in-vessel RMP conductors), which the centroid pin — a linear moment of the same magnetics — propagates with weight. So this census cannot license retiring the pin (confound confirmed), but the remedy is to correct the responsible coupling, not to retire the pin (f-ctr-18 confirmatory re-score). Mechanism updated same day: a per-shot-resolved sweep shows the bay-loop gain is bimodal in every era (no step at the RMP install boundaries) — the era drift is a drive-composition mixture shift over a time-invariant coil-specific coupling error, not installed-hardware screening; attribution routes to the per-(sensor, coil) decomposition in nonaxisymmetric-field-subtraction. Sensor disambiguation by the Hole et al. signed-assignment is degenerate on the archived axisymmetric-PF vacuum shots (median C 0.002–0.09), but 864 dedicated-vacuum shots with in-vessel coils driven were found for a high-confidence disambiguation dataset (f-ctr-17).
