§1 — Thesis & motivation

At the start of ITER operations there will be no Motional-Stark-Effect (MSE) diagnostic, yet the internal current profile must still be recovered. That constraint — once read as a data wall — is the founding opportunity of this plan. The established tool for recovering internal plasma dynamics from external measurements without MSE is an Ensemble / Extended Kalman Filter wrapped around a current-diffusion forward model (the RAPTOR-EKF lineage, deployed on TCV, ASDEX-Upgrade and RFX-mod). That EnKF is the baseline we must beat.

We aim well beyond a hand-built Kalman filter. The pivot is to a deep, end-to-end observation→state neural filter / smoother that ingests raw multi-modal diagnostics — magnetics, interferometer, bolometer, soft-X-ray, visible camera, Thomson scattering — and emits a calibrated estimate of the internal current state, recovering MSE-derived current observables it never sees as input, and fully exploiting the 4×H200 reservation. This recovers the project's original motivation: use large volumes of raw, multi-modal data — including the camera streams — to go beyond what current equilibrium-reconstruction tools can do.

This plan is grounded in a deep review of all prior work (workflow wpy0754zv, 2026-05-31; 7 readers across Stage-1, Stage-2, code assets, EnKF/DA SOTA, the multi-modal corpus, and the eval-truth protocol). The arc that justifies it:

  • Stage-1 (plasma-state-space-v0) proved a calibration-honest latent state-space engine, but lost every headline proper-score to a trivial persistence-of-nowcast — because its Dα target is ~96.8 % quiescent, where a frozen nowcast is unbeatable. Dynamics did not pay on a near-stationary target.
  • Stage-2 (plasma-gs-prior-v0) confirmed, with an honest negative, that magnetics + boundary alone cannot constrain the internal current profile (classic equilibrium under-determination), and that MSE is unusable from level-1 as a dense input (~99 % NaN, beam-on only). But it delivered a validated, EFIT-free Grad–Shafranov forward operator (gs/operator.py) already wired as a differentiable observation operator.
  • This plan turns both findings into the design: a genuinely dynamic target (current evolution, where dynamics should finally pay), the GS operator as a physics-consistency head, and the MSE wall re-cast as the validation key rather than a blocker.

§2 — The measurable win condition (ratified 2026-05-31)

The eval-truth question — against what do we score "recovers the current profile" when EFIT is banned as a label and MSE is mostly NaN? — is resolved by the observation that MSE is a raw diagnostic, not a banned solver output. So:

Train MSE-free. Validate against held-out raw MSE. The filter sees raw multi-modal inputs with no MSE; at scoring time we reveal the held-out MSE observables on the beam-on slices where they are valid and measure how well the MSE-free estimate recovered them. Holding MSE out of inputs and scoring against it bends zero locked principles, and matches "recover the current profile without MSE" one-to-one.

The win is declared on three axes, all against the EnKF baseline at matched compute:

  1. Accuracy — MSE-observable RMSE (on-axis safety factor q0_kappa1.85, magnetic-axis rax, and live pitch-angle channels) ≥ 10–20 % lower than the EnKF on held-out beam-on slices.
  2. Calibration — conformal coverage in the [0.88, 0.92] band (the same gate Stage-1 met), with honest widening out-of-distribution.
  3. Proper score — a strictly-proper CRPS / NLL win over the EnKF, especially on transient windows (ramps, MHD, disruptive precursors) where a Gaussian EnKF is known to degrade — exactly where a learned non-Gaussian filter earns its keep.

§3 — Scope locks (ratified)

Three decisions were ratified at the gate and are binding for v0:

  • Target scope = MSE observables / on-axis only. We recover the on-axis safety factor, magnetic-axis position, and pitch-angle observables. The full continuous q(ψ) / j(ψ) profile is explicitly out of scope — there is no non-circular ground truth for it in the corpus (magnetics-only EFIT assumes a 2-coefficient linear interior, so scoring against its qpsi_c would score agreement-with-an-assumption). A full-profile track would first require building a trusted synthetic-equilibrium + synthetic-diagnostic forward model; that is deferred (see §8).
  • EFIT (efm) = eval-only boundary/global sanity-check. EFIT may cross-check boundary / global quantities (LCFS shape, total Ip, q95) where magnetics genuinely constrain — never as a training input, label, or interior-current reference. The primary internal-current score is held-out MSE. This continues the project's existing "EFIT-shape demoted to sanity check" stance.
  • Architecture = end-to-end observation→state. One model maps raw heterogeneous diagnostics straight to the plasma state — no hand-built assimilation pipeline (the Aardvark-Weather precedent for raw-obs→state). This is the most ambitious of the four reviewed classes, with the biggest H200 lift and the longest runway, so it is staged behind de-risking milestones (§7).

§4 — Why MSE-free recovery is physically possible

Stage-2's under-determination finding is a single-instant, spatial statement: external magnetics at one time constrain only Ip, boundary, li, and βp — not the detailed internal j(ψ). EnKF/RAPTOR recovers the interior through a different information channel: time-history pushed through a forward dynamics model, not an extra spatial measurement. The closure mechanism is the poloidal-field-diffusion equation, which deterministically evolves q(ρ,t) given three things a magnetics snapshot lacks:

  1. the loop-voltage / Ip boundary condition over time (the surface drive);
  2. the conductivity profile σ(Te) — set by neoclassical resistivity from Te, a non-MSE channel (Thomson / ECE) — which localises where current diffuses, substituting for the spatial pitch-angle constraint MSE would give;
  3. the non-ohmic (bootstrap + auxiliary) current sources.

This is precisely where a neural filter can exceed a classical EnKF: a learned (rather than hand-coded resistive-diffusion) forward dynamics model, non-Gaussian posteriors for the ELM / bifurcation / disruption regimes where the EKF's Gaussian assumption breaks, and native fusion of high-dimensional multi-modal channels (camera, bolometry, SXR tomography) that an O(N_ens × model_cost) EnKF cannot easily ingest. Secondary non-MSE constraints that further tighten the interior — diamagnetic βp, soft-X-ray / Mirnov rational-surface markers — come for free from the multi-modal input set.

§5 — Data plan

Input set (raw signals only). Magnetics ama+amb+amc, interferometer ane, bolometer abm (32-chan radiated power), soft-X-ray xsx (3×18-chord cameras, the densest internal-emission signal in the corpus), visible camera rbb (centre-stack), and Thomson ayc (ne/Te for the σ(Te) term). The exogenous control reference xdc is the conditioning input u (with its magnetics-derived shape_s_fluxerr* channels gated out).

Corpus tiers (co-availability, from the level-1 scan).

  • Tier-1 magnetics + camera + bolometer + SXR ≈ 7,349 shots — the practical multi-modal floor that includes imaging.
  • Tier-2 + Thomson ayc ≈ 3,657 shots (Thomson exists only from ~shot 23000).
  • MSE-eval overlap ≈ 2,000 shots with usable ams, of which ~128 sit in a held-out OOD slice — the effective independent eval N (the ~540–650 beam-on slices per shot are autocorrelated; the shot is the independent unit).

Excluded: IR cameras (rir=25, rit=14 shots — effectively absent); the banned solver outputs efm / esm from inputs and labels; the Dα leakage groups (ada/aim/xim) from inputs. MSE (ams) is never an input and never a training label or loss term — eval truth only.

Leakage note (corrects the Stage-1 circularity trap). Total Ip (from amc) is kept as an input: it is a legitimate magnetics measurement and is precisely the quantity that does not determine internal current shape, so it is not a leak for an internal-current target. (The Stage-1 audit's "amc leaks Ip" concern applies only when magnetics itself is the prediction target.) The held-out split must be re-derived for the current target — do not reuse the Dα dalpha_v0 split.

§6 — Architecture & reusable assets

The end-to-end obs→state model. Per-modality encoders map each raw diagnostic stream (1-D signals, chord arrays, camera frames) into a shared latent; a learned stochastic transition kernel evolves the latent on a common time grid; a readout head emits the current-state observables with a calibrated predictive distribution. The GS Green's-function forward operator (gs/operator.py) enters as a differentiable physics-consistency / observation term (latent → restricted currents → predicted magnetics), making the magnetics channel self-consistent without any EFIT label.

Reusable assets identified by the review (do not rebuild):

  • statespace/engine.py — RKN latent SSM + filtering / rollout / RTS smoothing + the GS grounding head + STOP-FILE / soft-time-limit hooks. The dynamics backbone.
  • statespace/filter.py — causal filter, h-step forecast, RTS smoother (structurally an EnKF-smoother analogue), Mahalanobis innovation OOD score.
  • statespace/calibration.py — target-agnostic CRPS / NLL / coverage / ECE / reliability — the acceptance layer, verbatim.
  • gs/operator.py, gs/residual.py, gs/grounding.py — the validated EFIT-free forward operator, the per-slice inverse solver (the linearised observation operator H for the EnKF), and the differentiable grounding head.
  • statespace/{splits,families,inventory}.py — regime-split, whole-family hold-out, leakage-audit machinery (re-target to the current observable).
  • statespace/{align,dataset}.py — multi-rate alignment (already documents camera rates) and the target-agnostic multi-family dataset (extend _open_group_as_dataset to admit 2-D camera / chord arrays).

H200 utilisation. bf16 + TF32 tensor cores, matmul_precision="high", deterministic cuDNN, multi-GPU data/model parallel — and the hardened in-process pattern (single long-lived process, SIGTERM handler, per-batch watchdog, STOP-FILE) mandated by AGENTS.md §2a/§2b. No subprocess-per-shot, no prefetch daemons.

§7 — Implementation sequence (de-risked)

The build is front-loaded so we learn the bar before investing in the large model — the explicit guard against repeating the two-stage "calibration-honest but the baseline wins" pattern. Each task lands a committed artifact + green tests.

  1. T1 — Eval harness + held-out-MSE split (the gate). Re-derive a cross-family + cross-shot split for the current target; build the MSE-observable scorer (CRPS / NLL / coverage / RMSE on beam-on slices only). Nothing downstream is scorable without this. (opus)
  2. T2 — EnKF baseline (the bar). Stand up the classical EnKF/EKF comparator — it does not exist today; "our EnKF" is a methods-survey entry, and the deep-ensemble MLP is a different, weaker comparator. Score it on T1's harness. (opus)
  3. T3 — Multi-modal loaders. Extend the dataset to SXR / bolometer / camera / Thomson with per-modality alignment + missing-channel handling. (sonnet)
  4. T4 — obs→state v0. A first end-to-end model on Tier-1 inputs with the GS consistency head; beat the EnKF on at least one win axis. (opus)
  5. T5 — Scale + non-Gaussian head. Full multi-modal fusion + heavy-tail / flow posterior; full 4×H200 training. (opus)
  6. T6 — Acceptance. The three-axis comparison vs EnKF, reported honestly (including where it loses). (opus)

§8 — Out of scope for v0 (candidate follow-ons)

  • Full continuous q(ψ)/j(ψ) recovery — gated on a trusted synthetic-equilibrium + synthetic-diagnostic forward model to supply non-circular ground truth.
  • Sourcing time-resolved MSE γ(t) from raw UDA / level-2 / an external DD (the level-1 ams gamma is a near-empty stub) — would expand both the eval set and a possible weak-supervision signal.
  • Real-time / control-coupled inference (the amortised single-forward-pass story) once the offline filter beats the EnKF.

§ Decisions

What does the filter recover and get scored on?

LOCKED: MSE-observable / on-axis recovery is the only rule-clean, non-circular, authorable-now win condition (held-out raw MSE on beam-on slices). Full continuous q(ψ) has no non-circular truth in the corpus and is deferred to a synthetic-truth follow-on (§8).

May EFIT (efm, a banned solver output) be used at evaluation time?

LOCKED: EFIT may cross-check LCFS / total Ip / q95 only (where magnetics genuinely constrain); never an input, label, or interior-current reference. Primary internal-current score stays held-out MSE.

Which "beyond-Kalman" architecture anchors the neural filter?

LOCKED: end-to-end obs→state — one model maps raw heterogeneous diagnostics straight to state, recovering the raw-multi-modal-from-day-one motivation and the largest H200 lift. Highest risk / longest runway, so staged behind T1–T4 de-risking. The amortized-RKN+GS path (option 2) is the natural v0 stepping-stone toward it.

What form does the EnKF baseline (the bar to beat) take?

LOCKED (S9, 2026-06-01): the validated EFIT-free GS Green's-function operator (gs/operator.py) is the observation operator H; gs/residual.py is the linearised per-slice update. BINDING caveat from Stage-2 + measured data: a transition-FREE EnKF (random-walk + GS obs) recovers NOTHING internal — magnetics+boundary under-determine j(psi). So the baseline MUST carry a 1-D poloidal-flux-diffusion transition with neoclassical sigma(Te) from Thomson (the §4 closure channel). This makes the EnKF an opus LONG-POLE (RAPTOR-core physics, no repo asset), not a 'wrap operator.py in an ensemble' sonnet task. 'Matched compute' = equal GPU/wall-clock budget; the neural filter keeps the camera/SXR modalities the O(N_ens) EnKF cannot ingest — that asymmetry IS the thesis, stated openly. A win over a transition-free EnKF that recovers no q0 would be vacuous.

How is the MSE observable read out from the latent state (kept clean of MSE leakage)?

LOCKED (S9, 2026-06-01), refined: the readout emits pitch-angle at the MSE sightline geometry (vx0/vy0/rpos/cosbeam, present in ams) and is scored directly vs held-out gamma/pitcha. q0_kappa1.85/rax are computed from the PREDICTED pitch via the SAME 2pt/4pt kappa=1.85 inversion the truth uses -> method-matched (residual = model error, not inversion mismatch). The GS operator stays the magnetics-consistency head, OFF the q0 path. No differentiable flux-solve in v0 (operator.py gives external B, not on-axis q; a full psi(R,Z) solve readout is deferred to a follow-on). Consequence: pitch and q0 are NOT independent win axes (q0 = deterministic fn of pitch + kappa assumption) and are reported as such.

How are visible-camera (rbb) frames encoded into the input set?

LOCKED (S9, 2026-06-01): a learned CNN encodes rbb centre-stack frames into the shared latent — native multi-modal fusion (the camera-from-day-one motivation; a high-dimensional channel the O(N_ens) EnKF cannot ingest). camera_boundary.py boundary/edge-feature is the cheap fallback if the pixel CNN does not pay its way on the win axes.

Which corpus tier does v0 train on?

LOCKED (S9, 2026-06-01) — MEASUREMENT-DRIVEN reversal of the kickoff 'Tier-1 first' recommendation. Co-availability scan (statespace_family_inventory.json): ams&Thomson=4591/4882 (94%); ams&Tier1&Thomson=3348 vs ams&Tier1=3500 (negligible loss). Dropping Thomson would remove the §4 sigma(Te) closure from ~96% of the scored beam-on regime for ~zero corpus gain — and beam-on = high-Te = exactly where Thomson matters most. So Thomson Te/ne is INCLUDED as a MASKED channel + missingness flag over the Tier-1 union training corpus (~7349 shots, MSE-independent; dataset.py already handles missing channels), NOT a restriction to the 3657-shot full-Thomson subset. The closure is present wherever it exists, including almost every eval shot.
