What this sprint delivers (one week, 2026-06-19 → 2026-06-26). A demonstrable, driveable plasma camera world model: give it a short seed context and a pulse schedule / actuator plan, and it generates the MAST rbb camera forward — the plasma responds to the controls (ramp the current, fire the gas puff) and stays coherent for a useful horizon without collapsing to a frozen frame. The committed output is a real-time playable plasma — drive it live (ramp the current, fire the puff) and watch it respond at interactive frame-rates. This sprint delivers the driveable proof (rendered demos: faithful replay, a counterfactual "same start, two plans", a "knob" demo, backed by honest distributional + controllability metrics); the real-time interactive loop is the headline deliverable (§6b, M6). Demonstrable capability is the primary goal; the science — virtual diagnostics, reduced dynamics, discovery — follows, and it follows cheaply because (per the data-assimilation literature) it is the same generative model with observation guidance switched on (§9).

§1 — Objective and the resolved strategy

The world-model line has, across three generations, produced a camera generator that collapses to persistence (predicts ≈ the last frame) under open-loop argmax rollout — the honest record is in world-model-demonstration. The lead has resolved the strategic fork: build a demonstrable "play the plasma" capability first; the science follows. This plan is that sprint.

The reframe that unifies the two long-term goals (and makes the science cheap later). "Drive the plasma from controls" (a WHAM/Muse-style playable model) and "recreate hidden diagnostic feeds from a subset" (the over-diagnosis / virtual-diagnostics thesis) are not two products — they are one generative model run with different amounts of observation withheld. The controls (the pulse schedule) are always present; the diagnostic feeds are optional observation channels. Withhold all feeds → free-run a plausible counterfactual (PLAY, this sprint). Provide a subset and correct against it → track the real shot and fill in the rest (TRACK / virtual diagnostics, the science follow-on). This is exactly the pattern proven in generative weather data-assimilation (Score-based Data Assimilation, Rozet & Louppe, NeurIPS 2023; Appa, 2025): one score/diffusion model does forecasting and assimilation, differing only by inference-time guidance. So building the PLAY core now is not a detour from the state-space north-star (physics-world-model-strategy) — it builds its front-end.

§2 — Diagnosis: why it collapses, and why the fix is cheap

The persistence collapse has two distinct causes, and conflating them is why "train more / scale up" never worked. Naming them tells us exactly what to fix this week.

  • Cause 1 — argmax is mode-seeking (regression to the most-likely token). Trained with cross-entropy and rolled out with argmax, the model emits the single most-probable next token at every position. On a quasi-stationary, mostly-dark plasma frame the most-probable next token is "the same token" — so argmax provably drifts toward "copy the last frame." This is the canonical token-world-model failure and it is fixed by sampling (temperature + nucleus), not by more training. The masked-latent world model EMERALD (2025) reports that stochastic sampling alone removes the mode-collapse-to-persistence; this is a zero-training change for us.
  • Cause 2 — open-loop rollout has no self-correction (exposure bias / drift). The model is trained teacher-forced (always sees true history) but at inference consumes its own imperfect outputs; errors compound and the safest place to drift is, again, "stop moving." The proven, low-engineering fix is context-corruption / noise augmentation: during fine-tuning, corrupt a fraction of the history tokens and let the model learn to correct its own errors — the trick that stabilised GameNGen's playable DOOM (Valevski et al., 2024) and, in continuous form, Diffusion Forcing (Chen et al., NeurIPS 2024) and Self-Forcing (Huang et al., NeurIPS 2025).

A third ingredient makes it a game rather than a passive video: the controls must be load-bearing — the world must visibly respond to them. The mechanism is classifier-free guidance on the controls (train with control-dropout, then amplify the control signal at inference), the same lever Genie 2 uses "to improve action controllability." We already have a falsifiable physical instance of this in hand: the inboard gas-puff command lights a bright emission spot left of the centre column (camera-dynamics-wm-v0 §4b) — the demo's "fire the puff and watch it light up" is also its controllability test.

What we deliberately defer: Self-Forcing-style own-rollout training with distribution-matching (the SOTA stability recipe, but a training-loop rewrite and data-hungry) and a full continuous-diffusion backbone (DIAMOND/DFoT — abandons our VQ investment). The first-pass recipe — sample + context-corruption + control-CFG on the existing checkpoint — is the most data-frugal path, which is why it goes first; real-time decoding (MaskGIT parallel decode / few-step distillation) is sequenced into M6 (§6b), and a sharp conditional-diffusion decoder is the deferred quality step (§6c).

One methodological non-negotiable (the project's recurring lesson, now backed by theory). Per-pixel MSE-vs-persistence cannot credit a generative model: the perception–distortion tradeoff (Blau & Michaeli, CVPR 2018) proves the MSE-optimal prediction is the blurry conditional mean — i.e. persistence wins MSE by construction against any sharp, plausible sample. We therefore score with distributional and controllability metrics (§7), keeping pixel-error only as a luminance-fair sanity check with that caveat stated.

§5 — Architecture: the playable model + the drive surface

We keep the encoder/decoder and the trained transformer; we change inference and fine-tuning, add a control surface, and wrap it in a play harness. Three components:

  1. Sampling rollout — replace argmax in spacetime_dream_v2.py with temperature + nucleus (top-p) sampling over the factorised token head; expose temperature/top-p as knobs. (Cause-1 fix; zero training.)
  2. Context-corruption fine-tune — short fine-tune of the existing checkpoint where a random fraction of history tokens is corrupted/masked each step and the corruption level is fed as a conditioning signal, plus control-dropout so classifier-free guidance is available. (Cause-2 fix + makes controls steerable.) Peak LR ≤ 1.5×10⁻⁴ (3×10⁻⁴ diverged the ~1 B model); warmup→cosine; held-out-best checkpointing; ordinary sbatch (scancel and normal --time are fine; node drains are an environmental SDCC matter, not an agent concern — AGENTS.md §2a).
  3. The play harness — imas_ambix/worldmodel/play.py: take a seed context + a (possibly user-edited / counterfactual) control schedule, roll forward with sampling + CFG, decode through the frozen VQ, and render a video. Supports schedule editing (ramp Ip, fire/zero the gas puff) to produce the counterfactual and "knob" demos.

The control surface (decision control-surface). The most tangible, leakage-clean drive surface is the actuator vector (coil currents, NBI power, gas-puff flow) — it is what makes "fire the puff and watch the spot light" work, and the loader + probe already exist in camera-dynamics-wm-v0. The pulse-schedule demands (XDC setpoints: Ip(t), ne(t), shape) are the purer "operator plan" surface but less visually tangible and carry a leakage caveat (XDC also embeds real-time reconstruction). Recommendation: actuator vector for the v0 demo, pulse-schedule demands as a fast-follow.

Substrate (decision play-substrate). Recommendation: build on spacetime_v2 (mature camera-dream code + decode pipeline + a trained checkpoint to fine-tune from = fastest to a demo), and port in the actuator-vector conditioning + gas-puff probe from camera-dynamics-wm-v0. Refactor its conditioning so the controls are the always-present drive surface and any measured diagnostics are clearly optional (so PLAY can withhold them and TRACK can later assimilate them — Fig 1).

§6b — The committed output: a real-time playable plasma (M6)

The lead's required end-output is a real-time playable plasma — drive it live (ramp Ip, fire the puff) and watch it respond at interactive frame-rates, not a pre-rendered film. The one-week sprint (M1–M5) delivers the offline-rendered proof that the capability exists; M6 makes it real-time and is the headline deliverable this plan commits to.

  • M6 — real-time interactive loop. The ~1 B model decoding 256 tokens/frame sequentially is not interactive as-is; the route to real-time is faster decoding, not a new model. Two composable levers: MaskGIT-style parallel masked decoding over the existing VQ codebook (≈4 confidence-ordered iterations/frame instead of 256 sequential steps — the EMERALD recipe) and, if needed, few-step distillation (LCM/DMD). Wrap it in a live UI (sliders/keys drive the schedule; frames stream). Done-when: a person drives the schedule live and the decoded plasma responds at interactive fps (target ≥ 10 fps) on held-out seeds, still passing the W1 control-response and W2 non-collapse bars.

§7 — Pre-registered demo bar & evaluation (locked before any number)

Following project practice, the win condition is registered before results, and it is a playability bar, not a point-accuracy bar.

  • W1 — it responds to the controls. From one fixed seed, two materially different schedules produce materially different, physically-sensible rollouts (control-divergence > same-schedule sample spread, CI clear of zero); the gas-puff command lights the emission spot and a zeroed-puff counterfactual does not. This is the falsifiable "it's a game, not a video" test.
  • W2 — it does not collapse, and earns it on a fair metric. The sampled rollout's frame-to-frame motion matches GT (change-fraction, not ≈0), and it beats persistence on a distributional score (FVD-vs-persistence / ensemble CRPS) on held-out shots including the bright, high-motion ones. Pixel-MSE and luminance-fair SSIM are reported only as sanity, with the perception–distortion caveat (persistence wins MSE by construction).
  • W3 — it plays as a demo. A driveable artifact exists: set/edit a schedule, watch the plasma evolve coherently for a useful horizon (target: seconds / tens-of-frames) without degenerating — delivered as the three M4 videos. The in-sprint bar is this offline-rendered proof; the committed end-output is the real-time interactive loop (M6, §6b) at ≥ 10 fps, still passing W1/W2.
  • Honest framing (no over-claim). Bursty events (ELM/sawtooth timing) are aleatoric — only their statistics are reproducible, not exact timing. Counterfactual plays have no ground truth; they are judged by plausibility + control-response, never pixel-match. Scarce data (thousands of shots, not billions of frames) means short-horizon, single-machine — not a Genie-3-grade open world.

§8 — Risks & honesty

  • Sampling may add noise, not meaningful diversity. On near-stationary plasma the "diversity" sampling unlocks could be incoherent jitter. M1 measures this directly before we invest in M2; if so, the context-corruption fine-tune (which teaches structured correction) is the real lever, and MaskGIT-style parallel decoding becomes the stretch.
  • Codebook redundancy on dark frames inflates token accuracy while pixel error stays poor — so we score decoded space and treat token-mismatch as the honest independent-information signal (the project's confirmed finding).
  • Drift beyond a few seconds is unsolved across the field. We target a useful short horizon and do not over-claim free-run coherence; longer rollout is the Self-Forcing follow-on.
  • Controls partly entangle with state. Actuator currents are achieved (measured) state, not pure demands; the cleanest "operator plays" semantics is the XDC demand surface, queued as the fast-follow once the tangible demo lands.
  • GPU ops. Ordinary SLURM: scancel and normal --time are fine — node drains are an environmental fabric/driver condition outside our control and an SDCC/admin matter, not an agent concern (AGENTS.md §2a; settled RCA). Standard practice for its own sake: in-process model-load-once for throughput (§2b) and a clean SIGTERM for lossless cancellation. Training runs use up to 6 cards under normal QOS (full GPU-server auth, AGENTS.md §2) and coexist with DeepSeek's 2 (6 + 2 = 8/8 — no need to take it down); design every run resume-safe (frequent latest.pt + clean SIGTERM) so it takes down or yields in seconds.

§9 — Relationship to existing plans & the science follow-on

This sprint is the demonstrable-capability front half of the world-model line; it sits under milestone M4 alongside camera-dynamics-wm-v0 (whose action-conditioning + controllability probe we reuse) and world-model-prototype (whose f-wmp-closed-loop-camera followup it refocuses: closed-loop correction is the TRACK mode, sequenced after the PLAY demo). It honours the camera-role = one-modality lock in physics-world-model-strategy in the precise sense that the playable object is the latent plasma state with the camera as its renderer — generation is the demo surface, the latent is the asset the science uses.

The science follows cheaply, by switching feeds on (Fig 1). Per Appa / SDA, adding observation guidance to the same generative model gives: (1) virtual diagnostics — withhold a feed, condition on the rest, predict it; the per-channel error is the over-diagnosis information-redundancy map (cf. FusionMAE), which honestly tests "tokamaks are over-diagnosed" per-target rather than assuming it; (2) reduced dynamics / discovery on the learned transition (Koopman/SINDy toward dimensionless coordinates); (3) physics-grounded correction via the working EKF substrate (sequential-current-da-v1) without hardcoding a forward simulator — physics stays a soft, falsifiable prior, never a hard law in the loop.
