§1 — Orientation: why this, why now

The 4×H200 Group-A reservation has been idle since S12 closed (2026-06-05) with a qualified negative and "no clean H200 GO". Meanwhile the project's single largest prepared asset sits unused: 9,527 shots of the MAST rbb wide-angle visible camera, encoded to 4.02B Open-MAGVIT2 tokens (S2–S4 era, in-process stream_encode.py path, 308 fps/GPU peak). The camera is unique in this dataset — it views essentially the whole machine: centre column, plasma boundary, both divertors, in one frame.

User directive (2026-06-10): scope fast-return, physics-backed world-model builds that properly use the reservation. The first is this plan: reconstruct the full time-dependent camera stream from a clipped sub-view, and make the reconstruction dynamics-backed — the infill must flow through a learned temporal latent state, not per-frame spatial pattern completion.

Two prior results discipline this plan. (1) S5's honest negative: fine-tuning the 2-D decoder against rFID was a dead end, and rFID is the wrong north-star for a dynamics model — so this plan trains and evaluates in token space with the decoder frozen (baseline imagenet OMAG2), and rFID is banned as a primary metric. (2) The strategy lock camera-role = one-modality (2026-05-29): generative video is a byproduct, never the guiding goal. This plan honours that lock — masked full-stream reconstruction is the training objective; the product is the dynamics-bearing latent state and what it knows about the plasma (§5 D4 probes). The decoded full-stream demo is the byproduct that falls out for free.

§2 — Thesis and pre-registered win condition

Thesis. The full-machine camera stream is governed by a low-dimensional latent plasma state evolving in time (boundary shape, emission front positions, filament/ELM activity, divertor attachment). A model that carries an explicit temporal latent state can therefore reconstruct what a clipped view does not see — including forward in time — where a per-frame spatial inpainter cannot. If true, the latent is a learned reduced-order dynamics extracted from raw data, and it should read out other diagnostics it never saw.

Pre-registered win condition (locked before any model number, per project practice):

  • W1 — dynamics beat statics. At matched parameter count and matched training tokens, the temporal model beats the per-frame spatial-inpainting baseline on masked-token NLL / top-1 accuracy over the held-out shot split, with bootstrap CI clear of zero.
  • W2 — forward-horizon reconstruction. Conditioned on the clipped stream up to time t only, the temporal model reconstructs full frames at t+h (h = 10, 50, 200 ms) better than (a) persistence of its own t-frame reconstruction and (b) the per-frame baseline given the t-clip. This is the axis where a spatial inpainter has no mechanism at all.
  • W3 — the latent knows physics. A frozen linear/shallow probe from the temporal model's latent predicts held-out raw diagnostics (Dα ada, line-integrated density ane, Thomson Te_core, n=2 mode amplitude ama) better than the same probe from the per-frame baseline's representation.
  • Stretch (honest-low prior) — interior probe. The same frozen probe scored on the S9 oracle sightline-pitch metric (D1 harness, 112 held-out MSE shots). Every multimodal arm so far has returned ~0.41 rad near-axis / no win over processed magnetics; expectation is a clean negative, and a clean negative is a result. No win is claimed in advance.

Primary metrics are token-space (NLL, masked-token accuracy) plus probe RMSE/CRPS on raw signals. Pixel-space L1/SSIM via the frozen decoder is reported as a secondary diagnostic only. rFID appears nowhere.

§4 — Architecture

§4a — Masking curriculum: the clip moves in position AND size

The clip is randomized per training sample along every axis that changes what the model must infer, so one model learns the whole family of partial-view problems:

  • Position: window centre uniform over the frame — the model cannot memorize "the centre column is always visible"; sometimes it sees only a divertor leg, sometimes only outboard midplane.
  • Size: visible-area fraction sampled over ~[5%, 50%] (log-uniform), with aspect jitter. Small clips force inference; large clips anchor learning early (curriculum anneals from large to the full range).
  • Moving clip (panning): the window drifts slowly across the frame during the sequence — a panning sub-view. This mode uniquely rewards a temporal latent: the model must fuse regions seen at different times into one coherent machine state. A per-frame inpainter gains nothing from the pan.
  • Temporal frontier: clip visible only up to time t; everything after t masked (the W2 forecasting mode).
  • Full mask: no camera input at all — reconstruct the stream from the actuator/scalar conditioning alone (hardest mode; doubles as a generative sanity check and measures how much of the camera is actuator-determined).

Evaluation freezes a small suite of named geometries — the §2 fixed window, a small divertor-only window, a centre-column strip, one standard pan, and the frontier mode — so every arm and ablation is scored on identical tasks.

§4b — Action conditioning: a controlled world model

With the actuator vector (coil currents, NBI power, gas-puff flows, Ip, density) as conditioning, W2 becomes the standard world-model rollout: predict the future camera stream given past observations and the future actuator commands. That is the form that matters for control and for what-if reasoning, and it gives the dynamics arm information a spatial inpainter cannot use even in principle. It also yields a directly falsifiable physics check: the inboard gas-puff flow (aga/inboard_*) is the known cause of the bright emission spot left of the centre column — D3 tests whether the model lights that spot when (and only when) the puff command fires, including on held-out shots where the puffed region is masked. Actuator channels are resampled/held to the camera frame times with their native Δt recorded.

The 218 LFQ vocab is predicted with a factorized bit-head (18 binary logits or 2×9-bit factorization) rather than a 262k softmax — decision vocab-head.

§7 — Risks and honesty

  • Static-scene degeneracy. Much of each frame is vessel background — a spatial inpainter may do well on bulk masked tokens. This is exactly why W1 is matched-arm and W2 (forward horizon) exists: the dynamics claim must be earned where statics cannot compete. Metrics are additionally reported on a motion-weighted token subset (tokens whose identity changes within ±50 ms).
  • S5 shadow. No decoder tuning, no rFID. Token-space + probes only.
  • Interior-information wall. Three sprints (S8/S9/S12) say the interior current is not recoverable from edge-view modalities; the stretch pitch probe is framed as a clean test and its prior is low. The plan's primary value does not depend on it.
  • Cadence heterogeneity. rbb frame rates vary 100–400 Hz across the corpus; the dataset carries per-frame timestamps and the model conditions on Δt (or resamples to a common grid — D0 decides and documents).
  • Camera-role lock. Generation stays a byproduct. If the project drifts toward video quality for its own sake, that is scope failure — the deliverable is the latent and its probes.

§8 — Transferability: what carries to other devices, honestly

What does NOT transfer: the trained weights' pixel/token mapping. The model learns this camera's pose, this vessel's geometry, MAST's emission patterns — pointing the checkpoint at another machine's camera is not a meaningful operation. Anyone claiming otherwise for a single-machine, single-view training set is overselling.

What DOES transfer — and why this is not a toy:

  • The capability finding. The decision-relevant question this answers is how much machine-wide plasma state is inferable from a partial view, and how much of the remainder is fixed by the actuator commands. ITER and most reactor-class devices will have viewport-starved, partially-occluded imaging; MAST's full-machine view is the rare dataset where the full-view ground truth exists, so the partial→full inferability curve (W1/W2 vs clip fraction, §4a) can actually be measured. That curve — not the weights — is the exportable result.
  • The recipe. Tokenize → masked spatio-temporal dynamics training → matched-arm dynamics-vs-statics attribution → action conditioning → frozen-probe physics readout. Machine-agnostic end to end; the tokenizer is already an imagenet-pretrained, non-MAST model.
  • The dynamics core as initialization. The temporal latent learns plasma phenomenology (ELM bursts, filament motion, puff responses, strike-point evolution) that is physics, not geometry. The cheap test of how much, without leaving our data: cross-camera transfer inside MAST — adapt the rbb-trained model to the rba camera (different view, same plasma; present in the corpus) with a small adapter and limited shots, against a from-scratch control at matched budget. Queued as a D5 stretch; a MAST→MAST-U fine-tune is the natural follow-on if MAST-U camera data is mirrored later.
  • Invariant-coordinates hook. The strategy's open extrapolation-coordinates decision applies here too: actuator conditioning is expressed in normalized/physical units (A, MW, mbar·l/s, not raw DAC counts) so a future cross-device latent has a chance of sharing coordinates. Designed in now, exercised later.

§9 — Pretrained models vs from scratch

Considered and decided as model-provenance (open, recommendation below). The honest trade:

  • For fine-tuning a video foundation model (NVIDIA Cosmos world models, V-JEPA-2-class video encoders): far more visual prior than 4.02B tokens can teach; plausibly better sample efficiency on W2.
  • Against (for v0): (1) the matched-arm science of W1 requires full architectural control — a foundation model has no honest "same network, time off" baseline, so the dynamics attribution collapses; (2) our prepared asset is OMAG2 token streams — pixel-space foundation models abandon the corpus and the S4 I/O work, and a Cosmos-tokenizer re-encode was already explored (/work/projects/imas_gpu/mast-tokens/cosmos/ exists) without being adopted; (3) S5 is the standing warning about fine-tuning pretrained vision models against objectives they were not built for; (4) a 150–300M scratch transformer trains in hours on the 4×H200 — sample efficiency is not the binding constraint, the idle reservation is.
  • Resolution: v0 from scratch in token space for the controlled science; a pretrained-arm bench (fine-tune one video foundation model on the identical eval suite) is queued as a D5-gated follow-on if W1/W2 succeed — at that point the question flips from "do dynamics help" to "what is the best dynamics engine", where foundation models legitimately compete. Weights would be staged via a network-connected node (GPU nodes have no outbound network).

User-locked 2026-06-10 on the v0 recommendation. Factorized space/time causal transformer gives the cleanest matched-arm W1 comparison — the per-frame baseline (D1) is the identical network with temporal attention disabled, isolating exactly the value of dynamics.

User-locked 2026-06-10 on the recommendation. 18 binary logits, native to the LFQ tokenizer and cheap; avoids the 262k-way softmax at the 16x16xT scale.

Which raw signals condition the model in v0?

User-locked 2026-06-10 on the recommendation.
