§1 — Headline

Two byte-identical 202M-parameter factorized space/time transformers were trained on the 9,527-shot / 4.02B-token MAST rbb camera token corpus under identical data, seed, masking curriculum, conditioning, batch and step schedules. The only difference is one boolean: the baseline arm's temporal attention is forced to a diagonal mask (each frame sees only itself — a pure per-frame spatial inpainter), while the dynamics arm's temporal attention is causal across frames. Any performance gap is therefore attributable to dynamics — the ability to carry a temporal latent state — and to nothing else.

Result: the dynamics arm wins, and it wins where it should. On 764,707 held-out masked tokens the dynamics arm improves reconstruction NLL by 0.46 nats overall, but the per-geometry decomposition is the real finding: the gain is small on pure-spatial masks (+0.04…+0.13 nats) and large exactly on the two modes where a per-frame inpainter has no mechanism — the panning clip (+0.51 nats) and the temporal frontier (+2.55 nats, top-1 accuracy ×40). The model earns its keep by remembering what it saw and propagating it forward in time, not by interpolating better within a frame.

§2 — The experiment

  • Corpus: 9,527 shots of the rbb full-machine visible camera, encoded to (T,16,16) int32 Open-MAGVIT2 LFQ tokens (vocab 218), ≈4.02B tokens. Shot-level splits: train 7,535 / val 942 / held-out 1,050 (the 108 token-carrying MSE shots are forced into held-out).
  • Model family: factorized space/time causal transformer, dim 1024 × 12 layers × 16 heads = 201.93M parameters (verified identical across arms), LFQ 18-bit factorized head, full physical actuator conditioning (coil currents, NBI power, gas-puff flows, Ip, line-integrated density; EFIT/Solov'ev/pulse-schedule banned as leakage) plus per-frame Δt.
  • Objective: bitwise BCE on the masked (clipped-away) cells; masking mixture of random window / panning / temporal frontier / full mask with a large→full-range curriculum; evaluation on a frozen, deterministic named-geometry suite so every arm scores identical tasks.
  • Budget: 20,000 steps × batch 32 × 16 frames × 256 cells ≈ 2.6B scored grid-cells per arm; 0.73 s/step; 7 h 17 m per arm, both arms concurrent on one H200 each, sharing the 4×H200 node with the DeepSeek-V4-Flash-2x serve on the other two cards.

§3 — Training behaviour

The dynamics arm's training loss separates from the baseline's early and stays below it for the entire run — visible already at step ~2,000 and persistent through the cosine decay. Final validation NLL is nearly identical between arms (10.65 vs 10.65) because the val protocol draws fresh random-window masks only, the mode where dynamics helps least; the held-out suite (§4) is where the difference shows.

§5 — The paired verdict: beating the baseline AND the trivial predictor

Aggregate gaps are not the pre-registered test. The locked win condition is a paired bootstrap: both checkpoints score the same windows under the same masks, so each masked token yields a paired difference, and the verdict requires the 95% CI on the mean paired diff to be clear of zero in the dynamics-favouring direction. A second, independent bar was added on user direction: the dynamics arm must also beat a zero-order-hold (carry-forward) reference — for every masked cell, predict the most recent observed token at that grid location (causal; equal to last-frame persistence in the frontier mode). Carry-forward is the "the scene does not change" null hypothesis: a temporal model that only exploited static-scene redundancy would not clear it.

Verdict: dynamics_wins = True — every bar cleared, decisively. Over n = 764,707 paired held-out tokens (the same windows, masks and token order for all three predictors):

Three readings of the carry-forward comparison are worth spelling out:

  • Static-geometry masks defeat carry-forward entirely (ZOH top-1 = 0.000 on the fixed window, divertor window and centre strip): when the same cells are always hidden, there is no past observation to carry — every reconstruction there is genuine spatial+temporal inference, not replay.
  • In the panning mode carry-forward gets a foothold (0.0020) because the moving window re-exposes cells; the dynamics arm still scores 3.7× higher (0.0074).
  • In the forecasting (frontier) mode carry-forward becomes last-frame persistence — the strongest trivial forecaster — and reaches 0.0178. The dynamics arm beats it at 0.0242 (+0.0064 [+0.0060, +0.0068]): the model predicts how the scene changes, not merely that it stays the same. This is the check that the temporal win is dynamics, not exploited redundancy.

§5a — Seeing it: decoded reconstruction demonstrations

The token-space numbers become tangible when the predictions are decoded back to camera images through the frozen Open-MAGVIT2 decoder (never fine-tuned). Every panel and animation below is a three-way comparison — ground truth | trained per-frame baseline | dynamics — with time increasing left→right (offsets in ms). Two display choices follow directly from the data:

  • The visual baseline is the trained per-frame inpainting arm, not the zero-order hold. ZOH carries no information outside the observed cells (it renders as corruption), so it stays a quantitative sanity floor (§5); a real trained model is the honest visual comparator.
  • Per-column brightness normalisation. The pipeline normalises each shot by a single global min/max, so genuine intra-pulse brightening would otherwise swamp the structure. Each time column is contrast-stretched to its ground-truth frame's 1st/99th percentile, and that scale is shared across all three rows — structure is legible while any reconstruction over/under-shoot stays honestly visible.

§5a.1 — ELMs: does the dynamics latent carry edge bursts?

ELMs (edge-localized modes) are fast, bright divertor/edge bursts — the sharpest visual test of whether the dynamics latent carries real plasma evolution. ELMy windows were located from the Dα signal (ada/dalpha_raw_full, a ~1.43 kHz radial-profile trace — adequate to locate ELM activity, though not the genuine sub-ms fast carrier xim/da_hm10_t at ~67–250 kHz; used only to select windows, never as a model input, so no leakage) cross-checked against camera divertor-band brightness, and the window is decimated to span a real ~1–2 ms ELM timescale (a native 16-frame window spans only ~0.1 ms, far shorter than an ELM rise/fall).

Honest caveat — corrected by measurement. Earlier text here attributed the missing filament detail to the 16×16 token grid; a tokenizer round-trip refutes that. Decoding the ground-truth tokens of a real ELM retains 95% of the fine edge (high-frequency) energy — SSIM 0.996, 99% filament contrast — so the tokenizer preserves the macroscopic ELM structure. The real limit is the camera (the prediction target): it samples at a median 0.2 ms (24% of ELM shots ≥ 1 ms) and integrates the sub-ms ELM onset into 1–2 frames, clipping to white at the peak. Sub-ms onset and timing are gone at acquisition — irrecoverable by any model, tokenizer, or conditioning. What these figures show is a clean morphology reproduction and a forward-evolving forecast, not deterministic onset/filament-timing prediction: a target-sampling ceiling, not a tokenizer or model limit. Full analysis, evidence, and the prioritized near-term plan: ELM Fidelity — Sampling Ceiling vs Fixable Morphology Response.

§5b — Forward horizons: 10 / 50 / 200 ms vs persistence

The explicit forecasting win condition asks for full-frame reconstruction at physical lead-times of 10, 50 and 200 ms beyond a conditioning frontier, scored against persistence and against the per-frame baseline. The cadence reality found above forced a protocol choice: rbb frame spacing is heterogeneous (~13 µs to ~1 ms per frame), so a contiguous 16-frame window cannot reach the longer horizons on most shots. The evaluation therefore reads wide 256-frame native windows and decimates each to the model's 16 frames spanning the horizon range — legitimate because the model is Δt-conditioned, so the wider spacing enters the conditioning vector. Shots still too fast to reach a horizon report valid = 0 rather than being silently dropped.

In short: dynamics genuinely buys ~10 ms of forward forecast over persistence, then gracefully converges to it as the scene decorrelates.

§5c — Gas-puff attribution: an honest null

The falsification probe asked whether the model lights the inboard gas-puff emission region when (and only when) the measured aga inboard flow command fires. Protocol: mask the inboard token-column band on held-out shots with non-trivial puff activity (45 shots, 195 windows, 2,005 frames), reconstruct with the dynamics arm, and score a predicted-change activity signal in the masked region against the command trace — plus a counterfactual rerun with the puff channels zeroed in the conditioning vector.

Result: null. Pooled Pearson correlation between command and predicted activity is −0.02; the counterfactual zeroed-puff delta is +0.0001 with a CI crossing zero; the per-shot correlation median is 0.004 with 54% of shots positive — a coin flip. With this token-change proxy at this cadence, no marginal puff attribution is detectable. This is consistent with the signals-only demo above: the conditioning fixes coarse, slowly varying geometry, while the marginal, fast, localized effect of a single actuator channel is below this probe's sensitivity (or genuinely not used by the model when visible context already constrains the region). A sharper probe — decoded-image photometry of the puff spot rather than token-change counting — is the natural follow-on if this question stays live.

§5d — Conditioning ablation: how much do the signals add?

Three dynamics arms differing only in their conditioning channels were trained at a matched 8,000-step budget — camera-only (no conditioning), Ip + ne only, and the full actuator vector — and scored on the held-out suite (the full-conditioning arm uses its own 8,000-step checkpoint so all three share an identical token budget; the headline 20,000-step result above is a separate, fuller-trained arm). The honest finding: on the partial-view mixture the conditioning is essentially a wash — held-out NLL is 10.326 (full) / 10.315 (Ip+ne) / 10.321 (camera-only), all within 0.01 nats over 764,707 tokens; full edges camera-only on top-1 (+0.00025, significant) but is marginally behind on NLL. The camera tokens themselves carry almost all the information a partial view needs.

Conditioning earns its keep only where the visible region is sparse or peripheral: on the divertor-only window it improves NLL by +0.115 nats (and the centre-column strip by +0.027), exactly the cases where the camera shows little of the bulk plasma so the actuator state has something to contribute. On the fixed window, pan and frontier modes the difference is within ±0.03 nats. This is consistent with the rest of the report: the signals-only reconstruction recovers gross geometry from actuators alone (§5a), and the marginal effect of any single actuator channel is below detection (the gas-puff null, §5c). For v0 the "action-conditioned world model" framing should therefore be stated precisely — the conditioning's measurable payoff is concentrated in the sparse-view / no-camera regime, not in general partial-view infill, at least at this training budget (8,000 steps is heavily undertrained relative to the 20,000-step headline; the conditioning gap may widen with more training).

§7 — Honest caveats

  • Single seed, single capacity. One matched pair at 202M, seed 0. The paired CIs quantify token-level noise, not run-to-run variance.
  • Top-1 is stringent by construction (exact 218-id match); absolute values are small everywhere and the NLL is the primary metric. rFID is banned; no pixel-space numbers are claimed.
  • Under one epoch of the corpus. 2.6B scored cells against a 4.02B-token corpus; neither arm is trained to convergence on the full data. The comparison is fair (matched budgets) but the absolute numbers will move with longer training.
  • The forward-horizon evidence thins with lead time (§5b): significant over persistence at 10 ms, an underpowered positive trend at 50 ms, n=1 at 200 ms — the wide-window protocol over 150 held-out shots simply yields few long-horizon windows. A larger wide-window eval set is the cheap fix. The frozen-probe diagnostics readout from the latent remains queued in the parent plan; the conditioning ablation lands next.
  • Validation used random-window masks only, which is why arm validation NLLs are nearly equal (10.65 vs 10.65) — by design it is the weakest axis for dynamics; the frozen suite is the discriminating instrument.

§8 — What this buys the parent plan

The matched-arm result establishes the core thesis of camera-dynamics-wm-v0: the full-machine camera stream is governed by a learnable temporal latent, and an action-conditioned model that carries one reconstructs and forecasts what a spatial inpainter cannot. The trained dynamics checkpoint (/work/projects/imas_gpu/mast-checkpoints/camdyn/cap_v1_dynamics/final.pt) is the substrate for the remaining deliverables: the conditioning ablation and gas-puff falsification probe, the physical-horizon forecasting table, and the frozen-probe readout of held-out diagnostics from the latent. The per-geometry decomposition also gives the partial→full inferability framing its first data point: information the clip never shows is recoverable in proportion to how much the temporal stream — not the frame — constrains it.
