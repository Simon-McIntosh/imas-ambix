§1 — Objective & scope

Build one probabilistic inference engine around the grounded latent and the pinned Nova transition:

  • Predict / forecast. Sample transferable closure, source and boundary uncertainty; advance each reference member through Nova under the known or proposed actuator trajectory. A learned stochastic proposal or space-time transformer may widen or distil the ensemble, but it does not replace the reference physics path without parity evidence.
  • Correct / reconstruct. A latent ensemble update folds in each step's absolute, geometry-tagged observations. Innovation updates state and uncertain assumptions; conservation and force balance remain Nova's responsibility. Discrete topology hypotheses stay branched rather than being averaged into a non-physical field.
  • Smooth / reanalyse. A fixed-lag and complete-shot ensemble smoother admits future observations to refine the trajectory. Smoothed products are explicitly non-causal and provenance-firewalled from forecast evaluation.
  • Generate. With observations withheld, the same posterior produces calibrated equilibrium and diagnostic ensembles under edited coil and actuator trajectories. Cameras are decoded as one conditional diagnostic by-product.

The initial operating hypothesis is a 1 kHz observation/innovation clock, a 100 Hz full Nova correction, 10/50/100/250 ms forecasts, a 100 ms fixed-lag smoother and complete-shot offline smoothing. The hybrid state-estimation smoke measures and revises that envelope before full engine training.

§2 — The gate (verifiable done-when)

  1. Overfit / smoke fits (belief NLL drops; decoded rollout recognizable).
  2. Closed-loop beats open-loop on held-out (no persistence collapse) — report the margin.
  3. Calibrated coverage in [0.88, 0.92] on held-out; predictive uncertainty inflates in dream mode.
  4. Dream + track both run from one checkpoint via observation-withholding; tests green; plan collapsed.

§3 — Approach

  1. Transition kernel: command-conditioned space-time transformer prior over the stage-2 latent; the §7a dissipative flux-diffusion structures the ψ evolution.
  2. Correction: a small ensemble in the learned latent; gain from the ensemble spread (LAE-EnKF); KL / free-bits on the belief.
  3. Dream/track harness: observation-withholding schedule; verify the same checkpoint filters and forecasts.
  4. Eval: closed-loop vs open-loop on held-out (the persistence-collapse test); coverage calibration. Score the latent / raw signals, never token-mismatch.

§4 — H200 footprint

Moderate–heavy. Engine training (transformer prior + ensemble) and the ensemble rollouts cost more than stages 1–2, but the gate work is overfit→subset-scale, not the full corpus. A short multi-card run; all 8 cards are free while DSv4 is down (check squeue). Per AGENTS.md §2a/§2b in-process, SIGTERM-clean, resume-safe, frequent checkpoints. The full multi-modal scale-up is stage 4, not here.

§5 — Inherited decisions

Locked in the parent: inference-paradigm = closed-loop filter (transformer prior + ensemble correction); correction-mechanism = small LAE-EnKF ensemble (not KalmanNet). No new architectural decisions here.
