Reading note (research). This synthesises three literature sweeps (conditioning architectures; the "world model ignores its actions" failure mode; causal/identifiability from observational data) against a concrete result: the playable-plasma-wm-v0 M4 gate, where the camera world model ignored its actuator plan — zeroing the entire plan changed the dream by ~0 even on an overfit of transient windows with 80% observation-dropout. It ends with a ranked, concrete re-design + a data strategy. Acronyms expanded on first use.

§1 — The finding

M1+M2 produced a genuinely good camera forecaster (beats persistence 4/4, drift-resistant). But M3 + the M4 gate showed it is not controllable: the actuator plan is not load-bearing. The M4 gate is decisive because it stacked everything toward controllability — actuator-PLAN conditioning (not measured signals), transient windows where the plan genuinely varies, 80% observation-dropout, and a full overfit — and still measured a true-vs-zeroed-plan margin of 0.0008 (vs a 0.02 bar) with a literal pixel counterfactual of 0.0000. Classifier-free guidance (CFG) could not help. The literature explains exactly why, and the explanation has three layers.

§2 — Why it happens (three converging causes)

(a) The conditioning architecture is the weakest one. The canonical DiT ablation (Peebles & Xie, 2023) ranks conditioning mechanisms AdaLN-Zero > cross-attention > in-context (prepended/concatenated) tokens — and prepended tokens, which is what our model uses, is last. The mechanism (AdaLN-Zero analysis): a prepended condition "must win a competition for self-attention against the content tokens and reach the network at a single injection point", so a model with a rich camera history simply down-weights it. AdaLN instead multiplicatively scales/shifts every block via per-layer affine parameters predicted from the condition, with a zero-initialised gate that earns influence — structurally load-bearing. Current world models inject actions this way: DIAMOND via AdaGN per residual block (Alonso et al., NeurIPS 2024), Cosmos by adding an action embedding to every block's timestep embedding.

(b) The predictable-history shortcut — a named theorem. Schmidt & Jiang (2026) prove that when the exogenous/predictable component of the dynamics dominates the controllable component, the action signal collapses and is ignored regardless of bottleneck size — driven by (i) predictable variance exceeding action-driven variance, (ii) near-deterministic data-collection, and (iii) observation leakage. Our quasi-stationary camera history is near-deterministic, so the plan carries little marginal information. This is also why CFG fails: CFG scales (ε_cond − ε_uncond), which is ≈0 when the action is ignored — it amplifies nothing. The History-Guided Diffusion work is explicit that "CFG-style history dropout performs poorly"; the fix is to corrupt the history itself.

(c) Causal under-identification from confounded, unexciting data. Operators set the plan in response to the plasma state, so the plan is confounded with the state; a model fit to logs learns the confounded correlation, and can route all predictive signal through the state/camera tokens while treating the plan as redundant — and be right on the training distribution (causal identification in time series). Worse, our windows are mostly flat-top, which is not persistently exciting: by classical system-identification theory the action→response map is identifiable only where the input actually varies (positive-definite input correlation). Offline-RL coverage/concentrability says the same: near-zero action variance ⇒ the action gradient is statistically unrecoverable ⇒ the model collapses to a persistence/autonomous predictor. This is the deepest cause, and the lead's instinct to add transients is exactly the theoretical remedy.

§3 — The fix (ranked by evidence × leverage)

  1. Bottleneck the camera history — strongest lever, and our key miss. The plan is ignored because the past frames alone suffice. Noise/mask the camera-history tokens (Diffusion-Forcing-style independent per-frame noise; Chen et al., NeurIPS 2024) so the model cannot coast on the predictive past and the plan must carry what history cannot. Critical correction: the M4 gate's 80% dropout was on the observation signals, leaving the camera history fully available — so the bottleneck was on the wrong channel. It must sit on the information content of the past frame tokens reaching the dynamics head. Caveat: too much history corruption aggravates drift/forgetting (FramePack) — sweep the strength against the gate.
  2. AdaLN-Zero per-layer plan conditioning (replace prepended tokens). Add a small MLP plan → (γ_ℓ, β_ℓ, α_ℓ) per transformer block, with α zero-init so it starts as identity and earns influence. A bolt-on to the existing token transformer; the one change the architecture literature unanimously endorses.
  3. Inverse-dynamics auxiliary head — predict the plan from consecutive latents (z_t, z_{t+1}). Schmidt & Jiang's Prop 4.4 shows this forces the action into the latent with as little as 1% labels; we have 100% (the plan is fully known), so it is cheap and directly attacks the root cause. Pairs naturally with (1).
  4. CFG last. Keep Vid2World-style per-frame action-dropout + guidance as a test-time controllability knob (Vid2World, 2025) — but only after (1)–(3) make the conditional score genuinely action-dependent. It is an amplifier, never the mechanism.
  5. (Optional) predict the residual / shorten context so the static, history-predictable background stops dominating the loss; and a counterfactual/contrastive control term only if (1)–(3) underperform.

§4 — The data strategy (the possibly-binding constraint)

Architecture and objective fixes cannot manufacture information that is not in the data. Because the plan is confounded and flat-top is unexciting, observational data may structurally under-identify the control map. The data levers, in order:

  • Transients are not optional. Ramp-up/down phases supply the input variation that makes the plan→camera map identifiable (persistent-excitation theory). The decisive precedent: the TCV neural state-space model (Nature Comms 2025) learned an actuator-conditioned plasma world model specifically by training on 442 rampdown/transient shots, and made actuators load-bearing by structuring them in (physics confinement scalings), not hoping a free network discovers them.
  • Action diversity, not just presence. What is load-bearing is coverage/variance of the plan across shots (different ramp rates, NBI/gas levels, timings). Dreamer 4 (2025) grounds actions from a small action-labeled subset (100h/2500h) — but only if that subset is diverse; diversity, not quantity, is the open requirement.
  • Cheap perturbative / interventional shots break the confounder. A handful of deliberate perturbations (modulate NBI/gas at a fixed operating point; small step responses) turn causal learning into supervised learning and are worth far more per shot than logged flat-top. If real shots are infeasible, simulator-swept inputs (RAPTOR/transport, the SOLPS-NN strategy) supply interventional coverage. A physics prior lowers the data bar (TCV extrapolated +20% Ip from 5 pulses on its scalings).

§5 — Recommended M4 re-design + the gate

Gate metric (adopt first). The normalised action-sensitivity delta (Vid2World ΔN-M): decoded-rollout divergence between the true plan and a random/shuffled plan, normalised — computed on transient windows with a known plan-induced response (per §2c, a near-zero reading on a window where the plan genuinely doesn't move the near-future plasma would be correct, not a bug). Our current reading is ≈0; this scalar is what every fix must move, and the go/no-go for the re-train.

Recommended re-design (apply together, they are complementary): (1) camera-history noising/bottleneck + (2) AdaLN-Zero plan conditioning + (3) inverse-dynamics auxiliary, trained on (4) transient + diverse windows, scored by the ΔN-M gate; CFG added last. Re-run the overfit gate with the corrected history-bottleneck and the ΔN-M metric on transient windows; only on a clear PASS commit the 6-GPU re-train. Honest expectation: the architecture+objective legs (1–3) are high-confidence and cheap; but if the data are genuinely unexciting/confounded, no model tweak will suffice and the binding next step becomes the data strategy (§4) — a small set of perturbative shots or simulator-swept interventional data. That is the most important strategic finding: making the plasma driveable is a data/excitation problem as much as a modelling one.
