Research notes feeding mast-machine-configuration-map. Two coordinated surveys run 2026-07-22: a cited literature/API chronology of MAST machine changes, and a local-corpus scan of what our geometry layer actually sees. Headline agreements: the archive-era machine gained in-vessel hardware (RMP coils) and lost working sensors while the modeled geometry stayed frozen; no central-solenoid or PF coil module ever changed within M5–M9 (that hypothesis maps to the MAST-U rebuild, outside the archive).

§1 — Campaign map and hardware chronology (literature + FAIR-MAST API)

Coil set — constant within the archive

P1 (central solenoid), P2 (divertor field), P3 (startup rings), P4/P5 (symmetric shaping), P6 (antisymmetric vertical control): no documented addition, removal, or rewiring within M5–M9. The CS/centre-column rebuild (0.9→1.7 Vs solenoid, +17–19 PF coils, Super-X divertor coils) is the MAST→MAST-U upgrade (2013–2020) — outside this archive. The in-vessel RMP coils are the archive era's only coil-hardware change, and they are absent from the efm model our solves consume.

Magnetics geometry provenance

The archive's flux-loop geometry derives from detectors.dat_M4 — a pre-2004 configuration file (per the FAIR-MAST geometry documentation and its referenced configuration PDFs: XMB_SAD, XMC_OMV, CCMV, CCMT, XMA_OBV_OBR, XMA_CCBV). The fixed sensor geometry therefore predates every archive-era hardware change. Per-campaign recalibration records are NOT in the open literature (uncertain — flagged for confirmation from MAST calibration records/UDA in the registry work).

Heating & profile diagnostics

  • NBI: two positive-ion injectors (South = SS on-axis, South-West = SW), ~75 keV D, ~2.5 MW each; long-pulse upgrade from 2003; P_NBI ≤ 3.8 MW by 2009. Off-axis deposition was achieved by displacing the plasma vertically up to 0.3 m (M7+) — not by moving the beamline — so off-axis-NBI shots are systematically vertically-shifted equilibria.
  • MSE: pilot 2006; full 35-channel system routine by 2010 (2.5 cm / 1 ms); local corpus sees the ams group from ~shot 20400.
  • Thomson: ~2009-10 major upgrade — rebuilt Ruby (512 points) + new 130-point Nd:YAG core (8×30 Hz, 240 Hz) + edge system.

Ranked relevance to magnetics-based reconstruction

  1. In-vessel RMP coils + casings (≥19031; 12→18 at ~25404) — unmodeled conducting structure between P4 and P5 plus non-axisymmetric applied fields when energised.
  2. Frozen pre-2004 sensor/geometry model — drift grows wherever the real machine diverged (M7+).
  3. Vertically-displaced off-axis-NBI plasmas (M7+) — stress position reconstruction and the P6 model.
  4. Possible mid-2009 / 2011 magnetics recalibration (UNCERTAIN) — would explain a sharp mid-M7 break; confirm from MAST records.
  5. (ruled out) CS/solenoid segmentation change within M5–M9.

Interpretation for the shot map

  • The efm signature is necessary but far too coarse as a configuration key: it is blind to the RMP installs, the sensor erosion, the MSE onset, and the amm availability bands.
  • The registry's era enumeration therefore needs at minimum: 13370 (geometry freeze), 19031 (RMP-12), ~20400 (MSE), 25404 (RMP-18), the amm bands, and the per-shot working-channel sets.
  • Level-2 equilibrium reference is present in every era (65×65 grid) — referee availability is not an era confound.

§2b — Archive tiers, coverage, and the full REST surface (follow-up survey)

Level 1 vs Level 2 — official definitions and counts

From the archive's own tutorials: Level 1 is raw / minimally processed data "without any preprocessing, interpolation, calibration, cropping" applied, "under the original names used during the time of MAST's operation" (ama/amb/amc/amm/efm/…), and includes "all shots that could be pulled from the MAST archive, including instrument calibration and testing shots". Level 2 "includes cropping, interpolation, calibration, etc. of each signal", maps diagnostic groups toward IMAS naming, is FAIR-indexed, and its shot set is filtered by plasma current to remove testing/commissioning shots. L2 groups: summary, pulse_schedule, magnetics, spectrometer_visible, pf_active, soft_x_rays, thomson_scattering, cxrs, equilibrium, gas_injection.

Our pipeline's tier split (verified in code): the engine consumes Level 1 — efm geometry tables (gs/geometry.py), ama/amb/amc/ane sensor windows (latent/data.py), ams MSE truth (mse_eval), amc tf_current for Bt0 — with the project's OWN absolute calibration layer on top; the census/crossval referee and the shot index consume Level 2 (equilibrium group; parquet/level2/shots). Implication: UKAEA's L2 magnetics calibration is an independent instrument against which our L1 calibration can be cross-checked per configuration era — directly relevant to the late-campaign sensor-calibration question.

Pre-M5 coverage

The archive simply begins at M5 (shot 11695, 2004-12-13); the L1 counts confirm nothing earlier is ingested. The FAIR-MAST paper gives no explicit rationale; M1–M4 (1999–2004, shots below 11695) exist only in UKAEA's internal UDA archive (the paper's motivation section: historical data was accessible only through the in-house UDA library, not publicly). A plausible scope choice — labelled inference, not documented fact — is that ingestion targeted the post-2004-rebuild machine (the divertor/centre-column rebuild immediately precedes M5). Access to M1–M4 would go through UKAEA/UDA collaboration, not the open archive.

Full REST surface (OpenAPI)

  • Indexes: /json/shots (L1, 281 CPF-derived fields) and /json/level2/shots (separate L2 index); /json/signals + /json/level2/signals (per-shot signal instances with quality flags); /json/sources + /json/level2/sources (per-shot diagnostic-source instances with quality annotations, e.g. "abm: multi-chord bolometers | Bad").
  • Aggregates: …/aggregate on every index (count/min/max with groupby + filters) — the campaign tables above.
  • Dictionaries: /json/cpf_summary (the description of every CPF summary field) and /json/scenarios (scenario lookup: S1–S8, named scenarios like DN-450-CD-OH / DN-750-CD-1BSW, plus a MAST-U placeholder).
  • Bulk: /parquet/{shots,signals,sources} and /parquet/level2/… (+ ndjson variants) — the right path for the registry's full sweep.
  • Shot logs — yes: per-shot free-text preshot_description / postshot_description (populated from the very first archived shot) plus shot_program / shot_objective / shot_summary / shot_physicist / shot_experiment_tags (fill improves in later campaigns). Campaign logs — no dedicated endpoint: campaign exists only as a per-shot label; campaign-level narrative would come from the literature chronology (§1) or UKAEA directly.
