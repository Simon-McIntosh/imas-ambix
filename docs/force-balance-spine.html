§1 — Thesis and scope

This plan operationalizes the strategy locked out in physics-spine-findings (2026-07-10): the equilibrium line is the project's physics spine, organized in three tiers — exact structure imposed by construction (Maxwell maps, the free-boundary GS fixed point, the force-balance form and its known extensions, sign facts), structured unknowns whose coefficients the data supplies (profile closures, rotation coefficient, static instrument model, passive modes, transport closures), and free residual instruments that are never load-bearing (the patch basis as audit projector, the structure residual, the GS/transport residual fields). "Physics is physics — it does not need to be rediscovered": known forms are never penalised into the solution or learned from scratch; data effort is spent exclusively on what physics leaves free, and the residuals nominate what the term dictionary is still missing.

Scope: the five workstreams below. Out of scope (owned elsewhere): interior-diagnostic fusion and the learned equilibrium prior (equilibrium-topology-fidelity threads 2/4 — this plan feeds them a physical carrier), the toroidal-harmonic annulus read (f-etf-04, unchanged owner), and the 1-D dynamic closures (learned-closure-current-diffusion-v0; W5 only registers the coefficient-level tie). This plan extends and absorbs the remaining scope of plasma-current-priors-hardening f-pcph-04 (static audit + low-DOF closure arm), whose first artifacts — the closure gate and the offset-only calibration — landed in commits 657e1d8…832e2a9 and are consumed by W1/W2.

Wire coefficient_smoothness_penalty where the ladder exceeds the information budget. §3 — W2: harden the Tier-2 nuisance layer

Goal: the static instrument error attributed and independently validated, so no physical arm pays it as misfit and no calibration term launders model error.

  • Independent validation of the static calibration. The offset-only arm is currently a statistical fit against one inverse arm's residuals. Validate it where the truth is pure Tier 1: coil-only/vacuum intervals (pre-breakdown, post-disruption dwell) where predicted sensor values involve no plasma inversion at all. Cross-check offsets fitted against different arms (free, priors, closure) — a genuine instrument offset is arm-independent by definition; divergence flags physics laundering.
  • Gain-arm audit. The full gain+offset fit spreads p10–p90 0.90–1.14, and the held-out arm sweep already shows those free gains are destructive — applying them crashes the prior arms (softplus axis skill −0.05 → −8.9, winner −2.0 → −7.9) while offset-only leaves them intact, i.e. the gains launder model/inverse error rather than measure the instrument. Any real gain error is therefore currently unmodelled: adjudicate on the vacuum intervals and against the machine-description audit (turns/geometry/circuit mapping — the f-pcph-04 attack, continued here).
  • Passive eigenmodes. Wire the existing g_passive block (146 passive nodes) into the closure solve as a rank-k eigenmode sidecar (k ≤ ~8, ridge-limited), OFF by default, measured at the gate — the structured carrier for whatever inductive signal remains after static calibration, and the prerequisite for honest ramp/transient work.
  • Re-gate R1/R2 with calibration on. The priors-hardening re-gate followup is superseded in the calibrated world: re-run Gates A/B with the frozen winner flags on calibrated payloads; revisit the prior-defaults decision with the new evidence (calibrated priors arm already reads axis 3.8 cm median, X-point-set skill +0.29 [0.03, 0.41]).

§4 — W3: the synthetic-truth identifiability harness

Goal: the standing instrument that would have caught the null-space fill automatically, and the gate every future term promotion must pass.

  • Manufactured equilibria with known Tier-2 coefficients — p₀′/FF′ families across the realistic range, ± rotation (known γ), ± static calibration corruption (known offsets/gains), ± passive currents (known mode amplitudes) — pushed through the full forward chain to synthetic sensor values at MAST geometry and realistic noise.
  • Recovery tests with CIs for every arm (free, priors, closure, calibrated closure): which injected coefficients are recovered, which alias, and at what noise each becomes invisible. The aliasing matrix (e.g. rotation → p₀′ contamination magnitude; calibration → halo) becomes a reference artifact.
  • Information accounting: effective rank and per-coefficient Fisher information of each sensor set (magnetics alone; + interferometer; + SXR; + CXRS), giving equilibrium-topology-fidelity thread 2 a quantitative shopping list instead of a plausibility argument.
  • Wired as a pytest-marked suite (fast subset in CI; full sweep on demand) so the gate is standing, not a one-off study.

§5 — W4: the rotation ladder (first traversal of the discovery loop)

Goal: the rotation term measured through the full promotion path — or a recorded, CI-backed negative. Physics and staging per physics-spine-findings §5: the form is exact (Maschke–Perrin; R·jφ ≈ aR² + b + cR⁴ with c ≈ ½p₀(miΩ²/T)′), the coefficient is one sign-definite flux function γ(ψ) = miΩ²/2T ≥ 0, MAST is where the term is both largest (thermal Mach 0.3–0.8 ⇒ in-out pressure ratios 1.2–4) and most identifiable (R⁴ column ~15% orthogonal to the static design vs ~7% at conventional aspect ratio).

  • Stage A — residual diagnostic. Wire affine-r2-rotation as a gate reporting arm: held-out per-bin unexplained-fraction delta (3-column vs 2-column) with CIs, and the recovered c(ψ) profiles stratified by measured rotation. Cheap; no solver change; answers "is rotation visible in our residuals at all".
  • Stage B — measured-rotation forward test (zero new DOF). γ(ψN) built from CXRS toroidal rotation (act, 7,991 shots, ~5 ms) + Thomson Te→T proxy (ayc); the closure-arm ansatz extended with the fixed centrifugal factor; the calibrated closure gate re-run. Falsifiable prediction: held-out misfit and topology skill improve on high-rotation shots. Firewall-clean (all raw measurements). Either verdict is a result.
  • Stage C — discovery mode (gated on W3). Free γ(ψ) ≥ 0 (softplus) as a Tier-2 coefficient, only after the W3 harness demonstrates recoverability at realistic noise; natural pairing with SXR in-out asymmetry when thread 2 lands it.
  • Register (build nothing): the toroidal momentum budget (NBI torque in the plan − sign-definite losses) as the future dynamic guard-rail slot when rotation joins the latent state.

§6 — W5: formalize the discovery loop

  • Term-dictionary registry: a versioned artifact (candidate form, physics reference, identifiability status, gate verdicts, promotion state) covering at minimum: rotation R⁴, pressure anisotropy, passive modes, calibration terms, halo/SOL budget — turning §6.3-style inventories into a standing measured/unmeasured table.
  • Metric hardening: trimmed/median skills reported alongside RMSE skills (the Gate-A tail-domination lesson); the cost-threshold sweep as the standard accuracy/coverage report.
  • Coefficient-evolution tie: couple the closure coefficients to the transport prior at the state level — (β0, α, γ) as slowly-evolving quantities in the filter, giving temporal coherence as a regularizer and connecting the static spine to the M3 dynamic-closure line without merging the plans.

§7 — Gates, sequencing, relationships

  • Sequencing: W1 and W2 start immediately and in parallel (W1 consumes the in-flight calibrated gate; W2's vacuum-interval validation is independent). W3 starts once W1's continuous-fit refactor stabilizes the chain it must instrument. W4 Stage A rides any gate run after W1; Stage B needs W1+W2 landed; Stage C needs W3. W5 accretes throughout and closes last.
  • Gate FB-1 (carrier): calibrated closure arm — axis and LCFS skill vs the referee with CIs at ≥ 50% coverage, plus the full cost sweep. Pass = LCFS skill CI clear of zero at that coverage; either way the representation-default decision is taken on the numbers.
  • Gate FB-2 (nuisance): static calibration validated on coil-only intervals (offset agreement within CI across arms); gain-arm adjudicated; passive-mode arm measured load-bearing-or-not.
  • Gate FB-3 (rotation): Stage A + B verdicts recorded with CIs either way; Stage C only if B is positive and W3 shows recoverability.
  • Relationships: depends on plasma-current-priors-hardening (harness + priors + the f-pcph-04 scope this plan absorbs), patch-current-force-balance (substrate) and gs-grounded-latent-engine (solver). Feeds equilibrium-topology-fidelity (threads 2/3/4 inherit the carrier + the information accounting), the amortized encoder line (the spine coefficients are its prediction target), and the North-Star discovery layer (W5's registry is its equilibrium instantiation). EFIT stays firewalled referee everywhere; leakage-free tune/eval protocol unchanged.
