§1 — Thesis and scope

This plan operationalizes the strategy locked out in physics-spine-findings (2026-07-10): the equilibrium line is the project's physics spine, organized in three tiers — exact structure imposed by construction (Maxwell maps, the free-boundary GS fixed point, the force-balance form and its known extensions, sign facts), structured unknowns whose coefficients the data supplies (profile closures, rotation coefficient, static instrument model, passive modes, transport closures), and free residual instruments that are never load-bearing (the patch basis as audit projector, the structure residual, the GS/transport residual fields). "Physics is physics — it does not need to be rediscovered": known forms are never penalised into the solution or learned from scratch; data effort is spent exclusively on what physics leaves free, and the residuals nominate what the term dictionary is still missing.

Scope: the five workstreams below. Out of scope (owned elsewhere): interior-diagnostic fusion and the learned equilibrium prior (equilibrium-topology-fidelity threads 2/4 — this plan feeds them a physical carrier), the toroidal-harmonic annulus read (f-etf-04, since migrated to its own plan toroidal-harmonic-annulus-read — the parallel boundary lane whose interior carrier follows this plan's representation-default decision), and the 1-D dynamic closures (learned-closure-current-diffusion-v0; W5 only registers the coefficient-level tie). § Decisions

Which representation is the default equilibrium carrier for the world model?

Taken on the Gate FB-1 numbers (held-out, 160 slices, CIs): with the fit machinery fixed the closure carrier explains the magnetics at cost median 0.56 with 2 profile DOF + rank-8 passive modes (grid arm: 12.2), reads the axis at 3.7-4.0 cm median at 100% coverage (was 86 cm at 4% self-consistent coverage), posts axis skill -0.31 [-1.09, -0.00] and the closure line's first positive X-point-set point estimate (+0.11), and satisfies every contested constraint by construction. The LCFS bar itself failed (-3.6, negative in EVERY representation) with the cause localised OUTSIDE the representation: vacuum-clean sensors + broad-FF' railing + the outboard fixed-point attractor all point at the static coil model. The ladder measured the information budget at ~1-2 profile DOF (K=2..10 identical to two decimals) — more DOF is not what the data want, so the low-DOF closure carrier is the right altitude. Patch basis re-scoped to Tier-3 audit + Tier-2 nuisance carrier; annulus read stays the boundary cross-check. Record: archive/force-balance-spine-w1-w2-landed.html.

How does the rotation coefficient γ(ψ) enter the solve?

Stage A ran held-out (commits fcc5489/8030c3f, artifact rotation_residual_diagnostic.json): the Maschke-Perrin R4 column is VISIBLE but sub-dominant — it absorbs ~10.4% (free arm) / ~6.1% (sign-constrained arm) of the per-bin unexplained residual with bootstrap CIs excluding zero; the recovered c(psi) is cleanly one-signed (dominant-sign fraction 1.0) peaking near-axis, and |c| correlates with measured CXRS line-average rotation on the sign-constrained arm (Spearman 0.54 [0.21, 0.72]). BUT high/low rotation strata do not separate (the residual delta concentrates at the edge while rotation-correlated |c| lives near-axis), a third LSQ column mechanically lowers residuals, and CXRS coverage is only 3/8 held-out shots — so the signature does not yet justify a free Tier-2 coefficient. Verdict: keep the staged path. Stage B (fixed gamma(psiN) from CXRS+Thomson, zero new DOF, falsifiable) proceeds behind the coil-model remainder (f-fbs-05) and needs a broader-CXRS cohort to power the stratified test; Stage C stays gated on the identifiability harness (f-fbs-06) whose aliasing matrix must show the R4 coefficient is recoverable at realistic noise.

What static-calibration model ships as default on gate payloads?

Offset-only ships flag-gated on gate payloads, NEVER promoted to default, and is now correctly understood: coil-only-interval validation (pre-breakdown + post-dwell, pure Tier-1 forward prediction, no plasma inversion, no EFIT) shows the true per-channel instrument offset is ~0 (median agreement 0.004 sigma) and the six largest plasma-fitted corrections (P4/P5 flux loops, -0.95..-1.50 sigma) are contradicted — the fitted offsets are plasma-coupled static coil-MODEL error laundered into constants, kept only as an explicit empirical residual absorber. Gain+offset stays rejected with vacuum evidence: gains are unidentifiable on quasi-static intervals and eddy-driven on ramps (median 1.25, p10-p90 0.88-1.96, uncorrelated with the rejected plasma-fitted gains). Offsets are shot-stable (between/within variance 0.084) — the cross-shot averaging/filtering proposal is supported, with the caveat that what is averaged is model error. The promotion path for any default calibration runs through the physical coil-model audit.
