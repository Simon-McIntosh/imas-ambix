Plasma-domain decoder fine-tune (Open-MAGVIT2)

Status: Active — scaffold exists at imas_ambix/tokenizer/finetune_decoder.py; no run yet. rFID gate triggered at 16.25 (100-shot in-process bench, job 1208918). S5 sprint deliverable; blocks world-model-v0.

§1 — Why

The 100-shot rbb bench (job 1208918) landed rFID 16.25 — more than 3× the rFID ≤ 5 gate. The primary signal is codebook utilisation: only 2.5% of the 218 = 262,144 ImageNet LFQ codebook entries were touched across 100 plasma shots (~6,554 unique entries). The encoder has converged on a narrow vocabulary for plasma frames, but the decoder still carries full ImageNet-trained weights — so reconstructions look plausible in a generic image sense but are not plasma-faithful (edge artefacts, colour shift, loss of fine boundary structure).

Fine-tuning only the decoder solves this without disturbing the corpus. The encoder and LFQ codebook are frozen — the 9,528 rbb-tokenized shot files at /work/projects/imas_gpu/mast-tokens/v1/rbb/ remain valid. No re-encode pass is needed. The decoder learns to render the narrow plasma vocabulary faithfully.

§2 — Approach

What is frozen: encoder weights + LFQ.codebook (all parameters in model.encoder and model.quantize). What is trained: decoder only (model.decoder parameters).

Loss: L = l1_weight × L1(pixel) + perceptual_weight × LPIPS(perceptual). Defaults from scaffold: l1_weight=1.0, perceptual_weight=0.1. See Decisions for the lpips-weight open decision.

Training data: random sample of the 9,528-shot rbb-tokenized corpus. Split: 7,500 train / 2,000 val (see Decisions for train-val-split). Images are sampled as 256×256 uint8 rbb frames (pre-decoded via the frame-precompute LMDB store); tokens from the matching .npy shard are decoded through the frozen encoder+codebook path to produce the encode→decode round-trip for the L1 + LPIPS loss.

Optimiser: AdamW, LR 1e-4 (from scaffold default), cosine decay, 200-step warmup. Steps: 10,000 max; early-stop if val rFID plateaus for 3 consecutive evals (eval every 1,000 steps). Batch: 16 frames/GPU × 4 GPUs = 64 frames/step.

§ Decisions

How to split the 9,528 rbb-tokenized shots into train / val sets?

Need stable val set for rFID tracking; temporal split guards against leakage if campaigns differ systematically.

What LPIPS (perceptual) loss weight to use for decoder fine-tune?

Scaffold defaults to perceptual_weight=0.1 (l1_weight=1.0). Higher LPIPS weight pushes toward sharper boundaries but may destabilise early training on the narrow plasma vocabulary.
