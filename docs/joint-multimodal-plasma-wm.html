§1 — Goal & motivation

Turn the camera-predictor into a proper generative world model of the plasma state: one model that dreams the cameras and the diagnostics forward, driven by the actuator commands, and that learns the reduced physics of the coil→plasma map rather than memorising token patterns. This plan operationalises the lead's four pushes from the playable-plasma work: (1) the diagnostics should be on, not opt-in; (2) we must be able to dream from a reduced control set; (3) we should dream all cameras and diagnostics; (4) we should train on an objective that matches the diagnostics — plus a state-of-the-art stance on dropped inputs + a reduced latent so the model learns physics, not lookup.

Entry baseline (what we have). The conditioning expansion already paid off. Adding the Dα-boundary (ada/adg/aim) + calibrated L2 magnetics (94 ch) signals to the 6-camera / multi-timescale run flipped the held-out controllability margin from −9.5 (baseline 1221563, 9 streams — true plan moved the dream less than random) to +18.1 (enriched 1221741, 13 streams — true plan now moves it more, and the random-noise floor dropped 60→36). The gate is at threshold (1/3, 1.5× floor), not robustly passed. Direct position observation demonstrably helps — but as conditioning input only. The model still does not generate the plasma state, and a high-capacity token transformer can still memorise. This plan is the next architecture to cross the gate robustly and earn the "in-silico tokamak" claim.

§2 — Architecture & the four levers

The model keeps the proven substrate — frozen Open-MAGVIT2 camera tokens, the factorised space-time transformer, AdaLN-Zero plan conditioning, Δt + camera-id embeddings, the camera-history bottleneck, inverse-dynamics aux, scheduled sampling — and adds four levers, each mapped to a lead question:

  • Joint generation (Q3) + diagnostic-matching objective (Q4). The diagnostics are already quantised to the L2 257-id vocab. Add a lightweight prediction head per stream on the shared backbone and a next-step cross-entropy on the diagnostic tokens. The model then autoregresses cameras and diagnostics — a joint state model. This is the highest-value change: it lets us dream the full state, gives a far richer physics signal (predicting magnetics/Dα forward forces the dynamics into the model), and yields a quantitative eval (dreamt-vs-real diagnostics), not only pixel distance.
  • Per-modality masking (Q2). Today's observation_dropout=0.8 is all-or-nothing (all signals on, or all off). Replace it with independent per-stream Bernoulli masking so the model trains on arbitrary subsets and can dream from any combination — including commands only. Commands stay the load-bearing control; diagnostics become droppable and dreamt.
  • Contrastive levers (all three, ablated). Cross-modal alignment (camera ↔ diagnostics share one plasma-state latent), self-predictive latent (BYOL/SPR future-latent — compact, anti-memorisation), and action-contrastive (true-plan vs wrong-plan next state — which directly trains the ΔN-M gate the baseline failed). Auxiliary to the generative loss; each ablated to prove marginal value.
  • RSSM latent bottleneck (Q5, Phase 2). A compact recurrent latent the dynamics must pass through (Dreamer-style): predict the future in latent space, decode to cameras + diagnostics, with a KL/rate penalty. This is the structural pressure for reduced physics, not memorisation — the latent is the in-silico plasma state.

§4 — Phase 2: RSSM compact latent-dynamics bottleneck

Introduce a low-dim recurrent latent state zₜ that the forward dynamics pass through: encode the masked observations + commands into zₜ, roll zₜ forward conditioned on the commands, and decode zₜ to the camera + diagnostic tokens. Train with the generative decode losses + a KL/rate penalty on the latent (the information bottleneck) + the contrastive levers (self-predictive now operates naturally on zₜ). This is the structural anti-memorisation lever and the genuine "playable" state: in PLAY we provide commands + an initial zₜ and roll forward, dreaming the full state. Phase 2 is gated on Phase 1 validating that joint generation + masking + contrastive move the gate; it is designed-for from the start (the heads/latent interfaces in Phase 1 anticipate the latent) but built second to keep each lever measurable.

§5 — Training objectives

Total loss = camera-token CE + diagnostic-token CE (new) + inverse-dynamics aux + λ·(contrastive terms) [+ β·KL latent rate in Phase 2]. Per-modality masking applies to all observation streams; commands are never masked (CFG/control-dropout only). Each contrastive term and the diagnostic loss carries a weight + an on/off flag so the §6 ablation can attribute the gate movement. Excitation weighting + scheduled sampling + the history bottleneck carry over unchanged.

§6 — Evaluation & ablation grid

Three eval axes, all on the held-out shots (18502–05), reported against the 1221741 baseline:

  • Controllability gate ΔN-M (true vs wrong-plan rollout divergence) — the headline; target a robust pass (≥2/3, comfortably above the 1.5× floor), up from the baseline's borderline 1/3.
  • Diagnostic-match — dreamt vs real magnetics / Dα-boundary forward (new, quantitative): does the model's dreamt plasma state track reality?
  • Dream fidelity — decoded-pixel error vs persistence (the standing honesty check).

Ablation grid (each lever toggled): joint-generation on/off, per-modality masking vs all-or-nothing, and the three contrastive terms individually — to prove each lever's marginal contribution rather than shipping a confounded stack. Phase 2 adds the RSSM latent as a further arm.

Gate robustness audit (2026-06-23): the 18502–05 held-out set is NOT robust — it is effectively 1-shot. Frame evidence decoded from the EXACT gate window classified the shots: 18502 GOOD (bright, transient motion 8.2, true plan drives the dream → +91.6/3.5×); 18503 DEGENERATE by METRIC (GT bright + coherent, true-plan forecast fine, but the RANDOM-plan rollouts collapse and diverge among themselves → an inflated noise floor 29.6 drags the margin negative — the gate fails a good shot); 18504 DEGENERATE by DATA (GT near-black, mean 2.7 ≈ 6× dimmer; the model dreams black regardless of plan → true-vs-random = 0); 18505 DROPPED (span 0.238 s < 0.25 s horizon). All four are real plasma shots (presence 0.75, |Ip| ≈ 745 kA) — Ip does not discriminate; the degeneracy is in the camera window + the metric. Two root causes: bad shot SELECTION (18504) and a fragile METRIC (the random-vs-random floor is inflated by random-rollout COLLAPSE). Revision = f-jmwm-005: screen a larger eval-only cohort (train-disjoint, brightness + transient-motion + transient-plan screened, no retrain) + a noise-floor-normalised, collapse-rejecting ΔN-M.

§7 — Build sequence & risks

Sequencing (staged A→B, the locked approach): (1) diagnostic heads + matching loss + canonical "all-on"; (2) per-modality masking; (3) contrastive auxiliaries + the ablation grid; (4) a Phase-1 broad run + verdict vs 1221741; (5) Phase-2 RSSM latent + KL + re-eval. Each lands behind a config flag, defaulted to the new behaviour once validated. Code edits land in a worktree while any training job is live (the editable install + DataLoader re-import hazard); merge when the node is free. Risks: (a) the diagnostic CE can dominate/destabilise the camera loss — weight + warm up; (b) per-modality masking may over-regularise — tune the rate; (c) the RSSM warm-start is non-trivial (new latent) — expect a partial or from-scratch Phase 2; (d) contrastive negatives/temperature need care — prefer the BYOL self-predictive variant to avoid negative-sampling. The §6 ablation is the guard against shipping levers that don't earn their place.

§ Decisions

Overall architecture approach?

Staged A→B: land joint generation + masking + contrastive on the proven backbone first (measurable vs 1221741), then add the RSSM latent. Honours the reduced-latent goal as the destination while keeping each lever's contribution measurable rather than confounded in one rebuild.

Which contrastive levers to include (each ablated)?

All three: cross-modal alignment (camera↔diagnostics shared state), self-predictive latent (BYOL/SPR, anti-memorisation, no negative-sampling), and action-contrastive (true vs wrong plan — directly trains the ΔN-M gate the baseline failed). Auxiliary to the generative loss; the §6 grid isolates each.

What is the Phase-2 reduced-latent bottleneck?

A compact recurrent latent state (Dreamer-style RSSM) the dynamics pass through — the strongest "reduced physics" pressure; the latent becomes the in-silico plasma state and the PLAY rollout space.

Diagnostic generation: separate per-stream heads on the shared backbone, or one unified token stream (diagnostics as more tokens in the sequence)?

Locked by the lead. Per-stream output heads on the shared backbone: each diagnostic stream keeps its own vocab + channel count, the heads are lighter than a unified token stream, and they warm-start cleanly from the 1221741 backbone (only the new heads init fresh). Unified-token-stream is rejected — it would inflate the sequence length and entangle stream vocabularies.

Phase-2 RSSM: warm-start the encoder/decoder from Phase 1, or train the latent model from scratch?

Reuse is cheap + the latent must be learned fresh. Warm-start the EXPENSIVE, transferable pieces from a Phase-1 checkpoint where shapes allow — the Open-MAGVIT2 camera token embedding, the chunked camera-token decode head, and the per-stream diagnostic heads (all same vocab/d_model as Phase-1) — and train the RSSM recurrent latent core (GRU + prior/posterior + the latent→spatial decoder) FROM SCRATCH. The frozen Open-MAGVIT2 VQ stays the pixel decoder for eval. Rationale: the token I/O embeddings encode hard-won structure worth reusing; the dynamics (the whole point) must be relearned through the bottleneck.
