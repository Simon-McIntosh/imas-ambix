§1 — Objective

Physically ground the Stage-1 RKN latent state-space engine with a self-applied Grad–Shafranov (GS) soft, falsifiable prior on raw magnetics, and stand up the GS force-balance residual as a dynamics-native discovery + OOD instrument — then distil the latent dynamics toward dimensionless coordinates. Three gated deliverables:

  1. Standalone GS force-balance monitor (no engine retrain) — a dimensionless/fractional residual + persistence statistic computed from raw magnetics alone.
  2. Grounding head — a latent → (low-DOF plasma current jφ, restricted p′/FF′, ψ) → raw-magnetics decoder, added to the engine as a soft λ-weighted GS prior (gated on the monitor proving informative).
  3. Discovery layer — effective-dimension SVD + SINDy distillation of the closed-form transition kernel + DMD/Koopman, toward dimensionless coordinates.

The EFIT-label-free trick: the over-determining data constraint that replaces EFIT supervision is reconstruction of the ~50–120 raw magnetic sensors (amb flux loops [Wb] + B-probes [T]) from a low-DOF latent-decoded plasma current plus the known measured conductor currents (amc PF/plasma + amm passive). Raw measured signals only — never efm/esm as inputs OR labels. (The machine geometry source for the forward operator is a separate gating decision — see Decisions.)

§3 — Acceptance criteria (what "the GS prior earns its keep" means)

  • PRIMARY (Q1, leak-free + trivial-baseline-guarded): on held-out known-transient shots the GS residual rt spikes at ELMs (labelled from the raw-Dα compute_transient_mask — an independent label, since the residual consumes magnetics only) and disruptions (raw dIp/dt collapse), AND beats a trivial raw-magnetics-activity baseline (|dB/dt| / B-probe variance) at the same event AUROC, AND survives the eddy-current ablation (amc case/feed + amm folded in as known sources). Without these guards the monitor is just a high-pass filter / eddy detector — Stage-1's pick-a-metric-a-trivial-baseline-wins mistake, one level up.
  • SECONDARY (Q2, criterion-3 closure — honest, NOT the gate): Pt OOD-AUROC head-to-head vs Stage-1's innovation 0.748 / pred-σ 0.831 / static-ensemble 0.810 in the identical engine_metrics protocol — but reported ONLY after (i) regressing Iₚ+ne out (or Iₚ/ne-matched subsamples) and (ii) decomposing quiescent vs transient. Pre-committed reading: survives-deconfound + transient-carried = real physics-native detection; vanishes = the Iₚ²/ne scaling leak (fake win); uniform quiescent offset = closure-misspecification (the ensemble's epistemic mechanism re-derived).
  • FALSIFIABILITY: on the 2-D λ × profile-DOF frontier, the residual is NOT trivially ≈0 at the chosen DOF (p′/FF′ restriction makes r a real measurement) AND data overrides at high λ. Report the frontier, never λ alone.
  • GROUNDING HONESTY: report whether the grounding head HELPS or HURTS Stage-1 Dα forecast CRPS/NLL + filtering coverage on the same S7 harness (magnetics are already inputs → Ldata partly autoencodes; grounding is representation-shaping, not new-quantity prediction).
  • TERTIARY (first discovered structure — validated): an effective latent dim r (~3–5, with energy spectrum) + a sparse symbolic recurrence in reduced coords that PRESERVES Dα skill when simulated through the FROZEN obs head and re-scored. Spectral validation sub-500 Hz only (1 kHz Nyquist).

§5 — Top risks

  • Geometry-source principle deadlock — the whole GS chain (T1–T6) blocks on the user's geometry call; if efm-static-geometry is denied and amb-parsing proves unreliable, Phase 1 stalls pending external-DD ingestion. (Discovery T7–T8 is geometry-independent and proceeds regardless.)
  • Trivial-baseline / eddy-current confound — a residual that merely tracks |dB/dt| or spikes on eddy currents is not physics-native; T4 makes beating-trivial + surviving-ablation the PRIMARY gate.
  • Q2 scaling leak — a dimensional residual ~Iₚ²/ne reconstructs the OOD-box label axes; T5 reports only the deconfounded AUROC.
  • Falsifiability collapse — pointwise-free p′/FF′ zeroes the residual; the restricted-DOF basis is load-bearing, swept as a first-class knob.
  • drift_reg=0.3 confound — the landed engine's quiescent regulariser biases fθ→identity, confounding "discovered near-conservation"; T8 stratifies transient/quiescent and flags it.
  • Deepest risk — criterion-3 may be the wrong target: a force-balance-departure detector is suited to TRANSIENT/off-normal departures, not a STATIC high-Iₚ×ne regime label where force balance still holds — so Q2 may not close criterion-3 even when Q1 succeeds.
