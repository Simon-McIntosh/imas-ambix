§1 — The competition is a low-dimensional problem wearing a high-dimensional costume

The scorer asks for ψ(R,Z) on a 65×65 grid — 4,225 numbers per slice — plus q95 and β_N, at every labeled frame of held-out shots. Read literally, that is a high-dimensional regression: learn a map from actuators and Thomson to four thousand outputs, over roughly 1.5 million labeled DIII-D frames. Almost every entry will build some version of that map.

It is the wrong object. An axisymmetric plasma in force balance satisfies Grad–Shafranov, and the Grad–Shafranov equation has exactly two free functions:

Δ*ψ = −μ₀R²·p′(ψ) − FF′(ψ)

Everything else on the right-hand side is given by the challenge — the conductor currents are inputs, not unknowns, and the machine geometry ships with the data. So the entire 4,225-dimensional output is a deterministic function of two one-dimensional profiles plus a boundary condition. The question is not "what is the map"; it is "what are the two functions".

We now know how big that question is, because we measured it. Extracting p′ and FF′ from 4,141 labeled frames across 20 DIII-D shots and pooling them into a basis, three components capture 99.76% of the p′ variance, 99.85% of FF′ and 100.00% of ψ(ρ̂), with median frame-to-frame variation of 9.5%, 6.5% and 1.2% respectively (prototype §2). The true state is of order ten numbers, and it drifts slowly. The costume is 4,225 numbers wide; the body inside it is not.

§2 — Why one frame is not enough and one shot is

Thomson scattering measures T_e and n_e at points along a set of chords. Because both are flux functions, two chord positions reporting the same T_e are a statement that those two points lie on the same flux surface — an isoflux constraint, the same kind of information a magnetic probe gives, which is why we call Thomson channels pseudo-magnetics. But isoflux pins constrain the shape of ψ where the chords are, and say very little about the current distribution deep in the core, and nothing at all where there is no coverage. Per frame, the interior is under-determined. This is not a solvable problem with a better fit; it is a rank deficiency.

Time removes it. Resistive current diffusion has a time constant of hundreds of milliseconds to seconds, so the interior flux functions physically cannot jump between adjacent frames — and our measurement confirms they do not (1.2% median frame-to-frame variation in ψ). A shot with a few hundred labeled frames therefore supplies a few hundred sets of boundary and shape pins against a state with of order ten degrees of freedom that is constrained to move smoothly between them. Under-determined per frame, heavily over-determined per shot.

That structure — a prior propagated by dynamics, corrected by observations, with information accumulating along a trajectory — is a Kalman filter. Not by analogy: it is the definition. What makes ours unusual is that the transition and observation operators are exact nonlinear physics rather than learned or linearised surrogates.

§3 — A posterior built only from forward evaluations

Nova produces every response kernel and fits with none of them: it builds the magnetics design matrix it never inverts, the chord→ψ_N sampler, the Δ* extraction operator, the differentiable moment operator. The litmus is stated at the seam — differentiable physics with a geometry argument is a Nova kernel; anything carrying a σ, a prior or a decision is an Ambix measurement model. So how does a posterior appear if nothing is ever inverted? Three mechanisms, all of which only ever run physics forwards.

  • Ensembles turn evaluation into inversion. Draw members from the prior, push every member through the exact forward, and compare predicted observables to measured ones. The update uses the sampled cross-covariance between state and predicted observation. The only matrix inverted lives in observation space — a few dozen Thomson channels — never in state or physics space. The inverse is a statistical consequence of many forward runs, not a solved linear system. This is why nova's published throughput budget (slices per second, batched on the H200) directly sets how sharp our posterior can be: ensemble size is bought with forward evaluations.
  • Differentiability gives exact sensitivities without an inverse operator. The forwards are jit/vmap/grad-safe, and a converged equilibrium–transport window differentiates by the implicit-function theorem rather than by unrolling its iterations. That yields gradients of any misfit with respect to the state, hence MAP estimates and Laplace covariances, from the same forward code.
  • Amortization proposes; the forward disposes. A geometry-conditioned encoder trained across thousands of shots can propose a state or warm start far faster than a cold filter finds one — but its output is scored and corrected by the forward, so approximation error never reaches the submitted artifact.

The exactness of the forward is what makes any of this meaningful. With an approximate forward, a mismatch between prediction and measurement is uninterpretable: it could be state error or model error, and the estimator will happily absorb one as the other — teaching corrections to compensate the solver rather than the plasma. That is why nova's window must converge and must surrender its convergence behaviour as receipts, and why an unconverged member is refused rather than silently used.

§4 — How this differs from traditional Bayesian inference

Fusion already has excellent Bayesian inference. Minerva at JET and IDA at AUG and W7-X do full probabilistic inversion over jointly forward-modelled diagnostics from raw signals. The differences below are therefore real distinctions, not a claim to have invented probabilistic reasoning.

Where this architecture departs from classical Bayesian integrated data analysis and from EFIT-class reconstruction.
DimensionTraditional practiceHere
Time Static, per-slice inference. Slices are independent; there is no transition kernel. The transition kernel is the central object. Without it the magnetics-free frame stays rank-deficient no matter how principled the prior.
What the prior is A distribution over basis coefficients — smoothness, Tikhonov, a Gaussian process. A simulator. The regularisation is dynamical: the profile is not merely smooth, it arrived here by diffusion from where it was.
What carries uncertainty A distribution over the reconstructed output, or over map pixels. A distribution over a physical low-dimensional state. Every sample renders through GS, so every sample is force-balanced by construction.
Model discrepancy Absorbed into fitted coefficients or an inflated noise floor. Typed and attributed across named sources — closure, calibration, geometry, passive currents — each with its own forward. A persistent GS residual is a measurement of where force balance breaks, not an error to fit away.
Non-Gaussian structure Linearised around a reference; mean and covariance summarise the posterior. Weighted members, deliberately. A mean-and-covariance summary erases member-specific topology changes — and limited↔diverted transitions are exactly the discrete events the ramp must traverse.
What success is scored against Agreement with a reconstruction. Raw measurements. Claims of superiority to EFIT scored against EFIT-derived quantities are circular, so EFIT is a referee and never a supervisor.

§5 — The arrow of time, and what the learned components actually buy

Four distinct things travel under this heading, and they are worth separating because only some of them are claims we have earned.

Causality as a type, not a convention. Filtering (past and present), forecasting (future) and smoothing (all data, offline) are three different products, and reporting a smoothed profile as a causal prior is the easiest way to fake skill. The seam contract makes this a runtime property: forecast and analysis handoffs are accepted at the online boundary while fixed-lag and full-sequence smoothing are rejected there (landed, with tests). For the competition this matters twice over: the submission may smooth freely because scoring is offline, but the ITER rehearsal claim — the reason we are entering at all — is a claim about causal estimation and must be reported from the causal product.

Irreversibility is itself information. Resistive diffusion has a direction: running it backwards is the backward heat equation, exponentially unstable. Two consequences follow. Forward is where the information is, which is why an exact forward operator plus a filter beats an inverse operator. And the smoother's advantage over the filter is structured rather than generic — because diffusion destroys fine structure, a later observation strongly constrains earlier large-scale state and says nothing about earlier fine detail. We can therefore state which directions in state space the future can inform, instead of asserting that smoothing helps.

The unlabeled gaps are ours to cross. The challenge deletes limited frames corpus-wide, so efit_times is non-contiguous and every limited↔diverted transition is unlabeled — while the actuators remain continuous at 20 kHz. A per-frame regressor meets each labeled frame cold. A transition kernel carries state through the gap and arrives at the next labeled frame with an informed prior. This is a concrete scoring advantage that exists only because we have dynamics, and it is largest exactly where the entry is otherwise weakest — the ramp.

What the ML is for, stated against our own negative results. A supervised oracle already told us that for magnetics-only internal-current recovery, architecture is not the binding constraint — interior information is, and time-history added nothing (0.190 ≈ 0.188). We must not now claim a sequence model rescues us. The defensible roles are narrower and better evidenced:

  • Learned closures inside the differentiable solver — bounded, positivity-constrained corrections to η∥, Z_eff and the bootstrap multiplier, trained MSE-free through the adjoint against observable losses and amortised across thousands of shots. The information enters through the physics, so the highest-leverage learning improves the physics' weakest part rather than replacing it. Our simulability measurement just handed this lane its target: the transport prior tracks ψ (beating persistence by 1.63 in explained variance) but does not carry FF′ (margin −0.0006, indistinguishable from persistence in every regime) — see prototype §3, qualified by frozen geometry and a proxy radial coordinate.
  • A learned gain or bias corrector is principled precisely where the forward model is known to be mis-specified — and we have now measured that it is, on the FF′ channel specifically.
  • An amortised, geometry-conditioned posterior proposal is where a sequence model legitimately exploits the arrow: an encoder that has seen thousands of ramps knows what a ramp looks like and proposes the state a cold filter would search for. It proposes; the smoother disposes.

Learned corrections enter the coupled solve only as typed inputs — member closure parameters and declared model-error source terms through the seam's registry — never as invisible patches inside it. That restriction is what keeps the innovation interpretable and is the reason the architecture can be honest about which of its parts is failing.

§6 — Consequences for the entry

Three things follow directly from what has been measured, and each changes the plan rather than merely decorating it.

  • The low-dimensional state thesis is confirmed: roughly three components per flux function, slowly drifting. The windowed smoother's 12–16 parameters per window is the right order, and the submission does not require a high-dimensional map model.
  • The FF′ deviation amplitudes cannot be tightly priored as small departures from a transport-evolved mean, because the transport-evolved mean is not better than the initial value. The innovation update must do real work on FF′, and the closure-learning lane now has an explicit, measured deficiency to attack.
  • The FF′ result is qualified by frozen geometry, a q95-calibrated boundary F and a proxy radial coordinate — the three approximations FF′ is most sensitive to. The exact time-varying-geometry rerun is therefore a genuine open question and not a formality, and it is queued behind nova's TORAX geometry adapter.
