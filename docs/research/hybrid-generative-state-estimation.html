Hybrid Generative State Estimation with Nova

Decision synthesis. Ambix should pursue a GenCast-like probabilistic forecast capability through a Nova-in-the-loop transition, not choose between a purely learned forecaster and a purely deterministic physics loop. Nova carries the transferable laws and geometry. Ambix represents uncertainty in the assumptions that physics does not determine, conditions the resulting ensemble on signals, and distils repeatable model–data mismatch into transferable closures.

North Star

Build an ITER-ready, machine-portable probabilistic plasma state estimator in which known physics transfers exactly and learned uncertainty transfers explicitly. Conservation, force balance, circuit laws and coordinate transformations are not rediscovered by ML. Nova evaluates those laws quickly for the machine geometry and commanded coil or actuator trajectory. The ensemble varies the quantities that remain uncertain: transport and closure coefficients, sources, calibration states, boundary decisions and—where a Gaussian average would be unphysical—discrete topology branches.

The primary learning signal is the innovation: measured signal-carrying data minus Nova's observation of its predicted state. A persistent, cross-shot and cross-machine mismatch is a candidate missing closure or transferable coefficient. A one-off mismatch may instead be calibration drift, a bad channel, a geometry error, a numerical approximation or a regime outside support. Ambix must classify these alternatives before calling any residual “discovered physics.”

The purpose of the fast Nova loop is transfer. A new machine geometry and new coil currents produce a new physical flux map without retraining the laws. Learned closure distributions and boundary policies transfer only where their dimensionless context and uncertainty say they should. Surprise on the new machine expands the posterior and supplies the next learning signal. Robustness on present machines is therefore a rehearsal for useful state estimates from ITER's first plasma, before an ITER-specific empirical history exists.

One distribution, three estimator products

The authoritative object is a joint conditional distribution over plasma state x, diagnostics y, machine geometry g, actuator trajectory u, and uncertain closure/boundary state c. The same trained components expose three EnKF-class products by changing which observations are admitted:

ProductConditioningMeaningRequired label
ForecastSignals through the anchor time; known or proposed future commandsProbabilistic future state and diagnosticsCausal forecast
Reconstruction / analysisSignals through the state timeBest causal present-state estimateCausal analysis
SmoothingSignals from the complete fixed lag or complete shotBest retrospective trajectory and reanalysis corpusNon-causal smoothed estimate

A smoothed posterior is useful training data but is not ground truth. It must be provenance-tagged and excluded from causal forecast evaluation. Forecast, analysis and smoothed products must never share an unlabeled artifact namespace.

GenCast sharpness and NeuralGCM structure are complementary

GenCast demonstrates the value of learning a stochastic transition distribution whose individual samples remain sharp and whose ensemble is calibrated. NeuralGCM demonstrates the complementary value of retaining a differentiable physical core while learning unresolved process representations. For Ambix, these become two responsibilities inside one system:

  • GenCast-like responsibility: generate a sharp, calibrated joint distribution over future equilibria and measurements, not a blurry ensemble mean and not a deterministic trajectory with noise added afterward.
  • Physics-led responsibility: each primary ensemble member is advanced by Nova under explicit geometry, currents and transferable closure samples. Conservation and force balance remain inspectable.
  • Conditioning responsibility: an EnKF/ensemble-smoother layer—or particles for genuinely multimodal topology—uses signal innovation to reconstruct and smooth while preserving the same forecast model.

A direct learned state-to-state generative emulator is a later challenger, proposal engine or distillation target. It becomes authoritative only after it earns physical parity, calibration and extrapolation skill against the Nova-led ensemble. Speed alone does not promote it.

Eight H200s: decisive advantage, architectural constraint

The exclusive H200 server should be saturated where additional parallelism buys scientific evidence: wide independent Nova ensemble propagation; stochastic closure and observation-model training; complete-shot smoothers and reanalysis; camera decoding; bootstrap scorecards; and multi-machine ablations. Runs should load models once, shard ensemble members or data cleanly, checkpoint frequently, resume safely and demonstrate CPU/one-GPU parity before scaling.

The resource does not decide the model family. Every scientific claim needs a small CPU reference, a one-H200 capability demonstration and a measured eight-H200 scaling result. If a design only works by coupling eight cards into one irreducible state, it has compromised the ITER operations path. Conversely, refusing to widen an ensemble or complete a reanalysis when eight cards are idle wastes a genuine experimental advantage.

The imas-efit GHCR validation sets belong with Nova because they establish the fidelity of the physics engine independently of Ambix training or evaluation. They should test geometry/topology classes, axis and boundary placement, profiles, flux/current ledgers and CPU/GPU parity. Ambix must pin an exact validated Nova revision, record its evidence identity and test its thin consumer contract continuously while both repositories evolve. Waiting for a final API freeze would make tandem development artificial; silently following a moving branch would make experiments irreproducible.

Scorecard and anti-shortcut rules

  • Score forecast ensembles with CRPS or energy score, calibration/coverage, sharpness and event-conditioned tail skill; always compare persistence and the current EnKF baseline.
  • Score reconstruction and smoothing separately. A smoother win cannot be reported as an online estimator win.
  • Score physical validity per member before averaging: force balance, conservation ledgers, current and boundary constraints, topology validity and CPU/GPU parity.
  • Use actuator interventions and excitation-curated windows. History-only prediction on passive flat-top data does not establish controllability.
  • Evaluate cross-machine transfer with train-machine holdout and dimensionless context. Fine-tuning may be reported separately but cannot masquerade as zero-shot transfer.
  • Partition innovation into sensor/calibration, geometry, numerical approximation, missing source, uncertain closure and topology hypotheses. Only repeated held-out evidence earns “discovery.”

Current capability smoke, 2026-08-09

A single CPU run against the newly updated Nova pin exercised six focused capabilities and passed all six in 348.02 s:

  1. Ambix constructs and evolves Nova circuit/transport objects through the thin physics adapters.
  2. The existing Ambix state-space engine exposes forecast, causal filtering and non-causal smoothing with valid shapes and positive variances.
  3. The camera modality flows through tokens, embedding, prediction head, loss reduction and checkpoint round-trip.
  4. Actuator conditioning becomes load-bearing under a deliberately identifiable synthetic intervention.
  5. The controllable RSSM evaluation produces the expected full-rollout and controllability artifact structure.
  6. The exact pinned Nova dependency remains compatible across this combined slice.

Two warnings were non-fatal: the login node cannot initialise the installed CUDA stack, as expected for this CPU run, and one test converts a gradient-bearing tensor to a scalar for an assertion. This smoke proves that the required pieces exist and coexist. It does not yet prove a Nova-propagated ensemble, physical posterior improvement, real-shot controllability or cross-machine transfer. Those are the gates in hybrid-state-estimation-smoke.

A separate H200 capability run (SLURM job 1242807) used all five cards that were free while leaving three live service GPUs undisturbed. Five independent fp64 Nova-facing flux-surface workers processed batches of 128, 256, 512, 1,024 and 2,048 equilibria. Every worker selected the GPU backend, reported 100% well-posed surfaces and agreed with its CPU reference to a worst-case absolute difference of 1.14×10−13. Per-worker throughput ranged from 93,366 to 206,987 equilibria/s as batch size increased. This demonstrates clean ensemble partitioning and substantial kernel headroom; it is not yet an eight-card strong-scaling result or a full Nova equilibrium-solve benchmark.
