Plasma State-Space Engine v0 — shape parameters from raw diagnostics

Stage-1 of physics-world-model-strategy. The home of sprint S7. Goal: prove the probabilistic state-space engine — and that its uncertainty is honest — on a well-posed, general, multi-modal task, before we invest in physical grounding (GS prior) and discovery.

§1 — Objective (the clear sprint goal)

Predict MAST plasma separatrix shape parameters (e.g. elongation κ, triangularity δ, plasma volume, Rgeo, minor radius a) from raw diagnostic signals (rbb camera + magnetics) with a learned latent state-space model, and demonstrate that the predicted uncertainty is calibrated and that out-of-distribution regimes are flagged.

Why this task: it is general (a capability exercised across the whole operating space, not a single-phenomenon study), multi-modal (camera + magnetics, both available now in level-1 MAST data), physically interpretable (shape parameters are meaningful and low-dimensional), and it is the natural seed of Stage-2 (the same machinery, with a GS prior, becomes physical state estimation + force-balance-departure discovery). Generating camera streams is explicitly a byproduct, not a goal.

§2 — Data (raw, available now — no IMAS migration needed)

  • Inputs (raw): rbb fast camera + magnetics groups amb/amc/amm from /work/projects/imas_gpu/mast/level1/shots/<shot>.zarr. Exact signal selection is sprint task 1.
  • Targets (toy labels): separatrix shape parameters from the efm (EFIT) group. Honest caveat: these labels are EFIT output and carry EFIT's assumptions. That is acceptable for a Stage-1 learnability + calibration check — it validates that raw diagnostics carry the shape information and that our uncertainty is honest. It is not physics discovery; Stage-2 replaces "regress to EFIT labels" with "estimate state under our own GS prior" (per strategy §5/§9). We flag this limitation in every result.
  • Splits: held-out shots (calibration) AND held-out regimes (OOD test) — e.g. split by Ip/density/shape class/campaign so a whole region of operating space is unseen. Defining the regime split is sprint task 1.

§3 — Approach (baseline → engine, one variable at a time)

  1. Static baseline. Per-slice regression diagnostics → shape with a deep ensemble for uncertainty. Establishes the observation model, multi-modal fusion, and the calibration harness. No dynamics yet.
  2. Dynamic engine. A learned latent state + stochastic transition kernel + Bayesian filter (start light per strategy §3: ensemble/conformal, not full-variational). Now we get the three operations: filtering (track shape from the diagnostic stream), forecasting (predict shape forward — the what-if), smoothing (best trajectory, for later discovery).

The baseline and engine are evaluated on the same held-out shots/regimes with the same calibration harness, so the engine's value (does modelling dynamics improve calibrated forecasting over per-slice regression?) is isolated.

§4 — Acceptance criteria (what "calibrated and honest" must mean to count)

  • Calibration: 90% predictive intervals contain truth in 88–92% of held-out-shot slices (and similarly for other nominal levels — reliability diagram near-diagonal).
  • Sharpness: mean interval width reported; intervals as tight as possible subject to calibration holding.
  • Proper scores: CRPS and NLL reported vs the static baseline; the dynamic engine should not be worse, and should improve forecasting.
  • OOD honesty: on held-out regimes, the model must visibly widen intervals / raise a novelty score (quantify: coverage on OOD should not collapse silently; report an OOD-detection AUROC or a coverage-vs-distance curve).
  • Point sanity: RMSE per shape parameter (a comparator, not the headline — calibration is).

Note the acceptance is calibration, not rFID or pixel fidelity — deliberately, per the strategy.

§5 — Out of scope for v0

GS prior / physical grounding (Stage-2); structure discovery (SINDy/Koopman); cross-machine extrapolation; camera-stream generation; IMAS-format migration. v0 proves the engine + honesty; these build on it.

§ Decisions

Which shape parameters form the v0 target set?

Recommend the compact scalar set first — interpretable, robustly available in efm, and enough to demonstrate calibration. Boundary contour is a natural v0.5 extension.

Which input modalities for the v0 engine?

Recommend a magnetics-only baseline first (magnetics are the classical shape observable), then add the camera and measure the lift — this quantifies what the camera modality actually buys, cleanly.

How do we define held-out REGIMES for the OOD test?

Recommend holding out an I_p×density region as primary (a clear "operating-space hole" the model must flag); shape-class and campaign as secondary OOD axes. Final choice in sprint task 1 once the data distribution is scoped.
