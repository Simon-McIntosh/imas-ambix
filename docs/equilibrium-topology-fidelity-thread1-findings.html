Thread 1 findings — the boundary from a constrained external-field representation

Research findings for thread 1 of equilibrium-topology-fidelity: read the plasma boundary (separatrix / X-point / LCFS) off a constrained low-order current-moment representation of the external magnetics instead of the free ~5000-DOF interior patch current. Measured, leakage-free, against the firewalled EFIT referee on the 160-slice held-out set. Includes an evaluation of the MAST↔IMAS coordinate/sign (COCOS) exposure the lead flagged.

Verdict

The boundary deficit is substantially a REPRESENTATION artifact — confirmed, with a held-out recovery — but only PARTIALLY, and the split is finer than first drawn. Reading the boundary off a constrained current-moment field (no new diagnostics) improves the held-out X-point-set skill from −0.61 to −0.15 and the LCFS skill from −6.26 to −4.47 (median LCFS offset 34→24 cm), while pinning Ip exactly. This is direct evidence for §2: a smoother, moment-constrained current removes much of the near-boundary lumpiness the free-current read injected. But it does not reach referee grade: the X-point / limited↔diverted class (set by the low moments + coil field in the vacuum region) is externally well-posed and recovers; the detailed LCFS radial extent — especially the inboard corners / divertor-leg region — does NOT fully recover, because it depends on the finite interior current distribution (the genuine null-space). Thread 1 therefore closes the topology-class half of the boundary problem in the right direction and hands the LCFS-radius half to the interior-diagnostic threads.

§1 — The question

The equilibrium layer resolves the magnetic axis to referee grade (2.8 cm) but the boundary/X-point is an honest negative: read off the free ~5000-DOF interior patch-current ψ, the last-closed surface is under-sized by 34 cm and the X-point set scores −0.61 (LCFS skill −6.26). §2 of the driving plan argues this is a representation artifact: outside the plasma Jφ=0 so ψ solves the homogeneous Grad–Shafranov operator Δ*ψ=0, and the external magnetics fix the low-order current moments (Ip, centroid, elongation, triangularity) that set the boundary shape — so the interior null-space slack that a free ~5k-DOF current injects into the near-boundary field is curable by a constrained external representation, with the data we already have. Thread 1 tests that claim directly.

§2 — Method: a constrained current-moment read (decision locked: current-moment)

We represent the plasma current with a small moment basis and reconstruct a smooth total flux ψ(R,Z) whose boundary is a well-conditioned function of the moments the magnetics constrain:

  • Low-order moment basis (imas_ambix/latent/boundary_moment.py): monomials upvq (u=(R−R₀)/a, v=Z/a) up to a swept order K, on the conductor-clear in-limiter cells. The monopole is the candidate mask and every higher moment is zero-sum-centred, so the total current lands purely on the monopole and is hard-anchored to the Rogowski Ip (Ip reproduced to ~1e-16). The K−1 shape coefficients are fit by whitened least squares to the plasma sensor signature measured − vacuum over the trusted rows.
  • Same Green's operator, same referee: the current reuses the patch→sensor (m_sens) and patch→grid-flux (g_pg) maps, so ψ is reconstructed by exactly the free-current machinery and read by the identical topology read + firewalled-EFIT scoring (scripts/patch_gate_eval.py). The boundary representation is the only varying factor.
  • Hybrid read (plan §5): the patch-ψ stays the interior carrier — the LCFS ray-cast origin (the magnetic axis) comes from the interior estimate, while the boundary (bounding flux, X-point set, 8-angle LCFS radii) is read from the constrained moment ψ.

Protocol: the moment order was swept on the training cohort (leakage-free), frozen, and scored ONCE on the 160-slice held-out set (STANDING_HELD_OUT + test_ood_regime). Order 3 won the tune. Conventions preserved throughout: total poloidal flux Φ=2πR·Aφ [Wb], μ₀ explicit, MAST ψaxis>ψboundary; the EFIT referee ψ is ×2π to match (see the COCOS evaluation, §5).

§4 — The honest edge: the LCFS radial extent blends into the interior

The per-angle breakdown locates exactly what the external read recovers and what it cannot. The moment read improves the top, bottom, and outboard-midplane angles substantially (held-out median LCFS offset: 90°/top 40→26 cm, 270°/bottom 42→20 cm, 0°/45°/315° all ~24→~18 cm), but the upper and lower inboard corners (135°/225°) stay essentially unchanged (~39 cm) and the inboard midplane (180°) slightly worsens (30→32 cm) — the divertor-leg / X-point-adjacent region a smooth low-order current model cannot resolve. Across three current mechanizations — the low-order polynomial basis, a free compact Gaussian blob, and a blob centred on the true axis — the reconstructed ψ leaves this same near-edge residual even when the axis and Ip are correct. This is not a bug: the detailed last-closed surface is shaped by the finite interior current/pressure profile — the genuine null-space the balanced-sampling negative measured. In other words:

  • Externally well-posed (recovers): Ip, current centroid, and the X-point / limited↔diverted class — set by the low moments and the divertor-coil field in the vacuum region.
  • Not purely external (does not fully recover): the detailed LCFS radial extent at the inboard corners / divertor-leg region (135°/225°/180°), which couples to the interior profile.

This refines §2's split: the boundary is not monolithically "externally well-posed"; its topology is, but its radial shape shares the interior null-space. The residual LCFS deficit is therefore the correct hand-off to the interior-diagnostic thread (thread 2), not a defect of thread 1.

§5 — Coordinate / sign-convention (COCOS) evaluation

The lead flagged a fear that sign-convention misunderstandings were silently impeding progress. A dedicated audit (MAST↔IMAS COCOS evaluation, scripts/mast_cocos_audit.py) settled it with real-data evidence, and the result directly supports this thread's conclusion:

  • The MAST pipeline is sign-sound. On a genuine pre-breakdown vacuum slice the known-coil prediction correlates 0.83–0.87 with the raw magnetics at a least-squares slope 0.86–0.99 — sign and magnitude both correct. MAST is read raw from FAIR-MAST zarr; there is no IMAS auto-conversion on that path.
  • The boundary negative is NOT a sign flip. A ψ- or Z-flip would corrupt the axis too, but axis skill is positive (+0.019 in the P3 run) — a sign fault cannot be that selective. This is independent confirmation that the boundary deficit is a representation/interior effect, exactly as measured here.
  • The one real mismatch is latent, at the IMAS seam. MAST EFIT is COCOS-3-like (axis = flux max for Ip>0), not COCOS-11; harmless for today's MAST-only work but live the moment data is ingested through the IMAS readers (whose poloidal-angle assumption is currently unverified). Mapping MAST to IMAS is worth it — it retires per-machine sign bookkeeping and realises the machine-agnostic North Star — but only with an explicit, fail-loud normalisation boundary; a naive mapping would inject exactly the hazard feared. That work is captured as a separate, non-urgent plan: cocos-convention-hardening.

§6 — Conclusion and next steps

  • Thread 1 lands a measured, held-out, partial recovery of the boundary as a representation fix (X-point −0.61→−0.15, LCFS −6.26→−4.47, no new data) — validating §2's direction while refining it: the topology class is externally well-posed, the LCFS radial extent is not.
  • Warranted follow-up (per the decision rationale): the toroidal-harmonic expansion. The current-moment read reconstructs an interior current, which re-imports interior ambiguity into the near-edge ψ; a source-free vacuum-region ψ fit (half-integer Legendre Δ*-eigenfunctions) reads the separatrix without committing to an interior current and is the natural next attempt at the X-point / separatrix, plus it enables the vacuum-region consistency check.
  • The LCFS radial extent is handed to thread 2 (interior-diagnostic fusion): the residual is interior-profile information, not an external representation choice.

§7 — Review addendum (2026-07-07): caveats and a reopened attribution

The 2026-07 grounding review re-derived this document's claims from the artifacts and code. The Gate-1 directional improvement stands; the following corrections and caveats now apply and supersede the corresponding statements above:

  • Protocol disclosure (corrects §2/§3): the flagship eval artifact (boundary_read_moment-o3.json, arm current-moment-polynomial-centroid-axis) used the moment fit's current centroid as the LCFS ray-cast origin — not the interior patch-axis hybrid §2 describes. The moment arm's own axis skill is −1.62 (median origin error 7.9 cm), omitted from the §3 table (free-current baseline: −0.024). A first-order de-confound on the stored arrays shows the per-angle §4 pattern survives origin correction (medians move ±1–3 cm; corners stay ~37–39 cm), so the structure of §4 is real — but the flagship numbers should be re-scored with --axis-source patch for the record.
  • Gate 1a was never measured: the free-current baseline artifact carries saddles: null, so the claimed mechanism ("spurious saddles under-sized the LCFS; the moment read removes them") has no recorded before/after. The moment field itself averages 2.16 in-vessel saddles per slice with saddle_free_fraction = 0.0 — under a ≤1 "saddle-free" rule that additionally mislabels MAST's routine double-null (two genuine X-points) as spurious.
  • Selection fragility (caveats §3): on the tuning cohort order 3 scored X-point −2.31 — worse than order 2 (−0.50) and opposite in sign of improvement to its eval score (−0.15). No skill in this document carries a confidence interval; the tune→eval inversion is unquantified cohort noise until the bootstrap-CI harness rung lands.
  • The method contradicts its own premise (qualifies §2, reopens §4): the polynomial moment basis' monopole term spreads Ip uniformly over every conductor-clear in-limiter cell — jφ ≠ 0 throughout the vacuum region, so the reconstructed ψ does not satisfy Δ*ψ = 0 in the very region the boundary and X-point are read. The §4 attribution of the inboard-corner residual to "the genuine interior null-space" is therefore reopened: method-committed vacuum-region current and divertor-coil model error in those corners are equally consistent with the evidence. The discriminating experiments are Gate B of plasma-current-priors-hardening (does the corner residual move when current cannot sit there?) and the source-free toroidal-harmonic read (f-etf-04) — which, per the lead's expectation, must solve a zero-current field outside the LCFS and accept "nonsense" within, reading the boundary only in the vacuum annulus (its confined-side flux reference must come from the interior carrier, never from the harmonic field's own extremum).
