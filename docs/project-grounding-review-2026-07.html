§3 — The central finding: the null space is filled by numerics, not physics

The North Star contract (machine-agnostic-latent-world-model §5) is precise: "we make the X-point the natural consequence of explaining the raw magnetics. Physics enters as the coordinate system and soft priors (the language), never as a hard law." The figure the lead flagged shows the contract failing exactly where it matters — in the sensor null space, where "explaining the raw magnetics" does not pin the current and the priors must:

Why the loss cannot see this. The free inverse (imas_ambix/latent/patch_inverse.py:226) minimises misfit + w·Ip-penalty + λ·structure_residual. The structure residual (structure_residual.py) is a current-weighted, scale-free, sign-agnostic test of the flux-function relation R·jφ = p′R² + FF′/μ₀ per ψ-level-set. Three properties make it blind to the figure's pathologies: (i) j² weighting — a low-|j| halo spread over many cells carries almost no weight; (ii) sign-agnosticism — a negative-current region on its own level sets can satisfy an affine relation as well as a positive one; (iii) no support term — where current sits enters nowhere. The one support prior in the tree (edge_taper_penalty, structure_residual.py:810) is deliberately unwired ("halo/SOL currents are REAL and carry discovery signal"). The discovery-framing is legitimate, but unbudgeted it means nothing in the loop distinguishes a physical SOL current from a null-space dipole layer 10× larger. The optimiser (Adam from a Gaussian seed, 800 iters) then fills the null space with whatever minimises whitened sensor residual fastest — the halo and corner lobes are its signature, not the plasma's.

The substrate review sharpened this in four ways. (i) The scale of the under-determination: ~73 trusted sensors constrain ~4,787 candidate cells, and 35 of the 41 in-limiter cells in the corner box (R∈[0.6,0.9], Z∈[1.6,1.8]) are legal candidates — the conductor-clear mask works exactly as coded (winding packs + cases excluded with a one-cell dilation) but carries no plasma-support notion, and the amm passive structures (p2uarm, p2udivpl, topcol…) are not excluded at all. (ii) The structure residual is self-normalised (num/den, structure_residual.py:608-610): an isolated, internally-consistent blob at its own ψ-level fits its two-parameter affine relation trivially (≈0 added to the numerator) while inflating the denominator — so the physics term can actively reward parking current in corner blobs, not merely fail to punish it. (iii) The published figure's inverse ran with connectivity=None (patch_flux_map_report.py:67-73) while the gate CLI defaults to connectivity="locality" — the flagship image shows the weakest-regularised arm. (iv) The inverse's forward model has no passive/eddy term (g_passive exists in the operator but is never wired into the inverse), so unmodelled eddies and any PF/case vacuum-field error can only be absorbed by plasma cells — though a measured flat-top ablation (passive_ablation_probe2.json: rank-4 amm eddy block, misfit −0.06%, passive currents 6×10⁻⁵ of Ip) shows eddies are not load-bearing at flat-top. Separately, the free-boundary GS solver's coil handling was verified correct: the analytic-add split (FD solve of the plasma part + exact analytic coil field) is the right treatment for in-vessel coils, and the Δ*-harmonic boundary-continuation arm is correctly confined to bootstrap/diagnostic use — no bug in that path.

The moment fit replaced one unphysical current with another. boundary_moment.py:116: the monopole column is the candidate mask, so the hard Ip anchor places Ip/n_cand on every conductor-clear in-limiter cell — including the divertor legs, the corners, and the entire vacuum region between the true LCFS and the wall. The right panel's faint vessel-filling red is not a rendering artifact; it is the model. Two consequences: (i) the reconstructed ψ does not satisfy Δ*ψ = 0 in the region where the boundary and X-point are read — the method contradicts the §2 physics of its own driving plan; (ii) the "external field is fixed by the low-order moments" theorem invoked to justify it requires the field point to be exterior to the current support — with support filling the vessel, sensors and the boundary-read region sit adjacent to or inside the support and the theorem does not apply. The docstring's claim "no interior slack, hence no spurious near-boundary saddles" is empirically false on its own artifact: saddles_mean = 2.16, saddle_free_fraction = 0.0 on all 160 eval slices.

What the lead expected — and what f-etf-04 must be. The expectation ("zero-current fields solved outside the LCFS and then 'nonsense' within due to the low-order reconstruction fidelity") describes the toroidal-harmonic option of the locked decision — fit Δ*ψ = 0 eigenfunctions (half-integer Legendre) to measured − vacuum in the source-free annulus, read the boundary/X-point in the annulus only, and accept that the expansion is meaningless inside the plasma. That option was never implemented; the "current-moment" prototype was locked at kickoff for speed and the harmonic read deferred to the open followup f-etf-04. This review's design addendum for it: the confined-side flux reference must come from the interior carrier, never from the harmonic field's own extremum (the present hybrid read takes axis_psi from the moment field — boundary_moment_gate_eval.py:84 — a pattern that would silently break on a field with no valid interior).

§6 — The lead's prior questions, answered directly

  • "Should we impose a constraint that plasmas should be single joined blobs of current?" Not as a hard unimodality/single-blob prior — that would exclude doublets and bias limited↔diverted transitions. The physically correct form of the intuition is free-boundary support consistency: jφ = 0 outside the LCFS of its own ψ, imposed softly with a halo budget (R2 of the hardening plan). It kills the vacuum-region current and the corner lobes while remaining agnostic about the shape of the current inside the boundary.
  • "Should we go further and insist that currents share the same sign?" Yes — jφ·sign(Ip) ≥ 0 is the single safest prior available (R1). It eliminates the cancelling-dipole null-space directions outright, at essentially zero physical risk on this corpus.
  • "Would this prior rule out doublet plasmas?" No. A doublet doubles the flux topology (two O-points, an internal separatrix), not the current sign — both lobes carry current parallel to Ip, inside one outer LCFS envelope. R1 (same sign) and R2 (support inside the LCFS) are both doublet-safe by construction; the hardening plan carries an analytic doublet test to enforce this permanently. What R1 does exclude: reversed-current AC transients (outside every scored corpus). What it deliberately permits: current holes (j → 0 core), since ≥ 0 is not > 0. The connectivity machinery the structure residual already has (locality/labels arms) is the right home for doublet-aware closure fitting if that configuration ever enters the corpus.
  • "We want actual plasma topologies to 'pop' out from our soft priors and data." That is exactly the North Star contract, and the review's diagnosis is that it fails today because the priors span too little of the null space. The ladder R1 → R2 → R3 (sign → support → source-free exterior read) is ordered by how much of the null space each rung hands back to physics, each measured and ablatable per the P5 keep-only-if-load-bearing discipline.
