Reading note (educational dual-purpose). This plan doubles as a teaching document for the redesign. Acronyms are expanded on first use, every modelling technique is explained in plain terms, and the external methods we lean on are named and defined in the glossary (§13). §1 — Objective

Build, from the ground up, one data-led world model of the tokamak plasma that is not specific to a single machine. A world model is a learned model that predicts how a system's observations evolve over time in response to actions — here the actions are the pulse schedule (the pre-programmed actuator waveforms: demanded plasma current, density, heating, coil currents). A tokamak is a magnetic-confinement fusion device; our data comes from MAST (Mega-Amp Spherical Tokamak, UK).

The model must do three things with the same learned object — filter (estimate the plasma state now from streaming measurements), forecast (project the state forward under a plan, with no future measurements — the "controllable dream"), and transfer (carry to a new device — MAST → JET → ITER — by construction, not by retraining from scratch). It is the concrete v0 build of the North Star (§1a, refined 2026-06-24), and it replaces the camera-token generative direction that closed as a robust negative (§2). The /goal it serves: "discover the underlying latent dynamics and a physics-faithful, data-led world model capable of controllable dreams."

§2 — Why a ground-up redesign: the evidence that forces it

The previous direction — a generative model that carried the plasma state in camera tokens and tried to make the actuator plan drive the dreamed video — produced a robustly-measured negative. Two findings pin down where the physics is, and is not, recoverable, and they point the same way.

Finding 1 — generation works; control does not. Across both a token backbone and a Dreamer-style RSSM (Recurrent State-Space Model — a latent world model whose recurrent state carries the dynamics), and across every controllability lever (per-layer plan conditioning, history bottleneck, inverse-dynamics, joint generation, per-modality masking, action-contrastive, command-in-the-recurrence), the plan never became load-bearing. Finding 2 — the physics is recoverable, but from absolute signals, not from those dreams. Cameras cannot see interior geometry; per-shot z-scored tokens (each shot rescaled to zero-mean/unit-variance) are scale-free and lose absolute magnitude; but absolute, calibrated magnetics resolve the magnetic axis, the X-point, and the boundary at skill 0.5–0.7. The information was always there — the representation was throwing it away.

§3 — The unifying identity: a world model is a learned state-space model

A machine-learning world model — encoder → latent state → learned transition → observation model — is a learned nonlinear state-space model (SSM). An SSM describes a system by a hidden state zt that evolves through a transition kernel p(zt+1 | zt, action) and is seen only through an observation model p(yt | zt). This one object subsumes everything we want: state estimation = Bayesian inference of the state from observations (the Kalman filter is the linear-Gaussian special case); dynamics live in the transition kernel; discovery = reading interpretable structure out of that kernel. The strategy's central thesis (North Star §2) is that learning this kernel honestly — and doing real filtering through it — is the contribution fusion's static Bayesian machinery (EFIT, Minerva, IDA) structurally lacks.

§4 — Architecture: the shared skeleton

Every component below hangs off one pipeline. Tokens carrying absolute values and their sensor geometry are encoded into a belief over a hybrid latent; a space-time transformer advances that belief as a prior; when an observation arrives a learned ensemble correction folds it in to make a posterior; decode heads and a physics operator map the latent back to every raw signal. Dreaming = roll the prior with observations withheld; tracking = correct every step. One model — observation-withholding is the only toggle.

§5 — Representation: absolute calibration + sensor-geometry positional encoding

We keep the token substrate (the proven way to ingest high-bandwidth, multi-rate, multi-modal streams on a common footing — tokenisation maps continuous signals/images to sequences of integer codes from a learned codebook). We add two things that the machine-agnostic goal makes non-negotiable.

(1) Absolute calibration. Tokens are quantised at corpus level in physical (SI) units, not per-shot. A 0.5 T field maps to the same code in every shot and on every machine. This directly repairs the scale-free blocker of §2 — per-shot z-scoring destroyed the absolute magnitude that equilibrium reconstruction needs and the cross-machine comparability that transfer needs.

(2) Sensor-geometry positional encoding. Every token carries the physical geometry of its sensor: position (R, Z, φ), orientation / sensor normal, and integration geometry (line-of-sight chord endpoints for interferometer/SXR; pixel footprint for cameras), plus the machine geometry (vessel, poloidal-field-coil locations). This is the camera-spatial-position principle — camera tokens only decoded once each carried its 16×16 grid position — generalised to all diagnostics.

§6 — The hybrid latent state (anchored ⊕ free), in dimensionless coordinates

The shared latent zt has two parts. The anchored block is supervised only toward genuinely raw, measured quantities — plasma current Ip (from the Rogowski coil, a current-measuring loop), coil currents, line-averaged density ne, raw field magnitudes — and carries a poloidal-flux ψ representation (the scalar field whose contours are the magnetic surfaces). The free block is unconstrained, for the closure physics that first-principles cannot pin (edge turbulence, fast transients, the pedestal). Coordinates are dimensionless — ρ* (normalised gyroradius), β (pressure / magnetic pressure), ν* (normalised collisionality), a/R (inverse aspect ratio), normalised shape — because machine size spans an order of magnitude MAST → ITER and only dimensionless groups bridge it.

The crucial point (how we learn physics without being told the physics): the boundary, the X-point and the magnetic axis are never supervised targets — they are readouts of the learned ψ field (the off-axis saddle of ψ is the X-point). We do not tell the model where the X-point is; we make the X-point the natural consequence of explaining the raw magnetics. Physics enters as the coordinate system and soft priors (the language), never as a hard law or a borrowed reconstruction (the answer).

§7 — The heart: closed-loop filter + space-time transformer transition kernel

The previous generative model dreamed open-loop — it free-ran from a fixed plan and context, never re-reading a measurement, so error compounded until the dream collapsed to "don't move" (persistence). The structural fix is to make inference closed-loop: a predict → correct cycle, exactly as a Kalman filter works.

  • Predict (the prior). A command-conditioned space-time transformer advances the belief: self-attention over space (within a time-slice) and causal attention over time learn the dynamics — advection (transport/flow) and diffusion (spreading) as attention patterns. This is the "space and time transformers handling advection/diffusion" of the brief.
  • Correct (the posterior). A small LAE-EnKF ensemble folds in the step's observations. EnKF = Ensemble Kalman Filter, a data-assimilation method that represents the belief by a small set of sample states and updates them toward the observations by a gain computed from their spread; LAE = Latent AutoEncoder — the EnKF runs in the model's learned latent space (a Koopman-style linearisation), so the correction is cheap and the posterior can be non-Gaussian. Chosen over a single learned gain (KalmanNet) for its richer posterior.

The belief is probabilistic, so the model carries calibrated uncertainty (North Star §3): in dream mode, with no observations to correct against, the uncertainty inflates honestly rather than the model bluffing. And controllability becomes testable: on excited windows, does the command-driven prior move the state more than the posterior's inertia would? — not something we force, something we measure.

§7a — The temporal anchor: transport, irreversibility, and the arrow of time

The Grad-Shafranov operator (§8) anchors ψ spatially — force balance at a single instant. It says nothing about how ψ evolves in time, so without a second anchor the transition kernel is free to learn any ∂ψ/∂t. The physically-correct temporal counterpart is transport, and transport carries an arrow of time: the plasma's resistive evolution is irreversible. We encode that — and, crucially, mostly through inequality / sign-definite / monotone constraints, which are the safest possible physics to inject: they forbid the unphysical without prescribing a trajectory, so (unlike a hard equality law) they essentially cannot over-constrain. They are guard-rails, not rails.

  • Soft, learned flux/current-diffusion prior — ∂ψ/∂t = (η∥/μ₀)·𝒟[ψ] + non-inductive sources, with the parallel resistivity η∥ and the sources (bootstrap, NBI/ECCD current drive, boundary loop-voltage) as learned closures, not fixed inputs. The command enters through the source term (current drive / loop voltage are in the plan), so the plan drives the temporal evolution of ψ — controllability by construction on the identifiable chain.
  • η∥ > 0 ⇒ D ≥ 0 (dissipative by construction). Parameterise the learned diffusivity strictly positive (softplus/exp). A diffusion with D ≥ 0 is parabolic: forward-well-posed, backward-ill-posed. That is the arrow of time, baked into the operator — the model structurally cannot run the resistive evolution backward or spontaneously sharpen the profile.
  • Non-negative resistive dissipation (monotone magnetic-energy decay). The resistive channel only loses magnetic energy — an entropy-production-≥-0 / Lyapunov-like monotone (soft penalty on dEresistive/dt > 0).
  • Volt-second (flux) budget. Poloidal flux is supplied inductively (loop voltage / central solenoid — in the plan) and consumed resistively (∫η·j); a soft conservation / monotone-consumption prior on the cumulative budget (Ejima-like flux consumption) couples command → flux → Ip.

Why this is both safe and powerful: the sign/inequality priors forbid impossible dynamics — negative resistivity, anti-diffusion, spontaneous energy or flux growth, time-reversal — while prescribing no particular path, so they answer the over-constraint worry directly. They give the transition kernel a correct, strong inductive bias (irreversible, dissipative, budgeted) that a free transformer lacks; they are machine-agnostic (true on every device — η>0 everywhere) so they aid transfer; and they are the structural backbone the §12 neural-SDE inherits (drift = this dissipative flux-diffusion operator, the diffusion/noise term = stochastic transport, both respecting D ≥ 0). A persistent flux-diffusion residual, like the GS residual, is a discovery signal (anomalous current transport — sawtooth/NTM redistribution, dynamo), not an error to fit away.

§8 — Observation operators and decode heads

Each modality is reconstructed from the latent by its own observation operator — the bridge from hidden state to measurable signal. (The GS operator below is the spatial anchor on ψ; the temporal anchor is the transport prior of §7a.)

  • Magnetics — the EFIT-free Grad-Shafranov (GS) operator (gs/operator.py, the durable Stage-2 asset). The Grad-Shafranov equation is the magnetohydrodynamic force-balance relation for an axisymmetric plasma; its Green's-function form maps a current/flux distribution to the magnetic field at any point. We use it as a differentiable operator: latent ψ → predicted signal at each magnetic sensor's known location → trained to match the raw measured magnetics. This is the true, machine-agnostic "language" (Maxwell, not EFIT's fitting choices) that lets the boundary and X-point be read out without labels.
  • Other diagnostics — learned operators. Interferometer (line-integrated density), SXR (line-integrated core emission), Dα (edge/ELM) each get a learned head mapping the latent to the raw signal along the sensor's chord geometry.
  • Cameras — decoders for all five. The frozen Open-MAGVIT2 image tokeniser's decoder reconstructs frames for all five tokenised cameras (rbb / rba / rco / rgb / rgc) — the demoted autoregressive backbone becomes a pure decode path. Honest caveat: only rbb is densely populated on disk; the other four are sparsely recorded, so decode quality will track each camera's real coverage.

§9 — Training

The objective is raw-signal self-supervision through the physics-grounded bottleneck:

  • Raw next-step / forecast prediction of the measured tokens and signals (the model must explain reality, never a reconstruction). Cross-entropy / negative-log-likelihood (NLL) on tokens; proper scores on continuous signals.
  • GS soft-prior residual on raw magnetics — a falsifiable prior (the data may override it); a persistent residual is a discovery signal (where force balance breaks), not an error to fit away. (The spatial anchor on ψ — §8.)
  • Transport / irreversibility priors (§7a) — the temporal anchor. The soft, learned flux-diffusion prior on ∂ψ/∂t, plus the sign/inequality constraints that encode the arrow of time: η∥ > 0 ⇒ D ≥ 0 (strictly-positive learned diffusivity → parabolic, forward-only), non-negative resistive dissipation (monotone magnetic-energy decay), and the Volt-second / flux budget (inductive supply via loop voltage − resistive consumption). Guard-rails, not rails — they forbid the unphysical, prescribe no trajectory, so they cannot over-constrain.
  • Anchored-block supervision toward the raw measured scalars (Ip, coil currents, ne).
  • Belief regularisation — Kullback-Leibler (KL) term with free-bits on the latent belief, keeping the stochastic state informative without collapse.

Excitation-curated training (breaking the confound). Because flat-top is non-exciting (the literal root cause of §2), we up-weight / preferentially sample windows with genuine actuator excitation — coil-current ramps, large |dI/dt| transients, plan changes, ramp-up/down — so the command actually moves the state and the map becomes identifiable. An observation-withholding schedule trains the filter (track) and the forecast (dream) jointly, so the model is never purely teacher-forced.

§10 — Evaluation and the firewall (the honesty spine)

EFIT's reconstructed outputs are firewalled — a separate referee that only judges the model's readouts, never trains them. There is a hierarchy of validation honesty, and we lead with the strong tiers.

Controllability gate. The powered ΔN-M gate (true-plan vs random-plan rollout divergence over the latent that explains the raw signals, 10 random plans, collapse-rejecting, bootstrap CI) — run on excited held-out windows where the command demonstrably should move the state. Controllability is measured, not assumed. Calibration is checked with coverage, CRPS (Continuous Ranked Probability Score — a proper score rewarding sharp and calibrated forecasts) and reliability diagrams. Baseline to beat: persistence and the classical Ensemble Kalman Filter current-recovery bar.

§11 — Predictive capability and honest limits

Predictability splits by whether the plan determines the quantity. The controlled macroscopic response (Ip, density, shape/position, the equilibrium evolution) is sharply forecastable — a learned actuator→response map. The bursty micro-events (ELMs, filaments, sawtooth/MHD onset, turbulence) are aleatoric (irreducibly random given the plan): only their statistics are predictable, and sampled rollouts give plausible realisations, not exact burst times. And the interior current profile is genuinely under-determined from magnetics alone (the Stage-2/Stage-3 honest negative); the model must say "I don't know" (calibrated uncertainty) where the data cannot pin it, rather than extrapolate confidently. Over-claiming exact ELM timing or a pinned interior profile is the trap to avoid.

§12 — Scope, sequencing, and the neural-SDE upgrade

MAST-first, design-for-transfer. We build and validate on MAST now, but geometry-parameterise everything and bake in dimensionless coordinates so a second machine drops in without re-architecting. The v0 modality set is magnetics + plan + interferometer + SXR + all five cameras + the HF signals.

Where stage 2 stands, and the bridge to stage 3. The v0 skeleton's equilibrium layer is now substantively built (§12a): the patch-current ψ carrier + GS operator make the axis referee-grade (2.8 cm held-out median, skill > 0), cross-machine ITER ingestion is zero-code-change, and the stage-3 EnKF observation operator has been handed on (delivered by patch-equilibrium-wm-integration; see the f-malwm-02 status comment for the numbers). The one honest-measured gap — the X-point and the limited↔diverted boundary — is closed by the stage-2→stage-3 bridge plan equilibrium-topology-fidelity (§12a). The layered reading of the whole redesign is set out next.

§12a — The layered capability stack: the GS skeleton keeps the higher layers physical

The redesign is best read as a layered stack, not a flat pipeline. Each layer hangs off the one below and inherits its physical grounding, so capability accretes without the higher layers being free to hallucinate. This layering is what makes the headline ambition tractable rather than a leap: a data-led model that beats EFIT the way GenCast beat ECMWF — a learned probabilistic forecaster outperforming the incumbent physics-based reconstruction on its own turf. The full statement of that ambition lives in the North Star; here we connect the roadmap to it. GenCast did not discard physics — it learned the dynamics on a physically-meaningful state; our equivalent is the GS-grounded equilibrium latent that anchors every layer above it.

Layer 0 — physics-grounded equilibrium latent (the skeleton, now built). The patch-current ψ carrier and the EFIT-free GS operator (§8) give a latent whose equilibrium readout is force-balance-constrained and machine-portable. Substantively delivered by patch-equilibrium-wm-integration: the axis is referee-grade (2.8 cm held-out median, skill +0.019 > 0; flat-top +0.286 @ 2.09 cm); the X-point and boundary are an honest-measured gap (LCFS skill −6.26, located in interior structure, not the readout); cross-machine ITER ingestion is zero-code-change; and the stage-3 EnKF observation operator has been handed on. The skeleton is the physical constraint the higher layers cannot escape — it is precisely what the prior camera-token direction lacked (§2), which is why its dreams were free to be uncontrollable.

Layer 1 — forward decoders hang off the grounded latent. The camera-video / playable-dream work and the virtual diagnostics (the §8 decode heads) render from the physically-constrained state, not from a free generative prior. A dreamed camera frame or a synthesised diagnostic is therefore anchored to a real equilibrium, inheriting Layer 0's grounding rather than hallucinating — the decoders decorate a physical skeleton instead of inventing one.

Layer 2 — long-horizon steerable dreams. The closed-loop filter (§7) rolled forward under a plan, with the §7a transport prior folding the dynamics through (irreversible, dissipative, budgeted) and the §12 neural-SDE upgrade making them continuous-time and stochastic. The GS skeleton keeps every rolled-out step physical; the transport prior keeps it moving forward in time; controllability is measured on the identifiable chain (the §10 powered ΔN-M gate), not assumed. Stage 4 (controllable-forecast-and-gate, f-malwm-04) remains THE goal gate for this layer — the bar the prior camera/RSSM work failed.

The stage-2→stage-3 bridge. The one place Layer 0 is still incomplete is the X-point and the limited↔diverted boundary — an honest-measured gap (LCFS skill −6.26; Workstream A4 located it in interior structure, not the readout, so no post-hoc readout lever moves it). The bridge plan equilibrium-topology-fidelity closes it by interior-diagnostic fusion (interferometer / SXR / MSE), a learned equilibrium prior, temporal coherence, and a calibrated limited/diverted classification — carrying stage 2 from axis-referee-grade to boundary-referee-grade so that stage 3's closed-loop filter (§7, f-malwm-03) inherits a topologically-honest equilibrium rather than one with spurious saddles. It is the informs edge between stage 2 and stage 3.

Fleet suitability

  • P0 — fleet-suitable.   • P2–P4 — NOT fleet. The latent, the filter, and the training loop are tightly-coupled, coherence-critical model development; they need single-author Opus work with the full design in context. Locked by the lead 2026-06-24: the minimal TRUE, machine-agnostic structure (Maxwell/GS, not EFIT's fitting choices) that makes geometry recoverable without labels. Conservation/SDE structure is sequenced as an upgrade (§12), not baked into v0.

How is the state inferred and rolled forward?

Locked by the lead 2026-06-24: closed-loop predict-correct fixes the open-loop persistence collapse, gives calibrated uncertainty, makes controllability testable, and reuses sequential_da.py + the GS operator. The neural-SDE is the §12 transition-kernel upgrade.

What performs the closed-loop correction?

Locked by the lead 2026-06-24: a small ensemble in the learned latent gives a richer posterior than a single learned gain, at acceptable cost.

Which modalities are in the v0 build?

Locked by the lead 2026-06-24: include interferometer + SXR from day one (interior structure); decode all five cameras (decode quality tracks each camera's real-frame coverage — only rbb is dense).

Do we anchor the TEMPORAL evolution of ψ (∂ψ/∂t), and how?

Locked by the lead 2026-06-24. GS anchors ψ spatially (force balance at an instant) but there was no transport anchor on ∂ψ/∂t.
