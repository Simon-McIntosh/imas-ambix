§1 — Overview: the accelerator question, re-opened on the map it was waiting for

Why revisit. The shipped accelerator study (differentiable-solve-accelerator) ran on the FIRST smooth read — a host-side sigmoid weight over the hard connectivity binding — and the single-shot device rollout (gpu-accelerated-labeler §3) still builds its map from the HARD kernel: exact-min binding, boolean flood core mask. Two things have landed since that change the question: (i) the temperature-smoothed read (boundary_read_smooth_jax: softmin binding + retracted-gate sigmoid core weight, τ-controlled, 13/13 gates) makes the whole map end-to-end differentiable — exact autograd tangents flow from every read scalar through ψ to the currents; and (ii) the rollout showed the pin belongs inside the map (the per-sweep closed-form K=2 scaffold LSQ holds the basin on all 48 slices) but that on the pinned hard map Anderson merely ties Picard (~49% of warm slices converge at a 20-sweep budget) — convergence RATE, not basin control, is now the corpus engine's wall.

The probe (measured 2026-07-21, committed). scripts/tau_map_accelerator_probe.py re-uses the rollout's own staged shot (21978: 48 disc-readable slices, real campaign Green's matrix) and races four iterations under the hard and smooth (τ=10⁻³) reads at a shared 40-map-evaluation budget, cold from the disc seed — the hardest seeding class:

Why this is GEMM-batch compatible by construction. The exact tangent of the map is dominated by the SAME Green's GEMM that carries a Picard sweep: each GMRES matvec is one G·(∂i) column plus elementwise read tangents (the flood gate is a boolean selection — stop-grad, zero tangent cost; softmin/sigmoid tangents are elementwise). The probe's NK uses jax.linearize + fixed-iteration GMRES (restart = maxiter, batched solve method, no early exit): no data-dependent shapes, no host branching — jax.vmap over slices turns tangent passes and Picard sweeps into indistinguishable columns of one batched GEMM, and the small per-slice dense work (the m≈6 Hessenberg solve, the 2×2 or K×K Newton blocks) batches as tiny einsums exactly like the Anderson mixer already does. Per-slice heterogeneity is handled the way the labeller already does: fixed budgets + per-column convergence masking. The tangent GEMM can ride the tf32/bf16 tensor-core lanes measured in the gpu-accelerated-labeler §2 spike.

Two supporting probe facts. (i) Derivative health: the analytic jvp of the full pinned map agrees with central finite differences to ~10⁻⁹ at a settled iterate on BOTH reads — the hard map's non-smoothness lives on the mask-flip sets the iteration crosses, which is why even hard-map NK reaches 44% (clean tangents between flips) and why the smooth map, which removes the flip sets, compounds to 62%. (ii) The τ sweep: plain-Picard convergence RISES with temperature (17% at τ≤10⁻³ → 25% at τ≥3·10⁻³ on the subsample) — a smoother map contracts better, motivating τ-continuation (anneal from a hot, strongly-contracting map to the accurate τ=10⁻³ operating point the read's gates calibrated).

Firewall unchanged: GS force balance, physics + measured channels only, no EFIT; the current-centroid pin stays a measured-moment soft prior inside the map.

Calibrate the τ operating point: the read's own gates fixed accuracy (core count 0.99% at τ=10⁻³; ψ_bnd/radii exact); this rung fixes the SOLVE side — convergence rate vs τ on the full 48-slice set (the probe's subsample suggests hotter is faster), and whether a 2–3 stage τ-continuation (e.g. 10⁻² → 3·10⁻³ → 10⁻³) beats any single temperature at equal total evaluations. Gate: the smooth-kernel solve reproduces the probe's smooth-map convergence (≥19% Picard cold-seed baseline) and the monotone-residual signature, with the τ schedule's accuracy-at-emit equal to the τ=10⁻³ read (labels never emitted from a hot read).

§3 — Remove the read wall (the dominant per-evaluation cost, cut FIRST so every later rung inherits it)

The measured batched solve (76.4 solves/s at B=512, 60 sweeps) against the bare GEMM (0.84M slices/s) shows the topology read is ~99.7% of every evaluation — the cold binding-level search (48 flood levels + 12 bisections, each a ~(nr+nz)-step dilation saturation) dwarfs the matvec. This is the plan's critical-path rung: it lands immediately after the re-point so the accelerator tuning (§4), the coupled-profile work (§5), and the batched demonstration (§6) all iterate — and are all BUDGETED — on the fast read, not the slow one. Removals, each with a read-equivalence check (a faster read that reads a different boundary is a regression, not a win):

  • Warm-started binding search — between sweeps the binding level s* moves by ~τ; bisect inside a narrow bracket around the previous sweep's level (cold 48-level sweep only on the first evaluation or on a bracket miss, detected fixed-shape). ~10× fewer floods per read.
  • Log-depth flood fill — replace the (nr+nz)-step dilation saturation with dilate-by-doubling (~log₂(nr+nz) composite steps): ~20× on the flood kernel, exactly the same fixed point.
  • Iteration-resolution read — a coarser (n_levels/n_bisect/n_ray) read while iterating, with the calibrated τ=10⁻³ full-resolution read at emit only; the emit read is byte-identical to the §2 kernel by construction.

(The fourth removal — the read frozen per Newton linearisation, so tangent passes re-use it — is structural to NK and lands with §4.) Gates: (a) ≥ 5× per-evaluation wall at B=512 vs the §2 kernel, measured in the probe harness; (b) the warm-started binding reproduces the cold-sweep binding on the staged shot to the sub-τ level (and falls back to the cold sweep on bracket miss, counted and reported); (c) the emitted (full- resolution) read byte-comparable to the §2 kernel on every slice.

§4 — Adopt + budget-tune the batched Newton–Krylov (single-slice form)

Promote the probe's NK runner to the production single-slice solver form and tune its budget shape for the two seeding classes: the WARM chain (the march's steady state — the rollout's 49%-at-20-sweeps pain) and the COLD disc seed (the probe's class). Levers, all measured against the shared evaluation accounting: warmup length (8 cold — likely 2–4 warm), GMRES depth m (6 in the probe; smaller m = more Newton steps per budget), Newton damping / step cap, forcing sequence (loose GMRES early, tight late — Eisenstat–Walker style but with FIXED per-stage budgets so shapes stay static), and τ-continuation folded into the Newton path. Compare against smooth-map Anderson (retest on the warm class: its history should finally be clean — the probe's cold class barely engages it) and plain Picard. Gates: (a) warm-chain converged fraction at the 20-evaluation budget ≥ 80% (vs 49% today) on the staged shot's warm march, byte-comparable equilibria (axis ≤ 0.1 cm to the converged Picard fixed point on co-converged slices); (b) cold-seed converged fraction ≥ 60% at 40 evaluations (probe: 62% — must not regress under tuning); (c) an honest census of the never-converging remainder (the under-determined class) with the under-determination diagnostic recorded, not hidden.

§5 — Lift the rich profile into a coupled KKT Newton state (stop the higher-order thrash)

The probe and the rollout run the K=2 scaffold, whose closed-form fit is smooth and well-posed — the coefficients are safely implicit in the map. The PRODUCTION rich profile (K=6 non-negative ladder) is neither: the per-sweep LSQ re-solve thrashes the under-constrained higher-order coefficients along the null space (the solve already carries a profile_relax under-relaxation for exactly this), and the non-negative fit's active set flips discretely — a re-introduced non-smoothness that would corrupt the Newton tangents this plan just cleaned. This rung solves the COUPLED root problem in one Newton state x = (ψ, θ): F_ψ = ψ − G·i(c(θ), read(ψ)) − ψ_coil, and F_θ = the gradient/KKT residual of the regularised profile objective at c(θ) — data rows + smoothness ridge + hard Ip anchor + centroid tether + (when the temporal engine supplies one) the diffusion-evolved coefficient prior row — with non-negativity made smooth by reparameterisation (c = softplus(θ)), never an active set. Newton then moves the coefficients INCREMENTALLY along the regularised manifold: the ridge/prior rows sit inside ∂F_θ/∂θ, so under-constrained directions are damped by construction and the profile_relax hack retires. The coupling blocks stay GEMM-native: ∂F_ψ/∂θ is K extra Green's family-image columns; ∂F_θ/∂ψ flows through the smooth read's elementwise tangents. The same coupled residual, extended with time rows, is the natural 4D-Var / smoother substrate the labeller's differentiable-rollout ambition needs. Gates: (a) on the interior-null (previously thrashing) slices the coupled state's coefficient trajectory is stable (no sweep-to-sweep sign thrash; coefficient step norms decay) where the alternating ladder oscillates; (b) the coupled solve reproduces the alternating ladder's converged equilibria on the well-posed population (axis/LCFS/profile within the established smooth-read tolerances); (c) label honesty — under-determined slices remain flagged, never fabricated.

§6 — Batched demonstration on the H200 (the GEMM form, proven at B≥512)

vmap the §4 NK solver over B≥512 slices (unique staged slices tiled, as the foundation spike did) and demonstrate on one H200: (a) the whole batched NK graph compiles and runs fixed-shape on device (no host round-trip, no per-column branching), GPU/CPU fp64 parity at the spike's ~10⁻¹⁴ level; (b) throughput: batched-NK slices/s at B=512 vs batched Picard AT EQUAL CONVERGED FRACTION — the honest comparison is evaluations-to-converged × batch cost, and the tangent GEMM must ride the same tensor-core lanes (fp64 + tf32 variants, axis-delta re-checked against the §2 spike's 0.004 cm tf32 figure); (c) per-column convergence masking so converged columns stop paying (masked updates, not early exit). Gates: (a) batched NK at B=512 delivers ≥ 2× the converged-slices/s of batched Picard at matched convergence depth (a FLOOR — the per-converged-slice probe ratio is far larger); (b) the §3 read-cost removals hold their ≥ 5× per-evaluation wall inside the full batched NK graph; (c) equilibria byte-comparable per column to the single-slice §4 solver.

§7 — Integrate into the corpus engine and the windowed PinT solver

Wire the adopted accelerator into the corpus-labeller path (gpu-accelerated-labeler §4's batch-over-shots wrap: the NK inner solve replaces the fixed-sweep Picard inside the march scan) and into the windowed parallel-in-time solver (fewer, deeper outer iterations per window — the PinT pareto was chain-capped at 2.44×, and a faster inner contraction moves that pareto directly). Re-stamp the spine benchmark (new accelerator dimension on the existing topology-read arm) and record the corpus-scale projection honestly: converged fraction, evaluations budget, and slices/GPU-hour, against the §4/§6 gates. Binding corpus objective (the promise this plan serves): the WHOLE MAST corpus (~12.6k shots, ~600k slices) labelled in ≤ 1 hour on the 8×H200 node, converged-fraction and label-honesty flags at parity with the CPU reference. The measured foundations already put the inner solve at ~16 minutes on 8 cards (76.4 solves/s × 8 at B=512, before NK and before the §3 read-cost removals); the hour is the budget for the full pipeline — march chaining, diffusion carry, rich profile, I/O — not for the solve. A miss is reported against this number with the component budget attached, never re-scoped silently. § Decisions

Which convergence accelerator is adopted as the batched inner solver?

Presumptive nk-jvp on the probe evidence (48 cold-seed slices: 62% converged at 40 evals, residual 3.4e-5, lower wall than Picard; GEMM-native by construction). Locked by §4 on the warm-chain gate + §6 on the batched-throughput gate.

Single temperature or τ-continuation, and at what emit accuracy?

OPEN — §2 measures it. The probe's sweep shows hotter maps contract better (17%→25% Picard converged); the read's gates fix accuracy at τ=10⁻³ (core 0.99%). Labels are never emitted from a hot read regardless.

How do the rich-profile coefficients enter the Newton iteration?

Presumptive coupled-kkt-state for the rich profile (lead steer 2026-07-21): the alternating per-sweep LSQ thrashes under-constrained higher-order coefficients (the profile_relax hack exists for this) and the nonneg active set is a discrete flip — both cured by expressing the fit as a smooth KKT residual inside the Newton state. The K=2 scaffold's closed-form fit stays implicit (smooth, well-posed, probe-proven). Locked by §5 on the interior-null stability gate.
