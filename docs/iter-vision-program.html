§1 — ITER's AI vision, mapped to MAST

ITER operates beyond the knowledge frontier and will produce petabytes per day — a torrent no LLM can be synchronised with directly. The strategy: use frontier LLMs as "shovels" to prospect that information gold-mine, paired with two durable, shareable products — a World Model (an understanding of how the plasma evolves) and a Knowledge Graph (surprise-weighted findings, persisted and shared independently of the agent teams that mine them). During the operational day, agents compare World-Model predictions against experimental reality and commit findings to memory; at night they "dream" — an action-replay compacts and links memories, emphasising the surprising and filing the known, gamifying tomorrow's experiments and having nightmares about what to avoid. The treasures are the deliverable; the agents are the miners.

This is the thin spine mapping that vision onto the existing imas-ambix constellation — it extends physics-world-model-strategy, it does not restate it. The current phase is day-state forward-model identification: build the World Model that predicts the plasma state during a discharge, wringing the maximum information out of MAST's high-dynamic-range diagnostics, and persist its prediction-vs-reality residuals (the day-state "surprise" signal) toward the Knowledge Graph.

§2 — The substrate is the foundation, and it is built now

Everything downstream consumes a robust, multi-modal token store of all MAST high-dynamic-range inputs. Curating that store is a first-class asset with value beyond any single downstream proposal — it is the reusable substrate for the forward model, for diagnostic readout, for structure discovery, and for transfer to ITER. It is therefore developed independently and now, not gated behind one experiment. Two facts make it both necessary and tractable:

  • Today only one camera and low-rate magnetics are tokenised, and the fast signals are silently destroyed. The 4.02B-token corpus is rbb-only; eight other cameras + every high-frequency signal (~5 kHz Mirnov mode structure, 500 kHz SXR, 50 kHz Dα/ELM, reflectometry) are not in a token substrate — and the signal path resamples everything onto a 100 Hz grid (50 Hz Nyquist), annihilating exactly the dynamic range the physics lives in.
  • The tokenisers need real, independent development — they are not a convenience layer. They must be phase-aware (the interior-information study proved magnitude-only representations are blind to mode structure) and transformer-capable — patch transformers for 1D→3D signals (PatchTST/ViT-style patchification + a learned codebook) so a downstream attention model can consume any modality on a common footing. This is a genuine modelling effort, run on the idle H200s.

The idle 2×H200 are not a cost to conserve — they are capacity to use. Camera encodes, tokeniser training, and reconstruction-fidelity validation run on them now.

§5 — Locked decisions, the Knowledge-Graph stub, and the scientific discipline

Locked decisions:

  • The token store is a first-class, reusable asset; tokenisers are phase-aware and transformer-capable (patch transformers, 1D→3D); the substrate is developed independently and now.
  • Idle GPUs are used, not conserved.
  • Camera placement input-enrichment is killed (aleatoric within-view floor, measured) — a richer camera substrate is still built for the other modalities and for the forward model's other readouts, but we do not claim a second camera fixes placement.
  • The live camera lever is the temporal-evolution objective (frame-to-frame motion).
  • TORAX grounds the current-diffusion channel only, inside the M3 cluster; never the camera/edge/fast dynamics; never trained on as a fidelity target.

Knowledge-Graph stub (v0): a JSON surprise-event record — {shot/regime, modality, time-window, prediction, measured reality, residual, surprise-weight normalised by calibrated uncertainty, aleatoric-vs-epistemic split, links: predicts/contradicts/explains/supersedes} — emitted by the existing eval harnesses, seeded from our four honest negatives (interior-information QUALIFIED-NEGATIVE; GS Q1 fail; persistence-beats-engine on stationary Dα; the aleatoric placement floor). The KG persists physics findings; reckon persists plan state — no duplication. The queryable store + the day/night dream-loop are deferred until calibrated multi-modal surprise exists.

Scientific discipline (on claims, not on building): the substrate is built unconditionally. The forward-model fusion claim — "modality X improves the forward model" — is held to a clean test: scored on the motion-weighted / whole-family-held-out subset against a matched static baseline, paired bootstrap CI clear of zero, with a negative reported as a result. That is how we wring maximum information honestly: build the best inputs we can, then let the measurement, not the hope, decide what they buy.
