§1 — Overview: the classical headroom the MSE gate exposed

The §4 held-out-MSE gate proved the reanalysis engine beats persistence CI-clear (0.362 rad) but sits 0.137 rad short of the classical EnKF (0.225), and the profile comparison localised why: our GS current profile is too broad and over-predicts the outboard pitch, while EnKF’s Te-informed profile concentrates current core-ward. That gap is admissible physics the firewall-clean engine chose not to use — the frozen spine kept the interior minimal on purpose ("interior profile fidelity intentionally NOT chased classically; flows to the learned operator"), tuned for boundary fidelity, with the resistivity frozen and the q ≥ 1 and SOL terms off. Now that the binding gate is interior pitch, that minimalism is leaving real, firewall-safe physics on the table. This plan closes it — on the faster, cleaner-metric grid-free substrate (greens-filament-solver, its hard prerequisite) — so the learned operator inherits a smaller, genuinely-unmeasured residual instead of one that still contains classical physics (the residual the learned operator’s R2 already failed to extract from raw sensors).

§3 — A fittable pedestal model

Add a properly parametrized pedestal — an edge transport barrier with a steep p′/current gradient just inside the separatrix, with fittable position, width, and height — distinct from the SOL-current foot. The low-order smooth basis cannot represent a pedestal, and its absence shows in the larger outboard pitch residual (0.62 rad vs 0.43 near-axis). The pedestal DOF is fit to the magnetics and validated on held-out MSE, never asserted. Gate G3: on H-mode-class slices the pedestal earns a CI-clear held-out-pitch improvement (particularly the outboard channels), or an honest null; it must not degrade L-mode slices.

§ Decisions

How is the parallel resistivity η(ψ_N) determined?

Locked by the lead (2026-07-20). The §4 gap to EnKF is specifically the measured-Te → σ(η) closure; Te is a measurement, so using it is firewall-safe (not a tuned gain). It must be OPTIONAL because Thomson is not available on all shots — hence the frozen-η fallback and the provenance/bias audit so mixed-η labels stay honest. Fully-learned η is deferred to the learned operator (the residual after this physics closure).

Do we model the H-mode pedestal, and how?

Locked by the lead (2026-07-20): the SOL foot models SOL current, NOT a pedestal; we are missing a good pedestal model and it needs to be properly parametrized so we can fit it. A pedestal is a steep edge gradient inside the separatrix that the low-order smooth basis cannot represent; its absence shows in the larger outboard pitch residual. Admitted as a fittable physical DOF, validated on MSE — never asserted.

How do heterogeneous-η (mixed measured-Te / frozen) labels stay clean for training?

Locked by the lead (2026-07-20), answering "will optional η(Te) affect the quality of the labelled dataset?". Label-honesty already firewalls the obvious risk (only MSE-validated profiles are asserted, on one ruler). The residual risk is selection bias — if Thomson-covered shots are a systematically different regime, the high-fidelity interior labels concentrate there. Recording provenance + auditing coverage turns that risk into metadata the learned operator can condition on, rather than a silent bias.

How is richer interior representation kept from becoming over-fitting?

The direct internal measurement (held-out MSE) is the discipline: more DOF is admitted only when MSE says it recovers real structure, not because it fits the magnetics better (the magnetics under-determine the interior — the whole premise). This keeps the firewall-safe physics from degenerating into a well-fitted guess.
