§1 — Why: the v0 EnKF is weak, and the bar must be strong first

The S9 sprint settled the strategic question: on mse-free-current-recovery-v0 a supervised oracle proved that the binding constraint for MSE-free internal-current recovery on MAST is interior information, not ML. Time-history adds nothing (mag-history RMSE 0.190 ≈ mag-instant 0.188 on the locked mse_eval sightline metric), and near-axis interior is model-failure-floored at ~0.41 rad for magnetics and all multimodal channels — a third independent re-confirmation of classic equilibrium under-determination (external magnetics fix the boundary and total Ip, never the internal j(ψ)). The pivot directive that follows is unambiguous: shore up a strong physics baseline, then chain more internal-current diagnostics, before building any ML. This plan is the first half — the strong physics baseline.

The v0 EnKF baseline (the D2 TORAX parameter-ensemble smoother) is genuinely weak. It is a parameter-space ensemble smoother: it perturbs 3–5 global scalar parameters θ, runs each ensemble member as a free-running TORAX trajectory over the whole shot, and applies one Ensemble-Kalman-Inversion batch update to those parameters against the pooled magnetics innovation. That construction produces four diagnosable symptoms, each of which has a known cure in a properly sequential filter:

  • Noisy late-time j(ρ,t) — an unconstrained ensemble of free parameter trajectories diverges over the shot; some members' on-axis safety factor q0 blows up because the batch update never re-anchors the state mid-shot.
  • Poor q0(t) match — the free-running prior is biased (default TORAX geometry, no per-shot R0/Bt0), and a single batch update over global parameters cannot correct a time-varying state error.
  • Uncalibrated UQ — measured conformal 90% coverage cov90 = 0.465 against the target band [0.88, 0.92]: the ensemble spread is not a calibrated posterior.
  • Fragility & cost — the JIT/stiffness late-time divergence forced the provisional N=3 subset (the JIT-fast shots); the full held-out bar was only later established at pitch RMSE 0.225 [0.199, 0.259] across N=112 (persistence 0.719).

The bankable wins. A sequential per-timestep EKF over a full ψ(ρ) state cures all four: it re-anchors the state at every measurement, so it cannot diverge late-time; it filters a state (not a handful of global parameters), so it tracks a time-varying error; and its posterior covariance becomes a calibrate-able object. The goal of this plan is explicit and pre-registered: establish the true achievable frontier — what the physics alone can recover from MSE-free magnetics + a current-diffusion forward model — not to claim an interior win that the oracle has already shown is impossible from magnetics alone.

§2 — The reduced 1-D ψ-diffusion EKF (RAPTOR-faithful)

The substrate is a reduced 1-D poloidal-flux-diffusion forward model in the RAPTOR-EKF lineage (Felici et al., Plasma Phys. Control. Fusion 2012, and the 2018 real-time current-profile-control follow-on). The state evolution is the 1-D resistive poloidal-flux-diffusion equation discretised on the ρ grid, with neoclassical parallel resistivity η∥(Te, Zeff) from the Sauter formula driven by the measured Thomson Te (the §4 closure channel from mse-free-current-recovery-v0), bootstrap + auxiliary current sources, and the loop-voltage / Ip boundary drive. The forward map is mildly nonlinear and sparse: the EKF Jacobian ∂ψk+1/∂ψk is banded (nearest-neighbour coupling from the diffusion stencil), so the predict step is cheap and the analytic Jacobian avoids the finite-ensemble noise that plagues a parameter EnKF.

TORAX leaves the inner loop. The decisive architectural change. In v0, TORAX was the transition, run once per ensemble member with a batch update wrapped around it — the source of the JIT/stiffness late-time divergence and the 8–9 s/member slowdown on stiff/high-Ip shots. Here TORAX runs once per shot as a forecast reference (a free-running, physics-grounded trajectory the EKF is anchored to and cross-checked against), outside the sequential recursion. The recursion itself uses the reduced banded-Jacobian forward, which is JIT-stable and fast. Pulling the stiff JAX solver out of the per-step loop is what dissolves the late-time divergence symptom at its root.

§3 — Sequential recursion with per-step re-anchoring

The filter is a textbook EKF recursion advanced one measurement step at a time over the mse_eval time grid:

  1. Predict — advance ψ⁻k through the reduced diffusion forward using the banded analytic Jacobian; propagate the covariance with process inflation (§4).
  2. Update — assimilate the magnetics innovation through the EFIT-free GS observation operator (the linearised H from gs/residual.py), producing the analyzed state ψ⁺k and posterior covariance.
  3. Re-anchor — the next predict step starts from the analyzed ψ⁺k, not from a free-running prior. This is exactly the mechanism the v0 batch smoother lacks: per-step re-anchoring is what prevents the unconstrained late-time ensemble divergence and the q0 blow-ups.

The filtered state is ψ(ρ), not j. Poloidal flux is the smooth, well-conditioned primitive; the current density j ∝ ∂²ψ/∂ρ² is a second derivative of it. Filtering j directly amplifies high-wavenumber noise into the very interior structure we cannot constrain. Filtering ψ and deriving j (and q, and the pitch readout) post-hoc keeps the state estimate smooth and the covariance interpretable.

§4 — Per-shot geometry, inflation, and observation noise

The v0 q0(t) bias traces partly to default geometry. This plan derives the machine geometry per shot:

  • Geometry — R0 and Bt0 from the measured toroidal-field current amc.tf_current (vacuum Bt = μ₀ N ITF / 2πR0), so the free-running prior is no longer biased by a generic default. The minor radius / elongation come from the magnetics boundary fit that the GS operator already consumes.
  • Process inflation — a multiplicative covariance inflation on the predict step compensates for the reduced-model error (the reduced forward is not the full TORAX physics), so the filter does not become over-confident and ignore the measurements late in the shot.
  • Observation noise R — reuse the existing gs/residual.py robust_sensor_scale machinery (already used for the amb pickup-coil magnetics) to set the per-channel measurement-noise covariance, rather than a hand-tuned scalar.

§5 — Mandatory localization onto the leading singular vectors of H

This is non-negotiable, and is the single most important safeguard in the plan. A full-state ψ(ρ) filter has far more degrees of freedom than the magnetics actually constrain. The Stage-2 / oracle / GS-rank evidence is consistent: the row-scaled observation operator H has only ~5–6 informative right-singular vectors — the external magnetics see a low-rank projection of the interior. Without localization, the ensemble's finite-sample null-space correlations let the filter fabricate spurious interior structure: it would manufacture an interior j(ψ) that the data cannot support, exactly the failure mode the under-determination wall predicts. The analysis update is therefore localized onto the leading ~5–6 right-singular vectors of the row-scaled H; modes below the cutoff are not updated by magnetics and stay on the physics prior. This is what keeps the strong baseline honest — it updates only what the measurements truly inform.

§6 — Calibrated UQ via split-conformal (solved here, not re-opened)

The v0 cov90 = 0.465 is cured by split-conformal calibration on the 3361 calibration shots from the locked mse_heldout_split_v0 manifest. Conformity scores are computed per-channel and per-radial-band on the calibration partition, the quantiles are frozen, and then applied to the 112-shot held-out test partition. The target is cov90 ∈ [0.88, 0.92] — the same gate Stage-1 met. This is the calibration construct used across the project; it is reused as-is here, the same engine in statespace/calibration.py.

Calibration is solved in this plan and is not re-opened downstream. In particular, the neural-KF survey (neural-kf-architecture-survey) must not treat cov90 as a second open problem to be fixed with a learned filter — split-conformal already delivers a frozen, distribution-free coverage guarantee here.

§7 — The decisive pressure experiment (handed in from the diagnostics doc)

The strong baseline is also the harness for the one genuinely new interior lever. The companion diagnostics plan, Internal-Current Diagnostics: EFIT Taxonomy → MAST Availability → Pressure-as-GS-p′, defines an Hpressure observation operator that maps the filtered state to a predicted kinetic-pressure profile p(ρ) via Grad–Shafranov force-balance — coupling p′ into the FF′ / current decomposition — and a decisive 45-shot cohort (held-out shots with usable near-axis atm Thomson). That experiment runs inside the EKF substrate built here: with-vs-without GS-p′ pressure assimilation, scored by near-axis interior RMSE against held-out MSE. This plan provides the substrate and the localized analysis update; the diagnostics plan provides the Hpressure operator and the cohort.

§8 — Acceptance & the pre-registered frontier

All scoring is on the locked D1 mse_eval harness (the ShotPrediction / score / pitch_from_current_profile contract, gated + error-weighted on beam-on slices, the shot as the independent unit, N=112 held-out). The v1 bar is reported with bootstrap CIs against the v0 EnKF bar (pitch RMSE 0.225 [0.199, 0.259]) and persistence (0.719).

Pre-registered expected frontier (locked before any v1 number): the sightline pitch RMSE achievable from MSE-free magnetics + current-diffusion physics is ~0.19 (the oracle's mag-instant 0.188 / mag-history 0.190 ceiling on the same metric); the near-axis interior remains model-failure-floored at ~0.41 rad. No interior win is claimed from magnetics alone. A v1 that reaches ~0.19 sightline with calibrated cov90 and a clean near-axis ~0.41 floor is a success — it establishes the true physics frontier the pressure experiment (§7) then tries to break, and against which any future ML (the survey) is measured.

§ Decisions

What is the primary data-assimilation substrate for the strong baseline?

LOCKED (S10, 2026-06-02): the reduced banded-Jacobian 1-D ψ-diffusion EKF is PRIMARY. TORAX runs once per shot as a free-running forecast reference and cross-check only — pulling the stiff JAX solver OUT of the per-step loop is what dissolves the v0 JIT/stiffness late-time divergence and the 8–9 s/member slowdown. FIRST validate that the reduced model reproduces TORAX q(ρ,t) on a few representative shots before trusting it as the substrate; only then run the full filter. The v0 parameter batch-smoother is retired as the baseline.

What quantity is the filtered state vector?

LOCKED (S10, 2026-06-02): filter ψ(ρ), the smooth well-conditioned primitive; derive j ∝ ∂²ψ/∂ρ² , q and the pitch readout post-hoc. Filtering j directly would amplify high-wavenumber noise into the very interior structure the data cannot constrain.

Is rank localization of the analysis update mandatory, and to how many DOF?

LOCKED (S10, 2026-06-02): localization is MANDATORY. The row-scaled observation operator H has only ~5–6 informative right-singular vectors (Stage-2 / oracle / GS-rank consistent). Without localization, finite-ensemble null-space correlations let the full-state filter fabricate spurious interior structure the data cannot support. Modes below the cutoff stay on the physics prior. This is the safeguard that keeps the baseline honest.

How is the cov90 calibration problem solved, and is it re-opened downstream?

LOCKED (S10, 2026-06-02): split-conformal on the 3361 calibration shots (per-channel + per-band, frozen, applied to the 112 test) → cov90 ∈ [0.88, 0.92], the Stage-1 gate. Calibration is SOLVED in this plan and is NOT re-opened in the neural-KF survey — that doc must not treat cov90 as a second open problem for a learned filter.

What frontier is pre-registered as success, before any v1 number is produced?

LOCKED (S10, 2026-06-02) — pre-registered BEFORE any v1 number: the achievable sightline frontier from MSE-free magnetics + current-diffusion physics is ~0.19 (oracle mag-instant 0.188 / mag-history 0.190); near-axis interior stays model-failure-floored at ~0.41 rad. NO interior win is claimed from magnetics alone. Reaching ~0.19 sightline with calibrated cov90 and a clean ~0.41 near-axis floor IS the success — it establishes the true physics frontier, against which the §7 pressure experiment and any future ML are measured.
