Candidate colour-role palettes on the OP1/OP2 cross-section

Three candidate palettes for the JT-60SA geometry and signal figures, each drawn on the real OP1 and OP2 cross-section read from the converted description store (load_packaged_machine_map("jt-60sa") joined with description_store_root_path(), IDS read with imas.DBEntry). The reader and the one cross-section function live in scripts/figures_jt60sa_geometry.py; the module imas_ambix/plot_style.py holds the named colour roles. The two vessel roles are lightness steps of one hue (the OP1 and OP2 vessels are the same structure at the two phase geometries); the remaining four roles take distinct, non-red hues — red stays reserved for a limit being broken. The lead chooses one palette before later figure sections draw with it.

Every candidate was checked with node /home/ITER/mcintos/.cache/dataviz/validate_palette.js <hex-list> --mode light --pairs all. All three report ALL CHECKS PASS, including the contrast line: every one of the six roles holds at least 3:1 against the white surface. Each family carries a direct label in its own colour, so identity never rests on colour alone.
