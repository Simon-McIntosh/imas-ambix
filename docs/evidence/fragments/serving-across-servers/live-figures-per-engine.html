The watch document reports one live entry per engine, and the period ledger prices each model's traffic at that model's own listing.

  • The live block is per serve. A raw receipt file is selected for each serve it names, keyed on hostname, served model and job, and each selected file's figures are published under live.engines. The newest serve's figures stay at the top level, so a reader that knows only the flat block sees what it always saw.
  • Shelf life. A serve whose newest readable row is older than the shelf life yields no entry at all. The shelf life is read from the lane document's suggested_shelf_life_seconds where it publishes one, and defaults to 120 s otherwise.
  • A tail that cannot be read is one engine's loss. One unparsable trailing line is a file caught mid-append and the rows before it stand. Two or more mean the newest samples were never written, and that engine's entry says so — state: unavailable, with the count and the reason — rather than presenting older rows as its present state.
  • The ledger is per model. Readings are grouped by the model that wrote them, resolved from the served name stored beside each sample and from the job each reading belongs to, so no sample payload is decoded. A window holding two models emits one row per model plus a total row whose cost is the sum of those rows; a window holding one model keeps exactly the single row it had.

Measured. Two serves placed at the same instants — deepseek-v4-1-flash on 98dci4-gpu-0003 job 4811 advancing 1,000 generation tokens over 10 s, and glm-5.3 on 98dci4-gpu-0007 advancing 500 — yield two live entries with 100.0 and 50.0 tok/s respectively, each carrying its own host, served name and job. A third file whose newest row is 600 s old yields no entry while the fresh serve yields one at age_seconds 0.0. In the ledger, a period carrying deepseek-v4-flash at 1,000,000 prompt / 100,000 generated tokens and glm-5.3 at 2,000,000 / 100,000, priced from their two listings at h = 0.55, gives $7.05 and $24.20 and a total of $31.25, which is the sum of the two rather than either rate applied to combined tokens ($14.10 or $28.20), so the total is not a third pricing. The three gated files pass 95 tests, the wider agent-watch set passes 119, and ruff check imas_ambix tests exits 0.

Negative control. Two unparsable lines are appended to one of two fresh receipt files. That engine's entry reads state: unavailable with 2 trailing lines of the receipt file do not parse, so its newest samples were never written and tail_breaks: 2, while the other engine's entry stays measured at 100 tok/s. Run against the previous revision's module, the same fixture publishes one block — glm-5.3, state: measured, 50 tok/s, no reason — and the other serve is absent entirely, which is the defect this change removes.
