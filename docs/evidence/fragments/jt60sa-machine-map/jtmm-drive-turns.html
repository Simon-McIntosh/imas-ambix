Drive ampere-turns applied per channel in the current assembly

  • ForwardOperator carries a per-column pf_current_scales vector; assemble_pf_currents multiplies each named channel by its own scale instead of the flat _KA_TURN_TO_A.
  • build_operator sets the scale from the drive's declared ampere_turns_per_ampere. Where the description has already folded that weight into the element xmult (column weight equals the declared weight — MAST's amc channels, kA·turn, turns = 1) the scale stays the flat × 1000; where the elements are normalised (Σxmult = 1 — JT-60SA's SELENE deck) the declared weight is the missing turn count and becomes the scale (40 A·turn/A for CS, 16 for EF).
  • Real-cache MAST check: every pf column scale equals _KA_TURN_TO_A and assembled currents equal raw × 1000, so the near-vacuum SI round trip is unchanged.
  • Synthetic JT-60SA check: a CS drive declaring 40 A·turns/ampere over a normalised coil gives a scale of 40 and 1 A of channel contributes 40 A·turn.
  • Gate tests/gs/test_operator.py: 23 passed, EXIT=0; ruff on the changed paths exits 0.
  • Negative control: restoring the fixed kA-turn conversion in assemble_pf_currents leaves the JT-60SA test red — obtained 1000.0 against the expected 40.0 (1 failed, 22 passed, EXIT=1).
  • The fold test keys on the column weight, the only in-scope signal of which convention a declared drive uses; a source that normalised Σxmult while declaring a weight of 1 would be indistinguishable from a folded kA·turn channel.
