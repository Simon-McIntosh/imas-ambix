Drive-topology turns carry the deck's coil totals

  • Both drive topologies in imas_ambix/data/machine_maps/jt-60sa.json now take each connection's turns from pf_active/coil/element/turns_with_sign through their declared turns_path. The ampere-turns per ampere summed over a circuit's connections read 549 for each CS coil, 142, 154, 247, 353, 152, 180 for EF1–EF6 and 23 for each FPPC coil — the SELENE deck's own coil totals (EQSLE.DATA), not the circuit's filament count. 536 connection lines changed, all turns values, no other content.
  • The operator column a coil current multiplies ALREADY sums the circuit's filaments, so build_operator now sets each column's scale to the drive's declared ampere-turns per ampere divided by the column's own summed filament weight: CS columns scale 549/40 = 13.725, EF columns 8.875–22.0625, FPPC 23/6 = 3.833. One division distributes the winding's ampere-turns across the filaments taken and counts none twice, so the predicted ampere-turns equal the deck total rather than the filament count squared.
  • The folded kA·turn convention is retained as the discriminator: a drive whose declared total equals the column's own summed weight has that weight already folded into the element xmult and its channel is left in kA·turn (MAST amc, turns = 1), so only the flat × 1000 conversion remains. Dividing there would drop a factor of 1000 rather than remove a double count. This is the branch the prior jtmm-drive-turns record flagged for revisit once the converter repair confirmed the turns were not dropped by the converter — the fix lands here, in the else branch, not by deleting the discriminator.
  • A drive stating a real turn total is reported by ForwardOperator.shapes() as the applied pf_current_scales beside the pf_amc_channels they scale, so the factor a column actually received is readable, not just its input.
  • MAST unchanged: real-cache MAST tables carry no circuit_drives for their amc channels, so drive is None and every column keeps _KA_TURN_TO_A; the frozen MAST near-vacuum SI round trip stays inside its [0.5, 1.5] band.
