1. What the SELENE deck states (EQSLE.DATA, line-numbered)

The 12  PF COIL header is line 22; line 23 carries the twelve filament counts 40 40 40 40 16 16 16 16 16 16  6  6. The per-filament turn count is fractional because the coil's whole turn count is spread evenly over its filaments: 13.725 × 40 = 549 for every CS module and 8.875 × 16 = 142 for EF1, and so on. The FPPC block carries two values (3.83 and 3.84) across its six filaments; 3.83 × 6 = 22.98, i.e. 23 turns.

2. What the converter writes into pf_active

build_pf_active writes element.turns_with_sign = abs(el.turns), i.e. the deck's per-filament value straight through. Read back from the committed store machine_description/OP1/pf_active.nc, every element of CS1–CS4 carries turns_with_sign = 13.725, and EF1–EF6 carry 8.875/9.625/15.4375/22.0625/9.5/11.25. The converter did not drop the deck's turns into pf_active — the parenthetical “1.0 per filament” in the section-6 measure is not what the store holds.

3. Where the 1.0 is written, and where the turns were dropped

The 1.0 lives one layer up, in the catalogue's drive topology. Both jt60sa-pf-drive-op1 and -op2 carry 268 connections (one per deck filament; 40×4 + 16×6 + 6×2), and every connection has turns = 1.0 and current_weight = 1.0, even though each topology also sets turns_path = "pf_active/coil/element/turns_with_sign". Because geometry_adapter._materialise_circuit_drives forms each circuit's weight as ∑ turns × current_weight × direction over its connections, the declared ampere_turns_per_ampere comes out as the filament count: 40.0 for CS1–CS4, 16.0 for EF1–EF6, 6.0 for the two FPPC, confirmed by reading the packaged catalogue. Those 40/16/6 are exactly the deck's line-23 filament counts, not the 549/142/154/247/353/152/180 turn totals. So the deck's turns were dropped by the drive-topology emission, not by build_pf_active; the emission wrote a per-filament turn and weight of 1.0 where the deck states 13.725 (CS) and 8.875–22.0625 (EF).

4. What the vacuum response can and cannot identify

The operator G_pf was built for E100595, E100579 and E100642 (44 sensor rows × 10 driven-coil columns) and a free per-coil weight was fitted to the measured MDAC loops and probes. Two estimators were run: a joint least-squares over all 44 channels (unit-normalised per channel so the Wb loops and T probes do not compete), which gives one multiplier φk per driven coil on its declared ampere-turns per ampere; and a well-conditioned group/global scale.

The per-coil weights are not identifiable from these shots. All ten MMSYS coil currents are driven together in every commissioning vacuum shot, so the operator's ten G_pf columns are strongly collinear and a free per-coil fit trades magnitude between them: the fitted per-coil φk swing from −1.5 to +0.96 between coils and between shots, and the CS/EF group effective weights are not even of one sign across the three shots. No per-coil turn count can be reported as confirmed against this response: the fitted ampere-turns differ from any candidate by far more than 10 percent, and no per-coil figure survives across the three shots. What the fit does say reliably is that the declared 40/16 (the filament counts) sit within a factor of a few of the measured response — consistent with the "40/16 are filament counts, not turn counts" reading — but the shots do not separate one coil from another enough to fix each coil's turns.

5. The correct per-filament turns, and whether the converter dropped them

The correct per-filament turn count is the deck's own column 1, quoted above with its lines: 13.725 per CS filament (CS1–CS4, lines 24–183) and 8.875/9.625/15.4375/22.0625/9.5/11.25 per EF filament (EF1–EF6, lines 184–279). These are what a per-filament weighting should use. The converter is inconsistent, not simply dropping them: build_pf_active preserves them in pf_active/coil/element/turns_with_sign, but the drive-topology emission overrides them with 1.0 per filament (its own turns and current_weight), so the effective per-coil ampere-turns per ampere the operator sees is the filament count (40/16/6), not the turn total (549/142/154/247/353/152/180). The repair is in the drive-topology emission (turns/current_weight from the deck, or a turns_path read that is actually honoured), and both phase stores' drive topologies would regenerate; the pulse-ids plan's mint copies the description and must be told first.

Negative control: a per-coil turn count reported as confirmed whose free-fit ampere-turns differ from it by more than 10 percent. The free per-coil fit here fails that test for every coil (the weights are unstable across the three shots), so no per-coil turn count is reported as confirmed; the deck value is named from its source line, not from the fit.
