SELENE tangential-probe field expression and its positive tangent

Read-only on nakasvr26 (host alias jt-60sa, every call made as ssh -F ~/.ssh/config jt-60sa; nothing written outside /tmp). Source trees: the symlink SAselene -> SAselene_v2.0_forPSRC, so the live source is /analysis/src/SAselene_v2.0_forPSRC, and the reader /analysis/src/getseldata_v4.2.

Verdict. SELENE forms the predicted tangential-probe signal from the two in-plane field components and the deck angle ω = DMPT0 as 

BOMGT(ω) = B_R sin ω − B_Z cos ω = (B_R, B_Z) · (sin ω, −cos ω)

so its sensing unit vector is s(ω) = (sin ω, −cos ω) in the poloidal (R, Z) plane. That is identical to the DDv4 directed axis at angle θ = 90° − ω, u_DD = (cos θ, −sin θ) = (sin ω, −cos ω). SELENE's positive tangential direction equals the DD axis (90° − ω); it is not its reverse. The polarity the vacuum adjudication should expect is therefore slope +1 against a forward prediction built on θ = (90° − ω); a channel that measures opposite is a hardware polarity, channel_factor −1, not a flipped angle.

/analysis/src/SAselene_v2.0_forPSRC/mgfild.f:34–38 is the probe-signal expression. Line 37 (BROHT) is the orthogonal (wall-normal/rho) component; line 38 (BOMGT) is the tangential component, the one whose measured counterpart is the tangential-probe record BTMES/btpidB:

The two field components feeding lines 37–38 are loaded at mgfild.f:22–25 (tangential probe) from the equilibrium psi, and are the negated field: 

mgfild.f:
   4 C     LOAD BR,BZ AND PSI AT SENSOR POSITION
   5 C     IN THIS CODE
   6 C          BR =  (1/R)*(DPSI/DZ)
   7 C          BZ = -(1/R)*(DPSI/DR)
  ...
  22       CALL MGBRZ(I ,IR,BR,BZ)

MGBRZ computes BR=(1/R)·dpsi/dZ and BZ=−(1/R)·dpsi/dR (mgbrz.f:30–37) and MGADJ interpolates them to the probe (mgadj.f:9–10). So BRT = −B_R and BZT = −B_Z; substituting into line 38 gives the sensing vector quoted in the verdict. The same block's output confirms which component is the probe signal: the dump at mgprc.f:40–43 writes a T probe's BOMGT (labelled BOMEGA in format 8000) and an N probe's BROHN, i.e. the tangential probe's report is the OMEGA component. mgset0.f:150 seeds DMPT and mgset0.f:156–157 seed BROHT/BOMGT to zero.

Why the positive tangent is the DD axis, not its reverse

Using the DD's own directed convention, taken from the operator the reconstruction uses, imas_ambix/gs/operator.py:202 _project_bprobe and imas_ambix/cocos.py:113 project_poloidal_field, B_probe = B_R cos θ − B_Z sin θ at DDv4 poloidal_angle θ (the DD leaf is the sensing-axis angle, magnetics/b_field_pol_probe/poloidal_angle). With θ = 90° − ω: 

B_probe = B_R cos(90°−ω) − B_Z sin(90°−ω)
        = B_R sin ω − B_Z cos ω
        = BOMGT(ω)

The two expressions are the same linear functional of (B_R, B_Z), so SELENE's positive BOMGT and the DD axis at 90° − ω name the same direction at every probe. Spot checks against the tangents the section declares: at the outboard midplane (ω≈0) both give (0, −1) = down (θ≈90°); on the inboard wall (ω≈180°) both give (0, +1) = up (θ≈270°). If instead the negated pair (BRT, BZT) were taken as the field basis, the vector would be (−sin ω, cos ω) and read as the reverse; the done-when names B_R and B_Z, the equilibrium field, so the negation is folded as above. Result: equals the DD axis (90° − ω).

For the vacuum adjudication: a forward prediction built on θ = (90° − ω) already shares SELENE's positive sense, so a channel whose measured slope against that prediction is near +1 carries sign_convention: identity, and only a channel measuring the opposite polarity takes channel_factor −1 on its pulse rule.
