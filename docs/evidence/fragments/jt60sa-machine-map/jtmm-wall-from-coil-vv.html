wall and vessel from the coil_vv contour tables, both phases

Node jtmm-wall-from-coil-vv · date 2026-10-06 · plan jt60sa-machine-map §4

By the lead's decision wall-from-coil-vv-tables both phases now take the wall and vessel from their own coil_vv_OP{1,2}.dat. Each deck carries an nlim=51 first-wall polygon (OP1 line 393, OP2 line 371; the two differ) and a 128-row contour block whose Inner VV=50 and Outer VV=50 runs are the vessel inner and outer skins and whose 28 D-probe#n rows are probes, excluded. imas_ambix/data/selene_deck.py now parses that block, draws the limiter unit from the polygon and the annular vessel unit's outline_inner and outline_outer from the two skins, and takes the deck's first passive resistivity run as the vessel (the cryostat still comes from EQSLE.DATA).

The cryostat group is now accepted only when every filament lies outside the vessel's outer-skin polyline (a point-in-polygon test), replacing the R/Z bounding-box test that accepted filaments in the inboard and top/bottom gaps the box covers but the vessel does not.

The OP1 deck was added to the store beside the three copies already there: source/coil_vv_OP1.dat sha256 55d97f6989368348108aef384b1ff1b737803fa9be6ac0b84aa718bedfda01cb (matches the jtmm-geometry-source inventory), recorded in source/provenance.json with the other three.
