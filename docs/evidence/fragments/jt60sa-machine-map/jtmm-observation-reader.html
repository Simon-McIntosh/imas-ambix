Declaration

imas_ambix/data/cocos_convention.py splits the measurement from the source it reads. A private kernel _observation_from_series takes the plasma-current, flux-loop and toroidal-field time series plus an EfitLabels equilibrium record and returns the existing ShotSignObservation. read_level2_observation keeps its signature and its MAST level-2 reads and now calls the kernel. read_catalogue_observation(shot, catalogue, equilibrium) resolves the shot to a range-scoped map, reads the three quantities through the catalogue's channel bindings and the store engine for its declared format, and passes the given EfitLabels to the same kernel. The engine's StoreArrays.read seam is used so the reader measures the raw bound arrays rather than a COCOS-transformed emission.
