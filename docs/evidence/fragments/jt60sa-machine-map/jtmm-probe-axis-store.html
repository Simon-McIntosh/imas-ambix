The SELENE converter now stores each tangential probe's true sensing axis, both JT-60SA phase stores are regenerated with the fix, and both geometry figures are re-rendered so every probe tick lies along the vessel wall. The 17 stored axes moved from the deck's outward wall-normal angle ω to the wall tangent θ = (90° − ω) mod 360°; the outboard-midplane probe MP1 now reads 89.72°, within 1° of the 90° (down) the tangential convention requires.

The reproduced defect

Before the repair, build_magnetics in imas_ambix/data/selene_deck.py wrote poloidal_angle = deg2rad(probe.angle), the deck's ω converted to radians, so every tick crossed the wall. Read from the superseded OP1 store, the 17 angles (deg) were 0.28, 21.20, 34.89, 50.39, 85.14, 124.41, 179.06, 180.01, 180.21, 180.17, 180.27, 234.21, 274.28, 311.04, 324.55, 338.37, 350.24 — the wall-normal ω. Applying the declared mutation (restoring deg2rad(omega)) turns both the tangent test and the synthetic read-back assertion red (negative_control_probe_axis.log, EXIT=1: test_stored_probe_axes_lie_along_the_vessel_inner_skin, test_writer_reads_back_at_dd_4_1_1).

The repair

ω is the outward wall-normal angle measured counter-clockwise from +R; the DD leaf is the sensing-axis angle measured clockwise from +R, and these probes are tangential. The converter therefore writes θ = (90° − ω) mod 360° in radians — the wall tangent in the direction of increasing clockwise poloidal angle, which is 90° (down) at the outboard midplane. The direction along the axis is a declared convention, stated in the converter, not a measurement. A test in tests/data/test_selene_deck.py requires every stored axis of both phases within 5° (mod 180) of the vessel inner-skin tangent at the nearest skin point, and MP1 to read 90° within 1°. Measured on the real decks, the largest departure is 3.33° (OP1 and OP2 both, nearest skin point within 0.021 m of each probe).
