E101154 PSRC category: the equilibrium arrays, read through the on-demand cache

Read 2026-10-07 from the compute allocation. The single-channel arrays were read through fetch_channels with one RemoteEddbExtractor batch (one ssh jt-60sa session), writing into the shared cache /work/projects/imas_gpu/jt60sa/101154.zarr/PSRC; 78 channels were fetched and none refused. The 143 candidate data names come from the curated E101154 SELENE reference's data_psrc_* keys (imas-efit E101154_psrc_validation.npz). Of those, 86 are served by the PSRC category and 57 are absent.

The 57 absent names are FL1–FL34 and TC1–TC23, every one returning EDDB return code 1022 (name not in the catalogue). These are the curated NPZ's loop/probe labels, not PSRC data names: PSRC serves the same quantities as magFluxLp1–magFluxLp34 and magPbTC1–magPbTC23, both already cached and bound by the §5 pulse map. The inventory below is therefore indexed by the real PSRC names.

What the sanctioned reader cannot reach

Six served arrays are multi-channel and their EDDB record carries a per-channel unit list; fetch_channels refuses a multi-entry unit list outright (eddb_remote.normalise_unit), so the batch aborted before writing any channel until the six were excluded. The unit lists are uniform in every case — surfABVxp 604×mm, surfBLWxp 204×mm, surfp 8×m, ieddy 203×A, ishape 2×–, calMMS 310×– — so the refusal is not a genuine unit ambiguity for these arrays. They were read instead by a direct eddb_pwrapper probe on the analysis server: the wrapper returns a multi-channel record flattened time-major, reshaped here as (n_time, nch) to take the 6.0 s row. The boundary grids are in mm (surfABVxp, surfBLWxp) while the boundary summary surfp and every scalar boundary quantity are in m, so a reader of the flux grids must divide by 1000.
