Declaration

imas_ambix/challenge/convention.py publishes measure_convention(records, eligibility, *, shots), which reads canonical imas_ambix/challenge/loader.py EfitLabels records and a ConventionEligibility declaration and returns the existing ConventionReceipt. measure_diiid_convention keeps its signature and delegates to the kernel with DIIID_ELIGIBILITY, whose minimum_plasma_current_ka, standard_field_bcoil_maximum and minimum_audit_shots carry the former DIII-D constants. EfitLabels gains lcfs_r_m and lcfs_z_m, and the loader resamples the plasma-current and bcoil channels onto the EFIT time base into scalars. loader.load_labels reads only the label columns so the audit does not materialise the whole ChallengeShot per shot.

The audit's sign products are measured in the record's own canonical convention: psi_ip_sign and q_ip_bcoil_sign are the negatives of the source-convention figure, while the per-radian / total-flux discrimination and both ratio medians are unchanged. The twenty-shot DIII-D test asserts the canonical sign and the same medians (1.08030, 0.171935).
