What the census found

The E-series covers 2020-03 to 2026-06; the C-series is six 2026-06 commissioning shots (C060028..C060033); the A-series is 99 shots. Two facts decide where a raw-loop shot can live:

The raw MDAC flux loops magFlxLp1..34 and the probes magPbTC1..23 are acquired only on the OP1-era pulses numbered from E100383 onward: all 34 loops and all 23 probes are present (mdac_total 264 names) on every one of these, and the catalogue's OP1 range 100001–101173 covers them. The pre-E100000 series (E0631xx, E064xxx, PSRC Ip-carrying scenario shots) carry only 23 probes and no loops (E080298) or no MDAC block at all (E063641), so none is usable on the raw-loop criterion. The six C-series shots drive the TF only (25.7 kA) with the PF chain at the noise floor (tens of amperes) and are not covered by any catalogue range, so they cannot serve as vacuum shots either.

Peak PSRC/Ip separates the two populations on the OP1 range: the shots the facility drove without plasma report the channel refused (irc 1015) or zero, while the plasma shots carry a real current waveform. A handful of pre-plasma scenario shots report a smooth 2.5 MA programme waveform (a setpoint, not a measurement) — read as a measured-current screen it would misclassify them, so the vacuum verdict below rests on the coil-current drive and the refused/zero plasma channel together, never on the Ip value alone.

Negative control

The vacuum classifier is “peak |PSRC/Ip| < 10 kA”. Applied to the cached E101154, whose PSRC/Ip peaks at 998,576.25 A, it returns plasma, never vacuum — the refusal is applied to a shot known to carry plasma current and the classifier is shown to say so. Applied to a shot with no cached PSRC/Ip (e.g. E100595) the reader raises and the classifier returns no verdict rather than defaulting to vacuum; the vacuum verdict for those shots therefore rests on the coil-current drive and the refused/zero plasma channel, not on an absent read of Ip.
