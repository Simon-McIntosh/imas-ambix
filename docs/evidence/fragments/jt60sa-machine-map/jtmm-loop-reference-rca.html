Do the raw MDAC flux loops measure flux relative to a reference loop?

Read-only. The hypothesis is that each raw MDAC loop magFlxLpL measures its own flux minus a single reference loop's flux, rather than its absolute flux. For every candidate reference magFlxLpR and every loop magFlxLpL it fits measured_L by least squares against prediction_L − prediction_R, where the prediction is the operator's PF-only vacuum loop flux ForwardOperator.vacuum_prediction built from the OP1 description and the measured MMSYS coil currents, on the same quasi-static samples the adjudication uses: a sample is kept when every driven coil's |dI/dt| is at most 0.10 of that coil's own peak |dI/dt| in the shot (retained samples: 100579 145508, 100595 46425, 100642 26094). The fit is the same one the adjudication records: slope and Pearson r of the two mean-centred series. The verdict rule is the same: a loop tracks at slope +1 (0.1 tolerance) with |r| > 0.95 on every shot with one sign. The operator, the coalescing of measured channels to the operator's sensor columns and the quasi-static mask are imported unchanged from scripts/vacuum_loop_adjudication.py, so the no-reference column reproduces the recorded absolute fits exactly (verified below).

The fits support magFlxLp7 as the reference. With loop 7's prediction subtracted, 26 of the other 26 loops track with per-shot slope near −1 and |r| near 1 on E100642 (26/26), 13/26 on E100579 and 10/26 on E100595. Loop 7 is also the one loop whose own channel fits its own absolute prediction (slope −0.99 on each shot, |r| 0.993–0.9999) — the signature of the reference, which is not compensated. No other candidate reference makes as many loops track: the runner-up magFlxLp8 reaches 13/10/9 of 26, and under it loop 7 itself fails badly (slope −3 to −17, r near 0). The global sign is −1 because the operator's predicted sign is opposite the raw channel, the same sign loop 7 already carries absolutely.
