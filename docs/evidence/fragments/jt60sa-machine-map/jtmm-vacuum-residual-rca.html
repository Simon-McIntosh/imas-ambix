Refit 1 (operator passive columns enabled): the operator exposes only 2 passive columns; adding them changes the joint residual by nothing (2.91e+03 → 2.91e+03, ratio 1.000) and leaves every per-channel slope and r identical to the plain fit on all three shots. Induced currents in the passive structure, as the description represents it, are not the residual.

Refit 2 (each sensor moved within 50 mm): on E100595 the probes’ median |r| moves 0.940 → 0.948 at +R 50 mm and → 0.933 at −R 50 mm, and the loops’ median |r| 0.686 → 0.706 / 0.666; no channel reaches |slope−1|≤0.1 at |r|>0.98 on any shot. The signed, magnitude-scaling movement of the metric shows the refit is live, not a no-op. A declared-position error of ≤50 mm is not the residual.

Refit 3 (each probe axis flipped): flipping every probe negates each probe slope and r (E100595 MP1 +0.559/+0.966 → −0.559/−0.966) and changes nothing else; |slope| and |r| are unchanged and no channel reaches identity. A probe-axis or sign error is not the residual (the probe signs are already consistent, e.g. MP5 tracks at −1.03/−0.962).

Cause the fits support: the residual lies in the operator’s per-coil response pattern (G_pf), not in the acquisition, time base or units. A free per-coil least-squares fit of every channel against the ten MMSYS coil currents reaches r ≈ 0.98–1.00 for all 44 channels on all three shots (loops 1–14 median r_free 0.985/0.997/0.999 on E100595/E100579/E100642), so each measured channel is a clean linear function of the drive and the operator’s coil-mixing is what departs from it. For the residual scale: after the declared A·turn/A the tracking channels sit at 0.32–0.58, not 1, and none of the three refits recovers identity. For loops 1–14: the operator’s dominant coil disagrees with the measured dominant coil for most of them (table above) while their free-fit r is ≈1, so the description’s geometry for those loops is mismatched; a ≤50 mm shift does not repair it, so the error is larger than 50 mm or is a coil-filament-weighting / channel-identity error.

Inconclusive: the specific geometry defect (loop position beyond 50 mm versus per-coil filament/turn weighting) and whether the 0.32–0.58 scale is one factor or a composition. The operator as built exposes only two passive columns and no coil-to-vessel coupling, so the residual cannot be attributed further from it alone; naming it needs a free per-coil refit of G_pf against the measured response.

Negative control: the passive refit was expected to be discriminating (eddy currents) and is not — it reproduces the plain fit’s slopes and r exactly (residual ratio 1.000), while the sensor-move and axis-flip refits do move the metric (signed and monotone in the 50 mm step). The instrument therefore distinguishes a refit that changes the prediction from one that does not.
