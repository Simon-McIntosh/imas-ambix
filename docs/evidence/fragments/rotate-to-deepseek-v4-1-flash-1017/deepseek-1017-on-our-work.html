What was measured

Every mounted project's run ledger was scanned for records whose role is implement and whose backend is clive, and split at the checkpoint swap boundary 2026-10-23T20:14:30Z into equal three-day windows: 300 analysed runs before (14 straddlers excluded) and 97 after. The request record was joined by run_id. The analysis reads the whole request record and ran on compute node 98dci4-clu-2068 under job 1291586.

Positive control

Passed in both windows. Before: 314 ledger records independently counted, 300 analysed plus 14 straddling excluded, token join 297 of 297. After: 97 counted, 97 analysed, token join 97 of 97. Each window holds more than the required 30 runs. The moved-boundary control, with the boundary at 2026-10-22T08:14:30Z inside the before window, also passed and reported no detectable difference on promotion (+0.005 [−0.015, +0.030]) or first-attempt gate pass (−0.042 [−0.147, +0.058]).

Result

Verdict better. First-attempt gate pass rate +0.175 [+0.065, +0.291]; repair or resume share −0.122 [−0.235, −0.005]; review fit +0.36 [+0.10, +0.61]; input tokens per promoted run −11.35M [−15.35M, −7.25M]; output tokens −76,457 [−96,694, −56,912]. Restricted to the five projects present in both windows (291 before, 97 after) the first-attempt gain is +0.175 [+0.065, +0.285] and every material direction holds. Promotion rate is at its ceiling (0.99 vs 1.00) and cannot discriminate; wall time's interval spans zero; cached tokens are null in every request row and are reported as unmeasured, not zero.
