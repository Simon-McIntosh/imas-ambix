Declared JT-60SA coil-current alternates and the rescored coils hand-off

The six pf_active/coil conflicts in the JT-60SA coils score are a chain choice, not a mapping error: the imported draft binds the HiTe chain where the hand-built map serves the LKAT chain (EF1–EF5), and the LKAT chain where it serves the HiTe chain (EF6). Each JT-60SA pf_active coil-current rule names the other chain in its evidence, so this node declares that chain as an alternate_source on the rule, quoting the rule's existing evidence.
