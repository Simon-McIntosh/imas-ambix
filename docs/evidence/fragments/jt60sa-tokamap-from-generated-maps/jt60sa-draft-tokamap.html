Fixture-driven JT-60SA draft tokamap

The handoff is the byte-for-byte fixture from imas-codex main commit 2a20afb7a, not a JT-60SA discovery run. The exporter reads ordered names from the OP1 and OP2 IMAS machine descriptions: MP10 and MP11 are probe indices 9 and 10; CS1 and CS2 are coil indices 0 and 1. Covered IDS signal maps are replaced; the machine description and signal maps for other IDS stay from the catalogue bundle.

Output: /work/projects/imas_gpu/jt60sa/tokamap/draft-fixture/. Six groups, two shot partitions, 12 mappings.json files, 70 entries: 50 machine-description and 20 signal entries. Eight signal entries are draft (four distinct rules in each partition); 12 remain from the hand-built maps of uncovered IDS. Tokamap validator reported “Validation completed successfully.”
