E101154's coil currents and plasma current, drawn from its IDS

scripts/figures_jt60sa_signals.py is now a thin imas-ink caller. Its one opener, _read_pulse_ids(pulse_dir, ids_name), reads one netCDF IDS from pulse_dir / f"{ids_name}.nc" with imas.DBEntry(path, "r") and get(ids_name, autoconvert=False), where pulse_dir is JT60SA_ROOT / "ids" / "101154". It passes extract_signal_traces(pf_active, "coil/current") and extract_signal_traces(magnetics, "ip") to time_trace_figure_mpl, one panel per series, and writes the figure through render_to_bytes(format="svg"). The script constructs no TimeSeries and passes no label or unit string; every name and unit comes from the IDS. The two figures with no IDS behind them are withdrawn: jt60sa-E101154-flux-loops.svg and jt60sa-C060033-pf-coil-currents.svg are deleted, so docs/figures/jt60sa-signals/ holds exactly this one SVG.
