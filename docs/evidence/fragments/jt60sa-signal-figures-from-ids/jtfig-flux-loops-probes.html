Flux loops and probes of E101154 and E101031, drawn as measured

scripts/figures_jt60sa_signals.py now makes three figure calls per pulse through the existing _pulse_figure: the coil-current call, one with the pair ("magnetics", "flux_loop/flux"), and one with ("magnetics", "b_field_pol_probe/field"). The IDS root is resolved as before with require_setting("ids_root", …). Every panel name and unit is read from the IDS by imas-ink's extract_signal_traces; the script passes no label, unit, caption or array of its own, and it rescales and re-signs nothing. The flux loops are drawn as the files measure them.
