Each compacted row carries its gauge leaves' counts and their peaks

Node srw-tiers-carry-leaf-counts-and-peaks · date 2026-09-29 · worktree imas-ambix-0405601943d2/srw-tiers-carry-leaf-counts-and-peaks

A compacted row declared one sample count for the whole window, so a gauge observed in three of twelve samples was indistinguishable from one observed in all twelve, and no row could say whether a 40% mean occupancy was flat or one spike to 100%. Each gauge leaf now records its own observed count in the row's obs.leaves block, and the peak-read leaves also carry the window's minimum and maximum. Re-compaction sums the counts and folds the extremes by min and max, so the tier chain preserves what the raw tier measured.

What changed

  • imas_ambix/agent/telemetry_store.py: _Mean carries an observation count and, for the leaves named by PEAK_LEAF_NAMES (requests running and queued, KV pool occupancy, card utilisation and power draw), the window's extremes. A window writes them to obs.leaves keyed by the leaf's _merge path, and reads a source row's declared block back so a second level sums rather than recounts.
  • tests/test_telemetry_compaction_leaves.py: the counts, the peak through both tier chains, and the count-summing on re-compaction.
