imas-ambix agent watch now prices each period at a third-party cache-hit estimate rather than at our own recorded cached split. The old figure billed the input our prefix cache absorbed at the cache-read tier, and that cache is ours -- one large store shared by the fleet -- which a hosted endpoint serving the same model would not have. So the printed figure was the cheapest of the three possible readings, and the panel said nothing about it. The command now reads one estimate record through one reader, resolves it from the price table's own directory joined with the record filename, and bills each period as h of the same total input at the cache-read rate plus the remainder at the prompt rate plus output at the completion rate, at the rate in force. Our own cached count still occupies its token column and never enters the bill.

The figure prints once per period row, and the label prints once beneath the ledger rather than on every row, because one installed record answers for every period: the label gives h as a percentage, the source kind and the citation, or the plain statement that no estimate is installed. The single reader declines a record whose h is outside 0..1, non-numeric or a boolean, rather than clamping what it holds. No band and no second figure is printed beside the estimate, because two costs on one row invite the reader to pick an endpoint the command was never asked to name. The same figure and label are carried in the --json period rows -- each row carries its own cost and cost_label -- so a consumer renders the same figure the panel showed rather than deriving its own. Installing the estimate record beside the price table is a deploy step, not part of this change: with no record installed the cost column dashes and the label states that no estimate is installed.
