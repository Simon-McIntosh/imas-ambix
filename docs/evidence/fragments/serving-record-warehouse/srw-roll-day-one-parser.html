A receipt name's roll day has one parser

Node srw-roll-day-one-parser · date 2026-10-15 · plan serving-record-warehouse §23

telemetry_store.split_record_name now splits a receipt file name into its record stem, its roll day and its suffix, and the three readers that each matched the trailing eight-digit day themselves -- telemetry_store._ladder_stem, serving_receipts._roll_date and serving_receipts._record_stem_and_suffix -- call it and keep only their own rule about which suffixes they accept. Before the change the three matched the day in three places (telemetry_store.py:806, serving_receipts.py:623, serving_receipts.py:665); after it the day match appears in the parser alone. On the four probed names every reader returns exactly what it returned before, including None / ('foo-1', '.jsonl.gz') / 'foo-1.20261015.jsonl' on the compressed segment. The telemetry_store docstring that described a rolled segment arriving with a new inode is rewritten to say what the merged branch does: a roll renames the segment and keeps its inode, so the pass resumes at the watermark's offset, and only a different inode starts at zero.
