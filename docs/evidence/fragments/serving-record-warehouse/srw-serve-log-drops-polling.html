The serve's access log drops the polled request paths

Node srw-serve-log-drops-polling · date 2026-10-15 · worktree imas-ambix-0405601943d2/srw-serve-log-drops-polling

Generated serve commands now exclude /metrics, /v1/models and /health from the uvicorn access log: SGLang through --uvicorn-access-log-exclude-prefixes and vLLM through --disable-access-log-for-endpoints, each spelling read from the installed engine's own --help rather than carried here. Both flags render on the deepseek-v4-1-flash (SGLang) and deepseek-v4-flash (vLLM) launch lines with all three prefixes. The change takes effect at the serve's next restart; no serve was restarted.
