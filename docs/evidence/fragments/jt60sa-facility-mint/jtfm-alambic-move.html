The mint engine moved into packages/imas-alambic (import imas_alambic) by git mv: machine_map, signal_map, transform_engine, virtual_zarr, eddb, eddb_remote, pulse_mint and the COCOS layer, with the map schema. It declares requires-python ≥ 3.12, its own hatch-vcs version, and depends only on numpy, imas-python, zarr, pyyaml and nova-cocos. The engine's packaged roots and its lookup against ambix's paths module are replaced by bundle discovery over the imas_alambic.bundles entry-point group and IMAS_ALAMBIC_MAP_PATH; ambix registers its imas_ambix/data directory as its bundle. The mint retires from imas-ambix data and becomes imas-alambic mint.
