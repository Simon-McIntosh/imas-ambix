One borrow reserve for the lane, not one per session

The router held BORROW_RESERVE slots back for every working session below its share, so four quiet sessions reserved 8 slots against the 7 the lane had free and the session holding the queued work was offered none. _AdmissionLedger._borrowed_slots now holds the reserve once for the lane: a session at or above its share is offered every free slot beyond it, and a session below its share is still offered its room to grow. The second half of the deliverable was also unmet in the same reading: a run waiting at the gate was counted lane-wide and in no session, so the per-session block and the lane-wide count disagreed about which runs are live. A waiting run is now grouped under the session its last admitted request declared, and snapshot takes waiting_sessions for the unnamed runs the simulation counts, so both blocks read one record.

The §3 simulation with sessions

Every simulated worker now belongs to a session (lane_sim.Session), and a session launches only from the offer the published document makes it: the per-session figure when the document names the session, new_session_worker_slots when it does not yet. The scenario is one session with an unbounded queue beside four sessions that each hold one run and have nothing queued, on the fed window's calibration at the default margin over four hours and three seeds:

The probe calls the test suite's own rate helpers at four hours and three seeds, so these are the figures the assertions read.

The five-session lane under the lane-wide reserve delivers 444.0 tok/s against the 444.5 a single session delivers on the same workload — a share of 0.999, inside the 5% the measure allows.

The per-session reserve does not meet the named 5%. On the same runs it delivers 434.0 tok/s, 0.976 of the single-session rate — 2.4% short. The instrument says why: the reserve costs the lane about six workers of 24, and the lane's measured rate curve is flat above roughly twenty workers (fixed pools deliver 437.7 at 20 and 444.2 at 22, so 18.4 workers against 22.2 costs 2%), while the capacity estimator answers an under-filled lane with a larger capacity and so re-opens the gap the reserve closed. The unit-level offer — 3 against 0 — is where the two rules differ by the whole free-slot count, and that is the figure the change is landed at a 16-request capacity. The simulation's negative arm is committed as test_a_reserve_per_session_leaves_the_queued_session_below_the_single_rate asserting only the direction and the measured 0.976, never a 5% the simulator does not produce; the unmet threshold is carried in the landing record.
