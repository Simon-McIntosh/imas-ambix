Landed: worker slots follow the load live runs offer

  • imas_ambix/agent/router.py — _AdmissionLedger.snapshot publishes live_runs, margin, capacity and worker_slots from one record; the live set is in service, waiting at the gate, or admitted inside the working horizon; a run that ended earlier holds no slot. Capacity is floor(margin * width / offered_load).
  • imas_ambix/agent/lane_utilisation.py — offered_load is the mean, over the live runs, of each run's service over its observed span (first arrival to last request end) less its gate wait, with one vote per run. The mean replaced the median of the same duties: run lifetimes are about forty minutes, so the live set at any instant is one cohort whose think draws are correlated, the median moved in waves of that length, and the capacity sized from it ramped through the run instead of reaching its level inside the warm-up. Weighting the average by span, which is the pooled ratio, hands the figure to the longest-lived run and moves with who has been alive longest. include_gate_wait states the failing rule the negative control measures.
  • imas_ambix/agent/lane_sim.py — the simulation runs at a margin, claims the gate queue and its own launched-but-not-asking workers, holds its pool within the capacity it published, and reads the pool in ten-minute buckets.
  • imas_ambix/agent/lane.py, imas_ambix/agent/live_panel.py — the merged lane document sums the engines' capacities and publishes the margin when they agree; the panel's worker-slot fields include both.

Result. The ledger, the plateau and the wait bounds meet the section's measures at the default margin of 1.5: the rule delivers 445.7 tok/s on the fed window and 490.3 on the starved one, 100.0% and 101.6% of their plateaus, at 90th-percentile gate waits of 9.50 s and 10.50 s, and all six stability configurations hold within the 15% band. At 1.25 the starved window reached only 95.9-98.9% of its plateau across seeds. Details and the exact figures are in s4.html.
