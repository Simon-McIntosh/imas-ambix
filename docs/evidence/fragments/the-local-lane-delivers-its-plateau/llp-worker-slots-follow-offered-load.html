Landed: worker slots follow the load live runs offer

  • imas_ambix/agent/router.py — _AdmissionLedger.snapshot publishes live_runs, margin, capacity and worker_slots from one record; the live set is in service, waiting at the gate, or admitted inside the working horizon; a run that ended earlier holds no slot. Capacity is floor(margin * width / offered_load).
  • imas_ambix/agent/lane_utilisation.py — offered_load is the median, over the live runs, of service over the run's observed span (first arrival to last request end) less its gate wait; the median keeps one run returning from a long think from halving the lane's load. include_gate_wait states the failing rule the negative control measures.
  • imas_ambix/agent/lane_sim.py — the simulation runs at a margin, claims the gate queue and its own launched-but-not-asking workers, holds its pool within the capacity it published, and reads the pool in ten-minute buckets.
  • imas_ambix/agent/lane.py, imas_ambix/agent/live_panel.py — the merged lane document sums the engines' capacities and publishes the margin when they agree; the panel's worker-slot fields include both.

Result. The ledger, the plateau and the wait bounds meet the section's measures; five of six stability configurations hold within the 15% band.
