The lane row draws the window beside the instant

The serve band draws every instant figure beside the window figure it is read with, and a group row above the labels says which of the window each figure is: in use against the window mean, waiting against the window median s gate wait, and tok/s against deliv %, the window's delivered completion-token rate as a share of the window's own plateau estimate. The panel reads the window from the lane document's admission.utilisation block, which lane_block now copies whole, and the median gate wait is published by utilisation_block over the requests the window admitted.
