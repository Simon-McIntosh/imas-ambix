Gate wait bound: measured instrument

Implementation: _AdmissionLedger.snapshot publishes gate_wait_p90_seconds over the same record's last five minutes and zeroes worker_slots while it exceeds wait_bound_seconds (read from the gate's control file, 30 s by default). One field, worker_slots_reason, names a pause or a wait closure.

High-margin run (the pass condition the plan states)

§5's done-when asks for a margin at which the bound-disabled arm exceeds 45 s p90 in some five-minute window while the bound-enabled arm stays under 45 s in every settled window. At the default margin the offered-load rule holds the pool at that margin's equilibrium, so the two arms agree and the scenario does not arise; the run below is the calibration that reaches it, and it is the node's stated measure.

§3 instrument calibrated from the committed fed window 04:45–06:40 UTC on 18 November, 4.5 h, bound 30 s, margin 2.6. Five-minute windows start at the end of the 1800 s warm-up; the settled set drops the first ten minutes after it. Sweep over the merged tree (mprobe2.py, every margin both arms, all_debug); the margin was re-measured after the lane's borrow reserve landed, because that rule moves the pool the bound sits on.

Ten fixed seeds at margin 2.6, both arms per seed. The pass condition is the bounded arm and it holds on all ten: the worst is seed 9 at 44.00 s, every other seed lower. The bound holds by two mechanisms. A closure is released only when the windowed wait falls to half the bound, because the five-minute window still holds the burst that closed it. And the margin’s capacity is capped by the wait the lane measures on itself: the pool is scaled down in proportion to the p90 it produced, and by the pool a request span can be served inside the bound, so a burst the load model did not foresee shrinks the pool by the size of the wait it caused. The bound-disabled arm does not cross 45 s on seeds 3, 5, 6, 7, 8 and 9 — their offered load never produces that wait unbound — which is recorded here and is not part of the pass condition.

Think time dropping to a third at two hours (measured, not a pass condition)

Default margin 1.5, think time divided by three at 7200 s, both arms run with the bound on and off: each carries the same worst settled window, 16.0 s, and neither publishes a document closed for the wait. The bound has nothing to close here because the lane settles under it in both arms, so this run cannot separate the two and is recorded as a measurement rather than as a pass condition.
