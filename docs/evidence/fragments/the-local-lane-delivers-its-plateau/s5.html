Gate wait bound: measured instrument

Implementation: _AdmissionLedger.snapshot publishes gate_wait_p90_seconds over the same record's last five minutes and zeroes worker_slots while it exceeds wait_bound_seconds (read from the gate's control file, 30 s by default). One field, worker_slots_reason, names a pause or a wait closure.

Think time dropping to a third at two hours (measured, not a pass condition)

Default margin 1.5, early-window calibration (think 17.9 s, mean), think time divided by three at 7200 s. Both arms carry the same worst settled window, 35.25 s, because a pool already at its published capacity has no slot left for the bound to close: the dispatcher admits the lesser of the published slots and the published capacity less the workers held, and the capacity caps the pool.
