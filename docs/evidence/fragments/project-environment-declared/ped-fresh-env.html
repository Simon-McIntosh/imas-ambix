Collection gap closed by declaring the extra through its owner

The dependency is now declared through its owner: the dev dependency group carries nova-stella[bokeh], so a plain sync installs the extra nova itself declares. The fresh environment then carries bokeh 3.10.0 and both environments collect the same 3987 tests with 0 errors.
