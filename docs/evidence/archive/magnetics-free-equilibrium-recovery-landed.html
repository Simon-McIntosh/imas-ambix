§2 label pedigree — a clean inconclusive — 2026-08-17

Commit 1eca758b. Shape-only discriminators were run over 3,816 quality frames (92.15% of 4,141) across all 20 shots. The extracted profiles carry smooth, low-order, magnetics-only-like signatures: median quadratic-in-ρ² residual 0.505% for the q-shape proxy and 1.006% for normalised current density, explained fractions 99.964% and 99.896%, split-half residual coherence 0.958, median core coefficient of variation 7.83% and 10.93%.

Verdict: inconclusive, at 0.85 confidence that the discriminators cannot decide. An MSE-constrained EFIT sharing the same basis, regularisation and extraction stencil produces those same signatures, so the test does not separate the hypotheses. Identifying evidence would need reconstruction-constraint metadata, EFIT inputs exposing MSE weights, paired MSE-on/off reconstructions, or held-out pitch-angle residuals — none of which the corpus carries. The label-pedigree-response decision is therefore locked to edge-weighted, the plan's pre-registered default, as the correct posture under irreducible uncertainty, with the pedigree recorded as unknown rather than assumed. Limitation kept on the page: the q proxy is constant-F normalised to the parquet q95 and is not an independently extracted q profile, because the corpus ships no full q or F profile.
