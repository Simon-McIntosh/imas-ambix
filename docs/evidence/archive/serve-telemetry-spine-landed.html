One reader for both engine families

imas_ambix/agent/engine_metrics.py now owns the mapping from a family's series names to canonical quantities for sglang: and vllm: alike. Before it, the same knowledge lived in three places: bench.py resolved vLLM names only, the workstation watcher carried both by hand, and lane.py::parse_lane_capacity carried a working SGLang reader of its own. The recorded consequence of the split is in the plan: fourteen of the recorder's twenty fields were null in every one of 13,791 rows against a healthy SGLang serve.

The module is a reader, not a probe: it takes exposition text and returns quantities, and it neither fetches nor decides. Three properties are deliberate and each is asserted by test.

Absence is omission, and a published zero survives

A quantity a family does not publish is left out of a reading rather than recorded as null or 0.0, because a null that reads as a reading is the defect being repaired. The rule was shown to be load-bearing rather than decorative: a mutated copy of the reader that records every quantity regardless reproduces the defect exactly, emitting prefix_cache_queries: None for the recorded SGLang scrape — precisely the assertion the new test forbids. Beside it, a quantity genuinely published as zero is kept: the SGLang fixture's gauges are a measured zero and its host cache tier answered nothing, so dropping those would make omission indistinguishable from a missing measurement.

Which samples count, decided once

SGLang repeats every device-pool measurement on each tensor-parallel rank, so only the rank-zero sample is one shared pool rather than N pools to sum. vLLM publishes reason-labelled partial series beside their family total, and folding a breakdown into its total double-counts. It also pairs every counter with a same-named _created gauge — a creation timestamp, not a data point — which a substring test folds into the token total. All three shapes are covered by the new tests, and the _created and reason exclusions are exact-name and exact-label matches rather than substring tests.

§6 — Per-request receipts from the router

One append-only row per completed request, appended by the router as it relays. imas_ambix/agent/request_receipts.py holds the accounting and the sink; imas_ambix/agent/router.py tees its own relay into them. The row carries timestamp, model, upstream, prompt tokens, cached prompt tokens, completion tokens, time to first token, duration, the caller hint the router already derives, an outcome label, and the sampling fraction in force when it was written.

The tee never buffers the body the client receives: it is fed each relayed chunk and keeps only a bounded per-line remainder plus a bounded head for the non-streaming fallback, so a chunk the router forwards is never held; 8 MiB per line and 4 MiB of head are the two bounds, and neither limits what the client gets. Field names and the first-token definition are not re-derived — they follow bench.py::_stream_chat, and a test drives one SSE payload through both readers and asserts the agreement (1,234 prompt and 7 completion tokens from each), so a relay figure and a benchmark figure stay comparable rather than merely similar. The parse of usage.prompt_tokens_details.cached_tokens is new: nothing else in the repository read that field.

A record that compacts without distorting what it averages

imas_ambix/agent/telemetry_store.py folds the append-only raw JSONL record into one row per minute and one per hour. The recorder's file is the right durable shape on a network filesystem and is not a retention policy; this module is the tiering that turns it into one, leaving the near past exact and the year-long trend a few megabytes.

The load-bearing distinction is that two leaf kinds compact by different operations. A cumulative counter is meaningless to average, and every integral a reader derives differences two of its values, so a compacted row carries the endpoint the window last observed. A gauge compacts by a time-weighted mean, and the row carries the sample count and observed seconds behind that mean, so the next tier is the same weighted mean one level up rather than a mean of means. Classification is by leaf name: a numeric leaf is a counter when it ends in _total, is a known cumulative quantity, or lives under a histograms section; everything else numeric is a gauge, and strings and ragged lists are identity carried from the last row in the window.

A None is neither of the two, and this is where the record's own record is unusual: 13 of the 20 leaves it writes are None in every row, and both throughput gauges are None in one row of 3,943. A null is an absent observation rather than an observation of zero, so it is recorded only where its window holds nothing yet and can never discard what that window accumulated before it. A leaf unobserved across a whole window therefore compacts to None — the record's way of saying not observed — while a gauge observed in some rows and absent in others compacts to the weighted mean of the rows that did observe it, with the window's own sample and second counts still declared. A value written after a null is a fresh observation, so a null opening a window does not suppress the rows that follow it.

§6 — The outcome the relay observed, and who answered

Those rows were only as honest as the earliest moment their outcome could be set, and three ways the record disagreed with what happened had shipped. Each is repaired here, and each fix carries a control that was made to fail without it.

A 200 is what the engine answered, not what the caller received

The status was promoted to completed as soon as the upstream answered 2xx — before the body loop — and an exception out of the body read escaped the handler that rewrites the status for a cancelled caller, while the finally wrote the row anyway. An engine that answers 200, promises a content-length and then aborts its transport therefore left a completed row for a relay that delivered a fraction of the bytes.

The headers are now held rather than acted on, and the outcome is promoted only after the body has been forwarded whole. The control: with status = STATUS_COMPLETED set early again, the truncation test reddens with assert 'completed' == 'failed'; reverted, it passes.

A request the router answered itself left no row

Only _relay recorded anything, and the refusals and the catalog listing return before it, so the requests a caller reports as broken were absent from the record entirely — their only trace a log line. Every refusal the router produces now leaves through one funnel that also records it, so a new refusal path cannot widen what the router answers without widening the record; the catalog listing records after its 200. Rows carry a self-answered upstream sentinel rather than an engine origin, so a reader attributing traffic per upstream never folds them into a sink's share.

The sampling ceiling had no operator surface

The production sink was built with neither max_rows_per_s nor window_s, so the ceiling was fixed by source with no way to raise it. Both are now settings resolved from an explicit argument, then the environment, then the default, matching the environment surface the router already carries. The value in force is logged at construction, because a bound inferable only from behaviour is one that a later reader measures from scratch. A malformed value — not a number, zero, negative, or nan, which no arrival count can stay under — stops the launch rather than silently falling back to a default nobody chose.

Serve telemetry spine — node readings

§3 — The serving node's own cards, host and job table

imas_ambix/agent/node_probe.py produces the readings the engine's own /metrics scrape cannot carry: what the cards were doing, what the host was short of, and which other jobs were resident. All three are ordinary local calls inside the serve's allocation — one nvidia-smi --query-gpu, one /proc/stat and /proc/meminfo pair, and squeue scoped to the node — so none of them needs an srun step or a hop through the scheduler.

A probe that did not run contributes no section

Each reading is a sparse section in the row, following the rule §2 established for the engine fields: a field is present because it was measured. A command that failed, is absent from the node, or outlived its timeout yields None, and its section is omitted rather than written as an empty list or a zeroed mapping — both of which read as a measurement. The same applies inside a reading taken partially: a quantity a card does not expose (nvidia-smi prints [N/A]) is absent from that card, so a present key is always a reading. The distinction is asserted per source, and a probe with nothing at all to report emits an empty mapping rather than four empty sections.

Cards carry the node's index, not the step's

SLURM restricts a process to its allocated devices through the device cgroup, so nvidia-smi inside it numbers the visible cards from zero while the allocation names the physical indices they are. A two-card allocation on the node's cards 6 and 7 therefore reads 0, 1 and is recorded as 6, 7; recording the read order would make one job's card 0 and another job's card 0 different silicon. The map is all-or-nothing: an allocation whose length does not describe the cards actually read is not applied, because pairing a partial allocation positionally would label a card with another job's silicon — worse than leaving it under the numbering it was read with. The section names the variable it used and carries the raw allocation, so an unmapped reading is explicable from the row itself.

The allocation comes from the variable the serve's own launch path sets. A serve is submitted with sbatch and runs the engine inline in the batch step, so it holds SLURM_JOB_GPUS and no SLURM_STEP_GPUS at all — that variable belongs to an srun step, and there is no srun on this path. A serve launched under srun inside a job holds the step allocation, which is the narrower of the two, so the module reads a precedence ladder: the step variable first, the job variable otherwise. CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES is deliberately not on that ladder — SLURM remaps it to 0..N-1, so it states a position in the visible set rather than the silicon, which is the confusion the map exists to remove. The indices are paired ascending, because nvidia-smi enumerates the cards it can see in ascending device order and the pairing is positional.

The rule was shown to be load-bearing rather than decorative. A mutated copy that rebuilds the ladder as its single defective rung — reading only the variable the batch step never sets — fails exactly the three allocation assertions and nothing else, so the cards are then labelled with the read order in production while the reading still looks populated. The mutation, the command and the red are recorded verbatim in the node's negative-control log. Two further shapes are refused by construction: an unstated allocation keeps the read numbering and says so, and a one-element allocation for a two-card read is not guessed at.

The job table is read on its own clock, and omitted between

The cards and the host are cheap local reads on every tick. The job table is a SLURM RPC and changes on the scale of minutes, so it is read at its own interval and omitted from the ticks between — omitted rather than repeated, because a tick repeating an earlier reading dates a stale table as current. A failed job read advances that clock instead of retrying on the next tick, so a node refusing squeue is not asked twice a second. A node holding no jobs is a reading and is kept.

The CPU busy fraction is differenced from two cumulative /proc/stat samples with iowait counted as not busy, so it is the conventional CPU-busy reading rather than one that blames the CPU for waiting on I/O. The first tick of a run has no interval and reports no fraction; the cumulative jiffies are carried beside it so a reader can difference any two rows across a window that dropped a tick.

An index already on disk, and what happens to it

Every index written before this change is invalidated, and the failure is a refusal rather than a merge — which is the safe half of the pair. The tables are declared CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS and the index carries no schema-version marker, so an existing database keeps its old shape and the new code never notices until it writes. Measured against a database built by the previous revision: the first ingest raises sqlite3.OperationalError: no such column: host. It cannot silently merge, because every path that would merge goes through a column that is not there.

The recovery is the index's own contract — TelemetryIndex.rebuild() from the JSONL, which is the durable copy and the only one that matters. No in-place migration was written, for the reason the index exists: it is disposable by construction, and a half-migrated index would be the one object in the system whose contents nobody could describe. An operator running this for the first time sees a loud exception naming the missing column; the rebuild is one call.

§5 — the key names the boot as well, because a hostname does not survive one

A hostname is stable across a reboot, but the counters these rows carry are not: they are cumulative, and they start again from zero when the machine restarts. Two readings from one genuinely correct hostname, one on each side of a reboot, therefore difference as a sudden improvement — a real measurement of the wrong quantity, with nothing on either row to say a reboot happened. The host dimension, added for the two-machines-at-one-path collision, cannot separate the two sides of a reboot on a single machine.

Every key now carries the boot as well. source is (host, boot_id, path, inode) and sample is unique on (host, boot_id, inode, offset). The boot is resolved per row rather than per file, because one file can hold rows from both sides of a reboot while its hostname never moves; the host stays per file, since a file is written by one machine. Both are read from the row's own top level or from its own host section, which is where the recorder states them — reading only the top level would leave the key inert against the one producer here that emits a boot identity at all.

The read surfaces and the counter span, repaired

Review of this arm found two defects inside it and one guard missing. counter_span differenced its two endpoints with no regard for which source each came from, while the rest of the arm had already been partitioned by (host, boot): a window opening on one machine and closing on another returned the difference of two counters that never met. §5 — a hostless row says so, instead of becoming the reader

The host and boot dimensions are only as good as where their values come from, and the fallback this change removes supplied a value the record never carried. A receipts file is written on a GPU node and read wherever the reader happens to be — very often a login node — so a row that names no host, ingested by that reader, was keyed under the reader's nodename and the reader's boot with key_kind = host+boot. Nothing on the row marked the machine as inferred: the stored key is byte-identical to one built from a recorded pair, and a reader asking which machine wrote a window would be answered, confidently, with the wrong one. This is the same shape the host and boot dimensions exist to prevent, arriving through the one field they trusted.

The defect reproduced on the real record before it was repaired. deepseek-v4-1-flash-1271709.jsonl in the agents' receipts directory holds 514 rows carrying no host. Ingested on the login node under the old code they were stored as ('98dci4-srv-1006.iter.org', '82c41dfa-a445-4fb2-8416-04b44c3d874c', 'host+boot'). sacct -j 1271709 -X -n -o NodeList reports 98dci4-gpu-0003: the writer and the reader are different machines, and the key named the reader. This is not a fixture — it is the production directory, and the fallback had the direction of the fact backwards for every file in it.
