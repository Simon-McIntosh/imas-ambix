One reader for both engine families

imas_ambix/agent/engine_metrics.py now owns the mapping from a family's series names to canonical quantities for sglang: and vllm: alike. Before it, the same knowledge lived in three places: bench.py resolved vLLM names only, the workstation watcher carried both by hand, and lane.py::parse_lane_capacity carried a working SGLang reader of its own. The recorded consequence of the split is in the plan: fourteen of the recorder's twenty fields were null in every one of 13,791 rows against a healthy SGLang serve.

The module is a reader, not a probe: it takes exposition text and returns quantities, and it neither fetches nor decides. Three properties are deliberate and each is asserted by test.

Absence is omission, and a published zero survives

A quantity a family does not publish is left out of a reading rather than recorded as null or 0.0, because a null that reads as a reading is the defect being repaired. The rule was shown to be load-bearing rather than decorative: a mutated copy of the reader that records every quantity regardless reproduces the defect exactly, emitting prefix_cache_queries: None for the recorded SGLang scrape — precisely the assertion the new test forbids. Beside it, a quantity genuinely published as zero is kept: the SGLang fixture's gauges are a measured zero and its host cache tier answered nothing, so dropping those would make omission indistinguishable from a missing measurement.

Which samples count, decided once

SGLang repeats every device-pool measurement on each tensor-parallel rank, so only the rank-zero sample is one shared pool rather than N pools to sum. vLLM publishes reason-labelled partial series beside their family total, and folding a breakdown into its total double-counts. It also pairs every counter with a same-named _created gauge — a creation timestamp, not a data point — which a substring test folds into the token total. All three shapes are covered by the new tests, and the _created and reason exclusions are exact-name and exact-label matches rather than substring tests.

Evidence

A live SGLang scrape populates every canonical quantity. The recorder was run once, for one sample, against the running DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash serve on 98dci4-gpu-0003:18810. The row's engine section carried family: sglang, model_id: deepseek-v4.1-flash, requests_running: 7, requests_queued: 0, kv_pool_occupancy: 0.2, prompt_tokens: 2055673430, generation_tokens: 16090547, a cached split of {device: 1984344576, host: 6554368, storage: 0}, uncached_prompt_tokens: 68006400, and both histograms — the time-to-first-token histogram with 16,993 observations and the inter-token-latency histogram with 16,054,490. The prefix-cache keys were absent from that section, which is correct: SGLang publishes no such counter. In the same row the flat vLLM-shaped fields beside it stayed null, which is what a vocabulary with no meaning here looks like — and is why the canonical section, not the flat fields, is the record.

A recorded vLLM fixture yields the same field set through the same code path. tests/test_engine_metrics.py drives a recorded-shape vLLM scrape through the same engine_metrics.read_metrics call and asserts the full canonical set: three gauges, four counters, both histograms, the per-tier cached split with the uncached remainder, and the speculative-decode triple. A test whose presence rule is read from the family tables — not from a branch in the reader — checks that each family's section contains exactly the quantities its own tables publish.

Serve telemetry spine — execution record

§6 — Per-request receipts from the router

One append-only row per completed request, appended by the router as it relays. imas_ambix/agent/request_receipts.py holds the accounting and the sink; imas_ambix/agent/router.py tees its own relay into them. The row carries timestamp, model, upstream, prompt tokens, cached prompt tokens, completion tokens, time to first token, duration, the caller hint the router already derives, an outcome label, and the sampling fraction in force when it was written.

The tee never buffers the body the client receives: it is fed each relayed chunk and keeps only a bounded per-line remainder plus a bounded head for the non-streaming fallback, so a chunk the router forwards is never held; 8 MiB per line and 4 MiB of head are the two bounds, and neither limits what the client gets. Field names and the first-token definition are not re-derived — they follow bench.py::_stream_chat, and a test drives one SSE payload through both readers and asserts the agreement (1,234 prompt and 7 completion tokens from each), so a relay figure and a benchmark figure stay comparable rather than merely similar. The parse of usage.prompt_tokens_details.cached_tokens is new: nothing else in the repository read that field.

Serve telemetry spine — node readings

§3 — The serving node's own cards, host and job table

imas_ambix/agent/node_probe.py produces the readings the engine's own /metrics scrape cannot carry: what the cards were doing, what the host was short of, and which other jobs were resident. All three are ordinary local calls inside the serve's allocation — one nvidia-smi --query-gpu, one /proc/stat and /proc/meminfo pair, and squeue scoped to the node — so none of them needs an srun step or a hop through the scheduler.

A probe that did not run contributes no section

Each reading is a sparse section in the row, following the rule §2 established for the engine fields: a field is present because it was measured. A command that failed, is absent from the node, or outlived its timeout yields None, and its section is omitted rather than written as an empty list or a zeroed mapping — both of which read as a measurement. The same applies inside a reading taken partially: a quantity a card does not expose (nvidia-smi prints [N/A]) is absent from that card, so a present key is always a reading. The distinction is asserted per source, and a probe with nothing at all to report emits an empty mapping rather than four empty sections.

Cards carry the node's index, not the step's

SLURM restricts a process to its allocated devices through the device cgroup, so nvidia-smi inside it numbers the visible cards from zero while the allocation names the physical indices they are. A two-card allocation on the node's cards 6 and 7 therefore reads 0, 1 and is recorded as 6, 7; recording the read order would make one job's card 0 and another job's card 0 different silicon. The map is all-or-nothing: an allocation whose length does not describe the cards actually read is not applied, because pairing a partial allocation positionally would label a card with another job's silicon — worse than leaving it under the numbering it was read with. The section names the variable it used and carries the raw allocation, so an unmapped reading is explicable from the row itself.

The allocation comes from the variable the serve's own launch path sets. A serve is submitted with sbatch and runs the engine inline in the batch step, so it holds SLURM_JOB_GPUS and no SLURM_STEP_GPUS at all — that variable belongs to an srun step, and there is no srun on this path. A serve launched under srun inside a job holds the step allocation, which is the narrower of the two, so the module reads a precedence ladder: the step variable first, the job variable otherwise. CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES is deliberately not on that ladder — SLURM remaps it to 0..N-1, so it states a position in the visible set rather than the silicon, which is the confusion the map exists to remove. The indices are paired ascending, because nvidia-smi enumerates the cards it can see in ascending device order and the pairing is positional.

The rule was shown to be load-bearing rather than decorative. A mutated copy that rebuilds the ladder as its single defective rung — reading only the variable the batch step never sets — fails exactly the three allocation assertions and nothing else, so the cards are then labelled with the read order in production while the reading still looks populated. The mutation, the command and the red are recorded verbatim in the node's negative-control log. Two further shapes are refused by construction: an unstated allocation keeps the read numbering and says so, and a one-element allocation for a two-card read is not guessed at.

The job table is read on its own clock, and omitted between

The cards and the host are cheap local reads on every tick. The job table is a SLURM RPC and changes on the scale of minutes, so it is read at its own interval and omitted from the ticks between — omitted rather than repeated, because a tick repeating an earlier reading dates a stale table as current. A failed job read advances that clock instead of retrying on the next tick, so a node refusing squeue is not asked twice a second. A node holding no jobs is a reading and is kept.

The CPU busy fraction is differenced from two cumulative /proc/stat samples with iowait counted as not busy, so it is the conventional CPU-busy reading rather than one that blames the CPU for waiting on I/O. The first tick of a run has no interval and reports no fraction; the cumulative jiffies are carried beside it so a reader can difference any two rows across a window that dropped a tick.

Evidence

A recorded body yields every field the section names. tests/test_node_probe.py drives the module against recorded nvidia-smi, /proc/stat, /proc/meminfo and squeue fixtures — the job table captured from the serving node 98dci4-gpu-0003, the proc bodies from this workstation. A recorded two-card body asserts full per-card dict equality for both cards, including the unit-carrying key names; a card printing bracketed non-values asserts the two unexposed quantities are absent while its other six survive.

Absence is asserted per source. Six tests cover it: each of the three commands absent in turn while the other two contribute, a partially readable host keeping only the source that answered, and a probe with nothing to report emitting no sections at all. The runner these rest on is exercised against real commands — a clean exit, a non-zero exit that printed, and an absent binary — since a non-zero exit is not a reading even when the command printed.

Gates. uv run --no-sync pytest tests/test_node_probe.py -q reports 34 passed; uv run --no-sync ruff check imas_ambix tests exits 0; and pytest tests/test_agent_serve_registry.py -k serving_python reports 2 passed, which is the guard this module needs because the serve imports it under the engine's Python 3.12 rather than the repository's 3.14.

That last gate earned its keep here. The local runner's exception clause is accepted by the repository's interpreter in its unparenthesised form, and the repository's own formatter actively strips the parentheses back off when it runs, because the formatter targets the newer grammar — so a fix written the way the serving-interpreter rule says is silently undone by a formatting pass over the same file. The failures are therefore held as a named tuple constant, which is stable under both.

Every read is bounded by the timeout it was given

A probe runs inside the recorder's tick, so a source that hangs must be cut off rather than allowed to hold the tick open. Each read therefore takes a timeout and hands it to the runner, and the runner is described by a protocol whose signature carries it — the timeout belongs to the call rather than to the runner's construction, because it is the caller that knows how long this reading may take.

This was broken in the first landing and is the second review finding. Every read accepted a timeout and never passed it on, so the knob did nothing: a real two-second command behind a fifty-millisecond bound still took two seconds, which the reviewer demonstrated by calling the card read with that bound against that command and measuring the full duration. A parameter that silently does nothing is worse than none, because it reads as a bound that is in force. The assertions now cover the forwarding per read and through the probe's own configured timeout, and one drives the real runner against a command that would otherwise outlive it. A mutation that drops the timeout on the way to the runner fails exactly the two forwarding assertions; the third is deliberately unmoved by it, because it measures the runner's own bound, which that mutation leaves intact.

One bound was still unreachable after that repair, and this is the third finding. The job read declares a wider timeout than the local reads, because squeue crosses the scheduler while the other three read a file or the driver. The probe passed its single local timeout to every source, so the wider figure was never invoked with anything: a node whose squeue answered between the two bounds lost the jobs section from every tick while the parameter said otherwise. This is the same shape as the second finding — a knob that does not do what it says — and the repair is the same: the probe now carries the job read's bound as its own field and invokes the read with it, so the declared figure is the one on the wire.

The assertion was rewritten with it, and that is the part worth keeping. The earlier tests asserted the timeout from the point of view of the read being called, which a call that never uses its timeout passes just as well as one that does — which is how the unreachable figure survived the repair that introduced it. The tests now read the timeout each command was actually invoked with, from a runner that records every argument vector and its bound, and one of them asserts the invoked figure equals the declared class default rather than restating a literal. Its control wraps NodeProbe.sample so the job read is handed the local bound again, and exactly the two on-the-wire assertions fail, reporting squeue invoked at 10 s against a declared 15 s.
