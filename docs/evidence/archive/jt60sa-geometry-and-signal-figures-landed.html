§3 — the cached JT-60SA pulses drawn

imas_ambix/data/eddb.py gained read_channel, the raw cache read beside the engine path. It returns one channel's data, time base and unit as a ChannelRecord, and raises EddbCacheError when the time base length differs from the data width. tests/data/test_eddb_cache.py gained a round-trip test and a half-written refusal test: 32 passed, against 30 at base 7e975e9c. The reviewer showed that the refusal test fails when the guard is removed. Whole-tree ruff is clean.

scripts/figures_jt60sa_signals.py draws three figures through imas-ink's time_trace_figure_mpl and writes each through render_to_bytes as one SVG. Map-bound values come from VirtualZarrView, and the time bases and flux loops from read_channel. Each y-axis names the EDDB channel drawn and its unit, and no series carries a legend label. The figures were rendered on a compute node. Their sizes are 409,655, 601,998 and 415,512 bytes, down from 3,420,501, 3,321,629 and 1,768,864 when every sample was written.

The run went through two resumes. The first moved the labels to the y-axis and the save to render_to_bytes, and added the half-written refusal. The second replaced the map's semantic ids on the y-axis with EDDB channel names, because the ids were taller than a panel and ran into their neighbours. Review at 8da01d86 scored 110/120: goal fidelity 20, evidence 19, durability, fit and scope 18 each, reuse 17.

Three follow-ons were declined.

  • The 602 KB flux-loop SVG is inside the bound: it has 34 panels of two traces each, and shrinking it further would change the shared imas-ink threshold.
  • The script's 'units' constant names the attribute virtual_zarr writes, which has no exported owner. eddb.UNIT_ATTR is the cache array's attribute, a different owner with the same spelling.
  • Listing the cached flux loops has no owner in eddb, and adding a public lister for one figure script would widen eddb for a single caller.
