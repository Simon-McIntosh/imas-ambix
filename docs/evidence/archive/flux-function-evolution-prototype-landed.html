Reading the split. The transition kernel carries the flux it actually solves for — ψ evolution beats persistence by 1.63 in explained variance, and cross-shot tuning improves it further. It does not carry FF′: transport and persistence are indistinguishable there (margin −0.0006), in every regime. That is a plan-shaping negative rather than a failure of the architecture — it says the innovation update in the production smoother must do real work on the FF′ amplitudes instead of riding the prior, and that those amplitudes cannot be tightly priored as deviations from a transport-evolved mean.

Qualification, stated so it is not over-read. Geometry was frozen per shot, boundary F was calibrated from the initial q95, and ρ̂ remains the extraction bank's geometric proxy — precisely the approximations FF′ is most sensitive to. The FF′ negative is therefore qualified spike evidence, and its rerun on exact time-varying toroidal-flux geometry is queued behind nova's TORAX geometry adapter, with the TORAX cross-check non-blocking beside it.

The loop works. Normalised innovation RMS falls 0.7640 → 0.7555, posterior median |lag-1| autocorrelation is 0.135, and 8 of 11 channels pass a whiteness test at p > 0.05 — the filter is consistent with its own noise model rather than chasing structure it cannot represent. Discipline held throughout: all 11 channels retained with 26 channel-slice instances given widened σ and zero dropped, and plasma current entered only as the diffusion boundary condition, never as an observation.

The crux, and it is worth stating precisely. The update carries a quarter of the joint improvement but has no FF′ tracking skill at all — a covariance-mediated update through channels that cannot see a quantity is marginally worse than not updating it. Thomson pins pressure and boundary shape; the diamagnetic function is invisible to it. The rank-6 basis is therefore adjudged not adequate as a production Thomson-only correction basis.

Why this is a finding about the substitute, not a verdict on the architecture. This spike deliberately replaced nova's free-boundary solve with banked labeled geometry and a pressure-over-measured-density temperature proxy. That substitution is very likely what removed the FF′ sensitivity — because the operator that makes FF′ observable is force balance against the known external field. The coil currents are given; a plasma with different diamagnetism sits in a different place against them. Run the free-boundary solve inside the loop and FF′ acquires an observation path that no amount of Thomson processing can supply on frozen geometry. The free-boundary solve is not a fidelity upgrade to this loop; it is the missing sensor.

Note also that on this shot the transport prior does beat persistence on FF′ (0.5599 vs 0.6687), unlike the §3 pooled change-metric result. The two measurements use different metrics, scopes and a basis projection, so they are not directly comparable and neither overturns the other; both are recorded rather than reconciled by argument.
