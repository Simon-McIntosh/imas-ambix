§3 simulability — the verdict splits — 2026-08-17

Commit c24d3c5 (node proto-simulate). Reading the split. The transition kernel carries the flux it actually solves for — ψ evolution beats persistence by 1.63 in explained variance, and cross-shot tuning improves it further. It does not carry FF′: transport and persistence are indistinguishable there (margin −0.0006), in every regime. That is a plan-shaping negative rather than a failure of the architecture — it says the innovation update in the production smoother must do real work on the FF′ amplitudes instead of riding the prior, and that those amplitudes cannot be tightly priored as deviations from a transport-evolved mean.

Qualification, stated so it is not over-read. Geometry was frozen per shot, boundary F was calibrated from the initial q95, and ρ̂ remains the extraction bank's geometric proxy — precisely the approximations FF′ is most sensitive to. The FF′ negative is therefore qualified spike evidence, and its rerun on exact time-varying toroidal-flux geometry is queued behind nova's TORAX geometry adapter, with the TORAX cross-check non-blocking beside it.
