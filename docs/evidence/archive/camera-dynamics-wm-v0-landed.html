Camera-Dynamics World Model — Measured Evidence

Cumulative execution record for Camera-Dynamics World Model — Full-Stream Reconstruction from a Clipped View. One anchor per landed node. Two illustrated companion reports carry the figures and the long-form narrative and are not duplicated here: matched-arm results and ELM fidelity.

Substrate — dataset, masking, splits and the pre-registered metrics

The CPU-only substrate that gates every GPU arm landed with 70 tests green and the win conditions locked before any model number existed. Commits 67a6b7c (substrate), c1e1324 (figure), d7b7953 (gate hardening).

  • imas_ambix/camdyn/ ships the frame-grid FrameTokenDataset (V3 token store joined to the V2 level-1 rbb/time, native frames with per-frame Δt), the five-mode clip-mask sampler and a frozen deterministic named-geometry eval suite.
  • Conditioning is loaded in physical units with causal zero-order hold and per-channel missing flags; efm/esm/xdc are leakage-banned inside load_conditioning itself, not by convention.
  • Split manifest camdyn_split_v0.json: train 7,535 / val 942 / held-out 1,050, disjoint and covering all 9,527 shots; 108 of the 112 sightline-pitch held-out shots forced to held-out, the 4 token-less ones surfaced rather than silently dropped.
  • The metric module locks masked NLL + top-1 with bootstrap CI, physical horizon-h reconstruction against a persistence baseline, and the frozen-probe protocol; the bootstrap gate carries a directional verdict so a significant regression cannot be counted as a win. rFID is banned as a primary metric.

Matched arms — dynamics beats statics and beats carry-forward

Two byte-identical 202M factorized space/time transformers, differing only in the temporal-attention toggle, give a decisive paired verdict over 764,707 held-out tokens: dynamics wins NLL by +0.4587 nats [+0.4550, +0.4624]. Commit 03f99de; 20,000 steps per arm, 7h17m each, concurrent on one H200 apiece (jobs 1216236 / 1216237, exit 0).

  • The win survives the trivial predictor: dynamics also beats the zero-order-hold carry-forward reference on top-1 by +0.0066 [+0.0064, +0.0068], and beats persistence inside the forecasting mode (0.0242 vs 0.0178).
  • Gains concentrate exactly where statics has no mechanism: panning +0.514 nats, temporal frontier +2.548 nats with top-1 up roughly forty-fold. Every named geometry favours dynamics on both bars.
  • Checkpoints under /work/projects/imas_gpu/mast-checkpoints/camdyn/{cap_v1_baseline,cap_v1_dynamics}/final.pt; artifacts cap_v1_baseline.json, cap_v1_dynamics.json, arm_compare.json.

Filament fidelity — decode exhausted, placement at a measured floor

Persistent edge filaments were chased through decode, an auxiliary training loss and an input-enrichment gate; all three closed with the same answer — filament placement is at a floor that is aleatoric within this view. Gate commit 61fb85e; reveal-k oracle commit fa86538.

  • Decode is a visualisation lever, not a correctness lever. Sampled, beam and MaskGIT decoders all shipped (token_sampling.py, --decode), but on the hardened, background-decontaminated, coloured-noise-controlled metric no decoder beats MAP on located correctness: residual over persistence MAP +0.071 band / +0.094 filament-cell, MaskGIT +0.047, beam +0.040, bernoulli +0.013. MaskGIT's apparent temporal win is a saturated-metric artifact — coloured noise also beats MAP on Δframe SSIM.
  • The spectral structure-aux retrain was killed — λ = 0.05 was a no-op and the run was GPU-starved.
  • Convergence will not sharpen placement. The decontaminated within-arm placement trajectory over 20 checkpoints shows dynamics MAP forecasting filament placement +0.094 over persistence, but the residual plateaued by mid-training (tail slope +3.5e-5 per 1k steps) and filament entropy stopped falling.
  • A second view cannot lower the floor. Revealing k = 4 in-view filament cells of perfect truth moved the placement residual by −0.0014 SSIM against a pre-registered +0.02 threshold, and remaining-cell entropy by −0.8% against a 10% threshold — both fail. A perfect in-view reveal upper-bounds any real second camera, which is occluded, coarser and mistimed, so the rba encode and multi-view retrain were not spent. Artifact reveal_oracle.json.
  • Endpoint for the filament goal: MAP for the estimate, MaskGIT coherent sample for visualisation. Caveat throughout: the placement gate rests on n = 2 windows, though the within-arm trajectory flatness is robust regardless.

ELM-frame morphology — the fixable half reproduces the arm gap

On windows selected by native fast Dα bursts, the dynamics arm's decoded response morphology beats the per-frame baseline by +0.0384 [+0.0023, +0.1025] at the 10 ms horizon. Commit 47f074a; 8 canonical held-out window pairs, target exactly 10 ms after the frontier, bootstrap resampling independent windows rather than cells.

  • Baseline mean 0.000663 against dynamics mean 0.039039 — the baseline is essentially unresponsive on these frames.
  • Qualified, and the qualification stays visible: token NLL on the same selected edge/divertor frames is not significant (−0.19116 nats, CI [−2.49313, +2.11775]). The morphology gap is real at 10 ms; a token-level gap on those frames is not yet demonstrated.
  • Dα is used only to select burst target times and never enters either arm as an input, so the metric cannot be satisfied by leaking its own selector.
  • The metric was added additively with its source hash embedded in the artifact; the pre-existing reconstruction, horizon, bootstrap and probe definitions are behaviourally unchanged and their 23 tests stay green.
  • This closes only the first step of the ELM programme. The causal fast-Dα conditioning comparison with its shuffled and slow-only controls, and the gating of any FiLM or Mirnov arm behind a measured positive, remain ahead.

The one lever the filament arc left open was the temporal-evolution objective. It stays closed, because no readout built so far can separate the model's frame-to-frame motion from spectrum-matched coloured noise. Commit eaf8e31; job 1251883, evidence generated in 53 s on one H200.

Fast-Dα burst conditioning — wired, and blocked on a runtime gap worth finding

The causal burst-summary channel and its controls are built and tested, and the conditioning comparison was deliberately not launched, because the production data path would have invalidated it silently. Commit 05d7cac; 24 tests green.

  • xim/da_hm10_t reduces to native-sample interval RMS over the closed causal inter-frame interval. The causality proof is direct: a 1e6-valued sample planted at 2.01 s leaves the summaries ending at 1.0 s and 2.0 s unchanged.
  • Both controls the ELM analysis demanded are width-matched to the real channel — a shuffled variant whose source index is strictly earlier for every frame after the first, and a slow-only variant reducing ada/dalpha_raw_full on its own time axis.
  • A fail-closed guard rejects any channel marked simultaneously a fast input and a probe target, so the Dα selector can never leak into the arm that is being scored on Dα-selected frames.
  • Matched-pair configs over a 24-column catalog parse to forms differing only in temporal_attention and run_name, preserving the single-toggle contract the whole matched-arm result rests on.
  • The block. The production CamdynWindowStream bypasses load_conditioning and zero-order-holds every cached trace. Training on these configs would have computed interval-RMS normalisation statistics while feeding the model ZOH-ed samples — a comparison that would have looked clean and meant nothing. Wiring the reduction dispatch through the runtime path is a prerequisite, not a refinement.

The gap is now closed (commit 665cc34). The window stream applies each channel's configured reduction and variant, and the emitted matrix exactly equals the matrix the normalisation statistics are estimated from, so no path can drift from another. Measured: all three burst variants pairwise distinct and each numerically different from its zero-order-hold reference, with 23 of 23 non-burst value columns and 23 of 23 missingness columns unchanged; 26 tests green. One configured channel tuple is routed through the statistics estimator and every train, validation and evaluation loader; the causal shuffle is seeded by shot id; unknown variants and unknown reductions fail closed.

ELM fidelity — a target-sampling ceiling, with the tokenizer exonerated

Poor ELM reconstruction is predominantly a sampling ceiling in the camera itself, not a modelling or tokenization failure.

  • The tokenizer round-trip retains 95% of fine edge energy at SSIM 0.996 — exonerated.
  • The rbb camera integrates the sub-millisecond ELM onset (median Δt 0.2 ms; 24% of ELM shots at ≥ 1 ms) and clips to white at the peak, so the target itself does not carry the structure. Artifact elm_sampling_audit.json.
  • The bounded remaining win is the ELM brightness/morphology response at the only statistically powered horizon, 10 ms with n = 59, carried by the genuinely fast Dα channel xim/da_hm10_t rather than the slow radial profile.
