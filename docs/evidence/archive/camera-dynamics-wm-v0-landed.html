Matched arms — dynamics beats statics and beats carry-forward

Two byte-identical 202M factorized space/time transformers, differing only in the temporal-attention toggle, give a decisive paired verdict over 764,707 held-out tokens: dynamics wins NLL by +0.4587 nats [+0.4550, +0.4624]. Commit 03f99de; 20,000 steps per arm, 7h17m each, concurrent on one H200 apiece (jobs 1216236 / 1216237, exit 0).

  • The win survives the trivial predictor: dynamics also beats the zero-order-hold carry-forward reference on top-1 by +0.0066 [+0.0064, +0.0068], and beats persistence inside the forecasting mode (0.0242 vs 0.0178).
  • Gains concentrate exactly where statics has no mechanism: panning +0.514 nats, temporal frontier +2.548 nats with top-1 up roughly forty-fold. Every named geometry favours dynamics on both bars.

Forward horizons, the gas-puff null, and the conditioning ablation

Forward reconstruction is significantly better than persistence at 10 ms and crushes the per-frame baseline at every horizon; the gas-puff attribution probe returns an honest null; and conditioning is a wash except where camera tokens are scarce. Filament fidelity — decode exhausted, placement at a measured floor

Persistent edge filaments were chased through decode, an auxiliary training loss and an input-enrichment gate; all three closed with the same answer — filament placement is at a floor that is aleatoric within this view. Gate commit 61fb85e; reveal-k oracle commit fa86538.

  • Decode is a visualisation lever, not a correctness lever. Sampled, beam and MaskGIT decoders all shipped (token_sampling.py, --decode), but on the hardened, background-decontaminated, coloured-noise-controlled metric no decoder beats MAP on located correctness: residual over persistence MAP +0.071 band / +0.094 filament-cell, MaskGIT +0.047, beam +0.040, bernoulli +0.013. MaskGIT's apparent temporal win is a saturated-metric artifact — coloured noise also beats MAP on Δframe SSIM.
  • The spectral structure-aux retrain was killed — λ = 0.05 was a no-op and the run was GPU-starved.
  • Convergence will not sharpen placement. The decontaminated within-arm placement trajectory over 20 checkpoints shows dynamics MAP forecasting filament placement +0.094 over persistence, but the residual plateaued by mid-training (tail slope +3.5e-5 per 1k steps) and filament entropy stopped falling.
  • A second view cannot lower the floor. Revealing k = 4 in-view filament cells of perfect truth moved the placement residual by −0.0014 SSIM against a pre-registered +0.02 threshold, and remaining-cell entropy by −0.8% against a 10% threshold — both fail. A perfect in-view reveal upper-bounds any real second camera, which is occluded, coarser and mistimed, so the rba encode and multi-view retrain were not spent. Artifact reveal_oracle.json.
  • Endpoint for the filament goal: MAP for the estimate, MaskGIT coherent sample for visualisation. Caveat throughout: the placement gate rests on n = 2 windows, though the within-arm trajectory flatness is robust regardless.

ELM fidelity — a target-sampling ceiling, with the tokenizer exonerated

Poor ELM reconstruction is predominantly a sampling ceiling in the camera itself, not a modelling or tokenization failure.

  • The tokenizer round-trip retains 95% of fine edge energy at SSIM 0.996 — exonerated.
  • The rbb camera integrates the sub-millisecond ELM onset (median Δt 0.2 ms; 24% of ELM shots at ≥ 1 ms) and clips to white at the peak, so the target itself does not carry the structure. Artifact elm_sampling_audit.json.
  • The bounded remaining win is the ELM brightness/morphology response at the only statistically powered horizon, 10 ms with n = 59, carried by the genuinely fast Dα channel xim/da_hm10_t rather than the slow radial profile.
