A map that binds names is not yet a transform; MAST taught that the convention must be identified from data and proved for both current signs rather than asserted. JT-60SA offers a cleaner instrument than MAST had: the C-series commissioning shots drive the superconducting coils with no plasma, so every loop flux and probe field is a deterministic function of the measured coil currents and the declared geometry.
