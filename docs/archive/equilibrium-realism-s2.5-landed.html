The fix rung — a one-interaction-matrix flux solve

The plasma-driven vessel-eddy drive is now a forward-chain mechanism, not a diagnosis-script afterthought. The design rule locked this session: when solving for flux, every circuit — driven coil, measured case, passive structure, and the plasma itself — lives in one interaction matrix, so every component of dψ/dt (coil swing, vessel eddies, plasma back-reaction, and the dL/dt of the evolving column) is accounted for and the applied loop voltage is solved, never assumed. The plasma row is resolved from measurement — total current from the Rogowski trace, centroid from the first-moment trace, self-inductance L_p = μ0·R·(ln(8R/(a√κ)) − 2 + li/2) from the evolving (R, a, κ, li) — through plasma_screening.solve_pinned_plasma_circuit. On the 11766 flat-top the solved loop voltage is 1.13 V, the sum of self (+1.74), coils (−1.76), vessel (−0.25) and resistive (+1.40) shares — a physically sane cancellation, the ledger load-bearing.

The sign investigation — the whole chain is correct

Prompted by the lead's observation that a compact ST struggles with vertical, not radial, instability, the current/field sign chain was audited end to end on the 11766 flat-top:

  • Plasma current: Ip = +466 kA (positive), agrees with efm plasma_current_c; MAST reads COCOS-3-like (axis = flux maximum for +Ip).
  • Magnitude: Ip / (|P4|+|P5|) amp-turns = 1.18; Ip / |P4| = 1.99 — comparable to the divertor/shaping-coil amp-turns, as expected.
  • Divertor coil: P6 (nearest the X-point) is driven +7–10 kA — same sign as Ip, the parallel-current configuration that pulls out an X-point. The vertical-field coils P4/P5 are opposite-sign (−121 / −80 kA), the textbook-mandatory sense that produces a downward (confining) B_z for a +Ip plasma.
  • Relative sign is load-bearing and correct: a rigid-ring force balance confirms a stable equilibrium at the axis with the loaded signs; flipping Ip to be same sign as P4/P5 leaves no equilibrium anywhere and the force outward at every radius. The loaded relative sign is the only one that confines.

No sign bug. The low-field-side runaway seen with the two-term probe was not a force error.

The premise reversal — the profile-free solve confines

The earlier probe answered "no confined fixed point" using a fixed two-term jφ shape. That is a fragile assumption about the profile, not physics. Lifting it to the regularized profile-free solve (solve_equilibrium_lsq: a flexible non-negative basis n_p = n_f = 3 re-fit each Picard sweep, asserting only the Grad–Shafranov equation, jφ ≥ 0, and the Rogowski total ∫jφ = Ip) confines on the measured 11766 flat-top coils in every configuration:

The axis position is externally undetermined beyond Ip / centroid / βp+li/2 — it needs the measurement or dynamics, not a better coil model.
