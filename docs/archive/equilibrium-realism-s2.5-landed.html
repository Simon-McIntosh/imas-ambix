The fix rung — a one-interaction-matrix flux solve

The plasma-driven vessel-eddy drive is now a forward-chain mechanism, not a diagnosis-script afterthought. The design rule locked this session: when solving for flux, every circuit — driven coil, measured case, passive structure, and the plasma itself — lives in one interaction matrix, so every component of dψ/dt (coil swing, vessel eddies, plasma back-reaction, and the dL/dt of the evolving column) is accounted for and the applied loop voltage is solved, never assumed. The plasma row is resolved from measurement — total current from the Rogowski trace, centroid from the first-moment trace, self-inductance L_p = μ0·R·(ln(8R/(a√κ)) − 2 + li/2) from the evolving (R, a, κ, li) — through plasma_screening.solve_pinned_plasma_circuit. On the 11766 flat-top the solved loop voltage is 1.13 V, the sum of self (+1.74), coils (−1.76), vessel (−0.25) and resistive (+1.40) shares — a physically sane cancellation, the ledger load-bearing.

The sign investigation — the whole chain is correct

Prompted by the lead's observation that a compact ST struggles with vertical, not radial, instability, the current/field sign chain was audited end to end on the 11766 flat-top:

  • Plasma current: Ip = +466 kA (positive), agrees with efm plasma_current_c; MAST reads COCOS-3-like (axis = flux maximum for +Ip).
  • Magnitude: Ip / (|P4|+|P5|) amp-turns = 1.18; Ip / |P4| = 1.99 — comparable to the divertor/shaping-coil amp-turns, as expected.
  • Divertor coil: P6 (nearest the X-point) is driven +7–10 kA — same sign as Ip, the parallel-current configuration that pulls out an X-point. The vertical-field coils P4/P5 are opposite-sign (−121 / −80 kA), the textbook-mandatory sense that produces a downward (confining) B_z for a +Ip plasma.
  • Relative sign is load-bearing and correct: a rigid-ring force balance confirms a stable equilibrium at the axis with the loaded signs; flipping Ip to be same sign as P4/P5 leaves no equilibrium anywhere and the force outward at every radius. The loaded relative sign is the only one that confines.

No sign bug. The low-field-side runaway seen with the two-term probe was not a force error.

The regularized profile-free solve (solve_equilibrium_lsq: a flexible non-negative basis n_p = n_f = 3 re-fit each Picard sweep, asserting only the Grad–Shafranov equation, jφ ≥ 0, and the Rogowski total ∫jφ = Ip) reaches an interior O-point in every configuration — but the forward configs reach it at the WRONG (outboard) location, which a first version of this record mistook for confinement:

The decisive test (lead-demanded): warm-start the free-boundary solve at the reconstruction solution (R=0.82) with the magnetics OFF. It drifts straight back out to R=1.77 — so R=0.82 is not a free-boundary equilibrium. The magnetics were load-bearing. Three consequences:

  1. The forward operator does NOT hold the physical equilibrium. It has a spurious outboard attractor: the plasma falls into a flux pocket at the in-vessel P4/P5/P6 coils (the vacuum ψ has O-points sitting on the coils at R=1.5–1.67). This is the original §1 outboard attractor — it was never reversed. The plan's founding premise stands, with this refined mechanism.
  2. Only reconstruction (measurement-constrained) reaches the physical equilibrium at R=0.82, matching reality (0.9) and the rigid-ring point-balance (0.88). Per the locked vertical-stability-treatment decision, reconstruction mode is legitimately measurement-constrained; the FORWARD mode is the one V2 tests, and it fails.
  3. It is not the interior-null. The interior-null is a few-cm axis-position degeneracy; a 70 cm free-vs-constrained gap is a gross solver failure (the attractor), not the null. The earlier record conflated the two — corrected here.

Sign-convention audit (challenged on the figure, resolved by test)

An earlier version of the flux figure featured the pure-forward free-solve, whose plasma sits outboard at R = 1.51 — which reads as a radial-out diverted plasma and prompted the hypothesis of a plasma-current vs coil sign-convention error (the local solve is COCOS-17; MAST data is COCOS-3-like). Tested directly and refuted:

  • Clean calibration: a +Ip plasma with an opposite-sign vertical-field coil pair confines inboard (R = 0.478); a same-sign pair pushes it onto the coil (R = 1.766). A global sign inversion would make the opposite-sign case run out — it confined, so the solver's plasma–coil sign is correct.
  • Ip flip: flipping the applied Ip sign on 11766 makes the solve worse — the O-point runs to R = 1.81, Z = −0.31 (a corner blob), not an up/down-diverted inboard plasma.
  • The recon solve (matches data) is physical: O-point centred at R = 0.82, Z = 0, up/down-symmetric, X-points at Z = ±0.79 — a diverted plasma with the correct topology, not radial-out. The forward panel was the spurious-outboard-attractor free-solve, over-featured in the original figure — that was a presentation error, but the underlying forward-solve failure it exposed is a real physics/solver problem (§3), not merely cosmetic.

The solver's COCOS-17 convention (axis = flux maximum for +Ip, confirmed by test) is internally consistent, and it operates on physical currents and fields (both validated at the sensors), so no data-ψ conversion affects the plasma–coil coupling. One latent issue was found and flagged: factory_shot_payloads passes abs(Ip), which would flip the plasma–coil relative sign for a negative-Ip shot — 11766 is +Ip so unaffected, but it must be fixed before any −Ip shot enters the corpus.

Reconstruction confirms the physical equilibrium (V2-forward still fails)

The magnetics-constrained reconstruction — profile-free solve + measured magnetics + full passive/vessel circuit reconstruction (rank-8 eigenmode sidecar) + disc boundary prior — confines correctly across the V2 train-split shot set (11766, ramp-heavy 11767, flat-top-rich 11772): 23/23 flat-top slices within 15 cm of the referee axis (median 3.8 cm), no z-symmetry pin. This confirms the physical equilibrium exists and the reconstruction spine finds it. It is not the V2 gate: V2 tests the FORWARD operator, and the same forward solve without magnetics runs to R=1.51–1.77 (16/23 "confined" only in the sense of reaching an outboard interior O-point, median 32.6 cm off). The magnetics are doing the work. The plasma-driven eddies are ~0 at flat-top so their injection is immaterial there; the ramp-slice eddy drive and the plasma flux-diffusion screening modes are §3 refinements.
