What landed

topology_read='connectivity' on solve_equilibrium_lsq and solve_equilibrium_nk (imas_ambix/latent/gs_solve.py): the per-sweep hard-threshold read (find_critical_points + ndimage.label core mask) is replaced by the connectivity boundary binding (sub-grid wall tangency / X-saddle, continuous in ψ), the sub-grid stencil axis, and a smooth sigmoid core-membership weight (ψ_N width 0.02) gated by the axis-connected component of the padded support — the gate flips only where the weight is already ~0, so the fixed-point map is continuous. Default 'hard' is byte-unchanged. Threaded through fit_and_read_slice (+ a per-sweep iteration_trace pass-through). Tests: tests/latent/test_gs_solve_smooth_topology.py (5 new, 14/14 suite green).

Why D-A1 fails — and why that is not a lossy mask

  • The hard read's wall binding is grid-quantized. The hard LCFS contact reads ψ at limiter grid points (up to ~half a cell off the polygon); the smooth binding is the sub-grid interpolated tangency. On a synthetic ground-truth case the smooth binding reproduces the true interpolated wall tangency to 1×10⁻¹⁵ while the hard read is 0.19 of the span off (pinned in test_smooth_read_reproduces_hard_read). The both-converged pool is dominated by early-ramp limited slices (8/11 at t ≤ 0.11 s) — exactly where that bias is largest — so the 4 cm axis median measures the two solves converging to differently-normalised equilibria, with the smooth one better grounded at the wall.
  • The worst outliers are hard-map pathologies. On the worst paired slice (21986/k4, flat-top, 887 kA) the HARD solve converged with its axis 41 cm outboard of the measured current-centroid pin (the known outboard drift); the smooth solve sits 11 cm from the pin — inside the physical Shafranov band. The "disagreement" is the smooth map correcting a hard-map artifact.
  • Cost: the smooth solve is 2.63× the hard solve's CPU wall per slice (the jax connectivity read runs every sweep on host) — the CPU cost of smoothness that the batched on-device rollout amortises away.

Consequence (decision locked): the smooth read stays opt-in; the hard read remains the production/label default. The stop rule ("do not ship a lossy mask") is honoured — no label path changes. The smoothness deliverable — the prerequisite for the accelerator retest — is delivered.
