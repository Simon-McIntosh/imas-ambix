Calibration findings — feedback guard, weight regime, resistance scale

Constrained-refit feedback. The first single-shot smoke (w = 10, unguarded iteration) exposed a failure mode worth recording: the trajectory prior at tight weight dragged the per-slice fits (median cost 1.09 → 2.11, axis 5.4 → 20.1 cm), the distorted plasma history then re-drove the trajectory (history shift 0.669), and the re-iterated centers inflated further. Fix: a re-iteration whose constrained pass degrades the median cost is rejected — the trajectory drives from the unconstrained pass-1 history.

Weight regime (tune sweep, 64 slices, 4 shots). At w = 0.1 the ramp-up LCFS improves CI-clear (−0.95 cm [−1.36, −0.74]) with reseeds improved (0.047 vs 0.078) and axis/flat-top mildly touched; by w = 1 the "gain" grows to −4.3 cm but axis degrades 4.8 → 19.4 cm and cost 0.75 → 1.81 — the fit capitulating to an overpredicted magnitude; w = 3 breaks convergence (24/64 masked). The trajectory DIRECTION carries genuine ramp-up information; its MAGNITUDE overshoots what the Ip-anchored plasma columns can shield against.

Magnitude diagnosis. The trajectory's whitened sensor claim is ~475σ at the ramp-up slices, decaying to ~150σ at flat-top. Large vessel currents during the Ip ramp are real physics (the vessel shields the ramping plasma flux — the measured signal is the partially-cancelled sum), but the claim exceeds what the anchored plasma can cancel. The physically-motivated culprit is the resistance model: the efm passive elements are BOUNDING BOXES, so the ring-resistance area w·h overestimates the true steel cross-section — R underestimated, eddies too large and too persistent. The bounded cross-shot resistance scale (the plan's anticipated calibratable) is the lever; swept as stage 2.

Verdict and the road through

Qualified positive / formal gate FAIL. The mechanism is proven: the first CI-clear referee-measured ramp-up improvement on this problem, produced by pure deterministic physics with measured drives — where the R2 free-form learner converted 21% of sensor misfit into zero referee skill. The formal gate fails through axis non-inferiority, and the failure is attributed, not mysterious: the geometry-exact L is right, the bounding-box R is not, and the data (tune sweep, constraint-pull diagnostics, axis parity at R×4) points at a ~4× resistance underestimate. The road through is the lead-directed vacuum-trained resistance extraction: 85 coil-only shots (48 preludes + 37 dedicated vacuum), pure eddy signal, L exact, drives measured, 8 measured case-current channels as direct supervision — then re-gate P1 with the calibrated R.

Post-landing hardening (lead directive, same day)

Before the resistance solve, three physics refinements landed on top of this record: (1) the observer sub-gridding criterion became machine-agnostic — a fraction of the passive set's median section scale median(√(w·h)) (MAST: 0.0491 m, reproducing the hand-picked 0.05 m) instead of a fixed metre-level cell; (2) ring resistance now uses the TRUE cross-section — the 0.01 m kernel floor was inflating thin shells' conducting area up to 3.3× on 40/146 passive circuits (3 mm case walls / vessel shells, exactly the fast shield paths); (3) the coil↔case shielding coupling was verified on the real geometry: the cases are boxes of 3 mm plates enclosing the windings, the two-section kernel needs no special-casing, and k = M/√(L·L) = 0.854–0.936 (< 1, reciprocity ~10⁻³) across P2–P5. Re-gating the frozen config on tune with the corrected physics reproduces this record within noise (ramp-up −0.97 CI [−1.37, −0.76] vs −0.95) — the kept sensor-visible modes are bulk-vessel dominated, so the vacuum extraction remains the binding attack.
