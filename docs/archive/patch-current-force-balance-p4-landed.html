What was built

  • Oracle-recipe architecture, firewall-clean training: one token per (sensor, time-step) over a 12-step / 0.25 s raw-magnetics window — never pooled; per-sensor geometry (R, Z, sin θ, cos θ, kind) as additive positional encoding; KNOWN coil currents as extra tokens with centroid geometry; CONTINUOUS standardised value embedding; has-value flags + loss-side masking for missing data (never imputation); d160/4-layer/4-head norm-first transformer with a learned query-token pool. The architecture is the measured stage-1 oracle recipe — but training is self-supervised: whitened raw-magnetics misfit through Msens + Rogowski Ip anchor + the structure residual under the locked bounded-discrepancy weight policy (per-example λ buffer, warm-up → data-derived target). No EFIT anywhere in the training path; the referee only scores held-out readouts.
  • Single forward pass: I = x·Ip/n over 5084 patches; topology read from the assembled ψ at eval — no per-slice optimisation, no convergence masking, 160/160 readout availability.
  • 8/8 module tests (incl. single-example overfit smoke, set-permutation equivariance, mask robustness); SIGTERM-clean, resume-safe trainer; corpus spans 3 campaign signatures at 2,000 shots (S=97 pair trains together; the S=81 signature's 3,795 examples are dropped by the shared-geometry guard and counted honestly — a per-batch-geometry trainer is the recorded follow-up).

Decision locked: patch-head-parametrisation = direct

Two-arm ablation at both corpus scales (identical trainer, head swap). At 14,525 examples the direct 5084-dim head beats the low-rank(64)+residual head on every gate-relevant metric: in-signature axis skill −0.256 vs −0.323, median 8.4 vs 9.3 cm, X-point set skill +0.058 vs −0.060, grounding 14.3× vs 11.2×, and only the direct head clears the cross-signature grounding bar (5.54× PASS vs 4.85× FAIL). The null-space risk the low-rank bottleneck was designed to hedge is already owned by the physics residual; the bottleneck just costs capacity.

Cross-signature transfer (North-Star signal)

The geometry-as-positional-encoding design evaluates on an unseen coil-set signature without retraining: cross-signature axis median 10.8 cm vs 8.4 cm in-signature, grounding 5.54× vs 14.3× — degraded but functional, on a signature whose coil geometry the weights never saw. This is the first measured cross-geometry transfer of the equilibrium layer, the property the cross-machine ambition (P5 item 4) builds on.

Iteration log (honesty trail)

  • v0 (588 examples): axis 28 cm, grounding 2–2.7× — data-starved; eval initially skipped cross-signature shots and window-clipped slices (fixed in a99b663: rebuilt geometry buffers + padded partial windows → 160/160).
  • 2k corpus exposed a third campaign signature; dropped-signature example ids overflowed the per-example λ buffer (CUDA device assert) — fixed by contiguous renumbering (commit f-id-fix in train_patch_encoder.py).
  • Both 2k arms trained 60 epochs / 13,680 steps; held-back misfit converged ~0.45–0.55.
