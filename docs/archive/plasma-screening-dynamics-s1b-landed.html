Model form — visual verification (lead audit request)

The plasma representation in every solved equilibrium is exactly the cylinder-cell / GS-force-balance / flux-function-source model. Current lives on the in-limiter cells of the 65×97 rectangular (R, Z) grid; each cell is a rectangular-cross-section toroidal conductor whose field and couplings are the analytic finite-area cylinder kernel (the locked hybrid_greens convention — grid cell Green's, sensor rows, and coil columns all share it). Cell currents are set by the force-balanced free-boundary Picard solve of jφ = R·p′(ψ_N) + FF′(ψ_N)/(μ0·R) with BOTH source functions flux functions (truth family: both ∝ h(ψ_N) at fixed split β = 0.3; spine fit: the 3+3-coefficient non-negative ladder — flux functions likewise). The PATCH circuit is not the plasma representation: it is a rank reduction of the same cell grid used only to EVOLVE the current between labels; its state is projected onto the flux-surface profile h(ψ_N) and re-solved force-balanced at every step, and its non-flux-function poloidal content (median 0.73 of L1) is discarded at each projection — the recorded cost of frozen-geometry chaining, made visible below.

The one place the flux-function constraint is deliberately broken: the screening-mode COLUMNS added to the dynamic fit are patch-space redistributions with poloidal structure — they enter the solve additively (like vessel eddies), NOT through the GS source functions. The physical skin current is force-balanced flux-function current (a hollow h(ψ_N) on the evolving ψ); the columns only approximate that direction. Given the S1b negative, a successor representation should carry hollowness INSIDE the flux-function source (evolve h itself, e.g. a soft prior on the ladder toward the circuit-evolved h) — the H1b measurement that free hollow bases destabilise the Picard (0/16) is the constraint such a design must respect.
