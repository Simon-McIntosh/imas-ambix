Lever 2 — the per-era coil-circuit ledger

EFCC Green's columns — what geometry can and cannot constrain

The in-tree sensor geometry is axisymmetric (each sensor an (R,Z) with, for probes, a poloidal pickup angle — no toroidal position). Two things are reconstructable and both validate: full toroidal loops cancel the n≠0 field to machine precision regardless of phase (matching the empirical immunity), and the probe exposure envelope by (R,Z) orders the families the robust way (outboard ≫ centre-column ≫ loops). The one thing that is not reconstructable here — the obv-vs-obr fine ordering — is exactly the piece that needs the toroidal sensor positions and a pinned coil vertical extent, which is the D2 deliverable.
