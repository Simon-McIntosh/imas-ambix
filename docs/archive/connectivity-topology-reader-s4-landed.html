Outcomes

What landed

The wall is two per-campaign data objects, one code path. A wall is an arbitrary list of units: a vessel outline (occupiable interior) and any number of material tiles/limiters. The occupiable mask is inside vessel AND NOT material; every material unit — a closed tile or an open line primitive — is supercover-rasterised (every cell a wall segment crosses is material; a closed unit also fills its interior). Tiles are holes: the flood only ever grows the axis-connected component, so limiter contact and vessel poke-through are the same escape event, and whatever a hole disconnects is never flooded or queried. A single closed vessel loop (MAST) reproduces the plain point-in-polygon limiter mask byte-for-byte, so the shipped read is unchanged; AUG's discrete tiles and a movable WEST wall are just a different unit list.

Wall-node flux is part of the solve — the g_wall influence matrix. Every unit's surface is sampled at ~Δ/2 arc spacing into a wall-node string; a per-campaign g_wall matrix (coils + plasma cells → nodes, built with the hybrid_greens finite-area kernel because the nodes are NEAR-field to edge plasma cells) gives ψ at every node in one GEMM per solve — exact, linear, differentiable, cached beside g_edge. The tangency read becomes the minimum node flux over the REACHABLE nodes (reachability from the flood naturally excludes private-flux plate nodes and shadowed far-side limiters). This replaces the bilinear read off the grid, whose O(Δ²·∂²ψ) error is largest exactly at a tangency, where the plasma leans and flux curvature peaks. The unified binding — confined-most of {wall tangency, X-saddle} — is unchanged from §3.

Supercover guarantees a ≥1-cell obstacle for arbitrarily thin blades. The ≤1-cell thickening biases only the coarse flood bracket; ψ_bnd is re-read sub-grid from the exact reachable-node flux, so it carries no thickening error (T-D2). The X-candidate gate tolerates a one-cell dilation near material so a binding saddle beside a tile is not masked out — made grad-safe by freezing the gradient on padded/degenerate candidate slots (a near-planar cluster's ill-conditioned lstsq VJP would otherwise poison the differentiable read through the masked-where 0·∞ trap).

Loud diagnostics, never errors

At the cached campaign build the mask reports, as WARNINGs (never raised): every unit thinner than the grid (2·Area/Perimeter thickness proxy < Δ, or an empty interior fill — e.g. an open blade), and every pair of units whose polygons are disjoint but whose rasters fuse across a sub-cell gap. Erroring would reject AUG-class thin heat shields at standard campaign grids — and the binding flux never comes from the raster, so a fused or sub-grid unit is a report, not a failure. The one hard per-solve error is the flood seed (the axis cell) landing in wall material, where there is no axis-connected plasma to grow.
