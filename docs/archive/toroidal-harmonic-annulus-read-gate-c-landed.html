Verdict — qualified pass (honest)

  • Best-in-class LCFS. The source-free read is the strongest LCFS reader of the three physics arms (−2.98 vs the moment read −4.47 and the free carrier −4.91) — decoupling the boundary from the interior removes the near-edge lumpiness that the free/moment reads re-import.
  • Source-free premise confirmed. The harmonic ψ and the independent interior carrier ψ agree to a median 0.082 of the flux dynamic range in the SHARED annulus — the checkable consistency condition both representations must satisfy where both are valid (Gate C of the prior ladder). This is the strongest evidence that the read is physically well-posed, and it needs no EFIT.
  • X-point-set regresses vs the moment read (−3.06 vs −0.15). The plan's PASS bar was "beat the moment read on BOTH X-point-set AND LCFS"; the harmonic read beats it on LCFS but not on the X-point set. At the orders that stay well-conditioned (≤3) the external read localises the LCFS envelope well but under-resolves the divertor nulls; the orders that would sharpen the X-points (≥4) alias. This is the honest limit of a low-order source-free read and the driving followup.
  • Metric caveat: the raw in-limiter saddle_excess (median 7) counts residual near-pole/annulus-band nulls that the SCORED X-point read excludes (via the near-pole exclusion + in-limiter/conductor-clear filter); it overstates the physical over-counting and is not the quantity the gate is judged on.

Enhancement — machine-agnostic per-slice pole + regularised higher order (lead-directed)

After the first (fixed-pole) qualified pass, the lead directed using the high-accuracy carrier axis to place the harmonic pole, raising the order with regularisation, and making the offset machine-agnostic. Three changes landed:

  • Fast P1n−½ evaluator (ring_P1): order-0 elliptic-integral seeds + degree recurrence + order-raise, matched to mpmath <1e-8. ~0.4 s per (order,ridge,fraction) combo vs 24–40 s — the enabler for a moving pole (which defeats the mpmath column cache) and higher orders. TDD suite drops 49 s → 8 s.
  • Per-slice pole tracking the carrier axis, placed a dimensionless fraction inboard (pole_r = axis_R·(1−f), pole_z = axis_Z). A fraction (not a fixed metre offset) keeps it machine-agnostic. Measured finding: a pole at the axis (f=0) is worse (held-out lcfs_ft ~50 cm, X-point −7.6) — the P-expansion converges only when the focal ring sits inboard of the current centroid. A CI-gated order × ridge × fraction ladder under the consistency-RMS guard selected order 3, ridge 1e-8, fraction 0.41 on train (f=0.4 dominated; f=0.13/0.25 worse; higher f stabilises higher orders).
  • Tikhonov ridge in the fit: at the carrier pole, order 6 no longer aliases (consistency 0.086 vs the old fixed-pole o5's 2×105).
