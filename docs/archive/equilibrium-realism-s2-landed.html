The evidence

The lead's passive-structure hypothesis — tested in-session

Mid-session the lead proposed an alternative mechanism: missing passive structure (vessel / stabilizing plates) in the machine description. The scan of L1/L2 for pf_passive-labelled fields found the L2 pf_passive IMAS IDS (126 elements: outer vessel wall at R = 2.01 m, upper/lower horizontal walls, center-column rings/rods, P2 divertor plates + arms, 48 coil cases) — and a geometry comparison shows our 146 fcoil structural circuits already cover every family (18 circuits on the outer wall, 32 above |Z| = 2.1 m, 68 on the center column). The conductors are present; what was missing is a drive term: every vessel-eddy prediction (this sweep included, originally) drives the passive system with the measured coil histories only — the plasma current's own ramp (0 → 600 kA in ~250 ms) also drives the vessel, and its Lenz-anti-parallel image currents add field in the confining direction while they last. Quantified (plasma filament at the referee axis trace, exact-ZOH): the term supplies 6–10 mT of confining B_z on ramp slices — closing the ramp deficit from median +27.5% to +17.6% — and ~0 at flat-top (eddies decayed; median 15.2% → 14.5%), exactly the observed deficit shape. Recorded in the artifact as bz_eddy_plasma / rel_discrepancy_with_plasma_eddies (diagnostic columns; the pre-declared gate inputs are untouched). The dashed series in the discrepancy figure shows the folded values.

Honest caveats (recorded with the lock)

  • The flat-top gate crossing is thin — 0.152 vs the 0.150 line — and the pre-declared large-aspect identity (ln(8R/a) + βp + li/2 − 3/2) carries O(ε²) corrections at MAST aspect (A ≈ 1.42–1.54) larger than that margin; an elongation-corrected form (ln 8R/(a√κ)) would soften the read by roughly 7–15%. After folding the plasma-driven eddy term the residual deficit is ~+18% (ramp) / ~+15% (flat-top) — a genuine but smaller field-side gap, part of which the identity correction may absorb. The branch conviction rests on the systematic, same-signed residual across all 6 shots plus the probe's elimination of the solve branch; the fix rung opens with an identity-independent per-coil audit precisely because the Shafranov number alone cannot carry the remaining margin.
  • Referee coverage clips the early ramp: EFIT slices for these shots start at Ip-fraction ≈ 0.55–0.7, so the sweep's "ramp" slices are mid-to-late ramp; the earliest ramp is undiagnosed (dropped loudly, not silently).
  • Localization is partly structural: p4+p5 dominate because they ARE the vertical-field coils; the waterfall names them as where a coil-side error must live, not proof of one by itself.
  • The vessel-eddy contribution at the axis (exact-ZOH prediction from measured drives) is small at these slice times — the discrepancy is not an eddy-modelling artifact.

Why this is coherent with the vacuum campaign

The 0.1–0.44σ vacuum-shot validation measured coil→sensor predictions, and the standing coil-model audit finding is that the P2–P5 up/down pairs are unseparable in operating data — their symmetric combination is validated at the sensor ring, but a pair-level (or winding-pack geometry) error changes the field at the plasma differently than at the sensors. A ~25% vertical-field deficit at R ≈ 0.75–0.9 m, Z ≈ 0 is consistent with every prior measurement while explaining why build_confining_i_pf needed −60 kA on ALL P4/P5/P6 coils (vs the real 11766 pattern p4 −121 kA, p5 −62 kA) to manufacture confinement.
