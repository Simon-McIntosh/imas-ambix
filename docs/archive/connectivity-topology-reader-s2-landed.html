What landed

A fixed-shape, jit / vmap / grad-safe device reimplementation of the shipped host topology.lcfs_contour: a fixed-iteration flood-fill from the axis (reusing the contour-free FSA's flood_fill_core) + a candidate-level sweep + a fixed-count bisection over the connectivity-change level, replacing host contourpy and the bisection-over-traced-contours. ONE code path for limited and diverted, continuous through the marginal transition by construction; the wall enters only as a raster boolean mask (the machine-agnostic §4 abstraction, in place from the start). The LCFS radii are read a hair inside the separatrix by a differentiable ray-march so a ray never runs down an open X-point leg.

RCA — the boundary-size bias, found and fixed

A first single escape test (region touches the innermost in-wall shell) fired ~one grid cell inside the true wall crossing / saddle, so ψ_bnd was biased toward the axis and the boundary came out consistently too small (real-data ψ_bnd sat entirely on one side of y = x, median 3.7%; diverted lobes slightly small too). Fixed by reading ψ_bnd at the mean of an inner escape test (~1 cell inside) and an outer escape test (~1 cell outside), which brackets the binding from both sides. The mean is unbiased (real-data ψ_bnd 3.74% one-sided → 1.87% centred, radii 2.55 → 1.31 cm) and lands the diverted boundary on the X-point saddle (ψ_N = 1). No classification, no upsampling; ψ_bnd stays differentiable.
