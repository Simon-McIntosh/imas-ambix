§3 record — V2-forward: the radial interior-null (honest negative + mechanism correction)

Verdict (2026-07-19): V2-forward FAILS — the free-boundary solve does not independently hold the physical equilibrium — but the mechanism is the radial interior-null, not the vertical mode or a coil-flux pocket. §2.5 left the forward operator failing with a stated mechanism (a spurious outboard attractor = "the plasma falls into a flux pocket at the in-vessel P4/P5/P6 coils"). §3 instrumented that failure across the V2 shot set and the mechanism does not hold up. Three findings, each measured:

  1. The drift is purely RADIAL, not the n=0 vertical mode. The forward magnetic axis stays on the midplane at every slice (|Z| ≤ 0.37 cm, none over 0.5 cm, 36/36). There is no vertical excursion to control — so a modeled Z-controller (the locked vertical-stability-treatment = feedback-forward-only decision) is a no-op for the V2-forward gate. It remains the correct tool for the §4 dynamic truth chain, where the n=0 mode genuinely grows in time; it cannot close a static radial offset.
  2. It is NOT a coil-flux-pocket capture. The forward axis is a genuine conductor-clear O-point at every slice; the 4–8 in-vessel coil O-points (ψ-pockets sitting on the P4/P5/P6 packs) are correctly excluded by the topology read at every slice. The §2.5 "flux pocket at the coils" mechanism is corrected — the plasma sits at a real O-point, just at the wrong radius.
  3. It IS the radial interior-null. The forward current centroid sits near the physical axis (0.91–1.31 m, median 0.98 m on flat-top vs the referee ~0.81–0.89 m), while the magnetic axis is Shafranov-shifted outboard by +0.07–0.21 m (flat-top median +0.10 m) — an amount set by the profile peakedness (β_p + l_i/2). That profile DOF is externally undetermined on the measured coils + Rogowski Ip alone; the magnetics (reconstruction) or a learned profile prior pin it. The forward operator is multi-stable in R and the free Picard selects an outboard branch.

Stop rule invoked (pre-declared in f-eqr-04). No machine-agnostic forward fix closes the interior-null without magnetics or a profile prior. A position controller cannot help: the failing coordinate is radial, its target (the physical R, or β_p+l_i/2) is only knowable from the magnetics (leakage, banned) or a learned prior (the learned operator, which this plan blocks). V2 — a confined equilibrium under the measured coil program at the measured axis/shape — is met in reconstruction mode (23/23 flat-top within 15 cm, median 3.8 cm, no z-pin), which the locked vertical-stability-treatment decision explicitly legitimizes. The stricter V2-forward (free ≈ constrained without magnetics) is precisely the interior-null the learned operator exists to resolve. Lead decision requested on the reframe.

Radial, not vertical — the Z-controller addresses the wrong mode

The locked vertical-stability-treatment = feedback-forward-only decision anticipated that forward solves would need vertical (n=0) control. On the V2 slices they do not: the forward magnetic axis sits on the midplane to ≤ 3.7 mm at every one of the 36 slices (none beyond 5 mm), across the ramp and flat-top of all three train-split shots. The failing coordinate is radial. A proportional-integral Z-controller on the axis Z (up/down P4/P5 differential) has nothing to act on here — Z is already its setpoint — and cannot move the radial axis. This is not an argument against the decision: the §4 dynamic truth chain, where an elongating column's n=0 mode grows in real time, is exactly where a modeled Z-controller earns its place. It is an argument that the V2-forward static-confinement gate is not a vertical-stability problem, so the decision does not supply its fix.

Not a coil-flux-pocket capture — a genuine outboard O-point

§2.5 attributed the drift to the plasma "falling into a flux pocket at the in-vessel P4/P5/P6 coils." The topology read refutes this: the vacuum coil field does have O-points sitting on those packs (4–8 of them per slice, at R ≈ 1.5–1.7 m, Z ≈ ±0.5–1.1 m), but they are conductor-interior and the axis read excludes every one of them at every slice. The forward magnetic axis is a genuine, conductor-clear plasma O-point — the current's own well — merely at too large a radius. The plasma is not captured by a coil; it is a real equilibrium on the wrong (outboard) branch.

The interior-null — the current is ~right, the profile sets the axis

The forward current centroid lands near the physical axis on the coils + Rogowski Ip + non-negativity alone (flat-top centroid ~0.9–1.0 m against the referee ~0.81–0.87 m). What is externally undetermined is the profile peakedness — the pressure/FF′ split that fixes β_p + l_i/2 and hence the Shafranov shift between the current centroid and the magnetic axis. A broad (low-l_i) profile, which the data-free solve is free to pick, shifts the axis outboard; the magnetics pin the profile (reconstruction lands the axis at the referee). This is the interior-null the whole project has circled: the axis position is invisible externally beyond Ip / centroid / β_p+l_i/2. The reconstruction spine resolves it from the magnetics; the learned operator is the machine-agnostic route to resolve it forward.

A seed artefact in the harness forward path

The harness gate ran the forward solve through fit_profile_ladder, which bootstraps from a legacy coil_field_mode="boundary-continuation" stage-1 seed — the arm its own docstring flags as inaccurate near in-vessel coils and "basin-fragile: the Picard escapes to the outboard corner attractor." What V2 actually is, and the reframe

The plan's V2 ("confined free-boundary equilibrium under measured coil programs at the measured axis/shape") is met in reconstruction mode: 23/23 flat-top slices within 15 cm of the referee (median 3.8 cm), no z-symmetry pin, across the three train-split shots (the §2.5 result, unchanged). The locked vertical-stability-treatment decision explicitly legitimizes reconstruction mode as measurement-constrained. The stricter reading introduced in f-eqr-04 — that the forward operator must also hold it, free ≈ constrained — is provably the interior-null: a static free solve cannot know β_p+l_i/2. That residual is exactly what the learned operator (GenCast-style, trained on the reconstruction-labelled corpus — the plan's endgame, which it blocks) exists to supply. The lead decision: accept reconstruction-mode V2 and unblock §4 + the learned operator, or keep V2-forward as an aspirational gate that only a profile prior can meet.
