D3 — the make-or-break benchmark (decision proxy-retirement → REPLACE)

What was measured. Against an independent ground truth — the exact affine-tiled filament sum (area-conserving centroid tiling, converges O(1/n²)), Richardson-extrapolated in 1/n² from n=280,560 to ~1e-9 — the analytic polygon_greens and the operator's real fallback (a slanted section can only be tiled by axis-aligned PFFilament rectangles, so the lattice staircases the boundary) were compared per section shape and per sensor-distance band. Because the staircase makes the proxy error non-monotone in N (a thin rail aliases with the grid — the hollow frame swings ~30000× between adjacent resolutions), the reported "N to reach a tolerance" is the suffix-max envelope — the honest "commit at least this many filaments" count — and the cost race tunes the analytic φ-quadrature to the tolerance too, for a fair matched-accuracy comparison.

Verdict. The analytic kernel is exact (~1e-13) with no tuning and no tolerance dependence, while the staircased proxy needs thousands-to-tens-of-thousands of filaments for the shapes we actually have and is 3–14× more wall-time even at matched accuracy. It therefore REPLACES the multi-filament proxy as the representation for non-rectangular sections: the retained-lattice fallback in the filament-collapse gate becomes an analytic polygon section for any decomposable slanted / trapezoidal / hollow shape. The multi-filament lattice survives only as the general fallback for genuinely irregular packs that are not a clean polygon (mixed-sign windings, non-decomposable staggered packs), where collapse_rectangular_circuits already keeps the exact lattice; a true axis-aligned rectangle keeps the cheaper cylinder_greens kernel.

D4 — numerical robustness (no kernel fix needed)

The field is the complex-step curl of a semi-analytic ψ that is analytic for every target off the section boundary (D² ≥ r²sin²φ > 0 at the interior φ nodes), which makes the kernel robust by construction — the stresses the plan flagged all pass with the default 16×48 rule:

  • Near-boundary accuracy. Machine-precise (~1e-13 relative) for targets more than ~1 cm off a slanted edge; ≲1e-6 down to 1 mm — tighter than any physical sensor standoff. Sub-millimetre targets recover full accuracy by raising n_panels/ n_nodes (both exposed), and the self-convergence test confirms the knob works.
  • On the boundary. A target exactly on an edge midpoint or vertex stays finite — the complex-step increment (h~1e-30·i) nudges the evaluation off the real singularity.
  • Awkward geometry. A near-horizontal slanted edge (slope b₁≈20) and a thin 1 cm × 30 cm slanted plate both stay finite, match the exact-tiling oracle to 1e-4, and satisfy the Ampère circulation |∮B·dl| = μ0·I.

The elliptic characteristic-value singularity the plan anticipated is moot for this kernel: the field comes from the complex-step curl of ψ (arcsinh / arctan integrand), not from a closed elliptic Π assembly, so there is no pole to handle. The near-boundary accuracy floor is now documented in the module docstring.

D5 — wired as a forward-operator section type

Plumbing. A frozen PolygonSection dataclass (keyed to an fcoil circuit, carrying the (n,2) vertices + current-share xmult) sits beside PFFilament/PassiveStructure; GeometryTable gains a polygon_sections field defaulting to []; and parallelogram_vertices(r, z, width, height, angle) turns the MAST catalog's *_r/_z/_width/_height/_shapeAngle parametrisation of the slanted passives into explicit vertices.

Dispatch. build_operator now routes any passive circuit that carries a PolygonSection through polygon_section_column (the analytic Urankar kernel, same sign/units contract as hybrid_greens) in place of its axis-aligned bounding-box cylinder. The override is opt-in: an empty polygon_sections leaves every column byte-identical (tested); a box polygon reproduces the rectangular-kernel column in the finite-area near band to 1e-9 (tested); a slanted polygon reshapes the passive column, matching the direct kernel exactly (tested). All 240 tests/gs tests pass.

What this plan deliberately stops at. The exact kernel and its section-type wiring are done. Populating the 8 real elements' vertices per-shot (reading the catalog shapeAngle channels — the vertices are not yet ingested; they die in read_amm_passive's centroid regex today) and re-running the passive-resistivity / loop-gain extraction on the faithful shaped geometry is the empirical question the non-axisymmetric field subtraction plan (gate G-A / passive layer) owns — queued as the next followup. Extending the same dispatch to a non-rectangular active coil/case pack is a one-line addition in the KNOWN block when a coil needs it.
