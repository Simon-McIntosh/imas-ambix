The representation, made explicit

This is GS force balance with regularization, not an imposed flux profile. Grad–Shafranov has two free functions, p′(ψ) and FF′(ψ); force balance relates ψ to them but does not determine them, so they must be represented. The firewall-clean way — and the profile this rung earns — is a rich non-negative ladder (the frozen spine's np=nf=3, K=6, with jφ≥0 and a second-difference smoothness ridge), not a low-order polynomial whose assumed shape does the load-bearing work (EFIT's move). §2's free-sign K=2 solve is the one deliberately rigid element — a stability crutch that carries position, never a profile claim — and §3 lifts that rigidity: the K=2 solve selects the confined basin, then warm-starts the rich ladder, and the dynamics supply the internal-profile shape the single-slice magnetics cannot (the interior null).

The mechanism

  • Scaffold — the stable K=2 position solve. The §2 free-sign K=2 solve (Ip + R/Z current centroid, magnetics OFF) selects the confined inboard branch. Its converged current warm-starts the rich arms, so basin selection (the fragile step without magnetics) is done by the stable solve; the rich ladder then refines from the confined branch.
  • Evolution — 1D resistive ψ diffusion, measured drive. Each confined rich slice's flux-surface geometry (current_diffusion.flux_surface_geometry) drives the poloidal-flux diffusion (diffuse_psi) over the measured interval to the next slice, with the measured Ip as the edge boundary condition and η(ψN) the bounded low-DOF unknown — the only free function, by design.
  • Prior — the evolved profile, projected back. The diffused current (predicted_current) is projected onto the rich basis (project_coefficients) — source geometry and projection basis identical (K=6), so the prediction is well-posed — and enters the next slice's solve as a soft coefficient centre (SoftPriors.coeff_prior_*). The temporal link is carried by this physics prior, not by the numerical warm-start.

Notable findings

  • The coupling stabilises, it does not destabilise. On the same rich basis, switching the diffusion prior on lifts confinement from 88% to 94% (rescuing 11766 t=0.091 and 11767 t=0.123 from the outboard attractor) and regresses no slice. The physically-evolved profile is a better-posed target than the per-slice free rich fit.
  • The residual failures are fast-ramp slices, honestly flagged. The two coupled drop-outs (11772 t=0.059, a 379→530 kA ramp; 11766 t=0.113, masked with no upstream prediction) are the "ramp gap = representation deficit" regime. They carry scored=False or an unconfined axis — masked, never shipped as a readout.
  • Synthetic caveat, recorded straight. Even with exact η, the diffusion prediction carries an ~8% split error floor (discretisation + projection) and does not beat independent fitting where the magnetics are informative. This is the same signal the temporal-physics-spine thesis closed on: the temporal channel is marginal at the information floor. Its value is precisely where single-slice magnetics are blind — the interior null — which §4 scores on held-out MSE.
  • Stop rule NOT invoked. No profile was fabricated to force a pass; the fragile slices are flagged, not filled.
