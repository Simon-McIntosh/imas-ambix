§3 P1b — vacuum-trained passive resistances with case-current holdback (landed record)

Verdict (2026-07-17): the resistance extraction SUCCEEDS and is validated per-circuit on held-back measured case currents; the P1 re-gate still FAILS formally — the attribution is revised, not rescued. Bounded resistance multipliers fitted on the 85-shot coil-only pool (case channels held back as targets, never inputs) cut the held-out vacuum eddy residual 0.337 → 0.012 and reproduce the held-back P3–P5 case transients from independent coil drives at correlation 0.96–0.99; the data demands structure — bulk vessel ×11–13, outboard cylinder and measured cases nominal, inboard bounding boxes driven to effective open circuit. On the re-gate the calibration cures the axis magnitude-poisoning (tune axis parity at every weight where nominal R gave 9.7–19.4 cm) and halves the held-out axis damage (median 6.15 → 6.43 cm vs P1's 6.95) — but it also halves the ramp-up gain (−0.10 cm, CI [−0.197, +0.0004] grazing zero) and the axis leg stays inferior. The residual trajectory–data disagreement is structural (unpredictable P2 cases, un-separable up/down pair drives, 3-D paths), not resistance magnitude. The cases-predicted machine-agnostic arm matches the measured-driven arm to ~0.01 cm everywhere.

The discovery model — cases become passive, measurements become targets

The landed operator consumes the 8 measured *_case_current channels as DRIVES (known_case circuits with their own G columns). For the resistance discovery this inverts, per the lead's binding holdback: the circuit system is built with the P2–P5 case circuits moved into the passive set (build_passive_circuit_system(hold_back_cases=True), imas_ambix/latent/temporal_operator.py) — their currents are integrated states driven by the 13 remaining coil channels through the geometry-exact mutual couplings (k ≈ 0.85–0.94 coil↔case), and the measured case currents supervise the fit purely as held-back per-circuit targets. A model that reproduces a measured case transient from independent drives is validated at the per-circuit level — the strongest test of both L and R the corpus offers. The frozen spine's own vacuum prediction keeps its measured case drives untouched (byte-exact baseline).

  • Pool: the vetted coil-only set of the solenoid-attribution work — 85 shots (48 fleet −2.5 s preludes carrying the full CS precharge swing + 37 dedicated vacuum shots), leading coil-only run only (post-plasma tails carry plasma-origin eddies), 448k raw 1 kHz samples, two campaigns (fc1004: 146+8 passive circuits; fc938: 80+8).
  • Supervision: whitened magnetics eddy residual (measured − non-case static coil prediction; per-channel offset nuisance) + the held-back case currents, equal weight. The static coil calibration deliberately fit dI/dt-QUIET intervals, so these transients are untouched information.
  • DOF ladder (cross-shot, bounded ×[0.2, 64], never per-shot): global → vessel/case → 4 normalised-coordinate vessel regions + case pairs → per-case; each rung accepted only if 16 held-out shots improve. L stays geometry-exact throughout; a candidate R is one cheap re-eigensolve of the cached circuit system (imas_ambix/latent/passive_resistance.py; fit driver scripts/vacuum_passive_resistance_fit.py; full 85-shot ladder ≈ 8 min on one node).

Verdict and what it means

Formal gate FAIL sustained; the P1 attribution is revised, not rescued. The resistance magnitude WAS a real part of the axis failure — with the calibrated R the tune-split axis poisoning disappears at every weight (nominal R: 9.7–19.4 cm at w ≥ 0.3; calibrated: 4.7–4.9 vs spine 4.8) and the held-out axis damage halves (Δmedian +0.80 → +0.28 cm). But correcting the physics also SHRINKS the trajectory's claim, halving the ramp-up gain (−0.19 → −0.10 cm; CI grazing zero at the frozen weight). What remains is a real, structural trajectory–data disagreement the σ-weighted fit correctly refuses: the P2 case transients are unpredictable from the axisymmetric drive set (corr ≈ 0 whatever R), the P2–P5 up/down pair drives are un-separable in operating data (coil-audit finding — a shared-mode drive error no bounded R can absorb), and 94/146 efm inboard "conductors" are bounding-box artifacts the data opens. The honest conclusion for the temporal thesis: the vessel-eddy trajectory carries real but SMALL referee-visible ramp-up information (−0.1…−0.2 cm), and as a fit constraint it cannot clear the axis leg with the drive set as measured. The calibrated R itself is a validated standalone product every later rung inherits (P2's diffusion chain, P3's residual learner, the eddy features); the structural residual is the disciplined SINDy slot (missing circuits / 3-D paths), recorded, not chased.

Decisions honoured

  • passive-resistance-source = vacuum-trained-standalone (locked): the calibrated R is a standalone classical extraction the ML rungs inherit — never re-learned inside the pipeline.
  • passive-constraint-form = soft-prior-tight (locked): the re-gate keeps the trajectory soft prior, weight re-swept on tune with R frozen.
  • Case-current holdback (lead directive): honoured as binding — the case channels enter the discovery rung only as held-back targets; the re-gate reports both trajectory arms (cases measured-driven and cases predicted), gated on the measured arm.
