Honest limits (carried to P4/P5)

  • axis_Z skill −0.19: the referee Z RMSE vs the train-mean is ~2 cm — sub-grid territory at 3.3 cm cell size; the model's small systematic +Z offset costs skill even at 1–3 cm absolute error. Candidate causes: halo up-down asymmetry in the free inverse, FD-gradient refinement floor. P4's learned amortisation trains through the same read and can absorb it.
  • LCFS/X-point skills strongly negative: free patch currents carry sensor-invisible small-scale structure → spurious saddle points near the core → boundary-flux reads land far inside the referee LCFS (model radii systematically shrunken). This is a readout pathology on free-current ψ, not an axis problem; candidate mitigations (current-smoothness soft rung, boundary-read robustification) are P4/P5 material and must be measured, not assumed.
  • λ cap binds: median λ_final = 100 (the cap) on flat-top slices — the discrepancy principle wants more physics weight there; raising the cap risks the early-slice tail. The learned weighting inside P4 is the structural answer.
