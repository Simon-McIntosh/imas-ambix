Gate verdicts

  • Gate A (R1): FAIL as specified, with the headline measurement delivered. The sign prior collapses the anti-parallel fraction 0.75 → 0 exactly as designed — the sign-indefinite null-space fill of the free inverse is now measured at 0.75 Ip held-out (0.61 tune). But the axis skill is NOT CI-compatible with the baseline (−1.11 [−2.00, −0.88] vs −0.16 [−0.70, +0.14]) and the misfit triples: the halo was absorbing real, unmodelled sensor signal (eddy/vessel currents), not just noise. The soft-penalty ablation loses to hard softplus on every tune metric (axis −2.3/−2.6 vs −2.0) without reaching zero negative fraction — the hard-reparametrisation choice is confirmed.
  • Gate B (R2): the load-bearing rung, with a split verdict. Out-of-boundary current lands ON budget (0.030 ≤ 0.03). The axis MEDIAN halves (6.4 → 3.1 cm) and the X-point-set skill flips positive for the first time (−1.69 → +0.14) — topology reads genuinely improve when current cannot sit in the vacuum region. But the RMSE-tail metrics degrade (a few catastrophic slices dominate: axis skill CI [−5.25, −0.15]) and the LCFS ray-cast moves away from the referee (−4.91 → −7.57; median 24 → 33 cm).
  • Corner attribution — the review's decisive experiment, answered: the 135°/225° inboard-corner LCFS residual GROWS when current can no longer sit in the corners (27/26 cm → 39/40 cm; support-alone arm agrees at 38/37 cm). The corner residual is NOT a lobe artifact — it is genuinely interior (or coil-model) information. Thread 2 of equilibrium-topology-fidelity inherits it with evidence, exactly as the plan's if-no branch specified.

Decision — defaults

The priors stay OFF in the P3-winner defaults. The gate criteria as written (CI-compatible axis skill, LCFS toward the referee) did not pass, and P5 discipline keeps a rung only when load-bearing on the scored metrics. The deeper finding: the free inverse's better LCFS/misfit scores are bought with 0.75 Ip of unphysical current absorbing unmodelled eddy/vessel signal — the physics the priors expose is a coil/passive-model gap, not a prior defect. Re-gate R2 after the passive/eddy story improves; until then R1+R2 remain available behind flags (--sign-prior softplus --support-prior --support-weight 2000 --halo-budget 0.03) and are the recommended arm wherever topology reads (axis, X-points) matter more than LCFS ray-casts.
