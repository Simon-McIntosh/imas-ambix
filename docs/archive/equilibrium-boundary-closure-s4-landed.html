§4 — what was scoped (the learned-equilibrium-operator plan)

The endgame plan was authored on the evidence the closure investigation produced. Its core claim: the three characterised irreducibles of the classical per-slice solve — the p′/FF′ profile split (Te absent, TE_DATA_GATE), the axis-vs-centroid interior null (externally only Ip, centroid, βp+li/2 are visible), and the vessel-eddy L/R history (per-slice static passive fit under-determined by construction) — are all temporal or kinetic, so the learned operator is a causal sequence model wrapped around load-bearing exact physics layers, not a per-slice regressor. Headline design points:

  • Architecture ladder R0–R3, each rung gated vs the frozen classical baselines: R1 static residual learner (gate = non-inferiority + verified differentiable Green's stack — plumbing, so R2's wins are attributable to time), R2 temporal operator with an L/R-eigenmode-structured latent eddy state (the load-bearing rung; gate = beat disc flat-top 1.4–2.1 cm AND close the ramp-up 5–10 cm gap AND axis < D1's 5.1 cm median), R3 geometry-conditioned transfer (synthetic-machine sweep in-hand; real second machine data-gated).
  • Physics load-bearing (locked decision, from the lead's ship brief): thick-cylinder Green's operators, geometry positional encoding, and the spine's own profile basis sit inside the model; the network emits only physics-degenerate DOF.
  • Label factory: the frozen EFIT-independent spine (disc read + D2 solve) manufactures firewall-clean labels over the 12,590-shot MAST statespace corpus (CPU fleet, throughput measured before cohort sizing); synthetic_truth.manufacture supplies exact-truth pretraining (including the real-data-degenerate p′/FF′ and eddy histories).
  • Compute: O(1–50M)-param models, 1–4 H200 ≤1 day/run; label factory on sun CPU nodes; serving ms/slice (a real-time candidate as a bonus, not a gate). EFIT referee eval-only throughout; standing held-out shots never trained.

§5 — rotation ladder disposition (retired-to-trigger)

The centrifugal_gamma hook stays landed in gs_solve.py / structure_residual.py — zero maintenance burden. Trigger to reopen: a broader CXRS corpus lands, or the error floor drops enough that the rotation column's ~10%/6% absorption becomes resolvable. No open followup remains on this plan; the trigger is recorded here and in the f-ebc-04 resolution.

§6 — W5 term-discovery loop disposition (retired-to-trigger)

The mechanism of the discovery loop is landed code — the identifiability harness + synthetic-truth gates (identifiability_harness.py, synthetic_truth.py) are exactly the promotion gate the W5 registry was to formalise, and they gated every term this programme promoted (rotation R⁴, q≥1 clamp, soft priors). With the classical spine frozen (freeze criterion: boundary-faithful + attractor-stable; interior fidelity explicitly not chased classically), no further classical term promotion is planned — a versioned registry formalising a closed pipeline would be dead weight. Trigger to reopen: classical term promotion resumes (a new candidate closure term or a new data modality such as Thomson Te enters the corpus); the registry then builds on the landed harness. Discovery effort flows to the learned operator (R2/R3), where the identifiability question returns as ablation gates.
