§3 P2 — current-diffusion temporal prior (landed record)

Verdict (2026-07-17): the operator is built and verified — analytic pins + TORAX cross-check to 0.2% — but the formal gate FAILS: flat-top LCFS is non-inferior on held-out (Δskill +0.005, CI [−0.001, +0.011], margin −0.05) while the axis leg degrades (median 6.15 → 6.47 cm; Δskill −0.16, CI [−0.72, −0.04]), and the synthetic exact-recovery shows the per-interval diffusion prior does NOT materially recover the p′/FF′ split. With P1 and P2 both formally failed, rung P3 is a NO-GO under the plan's pre-declared rule — the temporal thesis closes at the physics level. The rung's durable products: a TORAX-verified differentiable ψ-diffusion operator, a cross-shot η(ψ_N) calibration that lands at the physical Spitzer scale when identified through the flux-budget closure, the explicit inductive/resistive consumption ledger with a windowed Ejima readout, and — from the lead's integrated-metrics directive — a shot-integrated route that backs the INTERNAL flux content out of external measurements, the opening the follow-up carries.

What was built

  • The operator (imas_ambix/latent/current_diffusion.py, torch/fp64): 1D flux-surface-averaged resistive ψ-diffusion on a uniform normalised-toroidal-flux grid, TORAX FVM formulation (toc·∂ψ/∂t = ∂/∂ρ̂[(g2·g3/ρ̂)·∂ψ/∂ρ̂], total-flux Wb convention), implicit θ-scheme with vertex-centred half-cell boundary balances: axis regularity + the measured Rogowski Ip as the edge gradient BC. η(ψ_N) = bounded 3-parameter monotone family (Spitzer/Sauter-informed, finite at the separatrix), cross-shot, never per-slice.
  • Flux-surface geometry from the spine's own equilibria: cumulative volume-integral (coarea) metrics binned on ψ_N — integrate-then- differentiate, robust where ring line-integrals of 1/|∇Φ| are not; F(ψ_N) integrated from the fitted FF′ family; the initial ψ(ρ̂) constructed identity-consistently from the fit's own current profile so the t = 0 round trip carries no resampling innovation.
  • Both consumption channels explicit (user directive): the surface swing decomposes structurally as ΔΨ_bdry = ΔΨ_internal (inductive) + ΔΨ_axis (resistive) — Ohm's law at the axis makes the axis drift purely resistive; the ledger reports both per interval and per shot, and C_E = |ΔΨ_res|/(μ0·R0·|ΔIp|) reads out wherever the covered window genuinely spans a ramp.
  • Split-sensitive projection: the evolved state predicts BOTH the toroidal current profile (dI/dS) and the ohmic parallel current (⟨J·B⟩ = σ∥·⟨E·B⟩); per unit coefficient the two families weight these differently (F·φ/R0 vs ⟨B²⟩·R0·φ/F), so the non-negative projection of the pair carries p′/FF′ information no single-slice magnetics fit has.
  • The prior in the fit: SoftPriors.coeff_prior_center/weight — identity rows on the ladder-coefficient block, weight RELATIVE to the data Gram (the smoothness convention), weight 0 byte-identical (test-pinned). Harness: scripts/current_diffusion_gate_eval.py (P1-pattern: byte-exact spine pass 1 → per-interval geometry + diffusion prediction → constrained pass 2 → paired bootstrap), synthetic gate scripts/current_diffusion_synthetic_gate.py, ledger report scripts/current_diffusion_flux_ledger_report.py.
  • Verification: 11 unit tests — Bessel eigenmode decay at the analytic cylinder rate (3%), ramping-Ip BC holds the enclosed current, late-time rigid consumption at the analytic ring voltage, Ampère closure of the extracted metrics on a solved equilibrium, projection round trip, flux-budget identity, zero-weight byte-identity, tight-prior pinning, and a TORAX cross-check: on a shared circular case (1→1.4 MA ramp, Sauter σ) the operator reproduces TORAX's ψ evolution to 0.2% RMS (scripts/torax_crosscheck_reference.py; reference NPZ committed under tests/data/).

Defects found by the verification ladder (all fixed, test-pinned)

  • Unstable current read-back: a pointwise (telescoping) gradient inversion was exact for the constructed initial state but amplified odd-even components of EVOLVED states into 6.7× RMS current-profile corruption on real geometry — replaced by the scheme's own stable midpoint fluxes.
  • Flux-sign error in the ohmic target: on MAST-sign equilibria (ψ_axis > ψ_bdry) ψ̇ < 0 during consumption, flipping the ⟨J·B⟩ target's sign so the non-negative projection could only reach it by annihilating the profile — fixed with the sign-invariant form ⟨E·B⟩ = flux_sign·ψ̇·F·⟨1/R²⟩/2π.
  • Invisible prior weight: the coefficient columns are Ip-normalised with whitened data curvature ~10⁴–10⁵ per unit coefficient, so an absolute O(1) weight did nothing (the first tune sweep was weight-flat) — made relative to the data Gram's mean diagonal.

The integrated-metrics route and the Ejima readout (lead directive)

The lead's directive mid-rung — "if we have a good model for resistive flux consumption we can compare to the boundary flux gauged from external measurements and back out internals" — is implemented as the flux-ledger report: chain Ψ_int(t) = Ψ_int(t₀) + [ΔΨ_bdry(measured) − ΔΨ_res(model)] along the shot and validate against every later slice's own fitted flux content (an independent per-slice read). This is the shot-integrated identification: the resistive signal grows with the window (~0.5–1 Wb) while per-interval read noise averages down — exactly where the per-interval prior is weakest. Results at the closure-fitted η: one tune shot closes to 68 mWb; three retain ~0.43 Wb of shot-structured residual that η alone cannot close (candidates: boundary-flux read drift, non-inductive drive, the flat-η restriction) — the quantified target the follow-up carries. li₃ from the 1D state's Wpol integral is implemented; the βp/li separation (ledger-li + the existing βp+li/2 moment rows → a DIRECT p′-amplitude constraint) is the concrete next mechanism.

Ejima coefficient: the covered windows span the late ramp (ΔIp ≈ 0.2–0.35·Ip_max), so the readout is the WINDOWED, incremental C_E. At the coefficient-fit η: held-out median 2.65 (range 0.59–2.91) — biased high by the same η overestimate the ledger closure exposes (V_res 3.3–4.2 V vs ~1 V typical; predicted surface swing 1.3–3× measured). Scaling by the closure-fit η (6.5× lower) puts the implied full-consumption coefficient in the ~0.4–0.5 literature band, but the honest statement is: a credible C_E requires the closure-identified η and early-ramp coverage (the corpus labels start at Ip ≥ 300 kA) — recorded as the condition, not claimed as a result.

P3 go/no-go — the pre-declared rule fires

The plan pre-declared: "If P1/P2 both fail their gates, P3 does not run — the plan records that the modelled dynamics do not carry the missing information and the temporal thesis fails at the physics level." P1 closed FAIL (sustained through the resistance calibration and the structure discovery; ramp-up signal real but −0.1…−0.2 cm small). P2 closes FAIL (flat-top non-inferior; axis degraded; no material split recovery). P3 is a NO-GO. The clean negative: at MAST label cadence the modelled axisymmetric dynamics — vessel eddies (P1) and resistive current diffusion (P2) — carry real but referee-marginal information, and both bias the interior null (the axis) when promoted to fit constraints. What survives is the integrated route: the flux ledger's shot-scale resistive budget is measurable, η-identifiable at physical values, and opens the βp/li separation — a fundamentally different information channel from per-slice or per-interval priors.
