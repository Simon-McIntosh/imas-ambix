The ruler — one shared observation model

Every predictor is scored through the identical forward model (mse_eval.pitch_from_current_profile, the LOCKED kind='j' representation from the S9 head-to-head coordination): a 1D toroidal-current-density profile jφ(ρ̂) on a ρ̂·aminor minor-radius grid maps to MSE pitch per sightline. The classical EnKF reads out its TORAX j_total(ρ_norm) the same way; the reanalysis engine’s force-balanced ψ + ladder coefficients give the flux-surface geometry (current_diffusion.flux_surface_geometry), whose flux-surface- averaged jφ(ρ̂) (predicted_current) is that same quantity in that same coordinate. Pitch is read at each shot’s sightlines with the per-shot R0 = 0.85 m and Bt0 from amc.tf_current (matching the EnKF baseline), then interpolated onto the manifest’s beam-on slice times. A head-to-head therefore isolates state inference, not the observation physics.

A calibrated posterior (physics ensemble over the under-determined interior) is a §5 uncertainty-quantification task, not a blocker on whether the profile is earned.
  • Label honesty held. 11 shots had too few confined slices to couple (fast ramp-downs / thin plasmas) and were skipped; unconfined slices within scored shots were masked. No fabricated profile entered the score or would enter a label — exactly the assert-only-validated decision.
