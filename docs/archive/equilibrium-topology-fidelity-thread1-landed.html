Verdict

The boundary deficit is substantially a representation artifact — a constrained current-moment read improves both boundary metrics on held-out data with no interior diagnostic, confirming §2's direction. The recovery is partial: the X-point / limited↔diverted class is externally well-posed and recovers (deficit more than halved), but the detailed LCFS radial extent does not fully recover — it couples to the finite interior current profile (the genuine null-space). This refines §2: the boundary topology is externally well-posed; its radial shape shares the interior null-space. Three current mechanizations (polynomial, free Gaussian, axis-centred Gaussian) all leave the same near-edge LCFS residual at the inboard corners / divertor-leg region even with correct axis + Ip — conclusive evidence the residual is interior, not representational.
