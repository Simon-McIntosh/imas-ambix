The retraction is load-bearing: a grid sample at a saddle vertex always sits an O(Δ²) hair below the sub-grid saddle flux, so an un-retracted flood walks through that cell and pours weight-1 into the pocket beyond (measured: private contamination 1.0 on the double-null fixture before the fix, 0.0 after). The cost is a one-sided O(τ) boundary shell that vanishes with τ.
  • Adversarial fixtures + gates (scripts/connectivity_boundary_gate_eval.py) — balanced DN / secondary separatrix / snowflake / private-flux synthetic fields with exact CPU references (lcfs_contour, classify_regions, emergent_xpoints), a 21-step near-double ordering sweep, the currents-gradient probe, and the held-out separation leg. Single-null fixtures live on a compact vessel — a taller vessel lets the CPU push-out close a peanut contour around both nulls (wall-limited), a genuinely different topology than these cases pin.
