Spatiotemporal Tokenizer Pivot Assessment

Coordinator (Opus) synthesis, 2026-05-29. Evidence base: the linked literature survey tokenizer-spatiotemporal-survey (B2 fleet agent, all claims source-verified). This plan assesses a strategic question raised directly by the user: a fusion world model must extract dynamics and transients, yet we tokenize the plasma camera stream frame-by-frame in 2D rather than taking 3D space-time cubes the way a video patch transformer would. Should we pivot?

§1 — Problem framing

The Fusion World Model is a two-stage system: (1) a tokenizer compresses raw MAST fast-camera frames into discrete tokens, and (2) a dynamics model (a sequence model over those tokens) learns to predict the future. Today the tokenizer is Open-MAGVIT2, a 2D image LFQ autoencoder (218 codebook, 16× spatial compression) applied independently to each frame. There is no temporal axis in the tokenizer at all.

The physics we care about is fast: ELMs, filament/blob ejection, and disruptions evolve on sub-millisecond to ~100 µs timescales on the rbb fast visible cameras. The user's concern is well-posed: are we throwing away the temporal structure by tokenizing statically?

§2 — Where do dynamics live? (the honest answer)

In the two-stage paradigm, dynamics do not have to live in the tokenizer. A per-frame tokenizer + a temporally-deep stage-2 model is a valid and widely-deployed design — GAIA-1 (Wayve), iVideoGPT, and the autoregressive Cosmos world model all place most of the dynamics in the sequence model, with tokenization doing compression. So frame-by-frame tokenization is not inherently broken for capturing dynamics, provided stage-2 carries temporal context (which ours will). The user's surprise is reasonable, but this axis alone is not a bug.

That said, 3D (spatiotemporal) tokenization carries three concrete advantages that matter specifically for plasma:

  • Token compression → longer physical-time context. Video tokenizers add 4–8× temporal compression (verified: Cosmos DV/CV 4×/8×, MAGVIT-v2 4×, VidTok 4×, LARP 4×). For a fixed stage-2 token budget, that is 4–8× more milliseconds of plasma evolution per context window — a large win when the dynamics are fast.
  • Motion primitives in-token. A 3D tokenizer encodes local space-time structure (a moving filament, an ELM burst) into single tokens, so the dynamics model learns higher-order evolution rather than re-deriving motion from static frames every step.
  • Transient fidelity. Temporally-causal 3D convolutions represent fast transients as coherent space-time codes — the exact thing the user wants preserved.

§3 — The deeper critique: rFID is probably the wrong north-star

The entire decoder fine-tune effort (see plasma-decoder-finetune) optimized and gated on rFID — a per-frame reconstruction Fréchet distance in ImageNet-InceptionV3 feature space. For a dynamics world model this is questionable on three grounds:

  1. It is per-frame and temporally blind. rFID is a set-level statistic over individual frames; it cannot see temporal coherence at all. A tokenizer could ace rFID and destroy dynamics, or the reverse.
  2. The feature space is out-of-domain. InceptionV3 was trained on natural images; plasma is far OOD, so rFID may not even rank plasma reconstructions meaningfully (cf. MedITok, arXiv:2505.19225, where domain-specific tokenizers dramatically beat natural-image ones on OOD medical imagery).
  3. Even FVD — the temporal upgrade — is weak. FVD (I3D-feature Fréchet) has documented content bias and is insensitive to temporal distortions (arXiv:2404.12391).

The right north-star for a dynamics-WM tokenizer is downstream: does the token stream support accurate multi-step rollout prediction of physics-relevant quantities (centroid, chord profile, stored energy, mode amplitude, ELM timing)? Or skip reconstruction entirely and go JEPA-style (predict in latent space — V-JEPA-2 has no pixel decoder and no rFID at all). This reframes the fine-tune saga: it may have been chasing the wrong metric. Whatever the bf16 diagnostic concludes (see §6), a decoder that is "worse on rFID" might be fine — or irrelevant — for the world model.

§4 — What is actually runnable (from the survey)

Full table + sources in the survey. The decision-relevant facts:

  • MAGVIT-v2 — the video sibling of our Open-MAGVIT2 (causal-3D, LFQ, 218, 4× temporal). Weights are closed-source; only a community reproduction (asymmetric_magvitv2) exists.
  • Cosmos CV/DV (NVIDIA) — open weights, commercial-OK, video, 4×/8× temporal + 8×/16× spatial; DV uses FSQ (~64K), CV is continuous. We have only ever run the image variant DI16x16 (downloaded; CV/DV not downloaded). This is the single biggest untested gap.
  • VidTok (Microsoft) — open, MIT, FSQ, 4× temporal + 8× spatial, supports arbitrary-length video.
  • V-JEPA-2 (Meta) — open, non-reconstructive latent-predictive; eliminates rFID; evaluation becomes downstream tasks.
  • DIAMOND — a world model with no discrete tokenizer at all (pixel-space diffusion); proof that the discrete-token route is not the only one.

§6 — Recommended sequencing & interaction with the bf16 verdict

The decode-precision diagnostic (job 1209184, see plasma-decoder-finetune) is testing whether the 2D decoder's apparent rFID regression is a bf16 measurement artifact. It interacts with this plan but does not block it:

  • If H-bf16 (fine-tune actually healthy): the 2D path is in good shape (rFID ~13). v0 proceeds on 2D; spatiotemporal becomes a clean v1 optimization.
  • If H-real (2D decoder reconstruction plateaued): per §3 this may not matter for the WM, but it strengthens the case to evaluate 3D + better metrics sooner.

In both branches the recommended actions are the same and low-risk: (1) do not pivot v0 wholesale or re-tokenize the corpus now; (2) run the cheap Cosmos-DV video zero-shot to get real plasma numbers on a 3D tokenizer; (3) add a temporal/rollout metric so future tokenizer decisions are made on the right signal. Lock the final v0-vs-pivot decision only after the bf16 verdict and the Cosmos-DV result.

§ Decisions

Which tokenizer powers world-model-v0?

Recommend stay-2d for v0: dynamics live in stage-2, the 4.02B-token corpus exists, and a pivot should be evidence-driven (gated on the Cosmos-DV zero-shot + bf16 verdict). Do not lock until both land.

Which 3D / spatiotemporal approach do we evaluate FIRST?

Recommend Cosmos-DV4x8x8 zero-shot: lowest risk, open weights, directly tests whether temporal compression preserves plasma transients. VidTok as a second data point.

What metric should gate tokenizer decisions for a DYNAMICS world model?

rFID alone is per-frame, OOD, and temporally blind. Recommend adding a rollout/temporal-consistency metric as the real north-star; rFVD is a cheap interim addition but carries known content-bias.
