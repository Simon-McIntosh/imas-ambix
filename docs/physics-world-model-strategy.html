Physics World Model — Strategy & North Star

The foundational objective the rest of the project hangs from. Refounded 2026-05-29 around probabilistic state estimation; sharpened 2026-08-09 around a fast Nova transition, learned model–signal innovation and ITER first-day transfer. Architecture synthesis: hybrid-generative-state-estimation.

§1 — North Star

Build an ITER-ready, machine-portable probabilistic plasma state estimator in which physics that is physics transfers exactly, while uncertainty in closures, sources, transport coefficients and boundary decisions is learned from the difference between Nova's fast prediction and signal-carrying data.

Conservation, force balance, circuit laws and geometry transforms are the transferable substrate. Nova advances them under each machine geometry and proposed coil or actuator trajectory. Ambix carries distributions over the assumptions those laws do not determine, conditions the resulting ensemble on magnetics, profiles, flux-surface averages, cameras and other signals, and distils repeated held-out mismatch into candidate transferable closures. Surprise is evidence to classify—not automatically discovery: calibration drift, bad geometry, missing sources, numerical error and out-of-support regimes remain competing explanations.

The operational model and the scientific instrument are one system. With observations withheld it produces a sharp, calibrated GenCast-like forecast ensemble. With observations through the current time it produces a causal reconstruction or analysis. With future observations admitted it produces a clearly labelled non-causal smoother and reanalysis corpus. The same posterior generates equilibrium maps and diagnostic streams under new commands; camera generation is a useful by-product, not the state or the project's guiding objective.

The reason to place fast Nova inside the inference chain is transfer: new geometry and coil currents generate new physical flux maps immediately, while closure and boundary posteriors transfer only as their invariant context and uncertainty permit. Robust cross-machine learning on present devices is our rehearsal for useful estimates from ITER's first plasma. The exclusive eight-H200 server buys ensemble breadth, complete-shot smoothing and training throughput; it accelerates this North Star without defining it.

§1a — North star, refined (2026-06-24): a machine-agnostic general fusion world model

Refined after the joint-generative-WM sprint produced a robust negative and a sharp feasibility map. New /goal (user, 2026-06-24): "discover the underlying latent dynamics and a physics-faithful, data-led world model capable of controllable dreams" — and, as the long-horizon destination, one model that is not MAST-specific but transfers across machines toward ITER.

The destination is sharpened, not changed: the goal is a single data-led latent state-space world model that ingests absolute, geometry-tagged, multi-modal diagnostic tokens, carries them into a shared latent whose dynamics are learned by space-time transformers, decodes back to every modality (cameras included), and is controllable through the actuator plan — and which, because its inputs and latent are expressed in machine-portable terms, extrapolates MAST → JET → ITER by construction rather than by retraining from scratch on each device.

Why this reframe is forced by the evidence

Two robustly-measured findings from the recent work pin down where the physics is, and is not, recoverable — and they point the same way:

  • Generation works; control does not — root cause is causal under-identification. Across both a token backbone and a Dreamer-style RSSM, and across every controllability lever tried (per-layer plan conditioning, history bottleneck, inverse-dynamics, joint generation, per-modality masking, action-contrastive, command-in-recurrence), the actuator plan never became load-bearing: a powered gate (true-plan vs random-plan decoded divergence, n=10) returned ratios ≈ 0.85–1.03, all failing. On observational flat-top tokens the next state is predictable from history alone; the command is confounded / non-exciting, so it is never necessary. Controllability cannot be extracted from a chain that does not identify it.
  • The physics IS recoverable — from absolute signals, not from these dreams. A feasibility oracle chain showed: cameras cannot see interior geometry (decoded-camera → axis/X-point skill ≈ 0); per-shot z-scored tokens are scale-free (diagnostics → equilibrium skill ≈ 0); but absolute, calibrated magnetics resolve axis / X-point / boundary at skill 0.5–0.7. The information is there; the representation was throwing it away.

The user's tractability decomposition is the constructive read of this: (a) plan (incl. pulse-schedule) → plasma current is a genuinely identifiable control map (pulses follow the plan closely); (b) magnetics constrain magnetic topology — they place the boundary and X-point well (an EFIT-input-class, well-posed problem, distinct from the S9/S12 interior-current under-determination); (c) other diagnostics contribute internal structure; (d) the camera contributes edge information and fast transients, and is a dream-decode target. The fix for non-controllability is therefore structural: move controllability off the chain that cannot carry it (plan → camera tokens) onto the chain that can (plan → Ip → magnetics → boundary), inside one shared latent.

What "machine-agnostic" makes load-bearing

Transfer across machines is the lever that disciplines the whole design — and it is the same lever as controllability, because the identifiable actuator→response chain, expressed in dimensionless / geometric terms, is exactly what carries from one device to another. Four requirements become non-negotiable:

  1. Geometry as positional encoding for every modality. Camera tokens only decoded once each carried its 16×16 spatial position; the same is true one level up. A magnetic-probe token, a soft-X-ray chord token, an interferometer chord token must each carry its diagnostic's physical location and geometry in the machine — position (R, Z, φ), orientation / sensor normal, and integration geometry (line-of-sight endpoints for chords, pixel footprint for cameras) — together with machine geometry (vessel, PF/coil locations). Without it the model memorises "channel index 7 on MAST means X," a mapping worthless on JET or ITER; with it the model learns "a Bθ probe at (R, Z, orientation) sees this field" — a mapping that is physics, and physics transfers. This is the camera-spatial-position principle generalised to all diagnostics, and it is what makes a heterogeneous, machine-varying sensor set ingestible by one attention model.
  2. Absolute calibration is mandatory, not optional. Per-shot z-scoring — the scale-free blocker that killed diagnostics → equilibrium — also destroys cross-machine comparability. 0.5 T must tokenise to the same code on every machine. Calibration is corpus-level and in physical (ideally SI) units, so a token's value means the same thing regardless of shot or device.
  3. Dimensionless / invariant latent coordinates become load-bearing (this was §6's aspiration; multi-machine makes it a requirement). Machine size spans an order of magnitude MAST → ITER; only dimensionless groups (ρ*, β, ν*, a/R, normalised shape) bridge it. The open extrapolation-coordinates decision is now strongly pulled toward design-for-invariance from v0 — retrofitting invariance after the latent is fixed is the costly path.
  4. Tokens are retained as the ingestion substrate (user lock, 2026-06-24). The discrete-token substrate is how we ingest high-bandwidth, multi-rate, multi-modal streams on a common footing, and it is already a first-class asset. We keep the tokeniser; we add the absolute-calibration + diagnostic-geometry schema to it. Camera stays a tokenised decode-only target + edge/transient source (the camera-role lock holds).

This refinement supersedes nothing already locked — it sharpens §1's "structured to extrapolate to ITER," promotes §6's invariant-coordinate aspiration to a v0 design constraint, and reaffirms the camera-role lock. The staged plan (§9) is superseded as the active sequence by a new ground-up redesign (brainstorm in progress, 2026-06-24); §§1–9 below remain the durable rationale and the record of Stages 1–3.

§1b — The equilibrium north star + the layered-capability stack (2026-07-05)

Added after the patch-current equilibrium layer shipped and its integration into the world model landed. This section names the concrete ambition for the equilibrium organ and the layered architecture the rest of the project stacks on it. It sharpens §1a; it supersedes nothing locked.

Beat EFIT the way GenCast beat ECMWF — match, then exceed

The equilibrium north star is now stated as a concrete, falsifiable ambition: beat EFIT on the X-point, the plasma boundary, and the limited↔diverted topology class — first match, then exceed. The template is GenCast (Nature, 2025), which beat the ECMWF ensemble on ~97% of forecast targets not by being a better deterministic solver but by being a better probabilistic model of the state — generative, multimodal, dynamics-aware, uncertainty-carrying. EFIT is a per-slice deterministic point solve on magnetics + a fixed current-profile parametrisation, returning a single answer with no honest uncertainty at the finely-judged transition. Our route to exceeding it uses exactly the moves EFIT structurally cannot make: fuse interior diagnostics (MSE / interferometer / SXR) that see the interior current magnetics cannot; a corpus-trained generative prior over the manifold of plausible equilibria that picks the physical X-point over a spurious ψ-saddle; temporal coherence over the smooth limited→diverted sweep; and a calibrated limited/diverted probability where EFIT gives a coin-flip. EFIT stays firewalled — a referee that only judges our readouts, never an input or a training label. The honest current state: the magnetic axis is solved (referee-grade — patch-current inverse 2.8 cm median, 160/160 held-out; flux-map axis 2.4 cm flat-top), while the X-point and boundary are the open frontier (honest negatives, LCFS skill ≈ −6, boundary under-sized 34–37 cm; update 2026-07-07 — thread 1 of equilibrium-topology-fidelity then showed a substantial share of that deficit is representational, X-point −0.61→−0.15 held-out with no new data, and the 2026-07 grounding review reopened the attribution of the remainder pending hardened current priors — see plasma-current-priors-hardening). That frontier is owned by the new plan equilibrium-topology-fidelity.

The layered-capability stack: a physics skeleton the higher layers inherit

The architecture is a stack, and the load-bearing insight is that the physics skeleton at the base keeps every layer above it physical. This is why the equilibrium work is foundational rather than a side-quest:

  • Layer 0 — the robust physics / GS skeleton. The patch-current equilibrium latent shipped this session (patch-current-force-balance → patch-equilibrium-wm-integration): exact linear Maxwell physics precomputed into interaction matrices (694k slices/s on an H200), force balance as a soft differentiable residual, per-bin closures (ak=p′, bk=FF′/μ₀, F²≥0 on 160/160) recovered from data, everything label-free. It grounds a learned latent on real magnetics (GS residual 12.15→2.29 = 5.3× held-out) and transfers across an unseen coil-set signature without retraining (cross-signature grounding 5.54×). This is the equilibrium organ of the world model.
  • Layer 1 — forward decoders hang off the skeleton and inherit its grounding. Camera-video generation and virtual diagnostics decode from the physically-constrained state, not from a free latent. A dream frame is rendered from a ψ that explains the raw magnetics and obeys force balance — so it is anchored, not hallucinated. This is the structural answer to the camera direction's collapse (§2/§11): the camera becomes a decode head on a grounded state, and the grounding is what a bare generative video model never had.
  • Layer 2 — long-horizon steerable dreams roll the grounded latent under a plan. Forecasting and controllable what-ifs advance the same physics-anchored latent forward under the actuator plan, with the transport/irreversibility priors keeping the roll physical and the ensemble spread inflating uncertainty honestly. Controllability rides the identifiable chain plan → Ip → magnetics → ψ → boundary inside this latent (§1a), and the skeleton stops the long roll from drifting off the equilibrium manifold.

The through-line: a dream is only as trustworthy as the state it is rendered from. Layer 0 makes that state physical; Layers 1 and 2 inherit it. Investing in the equilibrium skeleton — and specifically in closing its X-point/topology frontier — is therefore an investment in every capability above it, which is why the equilibrium north star (match-then-exceed EFIT) is the load-bearing near-term objective.

§2 — Why a "world model": it is a learned state-space model

The unifying identity: an ML world model — encoder → latent state → learned transition → observation model — is a learned nonlinear state-space model. That single object subsumes the three threads we care about:

  • State estimation / data assimilation = Bayesian inference over this model given streaming observations. The Kalman filter is the linear-Gaussian special case; the general case is a learned, nonlinear, possibly non-Gaussian filter/smoother.
  • Dynamics live in the transition kernel p(statet | statet-1, actuators).
  • Discovery = recovering the structure and coefficients of that transition kernel (Koopman modes, sparse terms, conservation). The kernel is simultaneously the dynamics and the physics we want to discover.

This is precisely what fusion's existing Bayesian machinery lacks. Integrated data analysis (Minerva@JET, IDA@AUG/W7-X) does excellent static, per-slice inference p(state | data) — but treats slices independently; it has no transition kernel, hence no dynamics. Learning that kernel, and doing honest filtering/smoothing through it, is the contribution. (Bayesian modelling's difficulty with dynamics is exactly this missing Markov transition; the state-space structure is the resolution.)

§3 — How much probability? (instrumental, not a religion)

The probabilistic framing is not inevitable — a deterministic observer is possible — but it is motivated, and only as far as it earns its keep. Probability buys exactly three things we genuinely need:

  1. Honest "I don't know" — the precondition for safe extrapolation: a model that flags when it is outside its support.
  2. Optimal assimilation — fusing model prior with noisy/partial observations is inherently a weighting-by-confidence operation (the Kalman gain is relative uncertainty).
  3. Calibrated decisions under scarce, expensive data.

There is a spectrum — deterministic observer → deep ensembles → calibrated/conformal intervals → full variational/MCMC posterior — and we adopt the lightest level that is genuinely calibrated, not the heaviest. The goal is the dynamics and the understanding; uncertainty is a property we require, not the point. We will not "go full Bayes" where a calibrated interval suffices.

§4 — Three operations, named precisely

"Predictive state estimation" conflates distinct operations of a state-space model; we name them separately because their value differs:

  • Filtering — estimate the state now from past + present observations. Causal/online → real-time state for control and commissioning.
  • Forecasting — project the state into the future with no future observations. The "what-if" → reduces wasted shots, accelerates commissioning.
  • Smoothing — reconstruct the best past trajectory using all observations. Offline → understanding and structure discovery.

§5 — Data discipline: raw signals, physics as our own falsifiable prior

Observations are raw measured sensor signals. Physics is a prior we add explicitly and can relax. We never train on regression/solver output. Training on EFIT-reconstructed flux surfaces would hoover up Grad–Shafranov-as-a-hard-constraint, a fixed current-profile parameterisation, and the fit weighting — we would be learning EFIT, not the plasma, and inheriting every assumption that breaks in a new regime ("modelling fails before it starts").

Instead we add Grad–Shafranov ourselves as a soft, falsifiable residual linking the latent state to raw magnetics. The payoff is the capability EFIT structurally cannot give: because GS is a prior the data can override, a persistent GS residual is not an error to fit away — it is a measurement of where force balance breaks (fast dynamics, non-MHD effects, off-normal evolution). We invert "inheriting bias" into "discovering departures from equilibrium" — the same logic as a filter's innovations: a residual against a physics prior is a discovery signal.

Clarification on IMAS: IMAS (the Data Dictionary) is a format for interoperability; it is orthogonal to which signals we can read. MAST signals — cameras, magnetics, everything — are already accessible in native MAST form (level-1 zarr groups: rbb camera, amb/amc/amm magnetics, efm EFIT equilibrium, …). "Go IMAS" is a later interoperability enhancement, not a prerequisite for raw-signal access. So the work is multi-modal from day one.

§6 — Extrapolation: robust structure, not brittle assumptions

Extrapolation needs structure, but structure usually means assumptions — the brittleness we are fleeing. The resolution: not all structure is brittle.

  • Brittle structure = phenomenological closures and boundary conditions. Keep these data-led and flexible — let the data write them.
  • Robust structure = invariances: conservation laws, symmetries, and dimensionless scaling. This is not an assumption about the plasma, it is an assumption about physics being physics. ITER was sized by dimensionless scaling (ρ*, β, ν*, …) from existing devices; a model whose coordinates are dimensionless extrapolates across machine scale by construction, without committing to a closure.

So physics enters as the coordinate system and soft regularisers, never as a hard dynamics law. Data-led flexibility buys assumption-tolerance; invariant coordinates buy extrapolation; and calibrated uncertainty makes extrapolation safe — the model says "out of support" near genuinely novel regimes (burning-plasma ρ*, α-heating) rather than extrapolating blindly. Cross-machine transfer is not aspirational: deep-learning disruption prediction has already transferred DIII-D → JET (Kates-Harbeck, Nature 2019), explicitly ITER-motivated.

§7 — Where ML is, and is not, the right tool

We agree ML is not the right tool to solve large known PDE systems — numerical solvers win on accuracy and guarantees, and ML surrogates merely trade fidelity for speed while inheriting the training simulations' assumptions. But that is not our job. ML/data is uniquely right for the part first-principles cannot do: the closure problem (turbulent transport, the pedestal, the L–H threshold, detachment, the disruption boundary) — where gyrokinetics is too expensive to run in the loop and reduced models rely on fitted closures that do not extrapolate. Closure uncertainty is the largest source of error in predicting ITER performance, and it is exactly a "structure + coefficients we don't know" problem. Don't use ML to solve the PDEs you have; use it to discover the closures and reduced dynamics you don't — and compose with first-principles where the equations are known and cheap.

§8 — Utility for ITER (the point)

  • Commissioning & operations acceleration — forecasting + active sensing reduce the number of wasted/risky shots needed to map the operating space; ITER shots are the most expensive in history.
  • Virtual diagnostics / real-time state estimation — infer the unmeasured from the measured, with calibrated uncertainty, when burning-plasma diagnostic access is limited.
  • Safety — OOD flagging and innovation/latent-trajectory signals for earlier off-normal and limit-proximity warning.
  • Transferable understanding — discovered structure (equation form, coefficient scaling) carries MAST → JET → ITER where raw data never will. This is the real answer to ITER's data scarcity: bootstrap on physics, not pixels.

§9 — Staged plan (three stages; Stage-3 pivot added 2026-05-31)

Stage 1 — the engine, on raw multi-modal MAST data (now). Build and validate a learned stochastic latent state-space model + Bayesian filter with calibrated uncertainty and OOD flagging, across the operating space. First concrete target: predict a whole held-out diagnostic family (e.g. Dα, or the camera-derived emission boundary) from the other raw families, and forecast it through its transients, with honest error bars — see plasma-state-space-v0. Cross-family (not within-family probe-from-probes), EFIT-free. Acceptance is calibration, not reconstruction fidelity.

Stage 2 — physical grounding + discovery. Add our own GS prior on raw magnetics; the latent becomes physically grounded; virtual diagnostics become physical; GS-residual monitoring becomes a discovery instrument. Structure/coefficient discovery (SINDy/Koopman on the latent dynamics) rides on top, expressed toward dimensionless coordinates for extrapolation.

Stage 3 — MSE-free multi-modal current-profile recovery (the pivot, 2026-05-31). Stages 1–2 delivered honest results that re-centred the project on its founding multi-modal motivation. Stage-1's engine is calibration-honest, but a trivial persistence-of-nowcast wins the headline proper-scores on its near-stationary Dα target (dynamics did not pay); Stage-2 confirmed that magnetics + boundary cannot constrain the internal current profile, and that MSE is unusable from level-1 as a dense input — while delivering a validated, EFIT-free Grad–Shafranov forward operator. Stage-3 turns both findings into the ITER-relevant thesis: at ITER startup there is no MSE, yet the current profile must still be recovered. We build an end-to-end observation→state deep neural filter on raw multi-modal diagnostics (magnetics + interferometer + bolometer + soft-X-ray + visible camera + Thomson) that recovers MSE-derived internal-current observables it never sees as input, and must beat a classical EnKF baseline (which we stand up — it does not yet exist). The MSE wall becomes the validation key: train MSE-free, validate against held-out raw MSE on beam-on slices. Current evolution is genuinely dynamic, so dynamics should finally pay where the near-stationary Dα target denied them. See mse-free-current-recovery-v0 (sprint S9). §10 — Honest limits

We will not rediscover gyrokinetics or two-fluid MHD from data — the true equations are high-dimensional, multi-scale, turbulent, stochastic, and not closed. The realistic, valuable target is reduced-order / effective dynamics and their coefficients: Koopman modes, sparse reduced models, effective transport coefficients, mode-coupling terms, force-balance departures. Camera emission is an indirect, projected, line-integrated observation — it constrains a latent that predicts camera dynamics, not the physical state directly; physical grounding needs magnetics (Stage 2). And some ITER regimes are genuinely outside existing dimensionless support — there, the right behaviour is the model flagging "I don't know," not a confident extrapolation.

§11 — Relationship to work in flight

The centre of gravity moves from generative camera-video world model to probabilistic state-space model + assimilation + discovery. The camera becomes one diagnostic modality feeding the state estimator; generating camera streams is a byproduct, never the guiding goal. The tokenizer/corpus work (now archived: world-model-v0, tokenizer-spatiotemporal-pivot) is not wasted — it is representation learning for one modality — but it is no longer the headline. The rFID dead-end (now archived: plasma-decoder-finetune) becomes a non-issue: reconstruction fidelity was never the objective; calibrated state estimation and discovered structure are.

§ Decisions

What level of probabilistic machinery for the Stage-1 engine?

The lead requires robust GenCast-like probabilistic forecasts plus forecast, analysis and smoothing. Start with a stochastic Nova-propagated ensemble, proper-score calibration and conformal coverage checks; add richer variational machinery only when measured posterior structure requires it.

Do we build toward dimensionless/invariant latent coordinates now, or defer?

Cross-machine transfer and useful ITER estimates from first plasma are first-order design constraints. Diagnostic geometry and invariant or dimensionless context must be load-bearing from the start.

What is the camera's role in the world model?

Locked by the user 2026-05-29: "eventually it would be nice to also have the facility to generate camera streams - but this is a byproduct and is in no way our guiding goal." Camera is one input modality to the state estimator.

What is the authoritative forecast transition?

Known physics transfers across machines. A direct learned generator may later challenge, propose or distil, but it must earn parity against the Nova-led ensemble before becoming authoritative.

Which state-estimation products are required?

The lead requires the full EnKF-class capability envelope: prediction, reconstruction and smoothing from one probabilistic system.

How should the exclusive eight-H200 resource influence the design?

The server is a decisive throughput advantage and should be fully used, but the scientific direction and deployed estimator must remain portable.
