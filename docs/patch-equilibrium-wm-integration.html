§1 — Why this plan: the reduced-order-physics-wrapped-in-ML pattern, aligned to the North Star

patch-current-force-balance shipped 2026-07-04 with all five phases landed and both decisions locked. Its architecture — exact linear Maxwell physics precomputed into interaction matrices, force balance as a soft differentiable residual, closures recovered from data, everything batched and label-free — is the pattern the lead wants this project built on: a reduced-order physics model wrapped in ML capability, with the data in the lead. This plan carries that shipped layer into the driving plan machine-agnostic-latent-world-model and closes the measured gaps, while staying inside the North Star (physics-world-model-strategy) by construction.

Compatibility is not asserted — it is itemised against the North Star's four non-negotiable requirements (physics-world-model-strategy §1a-reqs) and its two governing rules:

North Star requirement (physics-world-model-strategy)What the patch layer already delivers (measured)
“Geometry as positional encoding for every modality” (§1a-reqs #1) The amortised encoder ingests per-(sensor,step) tokens with (R,Z,orientation) additive geometry encoding, and evaluates on an unseen coil-set signature without retraining (cross-signature grounding 5.54×, axis median 10.8 cm vs 8.4 in-signature) — the first measured cross-geometry transfer of the equilibrium layer.
“Absolute calibration is mandatory” (§1a-reqs #2) All losses run on RAW SI magnetics with per-shot honest masks; the Rogowski Ip anchor is a raw measurement; corpus-level channel standardisation only feeds token values, never the physics losses.
“Dimensionless / invariant latent coordinates” (§1a-reqs #3) The recovered per-bin closures (ak=p′, bk=FF′/μ₀ with 1σ uncertainties, F²≥0 verified on 160/160 slices) are exactly the machine-portable profile coordinates the latent needs — Workstream B3 turns them into latent content.
“Tokens are retained as the ingestion substrate” (§1a-reqs #4) The encoder is token-native (continuous standardised values in the oracle-recipe token layout) — no bespoke ingestion path.
“Physics enters as the coordinate system and soft regularisers, never as a hard law” (§6) Ampère/div B are identities of the representation (coordinate system); force balance is a soft, ablatable residual whose weight policy was measured (bounded discrepancy, locked); the passive block was dropped on evidence — soft physics applied to itself.
“A persistent GS residual is … a measurement of where force balance breaks” (§5) The structure residual discriminates exactly the sensor-null-space (5.7× floor) and reports as a scalar discovery signal per slice; rotation extends the family (R⁴ column, CI-pinned).

Where it lands in the driving plan: the equilibrium readout is stage 2 of machine-agnostic-latent-world-model (followup f-malwm-02, done-when #2: “GS-readout axis/X-point/boundary skill matches the absolute-magnetics oracle (~0.5–0.7)”). gs-grounded-latent-engine recorded that bar as an honest negative for its training-free linear inverse and subsumed the fix into patch-current-force-balance P4; the P4 result moved the needle (grounding PASS, availability PASS, geometry gap measured with a scaling law) but the 0.5–0.7 bar on the learned latent is still open — that measurement, done properly through the world-model engine, is what Workstream B finishes.

  • Amortised encoder (P4): grounding 14.3× in-signature / 5.54× cross-signature (bar 5.53× — both PASS); 160/160 availability; head locked = direct. Geometry honest-negative: in-signature axis −0.256 @ 8.4 cm (axis_Z at baseline parity; axis_R carries the deficit; X-point set +0.058 is the first positive amortised quantity). Scaling law measured: 588 → 14,525 examples cut the median 26.9 → 8.4 cm (25× data → 3.2×).
  • Known pathologies, owned: LCFS/boundary reads on free-current ψ hit spurious saddles (skills ≈ −3 to −10 everywhere); the corpus spans ≥3 campaign signatures and the current trainer drops the S=81 signature (3,795 examples); the encoder head is deterministic (no calibration).

§3 — Workstream A: close the amortised geometry gap (measured levers, in order)

A1 — Full-corpus training. The splits manifest carries 10,846 train shots (~75k examples at the current stride) vs the 2,000 used so far. The measured scaling law is the prior for this bet. Practicalities: corpus assembly is the wall-clock cost (~6 h at ~2 s/shot) — cache the assembled per-signature corpus arrays (npz beside the interaction-matrix caches, keyed by signature + stride + window config) so both head-arm reruns and later experiments pay it once.
Gate A1: encoder gate re-run; scaling point recorded (examples vs axis median, in/cross-signature); target in-signature axis skill > 0 (parity with the training-free P3 bar is the stretch mark at ~2.8 cm).

A2 — Per-batch geometry binding. Bind sensor/coil geometry per batch rather than at construction so every signature trains (the S=81 signature's 3,795 examples currently drop). This is also the architectural prerequisite for co-training a second machine (§5).
Gate A2: all-signature training runs; cross-signature gate metrics improve or hold; tests green.

A3 — Gaussian patch head. Mean + log-σ head in the dimensionless x-space, trained with the same losses under a Gaussian NLL wrapper on the sensor misfit; delivers the per-quantity calibration the P4 gate recorded as N/A.
Gate A3: held-out coverage in [0.88, 0.92] band reported per quantity; no regression of the mean-readout skills.

A4 — LCFS boundary-read robustification. The spurious-saddle pathology is a readout problem on free-current ψ. Candidates, each measured and kept only if load-bearing (the P5 discipline): current-smoothness soft rung (classic tomography regulariser — Blum et al.), saddle-persistence filtering in the boundary read, or reading the boundary from the smoothed core-component ψ only.
Gate A4: LCFS skill on the P3 winner-config inverse improves from −6 without degrading the axis gate; verdict recorded either way.

§4 — Workstream B: wire the equilibrium layer into the world model

B1 — Structure residual + patch forward into the stage-2 engine loss family. GSGroundedLatentEngine.losses() (in imas_ambix/latent/engine.py) gains the patch observation path (I → Msens prediction vs RAW magnetics) and the structure residual as additive soft terms, replacing the low-DOF polynomial θ path as the ψ carrier; the flux-diffusion temporal anchor consumes patch-ψ through the already-shipped imas_ambix/latent/patch_transport.py glue. This realises, on the current-basis representation, exactly the spatial+temporal anchor pair the driving plan machine-agnostic-latent-world-model §6/§7a/§8 specifies.
Gate B1: engine trains with the patch anchors; GS-residual grounding on the learned latent ≥ the 5.53× bar; D≥0 and command-load-bearing checks stay green.

B2 — Measure f-malwm-02 done-when #2 properly. The stage-2 bar (“GS-readout axis/X-point/boundary skill matches the absolute-magnetics oracle ~0.5–0.7”) has never been measured on the plan's actual design — a learned hybrid latent trained on the corpus. With B1 the readout is the patch-ψ topology read.
Gate B2: per-quantity skills on held-out reported against the 0.5–0.7 bar; whatever the number, it closes the measurement debt recorded on machine-agnostic-latent-world-model f-malwm-02 and gs-grounded-latent-engine f-ggl-02.

B3 — Closures as dimensionless latent coordinates. The per-bin (ak, bk) profiles (with uncertainties) are machine-portable physics coordinates. Feed them as auxiliary latent content/readout targets — the concrete realisation of the open dimensionless-coordinate-set decision on machine-agnostic-latent-world-model (soft-learned arm), and the “coefficients and closures recovered from data” goal of the North Star physics-world-model-strategy.
Gate B3: closure readout from the latent matches the per-slice regression closures within their 1σ on held-out flat-top; F²≥0 rate reported.

B4 — Equilibrium layer as the filter's observation operator (stage 3). The batched linear map I → sensors (fp32, 694k slices/s) is an ideal EnKF observation operator: the stage-3 LAE-EnKF correction (machine-agnostic-latent-world-model f-malwm-03) can correct the latent against raw magnetics THROUGH the equilibrium layer, making the filter's innovation physically interpretable (which flux structure the data rejected).
Gate B4: design note + prototype hook in the stage-3 child plan (closed-loop-latent-filter when it opens); no gate number here — this item hands architecture, not results.

§5 — Workstream C: cross-machine ingestion (the North-Star transfer path)

The measured gap list (patch-current-force-balance P5): geometry readers are MAST-Zarr-hardcoded (read_efm_geometry); R0/minor-radius are baked constants; no IMAS static-IDS machine-description ingestion exists; campaign signatures are not machine-tagged. Only gs/cylinder.py ports as-is. Deliverables: a GeometryTable-producing reader interface; an IMAS pf_active/wall/magnetics static-IDS reader (imas-python, DD-version-pinned per the data-access rules); machine-tagged signatures. Acceptance is the plan-level property the North Star names: a second machine's table drops in with zero solver-code change — the substrate is already demonstrated geometry-table-driven (synthetic-machine CI) and the encoder cross-signature transfer is measured; this workstream turns that into a real second device and feeds machine-agnostic-latent-world-model f-malwm-06 (cross-machine transfer).
Gate C: ITER (or JET) machine description ingested to a GeometryTable; PatchBasis assembles; vacuum-field sanity checks pass; no MAST-specific code touched.
