§1 — Overview: the reanalysis engine, the firewall, and the MSE gate

End-goal (GenCast/ERA5). GenCast is trustworthy because ERA5 is, and ERA5 is trustworthy because its assimilating model carries all confident atmospheric physics. Our learned cross-machine operator (the GenCast) will be trained on the equilibrium labels this engine produces (the ERA5). The labels must therefore be (a) physically valid — the free-boundary solve must genuinely hold real equilibria — and (b) firewall-clean — only physics and direct measurement, never EFIT's assumptions — so the operator learns physics it can transfer across machines, not one code's per-shot beliefs. This plan builds and validates that engine; the learned operator (which this plan blocks) resumes once the labels are proven.

Two findings from the equilibrium-realism diagnosis set the whole design (full record: the equilibrium-realism §3 landing + this plan's prototypes):

  • The internal profile is externally invisible per slice. Given the coils + Rogowski Ip alone, the current CENTROID is roughly pinned but the magnetic axis is Shafranov-shifted by the profile peakedness (β_p + l_i/2), which single-slice magnetics cannot resolve — the interior null. A profile we cannot distinguish must never be passed as a label (it would train the operator on our guess, the exact poison the firewall exists to stop).
  • The free-boundary solve drifts outboard for a real physical reason. The measured coil field is radially stable at the physical axis (decay index n ≈ 0.07–0.12 at R ≈ 0.85, inside the 0 < n < 3/2 window) but n climbs with R and crosses 3/2 at R ≈ 1.34 m, giving a genuine radially-unstable band (n > 3/2) at R ≈ 1.34–1.68 m — right at the in-vessel P4/P5/P6 coils. An unpinned free profile slides out through that band and pins against the outer coils (R ≈ 1.72). The physical inboard equilibrium exists; the free solve simply does not select it without position control — which the real machine supplied.

The binding gate moves to held-out MSE (lead decision, 2026-07-19). The internal profile is not fabricated and not read off the EFIT axis (an interior null — the temporal-physics-spine thesis closed on it precisely because the axis is the one thing magnetics cannot see). It is earned from the physics that genuinely carries it — time / dynamics (resistive current diffusion) + position constraints + GS force balance + SOL-current freedom — and proven against the locked, leakage-audited 112-shot held-out MSE pitch split (statespace/mse_eval.py, mse_split.py, eval/prediction_bar.py). The bar is real and already partly won in classical form: the EnKF ensemble smoother over TORAX current diffusion reaches pitch RMSE 0.225 rad (CI 0.199–0.259) vs a persistence floor of 0.719, with a pre-registered physics frontier at 0.19 and a coverage gate ≥ 0.88 — proof the dynamics DO carry recoverable internal information when scored where it lives. MSE is held out of every fit; it validates the labels, it never trains them.

The magic sauce. The learned operator's job is to beat the classical EnKF (0.225) toward the frontier (0.19) by folding in the disparate high-frequency / high-bandwidth channels that EnKF, TORAX, and EFIT do not treat — fast cameras, fast magnetics, and the other fast streams already tokenised in the corpus — as optional inputs. That is the capability conventional reconstruction cannot match and the reason the learned operator exists.

The firewall — the discriminating principle. The risk is rebuilding EFIT in all but name by incrementally folding in beliefs. The guard is not "how much" but "what kind": fold in only physical laws and direct measurements; never assumed profiles, tuned beliefs, or EFIT outputs.

Why this before world-model training. Training the operator now — on a spine whose forward solve did not hold real equilibria and whose failure was not understood — would bake the pathology into the labels. The order is deliberate: understand the mechanism (done) → make the free-boundary solve genuinely hold real equilibria, firewall-clean (§2) → earn the internal profile from dynamics and prove it on held-out MSE (§3–§4) → then train the operator on clean labels (§5).

§ Decisions

What is the binding validation gate for internal-profile recovery?

Locked by the lead (2026-07-19): without internal measurements the profile cannot be distinguished, so the axis is an interior null — the temporal-physics-spine thesis closed against it for that reason. MSE pitch is the direct internal-field measurement, with locked baselines (persistence 0.719, EnKF 0.225, frontier 0.19) already built (statespace/mse_eval + eval/prediction_bar). The gate proves profile recovery is real physics, never a fabricated label.

May the label factory assert an internal profile the single-slice data cannot distinguish?

Locked by the lead (2026-07-19): "passing made-up ones as labels would hurt our training process, we should not do this." A profile the data cannot distinguish is a prior belief, not a measurement; shipping it teaches the operator our guess. The label carries only what is measured or MSE-validated.

How is the free-boundary solve made well-posed (held on the physical branch)?

Locked by the lead (proposed 2026-07-19) and prototype-validated this session: the current centroid is a direct magnetic moment (physics, not EFIT); constraining R+Z holds the current inboard against the n>3/2 coil band and recovers the reconstruction axis to a few cm from Ip+centroid alone (11766 0.781 vs 0.820; 11772 1.027 vs 1.025). It is faithful to the real machine's radial+vertical position control. Not full-magnetics (that is the reconstruction; this is the minimal firewall-safe position lever), not EFIT, not a z-pin.

What may be folded into the reanalysis engine without becoming EFIT-in-all-but-name?

Locked by the lead (2026-07-19): "physics is physics ... if we keep folding more stuff in then we could go too far down the EFIT route in all but name." The discriminating test is kind, not amount: fold in physical laws and direct measurements; never EFIT outputs, assumed profiles, or tuned gains.

May EFIT quantities enter any fit/tune/train path?

Carried from equilibrium-realism: EFIT appears in no fit/tune/train path. Held-out MSE is the physical validator; EFIT stays a diagnostic cross-check only.

How far up the current-moment ladder does the firewall-safe solve go?

Raised at the §3 ship (2026-07-19): should the engine fold in 2nd+ current moments (quadrupole: ∫R²jφ, ∫RZ jφ, ∫Z²jφ → elongation/shape and the βp+li/2 combination) in addition to Ip (0th) and the R/Z centroid (1st)?

Locked by the lead (2026-07-19). Physics: current moments are a change of basis on the external magnetics ordered by observability. For §3's target (the interior profile) higher moments are null — the βp vs li separation (profile peakedness → axis Shafranov shift) produces NO external field signature at any moment order (external magnetics fix βp+li/2, never βp−li/2), so they cannot break the interior null; that is exactly why §3 earns the profile from DYNAMICS (resistive diffusion) and §4 proves it on held-out MSE. For shape, higher moments are genuine firewall-safe info but near-redundant with the already-landed source-free boundary read (best-in-class LCFS). The firewall danger the lead flagged is real at the limit — climbing the moment ladder until the magnetics are reconstructed as completely as EFIT invites profile-parameterisation creep; the discipline is to truncate at moments that are observable AND not already supplied by the boundary read. Net: for the interior it is 'just the magnetics in another form' with zero payoff; cap at the dipole.
