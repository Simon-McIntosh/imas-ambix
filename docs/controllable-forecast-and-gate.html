§1 — Objective & scope

Train the Nova-led closed-loop engine at scale on absolute, geometry-tagged magnetics, plan, interferometer, SXR, camera and fast-signal streams. Excitation curation makes coil and actuator commands identifiable. Observation-withholding trains the same components for causal forecast, causal analysis and non-causal smoothing. The output target is a sharp, calibrated joint ensemble of physical state and raw observations—not a deterministic mean and not a camera-only dream.

The scorecard separates scientific skill from compute: persistence, the current EnKF/EnKS baseline, unconditioned Nova ensembles and any direct learned challenger receive the same horizons, commands and held-out observations. Cross-machine and OOD strata are reported separately. EFIT remains a firewalled referee; independent physics parity belongs to Nova.

§2 — The gate (verifiable done-when)

  1. Three products, one model: forecast, analysis/reconstruction and fixed-lag/full-shot smoothing pass causality and provenance tests.
  2. Probabilistic forecast skill: CRPS or energy score, calibration, sharpness and transient-tail skill beat persistence and the current EnKF forecast bar at the declared 10/50/100/250 ms horizons; individual members remain physically valid and sharp.
  3. Powered controllability: on excited held-out windows, edited coil/actuator trajectories move the equilibrium and diagnostic ensemble more than same-plan stochastic spread, with a bootstrap interval clear of the floor.
  4. Raw held-out skill: predict signal families the model did not observe at the anchor, reporting per-modality scores and calibrated “I do not know” behavior where internal state is underdetermined.
  5. Generative products: publish equilibrium ensembles and diagnostic trajectories for nominal and counterfactual plans; camera streams are included as a conditional by-product and never used as the sole controllability claim.
  6. Transfer and scaling: report leave-one-machine-out behavior plus CPU, one-H200 and eight-H200 throughput/parity. Eight-card speedup cannot compensate for a skill or physics failure.

§3 — Approach

  1. Excitation-window selector: score windows by actuator excitation (|dI/dt|, coil ramps, plan changes); curate / re-weight the training distribution so the command is necessary, breaking the causal-under-identification confound that defeated the flat-top-dominated prior runs.
  2. Full multi-modal DDP training of the closed-loop engine; observation-withholding schedule; frequent checkpoints + clean SIGTERM (resume-safe; request all free cards — all 8 while DSv4 is down — per AGENTS.md).
  3. Controllability gate: the powered n=10 ΔN-M on the latent (not brightness), on excited held-out windows; collapse-rejecting; bootstrap CI.
  4. Raw-held-out eval: predict withheld raw observables (held-out probes; MSE on beam-on slices) vs persistence + the EnKF bar; the firewalled EFIT referee as a weak agreement cross-check only.
