§1 — The charge, and the one-screen answers

Commissioned by the lead 2026-07-10 as a deep research exercise against the North Star (physics-world-model-strategy, machine-agnostic-latent-world-model) and the full recent evidence base (grounding review, priors hardening, closure and calibration arms). The framing the lead supplied is adopted here as the organizing thesis: this line of work is the project's physics spine, and its load-bearing realization is that physics is physics — it does not need to be rediscovered. The spine solves known structure in combination with large amounts of experimental data to discover the coefficients the structure leaves free, and — through its residuals — the terms the structure is missing. Two strategic questions were posed:

  • Q1 — rotation. The proof of concept uses static GS with p′ and FF′ terms. On compact machines rotation terms are known to be strong. Do we extend force balance to include them?
  • Q2 — representation and regularization. We currently solve forward models for given current distributions; the dimensionality of admissible current distributions is very high and the separatrix makes the target 2-D field sharply discontinuous. The alternative — parametrizing a flux map — must keep the map source-free (GS operator = 0) where there is no current and keep the GS coefficients constant along flux surfaces where there is. Current-first with a constancy penalty, or flux-first with a conservation difficulty — and how do we regularize either to shrink the search space?

Answer to Q2 (representation). The dichotomy is false: both horns describe penalty formulations of constraints that the profile-closure representation through the free-boundary GS fixed point satisfies exactly, by construction. Parametrize the low-DOF flux functions p′(ψN), FF′(ψN) (plus known coil currents and a structured nuisance layer) and obtain both jφ and ψ from the self-consistent solve: coefficient constancy on surfaces holds because the coefficients are functions of ψN; Δ*ψ = 0 holds exactly in the vacuum region because the support is inside the LCFS of the solved ψ; and the separatrix discontinuity is never represented in the search space — it emerges from the smooth ψ level-set, which is the classic interface-problem resolution (ψ is its own level-set function). Dimensionality collapses from ~4,787 per-cell currents against ~73 sensors (a ~4,700-dimensional null space that soft priors must span) to 2–10 profile DOF that the same sensors over-determine. This is not speculative: it is the measured convergence point of the project's own experiment sequence (§2, §4), and the freshest artifacts — the closure-arm cost sweep and today's offset-only calibrated gates — supply the first positive LCFS and first CI-positive X-point skills the project has produced. The two other representations keep precisely scoped jobs: the free current basis as measurement instrument and nuisance carrier (it is the instrument that measured the 0.75 Ip static error), and the flux-first representation exactly where its constraint is exactly satisfiable — the source-free vacuum annulus (the still-unbuilt f-etf-04 toroidal-harmonic read). Regularization then stops being generic penalty-tuning and becomes information-budget matching (§4.3).

Answer to Q1 (rotation). Yes — architecturally now, numerically staged. The rotation extension is the ideal first traversal of the spine's discovery loop because its form is exact, known physics (Maschke–Perrin: with toroidal rotation, p is no longer a flux function; R·jφ gains a centrifugal R⁴ design column with coefficient c(ψ) ≈ ½p₀·(miΩ²/T)′), while its coefficient is exactly one new flux function — γ(ψ) = miΩ²/2T ≥ 0, sign-definite like the other spine priors. MAST is the machine on which neglecting rotation is least defensible and where the term is most identifiable: NBI-driven thermal Mach numbers reach M₀ ≈ 0.5–1 (outboard/inboard pressure ratios of 1.5–4 on outer surfaces — leading-order, not a correction), and the ST's wide relative radial span makes the R⁴ column ~15% orthogonal to the static design (vs ~7% at conventional aspect ratio) — figure in §5. The residual-side slot already exists in-tree (structure_residual.py form affine-r2-rotation) but is wired nowhere and has never been measured. The staging that keeps the spine honest: (A) wire the residual diagnostic; (B) a measured-rotation falsifiable test — fix γ(ψ) from CXRS rotation + Thomson temperature (both in the corpus; act on 7,991 shots) with zero new free parameters and test whether held-out misfit and topology skill improve; (C) free-coefficient discovery mode, gated on a synthetic identifiability probe. All three sequence after the static-calibration story, which this week collapsed the misfit floor ~7× — rotation-scale shaping was unmeasurable under a 0.2 whitened-misfit floor and is becoming measurable now.

§2 — Where the evidence stands (including this morning's runs)

The equilibrium line's experiment sequence, re-read end-to-end for this review, is a textbook arc — each swing measured honestly, each reversal evidence-forced:

  • Profile-ansatz fixed point, first attempt (gs-grounded-latent-engine): the linear low-DOF current inverse was shown structurally incapable of an interior O-point (measured effective rank of the magnetics ≈ 5–6; localisation is inherently nonlinear), locking ψ-decoder-for-topology to the free-boundary GS solve — but plain Picard paid a convergence tax (83/160 scored; 12/160 after the 2π source-scale fix) and the per-slice fit was fragile.
  • The patch-current pivot (patch-current-force-balance): exact Maxwell interaction matrices (694k slices/s), force balance as a soft structure residual, no Picard, no topology extraction in the loop. It delivered the axis at referee grade (2.6–2.8 cm median, 160/160) and the eval spine — and then the grounding review showed the price: ~73 sensors against ~4,787 free cells leaves a null space that the soft physics term is structurally blind to (j²-weighted, sign-agnostic, support-blind, self-normalised — review register B2/B14). The null space was filled by optimiser defaults: a 0.75 Ip anti-parallel halo and corner lobes, current where no plasma is.
  • Priors hardening (plasma-current-priors-hardening, shipped 2026-07-10): R1 (sign) and R2 (support) measured the fill — 0.75 Ip anti-parallel, 1.01 Ip outside-LCFS — and R2 proved load-bearing for topology (axis median 6.4→3.1 cm, X-point-set skill −1.69→+0.14, first positive). But misfit tripled when the halo was forbidden: the halo absorbs real unmodelled signal, shown phase-independent (flat-top 0.715 vs rampup 0.754) — a static coil-model/calibration error, not eddies. And nothing shapes jφ within its allowed support, so the LCFS under-sizes (−4.91→−7.57): the missing ingredient is exactly EFIT's low-DOF p′/FF′ closure as the solve variable (plan §6.4).
  • The closure arm (commits 657e1d8→0062509, artifacts closure_gate_eval.json): the (β0, α) two-parameter profile family fit through the free-boundary fixed point on the hardened harness now converges on 160/160 held-out slices (bootstrapped two-stage solve; the Picard tax is paid off). Quality is cost-gated and — uncalibrated — the misfit floor dominates (cost median 12.2). The signal is in the cost sweep: at cost ≤ 3 (11 slices) axis skill +0.022 @ 6.4 cm; at cost ≤ 1 (6 slices) axis skill +0.34 [0.32, 0.38] @ 2.6 cm and LCFS skill +0.12 [0.10, 0.15] — the first positive LCFS skill anywhere in the project. Where the representation is allowed to fit the sensors well, it beats everything else the project has tried, including on the quantity (LCFS) that every other arm loses.
  • The static-calibration arm (commits a8a2b83→832e2a9, this morning's artifacts): a per-channel offset-only nuisance model, fit on 10 train shots against the frozen priors-arm winner and applied held-out (|offset|/σ median 0.10), collapses the free-arm misfit 0.049 → 0.0074 (~7×), halves the anti-parallel halo (0.72 → 0.33 median) and cuts outside-LCFS current (≈1.0 → 0.45 Ip). On the priors arm it yields axis error median 3.8 cm and X-point-set skill +0.29 [0.03, 0.41] — the first topology skill with a CI clear of zero. LCFS ray-casts remain negative (−7.2; offset ~31 cm): shaping within the support is still missing, which is the closure arm's job, not calibration's. The calibrated closure gate — the experiment where these two fixes compound — is in flight on a debug node as this review is written (_closure_cal.log; the working tree carries its uncommitted driver edits).

Read as one sequence: every hard-won result confirms the same lesson — constraints enforced by construction carry; constraints enforced by penalty leak in exactly the null space where they are needed most; and free representations are superb instruments precisely because they do not carry. That lesson, generalized, is the spine architecture of §3.

§3 — The physics spine, formalized: three tiers and a discovery loop

"Physics is physics" becomes an executable architecture when every quantity in the model is placed in one of three tiers by how well its physics is known — and the placement dictates the enforcement mechanism. The failure modes documented in the grounding review are, without exception, tier violations: exact physics enforced softly (force-balance structure as a penalty), or unknown quantities granted load-bearing freedom (the null-space fill).

Tier 1 — exact structure, imposed by construction. Maxwell/Green's-function maps (already exact in the patch substrate — Ampère and div B = 0 are identities there), the free-boundary GS fixed point Δ*ψ = −μ₀R·jφ(ψN, R) solved rather than penalised, the force-balance form including its known extensions (the rotation column of §5), Δ*ψ = 0 in vacuum, conductor geometry, and the sign facts: sign(Ip) from the Rogowski, F² ≥ 0 integrability, η∥ > 0 ⇒ D ≥ 0 parabolicity (the arrow of time, §7a of the driving plan), γ(ψ) ≥ 0 (rotation). Nothing in this tier has a tunable weight — that is the point. It is also what transfers: Tier 1 is machine-agnostic by construction.

Tier 2 — structured unknowns, coefficients from data. The profile closures p₀′(ψN), FF′(ψN) (today a 2-DOF (β0, α) family; a gated ladder toward ~10 spline DOF), the rotation coefficient γ(ψ), the static instrument model (per-channel offset/gain; coil geometry/turns audit), passive-conductor eigenmodes (genuinely not flux functions — currents in known conductor support, rank-k), the budgeted halo/SOL current, and — on the dynamic side — the transport closures (η∥ correction, Zeff, bootstrap multiplier) that learned-closure-current-diffusion-v0 already treats in exactly this pattern ("go through the physics, not around it"). This tier is where "large amounts of experimental data" enter: coefficients are amortised across the corpus by the encoder, and the corpus-level distribution over coefficients is the learned equilibrium prior of equilibrium-topology-fidelity thread 4 — hierarchical statistical sharing, not per-slice fitting.

Tier 3 — residual instruments, never load-bearing. The free per-cell basis, the structure residual's per-bin unexplained fraction and its within-surface R-shape, the GS and flux-diffusion residual fields, and the latent's free block. Their proven role is measurement: the free inverse is the instrument that measured the 0.75 Ip static discrepancy that closed-form codes silently absorb, and the North Star's residual-as-discovery contract (§5 of the strategy) lives here. The single discipline that §2's history demands: Tier-3 objects never appear in the solve loop — they are projections of what Tiers 1–2 leave unexplained.

The discovery loop closes the lead's brief — "discover both the underlying equations and their coefficients." Discovering coefficients is Tier 2's day job. Discovering equations is the promotion path: a persistent, structured Tier-3 residual (e.g. a systematic within-surface R-dependence the affine law cannot absorb) nominates a candidate term from the known-physics dictionary (rotation R⁴, anisotropy, a passive mode, a calibration term); the candidate must pass a synthetic-truth identifiability probe (can this term be recovered at all, from these sensors, at this noise?) and then a gated held-out measurement with CIs before it earns a Tier-2 coefficient — the P5 keep-only-if-load-bearing discipline, now applied to physics terms rather than regulariser rungs. A term that fails stays a recorded negative. This is SINDy-with-a-physics-dictionary rather than free-form regression, and it is the concrete mechanism behind the North Star's promise that structure discovery "rides on top" of the grounded model.

§4 — Q2 answered: representation and regularization

§4.1 — The trade-space, measured

The two proposed routes, assessed against §2's evidence:

  • Current-first with a constancy penalty is the measured status quo. Its strengths are real: the forward map is linear and exact (Green's superposition), coils and passives enter naturally, and the basis can represent anything — including the separatrix jump, trivially. Its measured weakness is structural, not a tuning failure: with ~4,700 null-space dimensions, everything rests on the penalties, and the review proved the flagship penalty blind (and locally even attractive) to the very pathologies it was assumed to prevent. R1/R2 hardened the sign and support of the fill; nothing shapes the distribution within the support, and the LCFS pays for it. The route's honest end-state is already visible: an ever-taller ladder of soft rungs approximating, term by term, what the fixed point gives exactly.
  • Flux-first over the full domain inverts the difficulty rather than removing it: a free ψ(R,Z) must be penalised into Δ*ψ = 0 in an a-priori-unknown vacuum region, penalised into coefficient constancy inside, and — a subtlety the in-vessel coil work made concrete — handled carefully wherever coils pierce the domain (their field is not Δ*-harmonic at the source; the analytic-add split exists precisely because of this). The feasible set is the same thin manifold, approached from the other side, with the added risk that small ψ errors differentiate into large j errors. Where flux-first is exactly right is the source-free vacuum annulus: the toroidal-harmonic expansion (R3 / f-etf-04, locked-then-deferred twice, still unbuilt) is conservative by construction there and reads the boundary/X-point with no interior commitment at all. Build it as the representation-side complement — not as the state carrier.
  • Profile-closure through the fixed point — the third route the dichotomy hides — is where both constraints stop being constraints: they are consequences of the parametrization. The separatrix worry inverts likewise: jφ = ĵ(ψN, R)·𝟙[inside LCFS(ψ)] is discontinuous, but the search space (profile coefficients) is smooth and low-dimensional; the discontinuity is derived, never fought. The measured objections to the fixed point — the Picard convergence tax and per-slice fragility — have been retired by the closure arm's bootstrapped two-stage solve (160/160 convergence) and are further addressed by amortization: the encoder predicts profile coefficients, the solve refines, and a differentiable fixed point (implicit-function-theorem gradients) makes the whole spine trainable end-to-end.

Recommended architecture — a hierarchy, not a winner-takes-all: Tier-1 carrier: profile-closure free-boundary solve (the equilibrium state the world model's latent maps to); Tier-2 sidecar: structured nuisance currents that genuinely are not flux functions — passive-conductor eigenmodes on known support (the unwired g_passive block), the budgeted halo/SOL term, the static calibration model; Tier-3 audit: the free patch basis run outside the solve as a residual projector, retaining the discovery channel that found the static error; annulus read: the f-etf-04 harmonic expansion as the interior-free boundary cross-check. The patch substrate is not discarded — its exact interaction matrices are precisely how the fixed point's Green's functions, the nuisance modes, and the audit projector are all evaluated.

§4.3 — Regularization = information-budget matching

"How do we regularize to reduce the dimensionality of the search space" has a quantitative answer once the representation is right, and it is not "add penalties":

  • Match parametrization DOF to measured information. The magnetics carry an effective rank of ~5–6. A per-slice parametrization above that is under-determined by measurement, not by opinion. The profile ladder therefore starts at 2 DOF (β0, α — below budget, currently over-regularized: the LCFS deficit partly reflects family rigidity) and adds knots/DOF one at a time, each addition gated on held-out evidence (misfit + topology skill with CIs), never on train-cohort preference. Two cautionary tales the CI-gated ladder exists to prevent: the moment fit (order 3 selected on a tune cohort where every order lost, then inverting on eval) and the support-prior sweep (train-split winner at axis RMSE-skill ≈ −0.06, held-out −2.06 — the thin 4-shot tune cohort selects noisily, and only the held-out gate catches it).
  • Grow information before growing DOF. The interior profile is genuinely under-determined from magnetics alone (the standing honest negative since S8). The principled way to afford more profile DOF is more information: interior diagnostics (equilibrium-topology-fidelity thread 2 — interferometer/SXR/Thomson/MSE, and CXRS for §5), and time — temporal coherence via the flux-diffusion transport prior ties adjacent slices together, so a shot's worth of slices collectively constrains a slowly-evolving profile far better than any slice alone (thread 5, and the §7a temporal anchor, put to work as a regularizer).
  • Amortize across the corpus. Fitting each slice independently wastes the strongest regularizer available: the corpus. The encoder that predicts Tier-2 coefficients is a hierarchical prior learned from ~10⁴ shots; the per-slice solve becomes a refinement, and coefficient distributions across the corpus become the learned equilibrium prior (thread 4) and the discovery dataset (W4 of the follow-on plan). The flat-MLP amortization negative (49/160 @ 49 cm) does not contradict this — it measured the wrong encoder (no geometry, imputed features, sparse targets); the oracle-recipe encoder remains the design, and a 2–10-DOF smooth target is a far easier amortization problem than 4,787 patch currents.
  • Keep the few remaining penalties honest. Where soft terms remain (halo budget, profile smoothness at higher DOF), fix the measured defects: the structure residual's self-normalisation (B14) can actively reward isolated blobs, and coefficient_smoothness_penalty exists but is wired nowhere. Every rung keeps the P5 keep-only-if-load-bearing discipline with CIs.

§5 — Q1 answered: the rotation term

The physics is known; only one flux function is not. The generalized force balance with rigid toroidal rotation Ω(ψ) (Maschke–Perrin; the same extension NIMEQ, HELENA and EFIT-with-flow use) keeps the GS form with p → p(ψ,R); expanding to leading order in the centrifugal exponent, R·jφ ≈ a(ψ)R² + b(ψ) + c(ψ)R⁴ with a = p₀′, b = FF′/μ₀ and c ≈ ½p₀·(miΩ²/T)′. The new object is the single flux function γ(ψ) = miΩ²/2T ≥ 0 — sign-definite, so it enters Tier 1 as a form and Tier 2 as one more coefficient function, in exactly the same pattern as p₀′ and FF′. Remarkably, the derivation and the three-column design matrix already sit in the codebase (structure_residual.py, form affine-r2-rotation, with precisely this docstring) — prototyped, wired into no live path, never measured. The strategic question is therefore not "do we build rotation support" but "when do we switch on what exists, and with what gates".

Why it matters here. Three reasons, beyond generic completeness. (i) Magnitude: MAST's low field and strong beams make it one of the fastest-rotating tokamaks per unit thermal speed; ignoring a factor-2 in-out pressure asymmetry while fitting p₀′ guarantees that the recovered "pressure gradient" is an effective, rotation-contaminated object — fine for boundary readout, poisonous for the discovery claims the North Star makes about recovered closures. (ii) Aliasing: a missing R⁴ term aliases into the fitted a(ψ), b(ψ) and into the misfit floor; with the static calibration collapse (§2) the residual budget is now small enough that percent-level shaping terms stop being noise. (iii) Machine-agnosticism: the term's form is universal; its coefficient is per-shot data. A spine that carries the rotation slot transfers to JET/ITER (where it is smaller but nonzero) with nothing to retrain — the exact robust-structure/data-led-closure split of North Star §6.

The staged, falsifiable path (recommended):

  • Stage A — residual diagnostic (cheap, near-zero risk). Wire affine-r2-rotation as a reporting arm of the gate: on the held-out cohort, does the three-column fit reduce the per-bin unexplained fraction, and is the recovered c(ψ) coherent (one-signed where p₀γ is peaked, larger on high-rotation shots)? No solver change; the output is a measured yes/no on whether rotation is visible in our data at all. This is the discovery loop's step 1→2 executed on a known term.
  • Stage B — measured-rotation forward test (zero new DOF, falsifiable). Build γ(ψN) from measured CXRS toroidal rotation (act, ~5 ms cadence, 7,991 shots) and Thomson T (ayc); extend the closure-arm ansatz jφ(ψN, R) with the fixed centrifugal factor; re-run the closure gate. Every input is a raw measurement (firewall-clean); the term adds no free parameters; the prediction is sharp — held-out misfit and topology skill improve on high-rotation shots, or the term is not load-bearing at our current error budget and the negative is recorded. This is the cleanest possible first traversal of the promotion gate.
  • Stage C — discovery mode (gated). Free γ(ψ) as a Tier-2 coefficient function (γ ≥ 0 by softplus, like every other sign prior), identifiability first demonstrated on the synthetic harness (recover a known γ from synthetic magnetics ± interior data at realistic noise). Pairs naturally with thread 2's SXR fusion — in-out emissivity asymmetry is the classic rotation observable and breaks the magnetics-only degeneracy.
  • Sequencing lock: all stages sit behind the calibrated-closure milestone (the in-flight run + its verdicts). Under a 0.2 misfit floor the term was unmeasurable; under ~0.01–0.04 it is in reach. Do not spend the rotation experiment against an instrument-error budget.
  • Dynamics counterpart (future slot, not now): toroidal momentum balance — d(angular momentum)/dt = NBI torque (in the plan/pulse-schedule) − losses, with sign-definite loss — is the rotation analog of the Volt-second budget: one more inequality guard-rail for the transition kernel when rotation joins the latent state. Register the slot; build nothing yet.

§6 — Critique of the work so far: the gap register

What a hostile-but-fair reviewer would say about the line to date. Each gap carries its evidence and its owner. The overall verdict first: the honesty spine is exemplary — firewalled referee, leakage-free splits, CIs after the hardening, negatives recorded as first-class results — and it is exactly why this critique can be specific. The gaps are concentrated in (a) constraints enforced at the wrong tier, (b) built-but-unwired machinery, and (c) missing synthetic-truth instrumentation.

  • G1 — The static instrument error is found but not yet attributed. The offset-only nuisance arm absorbs it empirically (misfit ~7× down) but is fit against one inverse arm's residuals — a statistical calibration, not an audited coil model. The held-out arm sweep already adjudicates the gain question's direction: applying the full gain+offset fit (gains p10–p90 0.90–1.14) crashes the prior arms held-out (softplus arm axis skill −0.05 → −8.9; winner arm −2.0 → −7.9) while offset-only leaves them intact — the free per-channel gains demonstrably launder model/inverse error rather than measure the instrument, which is why offset-only shipped as the arm (commit 832e2a9). Remaining risks: circularity (offsets fit against the priors-arm inverse could still absorb genuine physics), and any real gain error is now unmodelled. Needs: independent validation on coil-only/vacuum intervals (no plasma current — the predicted field is pure Tier 1), cross-validation across arms, and the physical audit (turns/geometry/circuit mapping, f-pcph-04).
  • G2 — LCFS skill is negative in every arm (best: closure-arm cost≤1 subset, +0.12 on 6 slices). The profile-shaping fix is identified and in flight, but until the calibrated closure gate reads positive at meaningful coverage, "beat EFIT on the boundary" remains an ambition with no positive-skill demonstration. The 2-DOF grid (5×3 candidates) is also visibly too coarse — no vertical position freedom, no continuous optimization, no per-shot warm-starting.
  • G3 — Penalty-tier defects are diagnosed but not fixed. The structure residual's self-normalisation can actively reward isolated blobs (B14) — it remains the live P3-winner term. R1/R2 remain OFF by default (correctly, per gates), so production inverses still carry the halo.
  • G4 — Built-but-unwired machinery accumulates. coefficient_smoothness_penalty (wired nowhere), g_passive (never in the inverse), edge_taper_penalty (deliberately unwired), affine-r2-rotation (never run). Each represents a decision made in code but never measured — the inventory in priors-hardening §6.3 should become a standing table with a measured/unmeasured column.
  • G5 — No synthetic-truth identifiability harness. The null-space fill was caught by the lead's eyeball on a figure, not by a test. Manufactured equilibria with known p′/FF′ (± rotation, ± calibration corruption, ± passive currents) pushed through the full inverse→gate chain would have flagged it years-of-agent-time earlier, and are the cheap gate every future term promotion needs. gs_solve has analytic solver tests; the end-to-end recovery suite does not exist.
  • G6 — No per-slice uncertainty or evidence-based model selection. CIs exist at gate level (bootstrap over shots); nothing propagates uncertainty onto profile coefficients or chooses DOF by evidence. The moment-order episode showed what hand-picked complexity does. The Bayesian machinery the project already committed to (calibrated belief, EnKF) should extend to the closure coefficients.
  • G7 — f-etf-04 (toroidal-harmonic annulus read) remains unbuilt after being locked, deferred, and re-specified twice. It is the only representation whose vacuum constraint is exact and interior-free — the natural cross-check for every boundary claim the spine will make.
  • G8 — Rotation is absent from every forward model on the machine where it is largest. §5. Until Stage A/B run, all recovered p₀′ are rotation-contaminated effective profiles, and the discovery-layer claims inherit that caveat silently.
  • G9 — Session hygiene around in-flight physics runs. This morning's calibration work left uncommitted worktree edits (scripts/closure_gate_eval.py --calibration support; regenerated gs_operator_summary.json under case-circuits-v2) while a SLURM job consuming that code runs — the exact editable-install contamination mode the workspace rules warn about. Commit promptly and pin configs for in-flight jobs.
  • G10 — Statics and dynamics are still separate spines. The flux-diffusion transport prior (D ≥ 0) lives in the engine; the learned-closure current-diffusion plan (M3) carries the 1-D dynamic closures; the equilibrium closure coefficients evolve slice-by-slice with no temporal tie. Temporal coherence is both physics (current diffusion is slow) and the cheapest large regularizer (§4.3); the spine should couple them at the coefficient level (β0, α, γ as slowly-evolving states in the filter).
  • G11 — Skill metrics are tail-fragile. Gate A's headline ("axis skill not CI-compatible") is RMSE-tail-driven while the median halved — the metric and the physics disagree. Report median-based or trimmed skills alongside RMSE-skills, and keep the closure arm's cost-threshold-sweep pattern (accuracy/coverage reported together) as the standard.
  • G12 — Amortization is unproven on the spine target. The flat-MLP negative is recorded; the oracle-recipe encoder → (β0, α, γ, calibration) prediction is the actual design and remains unmeasured. Until it lands, the spine is a per-slice method with corpus-scale ambitions.

§7 — Recommendations and the follow-on plan

Five recommendations, in dependency order, formalized as the new plan force-balance-spine:

  • W1 — Land the calibrated closure spine. Consume the in-flight calibrated closure gate; add continuous (β0, α) + vertical-position optimization and the CI-gated profile-DOF ladder; wire profile smoothness at higher DOF; then take the representation-default decision (closure-first carrier + patch audit + annulus read) on measured evidence.
  • W2 — Harden the nuisance layer. Independent validation of the static calibration (vacuum/coil-only intervals, cross-arm checks), the gain-arm audit, passive eigenmodes through g_passive, and the coil-model physical audit — retiring G1.
  • W3 — Build the synthetic-truth identifiability harness. Manufactured equilibria with known Tier-2 coefficients (± rotation ± calibration ± passives) through the full chain; recovery CIs; the standing gate for every term promotion — retiring G5 and gating W4.
  • W4 — Execute the rotation ladder. Stage A (residual diagnostic) immediately after W1; Stage B (measured-rotation, zero-DOF, falsifiable) behind the calibrated-closure milestone; Stage C (free γ, discovery mode) behind W3 — retiring G8 and exercising the discovery loop end-to-end.
  • W5 — Formalize the discovery loop. The term-dictionary registry (candidate form, identifiability status, gate verdict, promotion state), the trimmed/median skill additions, and the coefficient-evolution tie to the transport prior (with learned-closure-current-diffusion-v0) — retiring G4's drift and G10's split.

What this document does not recommend: abandoning the patch substrate (it is the spine's computational backbone and its audit instrument), hard topology priors (single-blob exclusions were rightly rejected — doublets stay representable through every recommendation here), or a full-domain flux-first pivot (its constraint burden is the current-first problem mirrored). The spine's identity — exact structure, data-led coefficients, residuals as discovery — is the projection of the North Star onto the equilibrium problem, and the evidence of §2 says it is working exactly where it has been tried.
