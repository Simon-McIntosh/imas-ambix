§1 — Thesis: the boundary and the interior are separate low-order problems

The force-balance-spine review reached a clean verdict: we had drifted into rebuilding EFIT. Chasing the LCFS ray-cast through a free-boundary Grad–Shafranov fixed point produced the outboard attractor, the reseed lever, and a −3.70 boundary bar that no coil-model correction moved — the same machine-specific topology tar pit that imas-efit (359 shipped plans, a Reeb/separatrix/wall-race apparatus) is still debugging. That is not our mission. Our North Star is a machine-agnostic, data-led world model, and the boundary is machine-dependent only through geometry — which belongs as a conditioning input, not as hand-coded per-machine topology.

The correction is to stop coupling two problems that are naturally separate:

  • The boundary is a property of the field OUTSIDE the plasma. There the poloidal flux is source-free (Δ\*ψ = 0); its eigenfunctions are the toroidal harmonics (half-integer Legendre ring functions). Fit the raw external magnetics + the known coil field + Ip + li to a truncated harmonic expansion and ψ is known throughout the vacuum annulus — the boundary is a contour, X-points are its saddles, limited-vs-diverted is a classification of that smooth field. No interior current, no EFIT. This is the classical result (Braams 1991; Alladio–Crisanti 1986) and it is what real-time codes do.
  • The interior is a fixed-boundary force-balance problem. Given the boundary ψ from the annulus read, solve Δ\*ψ = μ₀R·jφ(ψ) on the plasma domain with that Dirichlet condition — the well-posed, low-DOF reconstruction the spine already does well (axis 3.7 cm, effective rank ~1–2), minus the free-boundary attractor.

Do diverted and limited plasmas look different to magnetics? Yes. A diverted plasma carries an external X-point (a poloidal-field saddle) just outside itself that reshapes the external field; a limited one is set by wall tangency. Same Ip and gross shape → different external magnetics through that saddle. Ip and li break the residual field→boundary degeneracy. So a low-order boundary IS recoverable from low-order external moments — our own thread-1 moment read already recovered the X-point class held-out (−0.61 → −0.15); its LCFS was poor only because it reconstructed an interior current and re-imported the ambiguity. The source-free read removes that. The −3.70 was self-inflicted, not information-limited.

Re-scope the landed spine. The low-DOF flux-function reconstruction, the passive rank-8 sidecar, and the identifiability harness are kept — they are the machine-agnostic interior physics and are information-matched. What is removed is the free-boundary self-consistency: the interior solve takes the annulus-read boundary as a fixed Dirichlet condition and solves Δ\*ψ = μ₀R·jφ(ψ) on the plasma domain. This deletes the outboard attractor, the scout-certify recipe, and the reseed lever wholesale (they were artefacts of coupling the boundary into the interior fixed point). Re-gate the interior metrics (axis, interior flux) on the fixed-boundary solve; the boundary metrics now come from §2, not from the interior solve.

§4 — Endgame: a learned, machine-agnostic equilibrium operator (the GenCast analogy)

The physics-backed reads of §2–§3 are the baseline and the inductive bias, not the destination. The destination is one learned operator — raw sensors + coil/wall geometry (positional encoding) + Ip + li → boundary (and interior) — trained across machines so it transfers to unseen geometry, the way GenCast/ECMWF learned-weather models beat the numerical codes they were trained to emulate while generalizing. It is machine-agnostic by construction (all machine dependence lives in the geometry-encoding input) and never touches EFIT output (firewall). The toroidal-harmonic decomposition is the natural architectural prior; the classical annulus read is the baseline it must beat and the physics grounding that keeps it honest. This section is design/scoping now; training follows the cross-machine corpus (feeds machine-agnostic-latent-world-model).
