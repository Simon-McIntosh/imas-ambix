§1 — Objective: the dynamics get the same treatment as the statics

The static boundary problem was solved by building a physics spine — exact thick-cylinder Biot–Savart kernels + the GS flux-function structure, fit per slice, gated against frozen baselines — and only then letting a network correct its degenerate DOF. The temporal problem got the opposite treatment: the learned-equilibrium-operator R2 rung asked a causal transformer to discover the dynamics from sensor history, and it closed as a sustained negative after two clean tests (full record: §2 R2 record): the operator converts ~21% of whitened val sensor misfit, and with the topology anchors those gains come boundary-safe and spine-non-inferior — yet flat-top, ramp-up, axis, and the p′/FF′ split are all unmoved. The information the referee rewards was never in reach of the learner, because the two places it lives are both governed by known differential equations with measured drives:

  • Vessel-eddy transients obey the passive circuit equation L·dI/dt + R·I = −dΨ_ext/dt with the external flux swing computable from measured coil currents and the plasma current history through the exact cylinder kernels. The per-slice static passive fit is under-determined by construction (measured: th-boundary-robustness §18) — this is why ramp-ups sit at 5–10 cm.
  • Plasma current diffusion obeys the resistive flux-diffusion equation — the jφ profile at time t is not free; it evolves from the profile at t−Δt under a diffusion operator whose boundary conditions (surface loop voltage, total Ip) are measured and whose only unknown is the resistivity profile. The per-slice fit discards this entirely: successive profile coefficient fits are independent (the warm chain shares only an initial guess).

The lead's question — "do we repeat the same loop: reduced-order model first, then merge into the ML pipeline?" — is answered yes, and it is the whole plan. The R0→R1 pattern (deterministic physics spine → residual learner) is the methodology, not a one-off: build the reduced-order dynamics spine, drive it with measured histories, gate it against the frozen static spine on the standard referee harness with zero ML, and only then let a network correct the DOF the dynamics provably cannot pin (the resistivity profile, mode-resistance scales, un-modelled 3-D effects). If the deterministic dynamic spine cannot move the ramp-up gap, that is itself the decisive measurement: the temporal information is not in the dynamics we can model, and the ML rung was never going to find it.

§2 — What already exists (most of P1 is built)

  • The passive reduced-order model exists and is validated. build_passive_eigenbasis + physical_eddy_history (imas_ambix/latent/temporal_operator.py) already construct the L/R eigenmode reduction from pure geometry (taus 35.8→4.1 ms, exact-ZOH integration pinned by test against dense sub-stepping) and integrate it along a shot from measured coil currents + the plasma current history. Today its output is only a feature handed to the (now-closed) learned operator; the per-slice fit still solves for passive amplitudes as static unknowns behind a ridge. P1 promotes the trajectory from feature to constraint.
  • The per-slice fit machinery takes passive priors already. fit_and_read_slice carries the passive sidecar (passive_ridge); constraining the amplitudes toward a circuit-integrated trajectory is a prior-centre change, not new machinery.
  • Raw-cadence drives exist. The 1 kHz anchored streams (coil currents, flux loops, Rogowski) are in the corpus (load_shot_windows), so the circuit ODE can integrate on the raw cadence between labelled slices — removing the label-cadence approximation (a_phys[0] = 0, eddy state under-resolved exactly where ramp-up wins live) recorded in the R2 archive.
  • Synthetic truth generates exact temporal sequences. synthetic_truth.manufacture + the known-eddy sequence generator produce time-coupled equilibria with exact eddy histories and exact profiles — the P1/P2 verification substrate and P2's exact-recovery gate for the p′/FF′ split.
  • The referee harness is proven. n≥128 paired bootstrap, push-out reader, byte-exact frozen-spine reproduction, EFIT eval-only — two R2 gate evals ran it end-to-end without a defect.

P2 — current-diffusion temporal prior (TORAX-inspired, right-sized)

Between labelled slices, evolve the profile-coefficient state under a 1D flux-surface-averaged resistive diffusion operator on the spine's own ψ_N ladder basis: the diffusion structure, the measured surface loop voltage (flux loops), and the Rogowski Ip are exact physics and data; the resistivity profile η(ψ_N) is unmeasured (Thomson Te is gated — moment_priors.TE_DATA_GATE) and enters as the explicit low-DOF unknown — a bounded parametric profile, NOT a hidden nuisance. The prior enters the per-slice fit as a soft temporal consistency term on coefficient evolution (precedent: the locked soft-prior interior-boundary coupling — hard pinning injects model error as truth). What this buys that no static fit can: the flux-consumption budget (∫V_loop dt) constrains the p′/FF′ partition's evolution, and axis motion is tied to profile diffusion rather than free per-slice. Gate P2: on synthetic truth (exact η, exact profiles) the diffusion-constrained chain recovers the p′/FF′ split materially better than the independent-slice spine (bar: beat 0.120 median |err|); on real data, flat-top non-inferiority and axis median not degraded.

P3 — merge into the ML pipeline

Only after P1/P2 verdicts. The residual learner returns exactly as in R1: zero-initialised heads about the dynamic spine, corrections decoded through the same exact Green's layers, topology anchors retained (they are now proven degeneracy control), G2-class gates re-baselined against the dynamic spine. The network's target DOF are the ones P1/P2 deliberately left free: η(ψ_N), mode-resistance scales, and whatever structured residual the physics rungs' error analysis exposes. If P1/P2 both fail their gates, P3 does not run — the plan records that the modelled dynamics do not carry the missing information and the temporal thesis fails at the physics level, which is a far cleaner negative than R2's.

§4 — TORAX: what to take, what not to take

TORAX (cloned at ~/Code/torax, v1.0.x) is a JAX differentiable 1D core-transport simulator: coupled heat/particle/current-diffusion PDEs, FVM discretisation, Sauter neoclassical conductivity + bootstrap, poloidal-flux BCs from total current or LCFS loop voltage, geometry from external equilibria (CHEASE/FBT/EQDSK), explicitly designed for ML-surrogate coupling. It validates this plan's thesis twice over: current diffusion is tractable as a reduced 1D model layered on an equilibrium, and differentiability through it is a solved problem.

  • Take: the current-diffusion formulation and its discretisation (torax/_src/physics/psi_calculations.py, the FVM ψ solve), the Sauter conductivity/bootstrap structure as the η(ψ_N) parameterisation template (torax/_src/neoclassical/conductivity/sauter.py), the loop-voltage/Ip boundary-condition pattern, and — later — TORAX as an independent cross-check and a synthetic-truth generator for P2's exact-recovery gate.
  • Do not take (locked below): TORAX as a runtime dependency. Its scope is the full core-transport system needing Te/ne profiles and flux-surface geometry per time step — exactly what MAST's EFIT-free corpus does not provide — and it is JAX inside a torch/fp64 pipeline. P2 needs one equation with one unknown profile on a basis we already own; an in-house torch implementation is right-sized, keeps the differentiable-decode contract, and uses TORAX as the reference implementation to verify against.

§6 — Circuit matrices, state-estimation framing, dynamics data, SINDy

The L and R matrices — what is exact, what is calibratable, where the plasma enters

L is known exactly from geometry and is computed as the two-section thick-cylinder flux linkage (lead directive): the finite-area cylinder kernel integrates the source cross-section analytically, and the observer cross-section is averaged over a midpoint sub-grid — the nova biot.Inductance scheme (grid source and target, integrate the linkage out), applied where it matters: the large vessel elements (up to 0.24 × 0.42 m) whose flux varies materially across the section; small sections stay centroid-linked. The subdivision criterion is machine-agnostic (lead directive, 2026-07-17): sub-cells are sized as a fraction of the passive set's median section scale median(√(w·h)) — on MAST 0.0491 m, reproducing the previously hand-picked 0.05 m, and transferring to any machine with no metre-level lock-in. The full mutual matrix couples all 146 inferred-passive circuits; R is diagonal per circuit (toroidal rings share no conductor path), combining per-filament ring resistance at the TRUE cross-section (the 0.01 m kernel floor guards only the source-side flux integration — it was inflating the conducting area of thin shells up to 3.3× on 40 of 146 passive circuits, exactly the fast shield paths) with the xmult² current-share weights, at nominal steel resistivity with ONE bounded cross-shot scale as the calibratable unknown (the k_sol precedent). These are known priors an ML need not — and now cannot — re-learn.

Coil-case shielding is verified, not assumed. The cases are modeled as boxes of 3 mm plates enclosing their winding packs (4–8 filaments each, perimeter current-share xmult); the two-section kernel handles the enclosing-observer configuration with no special-casing (the finite-area kernel is smooth inside conductors, and the case plates sit outside the winding section). Measured on the real P2–P5 pairs: coupling coefficients k = M/√(L_coil·L_case) = 0.854–0.936, all strictly below unity, reciprocity to ~10⁻³ — the strong EM shielding of coil by case (and vice versa) is carried by the mutual matrix. P2–P5 cases are DRIVEN circuits (measured *_case_current channels, own G columns); the P6 cases are channel-less, constrained-zero → they live in the passive set with the now-correct thin-wall resistance. Test-pinned (test_coil_case_coupling_is_strong_but_below_unity, test_thin_shell_resistance_uses_true_cross_section). Re-gating the frozen P1 config on tune with the corrected physics reproduces the verdict within noise (ramp-up −0.97 cm CI [−1.37, −0.76] vs −0.95; reseed 0.047; axis/cost unchanged) — the kept sensor-visible modes are bulk-vessel dominated, so the vacuum resistance extraction below remains the binding attack on the magnitude.

The plasma enters the passive system twice, and the split matters. As a column (drive), the mode flux is Ψ_m(t) = m_coil·i_pf(t) + m_cells·i_cell(t) with i_cell(t) the spine's full time-varying cell-current distribution — so the drive is d(M(t)·I_p(t))/dt, never the fixed-mutual approximation M·dI_p/dt: the changing internal inductance, shape, and position are carried by the cell-history at full grid resolution. As a row (the plasma's own circuit equation, d(L_p(t)·I_p)/dt + R_p·I_p = V, with li(t) inside the time-varying self-inductance), the radially-resolved form IS the P2 flux-diffusion equation — P1 deliberately keeps the plasma row measured (Rogowski + fitted history) and P2 models it.

State-estimation framing (the ML-Kalman lens)

P1's two-pass scheme is one iteration of a fixed-interval smoother: the raw-cadence circuit integration is the predict step (exact-ZOH linear process model, measured drives), and the constrained per-slice refit is the update (whitened magnetics likelihood + trajectory prior). The soft-prior weight w is exactly the process/observation noise ratio — swept on the tune split rather than assumed. The upgrade path, if P1 gates positive but leaves structured residuals, is the full joint smoother over (eddy modes, profile DOF) with learned noise covariances — the principled form of "ML Kalman filter": the network parameterises covariances and closures, never the known dynamics.

Dynamics data audit (drives, observations, available-unused)

  • Drives (1 kHz raw, full shot): 42 amc current channels — P2–P6 coil / case / feed currents, solenoid (k_sol 1.0825 response scale applied), TF, and the Rogowski plasma_current (pre-label amplitude-following of the plasma flux pattern). Interior NaN dropouts interpolated per channel so a dropped sample never fabricates a flux step. Known limitation: P2–P5 up/down pairs are un-separable in operating data (coil-audit finding) — a shared-mode drive error the bounded resistance scale cannot absorb; it lands in the trajectory-prior residual by design.
  • Observations (per labelled slice): 73 measured magnetics — 59 B-probes (32 ccbv + 14 obr + 13 obv) + 14 fl_* flux loops — whitened by the robust channel scales (the observation covariance), frozen static calibration applied.
  • Available but unused — the calibration and P2 on-ramp: 8 measured *_case_current channels (p2l/p2u/p3l/p3u/p4l/p4u/p5l/p5u) are DIRECT time-resolved measurements of passive-structure (coil-case) currents — supervised targets for the circuit model that no rung exploits dynamically; the 14 flux loops at raw cadence give the measured surface loop voltage (V_loop = dΨ_loop/dt) — the current-diffusion boundary condition (no measured V_loop channel exists; the ESM reconstruction V_loop is firewalled); ane/density (line-integrated n_e) is loaded per slice and unused by the fit; ama n=2 / n-odd mode amplitude, frequency and signal (6 channels) are an MHD-activity flag no rung consumes yet; Thomson Te is absent from the corpus (moment_priors.TE_DATA_GATE) — η(ψ_N) remains the explicit low-DOF unknown in P2.

Vacuum-trained passive resistances (data-led R discovery — lead directive)

The tune-sweep magnitude finding (trajectory eddies overpredicted; axis parity restored at a ~4× resistance scale) makes the case the lead drew: R is not knowable from geometry — the vessel is a complex 3-D structure and the efm bounding boxes overestimate conducting cross-sections — but it IS knowable from data, separately from the ML pipeline. The training set already exists and is vetted: the coil-only pool of the solenoid-attribution work — 85 shots (48 fleet preludes + 37 dedicated vacuum shots, ~111k coil-only slices) — where every fleet shot's −2.5 s prelude carries the full CS precharge swing and the dedicated vacuum shots run whole coil programs with no plasma ever formed. With no plasma, measured − coil-vacuum is PURE eddy signal; L is exact from geometry; the drives are measured; the only unknowns are a few bounded resistance DOF (global scale → per-mode-group → per-region resistivity, in that identifiability order). The static coil calibration deliberately fit dI/dt-QUIET intervals (gains), so the transient decays that encode τ = L/R are untouched information. Two supervision channels: the 73 magnetics (the eddy field), and the 8 measured *_case_current streams — direct passive-conductor current measurements the circuit model must reproduce outright. Case-current holdback (lead directive, binding): today the landed code consumes the case measurements as DRIVES everywhere (known_case circuits carry their measured channels into the spine's vacuum prediction and the P1 trajectory's m_coil columns — legitimate for a static forward map, and the frozen spine stays untouched). In the resistance-discovery rung this inverts: the case channels are never inputs — the P2–P5 cases are re-classified into the passive circuit set (predicted by the L/R model from coil + plasma drives through their k ≈ 0.85–0.94 couplings), and the measured case currents serve purely as held-back discovery and validation targets for the case resistances. A model that reproduces the measured case transients from independent drives is validated at the per-circuit level — the strongest test the corpus offers of both L and R. This is classical bounded parameter estimation (the k_sol precedent, extended from gains to dynamics) — done standalone so the ML rungs inherit a calibrated R rather than re-learning it; if the post-calibration vacuum residuals still show structured dynamics, that residual is the disciplined SINDy slot (missing circuits, 3-D current paths).

SINDy — where sparse identification is complementary, and where it is not

Not at P1. The passive circuit dynamics are linear with structure known exactly from geometry; SINDy would re-identify from noisy data a state matrix we can compute, with worse conditioning and blurred gate attribution (the P1 verdict must credit the physics, not a fitted surrogate). Complementary from P2 onward, three slots: (1) the η(ψ_N) closure in P2 is a sparse model-selection problem — a SINDy-style sparse regression over a library of resistivity parameterisations, fitted cross-shot against the flux-consumption data, is a disciplined alternative to a fixed parametric form; (2) post-gate residual-dynamics discovery: sparse regression of the constrained-fit residuals against a physics library (mode amplitudes, drives, Ip) can expose compact unmodelled terms (3-D effects, halo paths) as interpretable candidates for P3, gated by cross-shot coefficient stability on the same referee harness; (3) structured discrepancy in R — the bounded resistance scale is the 1-DOF version; sparse selection generalises it only if the residuals demand it. SINDy is therefore scheduled as a P2/P3 instrument, not a P1 arm.
