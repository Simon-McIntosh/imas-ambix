§1 — Objective: the dynamics get the same treatment as the statics

The static boundary problem was solved by building a physics spine — exact thick-cylinder Biot–Savart kernels + the GS flux-function structure, fit per slice, gated against frozen baselines — and only then letting a network correct its degenerate DOF. The temporal problem got the opposite treatment: the learned-equilibrium-operator R2 rung asked a causal transformer to discover the dynamics from sensor history, and it closed as a sustained negative after two clean tests (full record: §2 R2 record): the operator converts ~21% of whitened val sensor misfit, and with the topology anchors those gains come boundary-safe and spine-non-inferior — yet flat-top, ramp-up, axis, and the p′/FF′ split are all unmoved. The information the referee rewards was never in reach of the learner, because the two places it lives are both governed by known differential equations with measured drives:

  • Vessel-eddy transients obey the passive circuit equation L·dI/dt + R·I = −dΨ_ext/dt with the external flux swing computable from measured coil currents and the plasma current history through the exact cylinder kernels. The per-slice static passive fit is under-determined by construction (measured: th-boundary-robustness §18) — this is why ramp-ups sit at 5–10 cm.
  • Plasma current diffusion obeys the resistive flux-diffusion equation — the jφ profile at time t is not free; it evolves from the profile at t−Δt under a diffusion operator whose boundary conditions (surface loop voltage, total Ip) are measured and whose only unknown is the resistivity profile. The per-slice fit discards this entirely: successive profile coefficient fits are independent (the warm chain shares only an initial guess).

The lead's question — "do we repeat the same loop: reduced-order model first, then merge into the ML pipeline?" — is answered yes, and it is the whole plan. The R0→R1 pattern (deterministic physics spine → residual learner) is the methodology, not a one-off: build the reduced-order dynamics spine, drive it with measured histories, gate it against the frozen static spine on the standard referee harness with zero ML, and only then let a network correct the DOF the dynamics provably cannot pin (the resistivity profile, mode-resistance scales, un-modelled 3-D effects). If the deterministic dynamic spine cannot move the ramp-up gap, that is itself the decisive measurement: the temporal information is not in the dynamics we can model, and the ML rung was never going to find it.

§2 — What already exists (most of P1 is built)

  • The passive reduced-order model exists and is validated. build_passive_eigenbasis + physical_eddy_history (imas_ambix/latent/temporal_operator.py) already construct the L/R eigenmode reduction from pure geometry (taus 35.8→4.1 ms, exact-ZOH integration pinned by test against dense sub-stepping) and integrate it along a shot from measured coil currents + the plasma current history. Today its output is only a feature handed to the (now-closed) learned operator; the per-slice fit still solves for passive amplitudes as static unknowns behind a ridge. P1 promotes the trajectory from feature to constraint.
  • The per-slice fit machinery takes passive priors already. fit_and_read_slice carries the passive sidecar (passive_ridge); constraining the amplitudes toward a circuit-integrated trajectory is a prior-centre change, not new machinery.
  • Raw-cadence drives exist. The 1 kHz anchored streams (coil currents, flux loops, Rogowski) are in the corpus (load_shot_windows), so the circuit ODE can integrate on the raw cadence between labelled slices — removing the label-cadence approximation (a_phys[0] = 0, eddy state under-resolved exactly where ramp-up wins live) recorded in the R2 archive.
  • Synthetic truth generates exact temporal sequences. synthetic_truth.manufacture + the known-eddy sequence generator produce time-coupled equilibria with exact eddy histories and exact profiles — the P1/P2 verification substrate and P2's exact-recovery gate for the p′/FF′ split.
  • The referee harness is proven. n≥128 paired bootstrap, push-out reader, byte-exact frozen-spine reproduction, EFIT eval-only — two R2 gate evals ran it end-to-end without a defect.

§3 — Rung ladder: two physics rungs, then the merge

P1 — dynamic passive spine (the highest-yield rung; attacks ramp-up directly)

Replace the per-slice static passive fit with circuit-integrated eddy trajectories. Two-pass structure per shot: pass 1 = the frozen spine as-is (static passive ridge) to obtain a plasma-current history; pass 2 = integrate the L/R eigenmode ODE at raw cadence, driven by measured coil currents and the pass-1 plasma history, then refit every slice with the passive amplitudes constrained to the trajectory (form per the open passive-constraint-form decision: tight soft prior vs hard pin); iterate once if the plasma history moves materially. Free DOF, deliberately few and physical: a bounded per-mode-group resistance scale (the eigenbasis is built at nominal steel resistivity — cross-shot calibratable, mirroring the k_sol precedent) and the initial mode state at the Ip threshold. Gate P1 on the standard harness: ramp-up LCFS median beats the frozen D2 spine (8.37 cm) with the paired CI clear, flat-top non-inferiority (−0.05 skill margin), reseed rate not degraded. The 5 cm G2 ramp-up bar is the aspiration, not the P1 gate — P1's job is a CI-clear improvement attributable to the circuit dynamics.

P2 — current-diffusion temporal prior (TORAX-inspired, right-sized)

Between labelled slices, evolve the profile-coefficient state under a 1D flux-surface-averaged resistive diffusion operator on the spine's own ψ_N ladder basis: the diffusion structure, the measured surface loop voltage (flux loops), and the Rogowski Ip are exact physics and data; the resistivity profile η(ψ_N) is unmeasured (Thomson Te is gated — moment_priors.TE_DATA_GATE) and enters as the explicit low-DOF unknown — a bounded parametric profile, NOT a hidden nuisance. The prior enters the per-slice fit as a soft temporal consistency term on coefficient evolution (precedent: the locked soft-prior interior-boundary coupling — hard pinning injects model error as truth). What this buys that no static fit can: the flux-consumption budget (∫V_loop dt) constrains the p′/FF′ partition's evolution, and axis motion is tied to profile diffusion rather than free per-slice. Gate P2: on synthetic truth (exact η, exact profiles) the diffusion-constrained chain recovers the p′/FF′ split materially better than the independent-slice spine (bar: beat 0.120 median |err|); on real data, flat-top non-inferiority and axis median not degraded.

P3 — merge into the ML pipeline

Only after P1/P2 verdicts. The residual learner returns exactly as in R1: zero-initialised heads about the dynamic spine, corrections decoded through the same exact Green's layers, topology anchors retained (they are now proven degeneracy control), G2-class gates re-baselined against the dynamic spine. The network's target DOF are the ones P1/P2 deliberately left free: η(ψ_N), mode-resistance scales, and whatever structured residual the physics rungs' error analysis exposes. If P1/P2 both fail their gates, P3 does not run — the plan records that the modelled dynamics do not carry the missing information and the temporal thesis fails at the physics level, which is a far cleaner negative than R2's.

§4 — TORAX: what to take, what not to take

TORAX (cloned at ~/Code/torax, v1.0.x) is a JAX differentiable 1D core-transport simulator: coupled heat/particle/current-diffusion PDEs, FVM discretisation, Sauter neoclassical conductivity + bootstrap, poloidal-flux BCs from total current or LCFS loop voltage, geometry from external equilibria (CHEASE/FBT/EQDSK), explicitly designed for ML-surrogate coupling. It validates this plan's thesis twice over: current diffusion is tractable as a reduced 1D model layered on an equilibrium, and differentiability through it is a solved problem.

  • Take: the current-diffusion formulation and its discretisation (torax/_src/physics/psi_calculations.py, the FVM ψ solve), the Sauter conductivity/bootstrap structure as the η(ψ_N) parameterisation template (torax/_src/neoclassical/conductivity/sauter.py), the loop-voltage/Ip boundary-condition pattern, and — later — TORAX as an independent cross-check and a synthetic-truth generator for P2's exact-recovery gate.
  • Do not take (locked below): TORAX as a runtime dependency. Its scope is the full core-transport system needing Te/ne profiles and flux-surface geometry per time step — exactly what MAST's EFIT-free corpus does not provide — and it is JAX inside a torch/fp64 pipeline. P2 needs one equation with one unknown profile on a basis we already own; an in-house torch implementation is right-sized, keeps the differentiable-decode contract, and uses TORAX as the reference implementation to verify against.

§5 — Gates, firewall, compute

  • Referee protocol unchanged and inherited: n≥128 held-out slices, 8-shot cohort, paired bootstrap, push-out lcfs_contour reader, EFIT referee at evaluation only, frozen baselines from closure_spine_frozen.json (D2 flat-top 1.50 cm / ramp-up 8.37 cm / axis 6.15 cm; disc read 1.99 / 11.30 cm). Standing held-out shots 18502–18505 + the eval cohort never fit against.
  • Firewall: all drives are raw measurements (coil currents, flux loops, Rogowski, ne); all couplings thick-cylinder hybrid_greens; labels remain spine-made; EFIT never enters a fit or a prior.
  • Compute: P1/P2 are CPU work (per-slice refits ~5.8 s/slice, the same sun-partition fleet economics as the label factory; the eval cohort re-scores in minutes). No GPU until P3.
