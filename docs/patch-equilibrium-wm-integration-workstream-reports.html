Illustrated implementation report for the near-complete plan patch-equilibrium-wm-integration, written per the standing mandate to close each workstream with a fully-illustrated synthesis. §1 — Executive summary

The run closed eight of nine core sections plus a user-directed coil audit: Workstream A (A1/A2 corpus scale-up, A3 calibration, A4 boundary read), Workstream B (B1 engine wiring, B2 the end-to-end learned-latent gate, B4 the filter observation operator), Workstream C (cross-machine ingestion), and Workstream D (the coil-circuit audit) all landed; the ninth, B3 (closures as dimensionless latent coordinates), is now measured too — its divergence was fixed and validated, its readout an honest negative (below). All nine core sections are complete. Two decisions were locked at kickoff — a staged, cached corpus scale-up and interleaved A/B execution — and both held.

The defining outcome was not a headline metric but a discipline result: three silent data-integrity biases surfaced and were fixed under scale-up, each caught by fleet cross-checks before it contaminated a recorded result. Every gate number recorded before 2026-07-04 carried at least one of these biases; the corrected baselines replace them.

  1. Case-circuit mis-drive. The GS operator's 8 cm nearest-centroid circuit classifier folded 8 of 10 coil-case circuits into the co-located active coil's Green's-function column and drove them at the active coil's 30–40× larger current. Measured impact on shots 18502–18505: all 73 B-probes biased, median 0.44σ, worst obr10 at 17.8σ, up to 39% of the RMS measured signal in the centre-column cluster. Fixed by classifying case circuits into their own columns (known coil columns 13→21) with a COIL_MODEL_VERSION=case-circuits-v2 cache-invalidation marker.
  2. Per-shot channel-set leakage. The campaign-signature digest hashes static geometry, but the sensor channel set was being read from each shot's own data-acquisition schema — so a shot missing a flux loop (present on another shot of the same signature) leaked a smaller channel set into what should be a geometry-determined quantity. Single-process runs silently locked in whichever shot assembled first; the 48-way sharded re-assembly exposed it (39 shards saw S=97, 9 saw S=96). Fixed by unioning the schema across a signature's shots (canonical_amb_channels()), expressing per-shot absence via honest masks, tagged GEOMETRY_TABLE_VERSION=amb-schema-canonical-v1.
  3. Whitening blow-up. Per-shot channel whitening divided by a barely-positive standard deviation, so a near-static flux loop (e.g. 3×10⁻⁵ Wb) whitened a modest mismatch to 10,000σ+ and the full-corpus retrain diverged on the tail (fc938 zero-current whitened misfit: mean 51,852 vs median 13.3). Fixed by a kind-median relative floor (5% of the same-kind median in the shot), which brought the fc938 mean to 11.8 (≈ its median) with fc1004 essentially unchanged.

With the corrected physics in place, the corrected 5k-shot encoder halves the axis to 4.3 cm median (from the pre-fix 9.56 cm at 2k). But the run's most consequential scientific finding is an honest frontier: scaling past 5k tightens the magnetics fit monotonically while the axis regresses, and signature/regime-balanced resampling does not recover it. Magnetics-only equilibrium placement saturates at a null-space wall — the interior current is under-determined by external magnetics — which neither more data nor resampling crosses. Crossing it needs interior diagnostics and a constrained boundary representation, which is the mandate of the new plan equilibrium-topology-fidelity.

§2 — Workstream A: close the amortised geometry gap SHIPPED

Workstream A hardened the amortised encoder — the token-native map from per-(sensor,step) geometry-encoded tokens to patch currents — through four measured levers. A1/A2 built a config-hashed corpus-assembly cache and per-batch geometry binding so that all three campaign signatures now train, none dropped (final per-signature counts: fc1004-9425=98, fc1004-edd7=97, fc938=98; full corpus 114,834 examples / 10,842 shots), verified deterministic across independently-sharded corpora. Assembly cost 1.54–1.67 s/shot (~7 node-hours for 5k+full on the sun partition). The staged retrains ran under the corrected physics (case-circuits-v2 + amb-schema-canonical-v1 + the kind-median whitening floor).

Scaling is non-monotonic, and that is the finding. 588 → 2k → 5k improves the axis median monotonically (28.0 → 9.56 → 4.3 cm; the 5k point halves the pre-fix 2k number). But 5k → full inverts the median (4.3 → 15.9 cm) while every tightness-of-fit metric improves (grounding 8.86 → 20.58×, misfit 0.167 → 0.069) and the catastrophic tail shrinks — the full corpus explains the magnetics substantially better yet places the typical axis worse. Cross-signature axis degrades most (3.6 → 17.1 cm), consistent with the full corpus being 92% fc938 (vs 82% at 5k) reshaping the shared trunk's priorities. A follow-on retrain with signature- and Ip-regime-balanced sampling did not recover the axis (17.5 cm) even as grounding rose again to 28.3× — so the regression is not a data-composition artifact resampling can fix. It is a null-space signature: across 5k → full → full-balanced the magnetics fit tightens monotonically while the axis does not follow, because the interior current is under-constrained by magnetics. The magnetics-only encoder saturates at ~4.3 cm, and the direct-5k checkpoint is the best readout of record.

A3 — calibration. An opt-in Gaussian (mean + log-σ) head with exact linear sensor-variance propagation was added. A from-scratch joint-NLL fit collapsed the mean to a near-constant (23 cm axis, recorded as the honest negative), so calibration runs on a frozen-mean warm-start — σ is trained from the direct-5k checkpoint while the mean stays byte-identical, making mean-regression zero by construction (axis-skill Δ +0.0006, verified twice). A leakage-free per-sensor-kind σ-temperature (fit on train shots, closed-form quantile match) brought sensor coverage into the [0.88, 0.92] band: b_probe 0.985 → 0.894 PASS, flux_loop 0.383 → 0.867 (90% of the gap closed, 1.3 pts short). Topology coverage (axis_R 0.70, axis_Z 0.43, X-point 0.26–0.52) is mathematically untouchable by a sensor-space temperature — it draws from the current-space distribution — so it stays under-covered, an honest downstream residual queued for a current-space heteroscedastic-σ mechanism. Verdict: the uncertainty machinery works and sensor calibration is essentially achieved; the sensors are calibrated, calibrated topology is a genuine downstream problem, not a tuning shortfall.

A4 — boundary read. All three candidate LCFS-robustification arms were measured leakage-free (tuned on train, frozen, 160/160 held-out; an invert-once grid isolated the readout as the only varying factor). HONEST NEGATIVE None is load-bearing — the default readout is unchanged. The saddle-distance guard and ψ-smoothing are bit-identical to baseline across the full swept range; current-smoothness (λ=0.001) moves every metric <0.01 in the wrong/negligible direction. The post-fix baseline (which replaces all pre-fix numbers) is axis skill −0.027 @ 2.58 cm median, LCFS skill −6.26, LCFS offset 34.1 cm (all) / 37.2 cm (flat-top). The deficit is unresponsive to every readout-level lever because the LCFS under-sizing is interior-structure-driven (null-space current concentration) — pointing at training-time / representation levers, not post-hoc readout fixes.

F — floor sensitivity. The whitening floor from §1 was itself swept (rel ∈ {0, 0.01, 0.02, 0.05}, leakage-free) to test whether it costs axis skill. It does not: rel=0.05 is load-bearing and frozen (the training default). rel=0 is catastrophic (all tune slices collapse to one degenerate point R=0.595 / Z=−1.825, misfit NaN, zero X-points — the near-zero-scale pathology, overwhelmingly vertical); rel ∈ {0.01, 0.02, 0.05} are statistically tied within the inverse's ±10–15% run-to-run noise, with 0.05 nominally best.

Standing caveat (programme-wide). The per-slice variational inverse is chaotically sensitive; its absolute axis/LCFS cm figures carry device+run variance of order several cm (measured: identical-config GPU baseline 2.58 cm vs CPU re-run 6.4 cm; CPU-vs-CPU reproduces exactly). Comparative verdicts scored under one device/run (A4 arm-vs-arm, F rel-vs-rel) are safe and the PASS/FAIL verdicts stand; the exact cm do not carry sub-cm precision and must cite their run.

MECHANISM FIXED · READOUT NEGATIVE A per-ψ-bin (ak=p′, bk=FF′/μ₀) closure-readout head was added to the hybrid latent, trained self-supervised against the engine's own fit_flux_functions targets. §5 — Workstream D: MAST coil audit (user-directed) SHIPPED

A user-directed audit of the MAST power-supply topology (commits 5fcbd2a + 6c7aeaa; full report mast-coil-circuits, 3 figures) answered three questions directly. All 13 active MAST PF circuits are properly powered — each has its own dedicated supply and a real measured current channel on every sampled shot across both filament signatures. The extracted machine description is now in code (imas_ambix/gs/circuits.py + gs/artifacts/mast_pf_circuits.json, cross-validated against efm fcoil_circ and amc channels).

The ohmic solenoid pair is the only series constraint on the whole machine — one supply, one measured sol_current channel, one actuator column at every layer from raw data to the model's i_pf array — and it is modelled correctly (the series tie is enforced structurally by the machine's own instrumentation, not by a code convention that could break). The apparent "multiple geometries" in the corpus are campaign description variants of one physical machine: fc938 vs fc1004 are different filament discretisations of the same 13 coils, with B-probe positions identical to within 12.6 mm.

The audit surfaced two load-bearing red flags in the L2 / world-model actuator path: (1) it reads per-turn *_feed_current where the GS/patch path correctly uses amp-turn *_coil_current (an exact, integer 8–23× per-coil factor — e.g. P4U's true peak contribution at probe obr02 is 277 mT vs the 12.1 mT the raw feed number would imply); and (2) P6U/P6L are dropped entirely from the L2 actuator set (small — 1.3–2.4% of active amp-turns — but a genuine, independently-controllable boundary-shaping degree of freedom). The audit also independently found the case-circuit mis-drive of §1, which was then fixed as Workstream E.

§6 — Where this leads: the layered stack and the topology frontier

This plan built and hardened Layer 0 of a layered-capability stack — the robust Grad–Shafranov physics skeleton. The load-bearing insight (physics-world-model-strategy §1b) is that the physics skeleton at the base keeps every layer above it physical:

  • Layer 0 — the physics / GS skeleton (shipped). Exact linear Maxwell physics precomputed into interaction matrices (694k slices/s on an H200), force balance as a soft differentiable residual, per-bin closures recovered from data, everything label-free. It grounds a learned latent on real magnetics (5.3× held-out) and transfers across an unseen coil-set signature without retraining (cross-signature grounding 5.54×). This is the equilibrium organ of the world model.
  • Layer 1 — forward decoders inherit the grounding. Camera-video generation and virtual diagnostics decode from the physically-constrained state, not a free latent — a dream frame rendered from a ψ that explains the raw magnetics and obeys force balance is anchored, not hallucinated.
  • Layer 2 — long-horizon steerable dreams. Forecasting and controllable what-ifs advance the same physics-anchored latent under an actuator plan, with the skeleton keeping the long roll on the equilibrium manifold.

The stated ambition is to beat EFIT the way GenCast beat the ECMWF ensemble — first match, then exceed on the X-point, the plasma boundary, and the limited↔diverted topology class — not by being a better deterministic solver but by being a better probabilistic model of the state (generative, multimodal, dynamics-aware, uncertainty-carrying), with EFIT firewalled to referee-only. The axis is already matched to referee grade; the open frontier is the boundary and topology.

That frontier is owned by the new plan equilibrium-topology-fidelity, whose organizing insight splits our two measured negatives into two physically different problems. The plasma boundary is externally well-posed: outside the plasma Jφ=0, so ψ solves the homogeneous Grad–Shafranov operator Δ*ψ=0, and external magnetics fix the low-order current moments (Ip, centroid, elongation, triangularity) that set the boundary shape — so the A4 boundary negative is a representation artifact of reading the boundary off a free ~5k-DOF interior patch current, curable with a constrained external-field read (toroidal harmonics / current moments) and no new diagnostics. The interior profile is genuinely ill-posed — the null-space wall the balanced-sampling negative measured — and needs the missing information: interior diagnostics (MSE, interferometer, SXR), a learned equilibrium prior, closure smoothness, temporal coherence, and calibrated uncertainty. This report's frontier finding is exactly that plan's empirical mandate.
