Plasma-Current Priors Hardening

Child of the 2026-07 grounding review (project-grounding-review-2026-07). The North Star (machine-agnostic-latent-world-model) states the contract: "the X-point is the natural consequence of explaining the raw magnetics — physics enters as the coordinate system and soft priors (the language)". The review found the contract is not being honoured in the null space: where the magnetics do not constrain the current, what fills the gap today is optimiser defaults and basis-support choices, not physics. This plan hardens the prior ladder so the null space is spanned by physics — the lead's directive: "if we build upon physics then this physics needs to be robust or else we are lost".

§1 — The evidence: the null space is filled by numerics, not physics

One figure carries the whole case. Left: the free ~5k-DOF inverse "explains the magnetics" with a negative-current halo hugging the outboard wall and positive lobes at the upper/lower inboard corners — current where no plasma is. Right: the current-moment fit is smooth, but its monopole term spreads Ip uniformly over every conductor-clear in-limiter cell, so jφ ≠ 0 throughout the vacuum region — the reconstructed ψ violates Δ*ψ = 0 exactly where the boundary and X-point are read, contradicting the §2 premise of equilibrium-topology-fidelity that motivated it.

Why the existing physics term cannot fix this: the force-balance structure residual (imas_ambix/latent/structure_residual.py) is a current-weighted, scale-free measure of the flux-function relation R·jφ = p′R² + FF′/μ₀. It is invariant to exactly the failure modes in Figure 1: a low-|j| halo is down-weighted by the j² weighting, a sign flip on its own level set can still satisfy an affine relation, and support location enters nowhere. The only support prior in the tree (edge_taper_penalty) is deliberately unwired. So the visible unphysicality costs the loss almost nothing — the physics is present but not load-bearing in the null space.

§2 — The prior ladder (design)

R1 — unidirectional toroidal current, jφ·sign(Ip) ≥ 0. Answering the lead's question directly: should currents share the same sign, and would that rule out doublets? A same-sign prior does not rule out doublets — a doublet's two current lobes are both parallel to Ip; it is the flux topology that is doubled, not the current sign. It also does not rule out "current-hole" plasmas (j ≈ 0 in the core is permitted by ≥ 0). The only configurations excluded are reversed-current transients (AC operation crossings), which are outside every corpus we score. Default arm: hard by reparametrisation (x = softplus(y) on the dimensionless shape, sign from the Rogowski Ip) — the null space then cannot produce cancelling dipole layers at all; soft-penalty arm as the ablation to measure what the constraint is worth.

R2 — free-boundary support consistency. Answering the second question — should the plasma be a single joined blob of current? — the physically correct form of that intuition is not "connected blob" (which a doublet also satisfies) but free-boundary consistency: jφ = 0 outside the last closed flux surface of the very ψ the current generates. This is a fixed-point property, imposed softly: penalise current carried by cells outside the current boundary estimate (from the existing topology read of the assembled ψ, recomputed on a cadence in the optimisation loop), with a halo budget — a few per cent of Ip exempted near the boundary — so the real SOL/halo currents the structure residual's discovery framing protects can still surface as a measured excess over budget, not be silently fitted away. A doublet's LCFS envelope encloses both lobes, so R2 is doublet-safe by construction.

R3 — the source-free exterior read (toroidal harmonics: half-integer-degree Legendre Δ*-eigenfunctions fitted to measured − vacuum in the vacuum annulus) is the representation-side complement, already queued as f-etf-04 on equilibrium-topology-fidelity. The review re-states its expected behaviour precisely so it is not "missed" again: the fit is valid only in the source-free annulus between plasma and sensors; inside the plasma the expansion is meaningless and that is correct — the boundary/X-point are read in the annulus, the axis stays with the interior carrier. Design note from the review: the confined-side flux reference must NOT be taken from the harmonic field's own extremum (the current hybrid read does this on the moment ψ — boundary_moment_gate_eval.hybrid_target reads axis_psi from the moment field), because the harmonic field has no valid interior.

Per the review's evidence-quality findings, every headline skill now carries a paired-bootstrap CI over shots, and two harness defects are fixed before any new physics is measured (§4).

  • Gate B (R2): with support consistency active, out-of-boundary current falls below the halo budget and the held-out LCFS/X-point skills move toward the referee (report Δ with CI). If yes, the "genuine interior null-space" attribution of thread 1 §4 was premature; if no, the corner residual is genuinely interior (or coil-model) information and thread 2 inherits it with evidence.
  • Gate C (R3, scored under f-etf-04): the harmonic exterior read beats the current-moment read on X-point-set and LCFS skill, and the vacuum-annulus consistency check (patch-ψ vs harmonic-ψ RMS in the shared annulus) is reported — the checkable condition both representations must satisfy where both are valid.

§4 — Gate-harness hardening (ships first, blocks the gates)

The review found the thread-1 verdict rests on a harness with four defects; they are cheap and ship before any new physics so Gates A–C are trustworthy:

  • Bootstrap CIs on every skill (paired over shots), in scripts/patch_gate_eval.py's score() and every consumer — the tune/eval order-sweep disagreement (o3 X-point −2.31 tune vs −0.15 eval) is unquantified noise until then.
  • Double-null-correct saddle metric: MAST is routinely double-null; saddles ≤ 1 as "saddle-free" mislabels two genuine X-points as a spurious read. Count saddles in excess of the referee's X-point count, and record the metric for BOTH arms (the free-current baseline artifact carries saddles: null — Gate 1a of thread 1 was never actually measured).
  • Origin-controlled LCFS read: the flagship moment artifact used the centroid ray origin (median 7.9 cm off-axis; arm axis skill −1.62, omitted from the findings table) while the findings doc describes the patch-axis hybrid. Make --axis-source patch the scored default; keep centroid as the fast smoke arm only.
  • Consistent model defaults: MomentFitConfig.model defaults to "gaussian" in the library but "polynomial" in the gate CLI — align to the locked decision and artifacts (polynomial) or rename explicitly.

§5 — Relationship to the standing plans

This plan blocks equilibrium-topology-fidelity per the lead's directive — threads 2–6 build interior information on top of the boundary story, and the review showed the boundary story's attribution (representation vs interior null-space vs coil-model error at the corners) cannot be settled with unphysical currents in the loop. It depends on patch-current-force-balance (the inverse + structure residual substrate it modifies) and patch-equilibrium-wm-integration (the payload / coil-model substrate). The grounding review (project-grounding-review-2026-07) is the evidence base; the amortised encoder (P4) inherits whichever priors survive their ablations, exactly as it inherited the oracle recipe.

§ Decisions

R1 — how is the unidirectional-current prior imposed?

Recommend hard-softplus as the default arm with the soft penalty as the ablation — the North Star allows hard FACTS (the Rogowski sign is a measurement, like the conductor mask "factual geometry"), and reserving softness for genuinely uncertain physics. Measure both; the ablation quantifies what the prior is worth.

R2 — how is free-boundary support consistency imposed?

Recommend the soft penalty: it keeps the loop free of hard topology extraction (the design rule the structure residual already honours), degrades gracefully near limited↔diverted transitions, and its budget term preserves the halo/SOL discovery channel. The iterated mask is the fallback if the soft form is too weak to move the corner lobes.
