Plasma-Current Priors Hardening

Child of the 2026-07 grounding review (project-grounding-review-2026-07). The North Star (machine-agnostic-latent-world-model) states the contract: "the X-point is the natural consequence of explaining the raw magnetics — physics enters as the coordinate system and soft priors (the language)". The review found the contract is not being honoured in the null space: where the magnetics do not constrain the current, what fills the gap today is optimiser defaults and basis-support choices, not physics. This plan hardens the prior ladder so the null space is spanned by physics — the lead's directive: "if we build upon physics then this physics needs to be robust or else we are lost".

§1 — The evidence: the null space is filled by numerics, not physics

One figure carries the whole case. Left: the free ~5k-DOF inverse "explains the magnetics" with a negative-current halo hugging the outboard wall and positive lobes at the upper/lower inboard corners — current where no plasma is. Right: the current-moment fit is smooth, but its monopole term spreads Ip uniformly over every conductor-clear in-limiter cell, so jφ ≠ 0 throughout the vacuum region — the reconstructed ψ violates Δ*ψ = 0 exactly where the boundary and X-point are read, contradicting the §2 premise of equilibrium-topology-fidelity that motivated it.

Why the existing physics term cannot fix this: the force-balance structure residual (imas_ambix/latent/structure_residual.py) is a current-weighted, scale-free measure of the flux-function relation R·jφ = p′R² + FF′/μ₀. It is invariant to exactly the failure modes in Figure 1: a low-|j| halo is down-weighted by the j² weighting, a sign flip on its own level set can still satisfy an affine relation, and support location enters nowhere. The only support prior in the tree (edge_taper_penalty) is deliberately unwired. So the visible unphysicality costs the loss almost nothing — the physics is present but not load-bearing in the null space. The substrate review found it is worse than neutral: because the residual is self-normalised (unexplained fraction, num/den), an isolated internally-consistent corner blob at its own ψ-level adds ≈0 to the numerator while inflating the denominator — the physics term can actively reward the blobs R1/R2 exist to remove (review register B14). The mask itself is verified functioning as coded (~73 sensors vs ~4,787 candidate cells; 35/41 corner-box cells are legal candidates), and a measured flat-top ablation shows amm-eddy absorption is not load-bearing — the lobes are null-space fill, which is exactly what a support prior addresses.

6.4 — Why EFIT's boundary is "trivial" and ours is not: what is missing

EFIT's boundary comes almost for free because of three ingredients our inverse deliberately does not have, and the figures above show exactly what their absence costs:

  1. A low-DOF flux-function closure AS the solve variable. EFIT parametrises jφ(R,ψN) = R·p′(ψN) + FF′(ψN)/(μ₀R) with ~5–10 polynomial/spline coefficients — 73 magnetics channels then over-determine ~10 DOF. Our inverse frees ~4,787 per-cell currents, leaving a ~4,700-dimensional null space that only soft priors span. R1+R2 fix the SIGN and the SUPPORT of the null-space fill, but nothing shapes how current distributes within the support — the flat-top current maps show the priors arm filling its support near-uniformly, which flattens ψ, pulls ψbnd inward, and under-sizes the LCFS (the measured Gate-B LCFS degradation, −4.91 → −7.57). The peaked-profile information EFIT injects through p′/FF′ is the missing shaping.
  2. The free-boundary fixed point solved, not penalised. In EFIT the current is a functional of its own flux surfaces at every Picard iteration (Δ*ψ = −μ₀R·jφ(ψ) solved each sweep), so support-consistency and Δ*ψ=0 in vacuum hold by construction and the separatrix is read off a field that cannot violate them. Our R2 imposes the same property softly on a cadence. The full fixed point exists in-tree — latent/gs_solve.py (training-free inverse: median axis 8.6 cm but only 83/160 slices scored, the Picard convergence tax) — and is the natural next arm: fit the closure coefficients THROUGH the GS solve on the gate cohort.
  3. A fitted static instrument/vessel model. EFIT co-fits vessel-segment/passive DOF and works against calibrated probes. §6.1 shows our unmodelled signal is static, not inductive — 0.7–0.75 Ip of halo absorbing it at every phase. Until the static coil/calibration error is found, ANY physically-constrained current pays it as misfit (0.05 → 0.20) while the free basis hides it as halo, which is why the junk-filled baseline out-scores the physical arm on sensor-weighted metrics.

None of this contradicts the discovery framing — the free basis is the instrument that MEASURED the 0.75 Ip static discrepancy the closed-form codes silently absorb. But for boundary-grade reconstruction the conclusion is concrete: add the low-DOF closure arm (profile-parametrised inverse through the GS fixed point) and find the static coil/calibration error; R3's source-free exterior read (f-etf-04) sidesteps the interior entirely and remains the representation-side complement.

§ Decisions

R1 — how is the unidirectional-current prior imposed?

Locked at §2 kickoff per the plan's own recommendation: hard by reparametrisation (x = softplus(y), sign from the Rogowski Ip) is the default arm — the Rogowski sign is a hard FACT like the conductor mask, and the null space then cannot express sign-indefinite fills at all. The soft penalty λ·Σrelu(−j·sign(Ip))² ships as the measured ablation arm so Gate A quantifies what the prior is worth.

R2 — how is free-boundary support consistency imposed?

Locked at §2 kickoff per the plan's own recommendation: soft penalty on current outside the current LCFS estimate with a halo budget, boundary recomputed on the λ-adaptation cadence (detached, no topology extraction in the gradient path). Degrades gracefully near limited↔diverted transitions and preserves the halo/SOL discovery channel. Iterated hard mask remains the recorded fallback if the soft form cannot move the corner lobes at Gate B.

Do the R1/R2 winner flags become the P3-winner defaults?

Held-out: axis skill −2.06 [−5.25,−0.15] vs baseline −0.16 [−0.70,+0.14] (RMSE tail-dominated), LCFS −7.57 vs −4.91 — not CI-compatible, so P5 discipline keeps the rungs flag-gated. The measured story: the free inverse buys its better scores with 0.75 Ip of unphysical current absorbing unmodelled eddy/vessel signal (misfit 0.049→0.205 when removed) — a coil/passive-model gap, not a prior defect. R2 IS load-bearing for topology (axis median 6.4→3.1 cm, X-point-set skill −1.69→+0.14 first positive); re-gate after the passive/eddy story.
