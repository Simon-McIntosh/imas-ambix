§1 — Objective: beat the frozen classical spine where it is provably blind

equilibrium-boundary-closure froze the classical spine (closure_spine_frozen.json): the staged-disc boundary read posts 1.4–2.1 cm flat-top / 5–10 cm ramp-up LCFS error vs firewalled EFIT, and the disc-anchored D2 interior solve posts 1.50 cm flat-top LCFS (skill −0.209, CI touching zero), axis median 5.9 cm, reseed 0.0% — all from raw external magnetics + Ip + ne, EFIT-firewalled. That spine is the baseline and the inductive bias; it is not the ceiling of what the data contain, because the closure investigation characterised exactly what a per-slice classical solve cannot recover, and every one of those irreducibles is temporal or kinetic, not static-magnetic:

  • The p′/FF′ profile split. External magnetics constrain only the sum; the corpus carries no Thomson Te (recorded as moment_priors.TE_DATA_GATE), so the split is degenerate per slice. But profiles evolve under transport with characteristic signatures (sawteeth, current diffusion, L–H transitions) — the time axis carries split information a single slice does not.
  • The magnetic axis is an interior null. Externally only Ip, the current centroid, and βp+li/2 are visible; the axis-vs-centroid offset is profile-degenerate (the D2 freeze paid +0.8 cm axis for its edge-capable profile). Same story: the offset is set by the profile, and the profile is temporally constrained.
  • Vessel-eddy currents are an L/R convolution of the current history. The per-slice static passive fit is under-determined by construction (measured: th-boundary-robustness §18 — no static gate separates help from harm); the eddy state at time t simply is not a function of the sensors at time t. This is why ramp-ups sit at 5–10 cm. It is pure arrow-of-time information.

So the learned operator is not "a neural net that fits equilibria" — it is a sequence model whose job is to carry the temporal information the classical read provably discards, wrapped around the same exact physics operators the spine uses. The GenCast analogy is precise: GenCast did not replace the atmosphere's physics, it learned the dynamics on a physically-meaningful state and beat the incumbent at its own game. Our incumbent is EFIT; our referee is firewalled EFIT; our physically-meaningful state is the GS-grounded equilibrium the spine already produces.

§2 — Architecture ladder: four rungs, each gated before the next

Capability accretes on a ladder; no rung ships until its gate clears, and every rung keeps the frozen classical spine as its paired baseline. The rungs are ordered by where the new information comes from: R1 adds learning capacity on the same static information (honest expectation: parity, not victory — its gate is non-inferiority plus a proven differentiable stack); R2 adds the time axis (this is the load-bearing rung — the irreducibles live here); R3 adds machine transfer.

§3 — Physics-embedded architecture: the network learns only what physics cannot pin

The lead's directive (carried from the closure plan): physics components are load-bearing inside the model, not preprocessing. Concretely:

  • Exact field operators. The thick-cylinder Green's kernels (hybrid_greens, cylinder-sensors-v4/v5 — never point-filament, per the standing directive) map any current set (coils, passive modes, plasma basis) to sensors and to ψ on the grid. They are differentiable (linear in the currents) and sit inside the model: every network output becomes a field only through them. The network cannot hallucinate a field the hardware could not produce.
  • Geometry as positional encoding. Every sensor token carries its (R, Z, φ), orientation/normal, and integration geometry; the machine context (coil/vessel filaments, limiter) conditions the operator. This is the machine-agnostic-latent-world-model §5 principle applied to the equilibrium operator — it is what makes a machine-varying sensor set ingestible by one model, and what R3 transfers on.
  • Structured latents. The eddy latent is L/R-eigenmode-structured (above). The profile latent is the spine's own low-DOF closure parameterisation (profile_basis, monomial-nonneg, edge-capable) — the network predicts coefficients in the same basis the classical solve uses, so classical and learned arms are directly comparable DOF-for-DOF.
  • Classical reads as prior and baseline. The disc read (and TH annulus read, retained for ablation) can be evaluated in-graph as a cheap physics feature; the frozen spine is the paired baseline at every gate. If a rung cannot beat the classical arm it does not ship — the physics keeps the learning honest, exactly the §12a layered-stack contract in machine-agnostic-latent-world-model.

The key economy of the decoupled design: the frozen classical spine is EFIT-independent, so its outputs are firewall-clean training labels. Running the disc read + D2 solve over the corpus manufactures per-slice (boundary contour, ψ(R,Z), axis, profile coefficients, passive amplitudes) targets at whatever scale we can afford CPU-wise — no leakage, because the labels descend from raw magnetics through our own physics, and the referee (EFIT) never enters.

  • Real corpus (MAST). The statespace split manifest (statespace_splits_dalpha_v0) carries 12,590 shots (10,846 train / 1,479 calibration / 265 OOD-regime test); the standing held-out shots (18502–18505) and the closure-gate eval cohort stay untouched for scoring. At ~10–100 labelled slices per shot this is an O(10⁵–10⁶)-slice label pool — label-factory throughput (solves/second on a sun CPU node) is measured in the first work item before committing to a cohort size; the D2 solve is pure-numpy per slice and embarrassingly parallel across a CPU fleet.
  • Synthetic pretraining (where the degenerate truth is known). synthetic_truth.manufacture (forward Picard, gs_solve) generates equilibria with exact known p′/FF′, axis, and boundary — the quantities that are degenerate on real data are supervised exactly on synthetic. Time-coupled synthetic sequences with known eddy histories (L/R evolution driven through passive_coupling_matrices) pretrain the temporal latent. Geometry perturbation of the synthetic generator is also the R3 synthetic-transfer corpus.
  • Cross-machine (data-gated, honest). Real second-machine operational data (WEST/CEA HDF5 pipelines exist organisationally; ITER has machine description only) is not in the corpus today. R3's real-transfer gate is therefore explicitly data-gated; the synthetic-machine sweep is the in-hand substitute and the feasibility evidence for the corpus ask.

  • Training: small models, modest GPU. R1 is O(1–10M) params on sensor vectors — a single H200 for hours. R2 is a sequence model over ~10²-channel streams at 1 kHz with O(10⁴–10⁵) training sequences — O(10–50M) params, 1–4 H200 for ≤1 day per run. Neither competes with the world-model training footprint, and neither requires stopping model serving (the DSv4 shutdown authorisation is narrow and is not needed here).
  • Serving: real-time relevant. A forward pass is milliseconds per slice batched — against ~seconds/slice for the classical Picard solve. A learned operator that clears G2 is also a real-time equilibrium candidate; that is a bonus, not a gate.

  • G3 (R3 ships): trained on N−1 synthetic machines + MAST, CI-clear beat of the classical spine on the held-out synthetic machine; the real second-machine gate activates when its data lands (trigger recorded).
  • Firewall (binding, inherited): EFIT output is never an input and never a training label anywhere in the ladder; the spine manufactures the labels; standing held-out shots never trained on. Sensor/machine geometry is apparatus metadata and freely used — plasma geometry from other codes is not.

§ Decisions

How do the classical physics components enter the learned operator?

Carried from the closure plan's ship brief: physics components are LOAD-BEARING inside the model (exact Green's operators, geometry encoding) AND the label factory — both roles, explicitly designed. The network learns only what physics provably cannot pin (p′/FF′ split, axis interior null, eddy history).

Does the network predict residual corrections to the classical spine's DOF, or emit the full DOF set directly (physics layer retained in both)?

Enacted by the shipped rung: R1 predicts additive DOF-space corrections about the classical solution through the frozen psi_N linearisation, and the composition cleared G1 with CI-clear wins (Δaxis +0.049, ΔLCFS +0.061) at byte-exact spine reproduction. Direct-DOF emission remains an R2 ablation arm exactly as the option specifies.

Which causal sequence backbone carries the R2 temporal state?

Transformer trunk + MANDATORY embedded L/R diagonal-SSM eddy block (the plan's structured-latent mandate — per-mode learnable decays initialised at physical L/R times), so the SSM option's physics content is fully retained inside the trunk. Trunk choice driven by: alignment with the world-model substrate (space-time transformer prior) and shared tooling; attention over geometry-encoded sensor tokens is the natural generalisation of the landed R1 token encoder and what R3's machine-varying sensor sets transfer on; lead preference for forward capability. Insurance: if long-horizon ramp-up training underperforms, a structured-GRU/SSM-trunk arm runs as a bounded G2 ablation, not a redesign.

After the R2 sustained negative: where does temporal information enter the equilibrium?

Lead directive at the f-leo-06 re-plan (2026-07-17): the missing information lies in the transients of the passive structure and the plasma current diffusion — both governed by known ODEs with measured drives. The static boundary won by building a physics spine (Biot–Savart + GS) before learning; the dynamics get the same treatment: reduced-order models first (P1 circuit-constrained eddies, P2 TORAX-informed current-diffusion prior), each gated with zero ML, then the ML pipeline merges back on the dynamic spine's residuals. Scope exceeded an in-plan update — executed as the linked plan temporal-physics-spine, which now blocks this plan's temporal rungs.

Topological design rationale (first-class — read before implementing)
  The push-out reader already GUARANTEES the emitted boundary terminates at a limiter tangency
  or a saddle (class emergent — locked by the connectivity-boundary decision; hard class
  supervision is BANNED). The topology enters as TRAINING SIGNAL, not output validity:
  a) Terminator-consistency anchor (spine-trusted quasi-flat-top slices only, mirroring the
     drift bound): penalise |∇ψ|² at the spine's X-point location (diverted slices) or the
     tangency condition at its limiter-contact point (limited slices), plus consistency of the
     boundary flux ψ_b = softmin(ψ_lim, ψ_X) — the either/or disjunction is differentiable as
     a softmin over candidate terminator fluxes. Phrase it as a penalty on the CHANGE the
     correction induces at the terminator (the decode is a frozen-ψ_N linearisation — an
     absolute condition is not representable; the delta form keeps the approximation honest).
     Cost: ∇ψ at 2-3 points = a few cylinder-kernel Green's rows — build once per campaign
     geometry and cache like the eigenbases. Spine labels carry the terminator targets
     (xpt0/xpt1 positions in the shard targets; limiter contour in the sequence payload).
     This is a 2-3 point anchor, NOT a ring anchor — lower-dimensional, less spine-emulation
     pressure, and it directly targets the measured X-point-set regression.
  b) Critical-point integrity regulariser (unconditional, label-free): penalise spurious
     interior O-points/saddles in the corrected ψ — keep the Morse structure to one axis null
     plus legitimate X-points. This attacks the sensor objective's degenerate directions
     (the grounding review's null-space fill) with no reference to any label.
  c) Ring-drift penalty (flat-top distance to the spine LCFS ring) ONLY as fallback if the
     pointwise terminator form under-constrains — it carries the strongest distillation
     pressure, keep its weight soft.

Remaining iteration scope (same cycle)
  d) Skill-aware selection: select on a composite (val misfit + drift/terminator penalty) or a
     val-shot boundary-skill proxy — NEVER on anchor satisfaction alone (a spine emulator
     maximises the anchor and scores exactly spine = FAIL).
  e) Wider sweep: leash/ridge ranges bracketing the 0.75 cm drift bound from BOTH sides.
  f) Locked ablation arm: direct-DOF emission (residual-vs-end-to-end decision).
  g) Fix the p′/FF′ split scorer operator-arm NaN (invalid sqrt in imas_ambix/gs/operator.py
     ladder refit when operator-corrected currents leave the valid domain) so the split
     criterion can score. Consider synthetic-truth sequences (exact boundaries, exact eddy
     histories) as genuinely-supervised boundary signal — ground truth there, not distillation.

Re-plan the temporal thesis — where can the time axis actually enter the equilibrium?

R2 is closed as a SUSTAINED negative after two clean, fully-instrumented tests: the operator reliably converts sensor history into ~21% whitened sensor-misfit gains, and with the topology terms those gains come without boundary drift, saddle damage, or spine regression — yet none of it converts into referee-measured skill (flat-top, ramp-up, axis, p′/FF′ split all unmoved; the ramp-up advantage over the disc read is inherited from the D2 spine). Objective degeneracy and plumbing are now controlled and are NOT the bottleneck. The re-plan must decide where temporal information can enter at all: (a) the frozen-ψ_N linearised decode caps boundary movement exactly where the spine is wrong (ramp-up) — an iterated/self-consistent decode may be required; (b) real-data boundary truth is unavailable by construction (EFIT firewalled) — synthetic-truth sequences with exact boundaries/eddies may need to become the PRIMARY supervised signal with domain transfer, not a pretrainer; (c) an information-ceiling probe on synthetic machines (can ANY function of sensor history beat the per-slice solve at a known-truth referee?) would test the thesis directly before more training; (d) the gate could re-scope to ramp-up where the irreducibles provably live. Lead decision needed on direction before any further R2-class training.
