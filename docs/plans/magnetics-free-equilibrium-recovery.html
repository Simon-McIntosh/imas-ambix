§1 — Objective and doctrine

Predict EFIT-scored equilibria (ψ on a 65×65 grid, q95, βN) from coil currents, plasma current and Thomson scattering alone — no magnetic diagnostics — on DIII-D, with zero-shot transfer to MAST. The purpose is not the prize: the challenge is the only available adversarially-scored, cross-machine rehearsal of the ITER data-analysis pipeline before ITER operations, and it stress-tests exactly the seam authority this project already committed to (nova transfers physics exactly; ambix learns closures and corrections from classified innovation). Grounded competition facts, the FAIR-MAST public-access statement, and the full data doctrine live in the research doc sophelio-fusion-equilibrium-challenge; the four doctrine decisions are locked below and bind every phase: the derivative-level firewall, the FAIR-MAST usage boundary (machine descriptions and signal semantics only — no training use, MAST EFIT firewalled), the sealed zero-shot rehearsal, and the two-rung quarantined referee-emission layer.

The physics thesis: because Te and ne are flux functions, every Thomson channel is a soft isoflux constraint — pseudo-magnetics — but only jointly with its chord geometry. Per frame this under-determines the interior; over a shot, current-diffusion inertia makes the interior flux functions slowly-drifting parameters that hundreds of frames of boundary pins over-determine. Thomson pins the boundary and pressure; transport pins the interior; the ensemble lets each fill the other's null space. Vertical placement carries no special constraint: the recorded coil currents are the closed-loop currents, so the converged free-boundary solve (NK-stabilised, in nova) places the plasma where force balance puts it, and any residual LCFS misfit against Thomson is attributed by the smoother across its typed error sources — coil-current nuisances, position and turns corrections, passive currents, flux-function deviations — never absorbed by a solver-side pin.

§2 — Challenge ingest and label-pedigree probe

Stand up loaders for the parquet corpus (nested per-shot arrays: actuator waveforms, Thomson profiles with chord coordinates, labeled ψ sequences on efit_times, per-shot coil geometry), verify the reported-but-unverified facts from the research doc §5 (frame counts, layout count, retention), and run the pedigree probe on the labels themselves: vacuum-region Δ*ψ≈0 receipts, basis-structure analysis of extracted profiles, and frame-quality flags. The probe's outcome locks the label-pedigree-response decision — how aggressively the interior of extracted flux functions may be trusted for closure tuning.

Done-when: corpus loads end-to-end with schema tests; research-doc §5 facts confirmed or corrected in place; pedigree probe report banked and the decision locked.

§3 — Thomson measurement models

The observation-model library, built over nova's response kernels (chord→ψN sampling is nova's; everything with a σ, a prior or a decision in it is ours): a pedestal-foot separatrix detector with calibrated uncertainty (trained on ψN-at-chord values from train labels — derivative-level consumption, disclosed), ELM-phase and channel-validity policy (widen σ, don't drop), isoflux pairing of matched-Te channels across non-collinear chords, and the isotherm-asymmetry Shafranov-shift operator (a direct βp+ℓi/2 measurement that needs no training data — its value on the zero-shot machine is exactly that).

Done-when: detector calibration curves on held-out DIII-D shots are banked (coverage within pre-registered band); validity policy is typed by topology class; each operator has synthetic round-trip tests against nova-solved equilibria.

§4 — Closure calibration from extracted flux functions

The strongest training signal in the challenge requires no equilibrium solves: applying Δ* to each labeled map gives jφ(R,Z), and per-surface radial separation recovers p′(ψ) and FF′(ψ) corpus-wide (nova owns the extraction operator with conditioning-aware uncertainty; this phase owns its statistical use). Integrating p′ gives total pressure, which beside Thomson pe calibrates the pe→p closure multipliers against collisionality and beam proxies. FF′ evolution calibrates the current-diffusion closure — multiplicative, tightly-priored corrections to Sauter/Redl-class dimensionless forms, never free-form profiles, so what is learned is machine-portable by construction. The aspect-ratio extrapolation (DIII-D A≈2.5 → MAST A≈1.3) is this phase's named risk: tuned forms are validated across the jump against nova's own MAST reconstructions before the transfer bet is trusted (validation only — the sealed-rehearsal decision governs; no MAST parameter feedback). This phase supplies the training corpus the learned-closure-current-diffusion-v0 plan has been waiting for.

Done-when: transport-evolved FF′ beats the untuned closure against extracted FF′ on held-out DIII-D shots by a pre-registered margin; ℓi skill measured (this is the ℓi gate); pe→p closure calibration banked with collisionality dependence.

§5 — Hierarchical nuisance fit and the windowed smoother

Two estimation tiers. Campaign tier, run once on train labels: machine-description corrections that are shot-constant — coil position/turns corrections (ECOILA, bcoil ambiguities), systematic actuator scale factors — fitted hierarchically across the labeled corpus and frozen. Shot tier: a windowed iterated ensemble smoother over ~12–16 parameters per window (pe→p multipliers, 3–5 FF′ basis amplitudes as deviations from the transport-evolved mean, Zeff, 2–4 vessel-current eigenmodes, tightly-priored actuator nuisances), windows-as-DA-cycles per the seam's waveform-exchange contract, innovations classified and attributed across typed error sources, only NK-converged members admitted (innovation purity). Ip enters via the contract's integral-constraint policy, not as an observation. Ensemble sizes are set by the measured throughput budget nova publishes, not assumed.

Done-when: flat-top held-out DIII-D R²_ψ meets the pre-registered floor bar — this gate is the minimum submittable entry; a first leaderboard submission is made from it; a magnetics-free ablation table (which observation family carries which score term) is banked. If the bar is missed, remaining schedule goes to diagnosis, not to later phases.

§6 — Limited-phase traversal

Labels for limited frames are deleted corpus-wide, but the transport and eddy state at the first scored (diverted) frame is an integral over the limited ramp — the ramp must be dynamically right, not scored-right. A reduced model (passive circuits + current diffusion, no full free-boundary solve) traverses breakdown to the first diverted frame and hands the smoother its initial state with honest spread. Validation is the standing first-diverted-frame-error vs time-since-transition diagnostic on train. This phase is deliberately general: the same traversal problem is the ITER first-plasma rehearsal's opening move, and the machinery feeds iter-first-plasma-rehearsal directly.

Done-when: first-diverted-frame error is statistically indistinguishable from mid-flat-top error, or its degradation is bounded and reported; ramp-frame score contribution measured on the public leaderboard split.

§7 — Amortizer on the token substrate

A geometry-conditioned amortized posterior — an extension of the absolute-geometry-token-substrate encoder (tokens carry signal + chord/coil geometry + validity), never a parallel module family — proposes warm-start states so the smoother runs with fewer outer iterations and smaller ensembles. Its training targets on train shots come nearly free from §4's extraction plus §5's frozen campaign tier, not from expensive smoother runs. The network proposes; the smoother disposes — approximation error never touches the scored artifact.

Done-when: warm-started smoother reproduces cold-started results within tolerance at ≥3× throughput on the held-out set.

§8 — Referee-emission layer (quarantined)

The only map-level EFIT contact in the entry, applied after the physics stack has emitted its equilibrium, and carrying its own provenance flags. Rung 1, deterministic and machine-portable: project our flux functions onto the referee's representation conventions (basis truncation, boundary smoothing, grid/COCOS conventions) and re-solve — on MAST this uses our in-house knowledge of EFIT++ conventions, no MAST data involved. Rung 2, learned residual: DIII-D-gated, trained last, switched off for every MAST prediction, justified only by a measured leaderboard delta. Our own model's skill bar remains >1 against raw reality; this layer exists because the competition's scoring function is EFIT agreement, and it is disclosed as such in the methods report.

Done-when: rung-1 delta measured on both machines; automated check proves the MAST emission path is rung-2-free; provenance flags round-trip through the submission format.

§9 — Sealed zero-shot rehearsal, submission, methods report

Pipeline freeze, then the sealed rehearsal: one pass of the frozen pipeline over MAST challenge inputs, referenced against nova's own MAST equilibrium reconstructions (from magnetics, outside the challenge, MAST EFIT never read), results banked as evidence with no parameter feedback. Then the blind-final submissions (three, Oct 19–26) and the methods report: external-material disclosure (machine descriptions, signal semantics, any archives), the FAIR-MAST statement — publicly accessible, deliberately unused for training — the doctrine decisions, and the provenance trail from receipts. The submission pipeline enforces the no-post-processing rule mechanically (the emitted map is written untouched).

Done-when: sealed-rehearsal evidence doc banked (with the seal's integrity honestly reported); blind-final submissions made; methods report published; a lessons-learned evidence record wires outcomes back to the north-star plan.

§ Decisions

How may EFIT-labeled maps be consumed without breaching the EFIT firewall?

We operate below the map: labels are mined for physically-typed coefficients that calibrate transport and pressure closures in machine-portable form. The physics stack is never EFIT-supervised at map level; the program's skill bar stays >1 against raw reality, which is the firewall's purpose. Claims of superiority to EFIT must be scored against raw measurements, never against EFIT-derived quantities, to avoid circularity.

What may be taken from the publicly accessible FAIR-MAST archive?

Training on public MAST data and claiming zero-shot transfer would make the transfer number meaningless to us, and the transfer number is why we are entering. The public accessibility of FAIR-MAST and our decision not to use it for training are stated in the deliverable (methods report and research doc §6).

How is zero-shot transfer demonstrated for ourselves before the blind final?

A single sealed pass demonstrates zero-shot in our own accounting without contaminating it; iterating would quietly convert validation into tuning.

What form may the competition-facing EFIT correction take?

The correction exists to match the referee, not to improve the physics; it therefore lives at emission, quarantined, where it cannot leak into the model whose skill target is better-than-EFIT.

How aggressively may the interior of extracted flux functions be trusted for closure tuning, given the §2 pedigree probe outcome (magnetics-only vs MSE-constrained labels)?

Where does the §5 flat-top floor bar come from?
