§1 — Thesis: solve the low-dimensional evolution, not the map

The magnetics-free entry does not need to invert a 65×65 flux map per slice. A diverted equilibrium is determined, given conductor currents and force balance, by two flux functions — p′(ψ) and FF′(ψ) — plus a handful of nuisances. Per frame those are under-determined by Thomson alone; over a shot they are slowly drifting parameters that hundreds of frames over-determine, because resistive current diffusion gives the interior real inertia. The estimation problem is therefore a low-dimensional state-space model: transport is the transition kernel, the free-boundary GS solve is the state-to-observable geometry, and the Thomson chord model is the observation operator. That is a Kalman-filter structure with exact nonlinear forwards, and the entry plan (magnetics-free-equilibrium-recovery §5) builds its production form as a windowed iterated ensemble smoother over ~12–16 parameters per window.

This plan is the fast spike in front of that production lane. It answers three questions on a ~20-shot DIII-D train subset, cheaply and honestly, before the full corpus ingest and the hardened kernels land:

  • Are the labels extractable? Does Δ* plus per-surface radial separation recover smooth p′/FF′ trajectories from labeled maps, with clean vacuum-region receipts? (A small-scale preview of the entry's second kill gate.)
  • Does transport carry the interior? How much of the extracted flux-function evolution does the ψ-diffusion forward explain, against persistence and against an untuned closure? (The quantitative form of the "current-diffusion inertia" claim the whole entry rests on.)
  • Does the joined loop close? On one shot, does a toy low-dimensional smoother — transport predict, GS solve, Thomson innovation, reduced correction — track the labeled equilibrium? (The Kalman demo, end to end.)

Authority and hygiene. Extraction, chord-sampling and transport kernels are nova's (consumed, never re-implemented); every σ, detector, prior and update is ambix's. Prototype code is a throwaway-labeled spike under scripts/ named by mechanism, and its measurements — not its code — are the deliverable: they feed the entry plan's open decisions (label-pedigree-response, floor-bar-source) and the closure plan's loss-set-v0. Nothing here touches the production statespace lane, and nothing here reads FAIR-MAST or MAST EFIT.

§2 — Subset pull and flux-function extraction

Pull a ~20-shot DIII-D train subset from the challenge corpus (https://fusion-equilibrium-challenge.sophelio.io/, parquet, one row per shot) to /work/projects/imas_gpu/sophelio/ — regime-spanning per the subset-composition decision, including at least three shots with long flat-tops and three with substantial ramp coverage. For every labeled slice: apply Δ* to the labeled ψ map to get j_φ, separate per-surface into p′(ψ) and FF′(ψ), assemble the flux-surface-averaged geometry record on the toroidal-flux coordinate, and bank the vacuum-region Δ*ψ≈0 receipt beside each frame. Where the existing transport-seam FSA machinery in nova covers a step, call it; report — do not patch around — whatever it cannot yet do (that gap list is direct input to nova:diiid-forward-onboarding §5 hardening).

Done when: p′/FF′/ψ(ρ̂) trajectories with FSA records are banked for ≥15 shots; vacuum receipts pass on the clear majority of frames with failures characterised, not discarded; on ≥3 shots the extracted flux functions re-solve through the nova forward to the labeled map near the grid-resolution floor (a small-scale preview of the entry's second kill gate); a smoothness/basis-structure summary of the extracted trajectories is banked as evidence for the label-pedigree question.

§3 — Simulability: how much of the evolution does transport explain?

Drive the native ψ-diffusion rung (nova/transport/current_diffusion.py) over each shot's extracted geometry and measured I_p waveform, from an extracted initial state. Fit the bounded low-degree-of-freedom resistivity profile across shots, never per slice. Score the transport-evolved FF′ (and ψ(ρ̂)) against the extracted trajectories at every labeled slice, against two controls: persistence of the initial state, and the same solve with the untuned default closure. When the TORAX ≥ 1.4.3 environment lands (nova:flux-function-forward-transport §3), repeat the headline row through the TORAX rung as a cross-check; do not block on it.

Done when: an explained-variance table (per-shot and pooled; flat-top and ramp scored separately) is banked with a pre-registered verdict line: transport beats persistence on FF′ evolution across the subset, or it does not, with the margin quantified. Either outcome is a result — a negative bounds how much §5-style smoothing correction the entry must carry, and how tight the FF′ deviation priors can be.

§4 — The joined loop, once, end to end

On one flat-top shot from the subset, close the loop in the figure: transport predict over a window, nova free-boundary solve per member, chord→ψ_N Thomson sampling, and a reduced-rank innovation update of the low-dimensional state — reusing the reduced-observable-correction machinery from imas_ambix/statespace/sequential_da.py with the observation operator swapped from magnetics to the Thomson forward. The Thomson measurement model is the minimal honest one: matched-Te isoflux pairing across non-collinear chords plus a pedestal-foot proxy, with widened σ rather than dropped channels. I_p enters as the transport boundary condition, never as an observation.

Done when: the one-shot demo is banked with innovation statistics (whiteness, per-channel normalised residuals), LCFS distance and q95/βN error against the label at each analysed slice, and a short lessons note stating what the production smoother (magnetics-free-equilibrium-recovery §5) should keep, change or drop — including whether the SVD-reduced correction basis is adequate for a Thomson-only operator.

§5 — Exclusions

No full-corpus ingest (the entry plan's §2 owns that); no closure learning (the learned-closure plan owns that); no leaderboard submission; no MAST data of any kind; no changes to the production statespace or gs modules; no new nova physics — gaps in nova kernels are reported to the owning nova plans, not patched here.
