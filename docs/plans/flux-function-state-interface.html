§1 — Authority boundary

Ambix owns inference, temporal evolution and correction of equilibrium source functions. It consumes diagnostics, actuators and prior transport state; propagates a causal ensemble; applies learned corrections to uncertain closures; and emits the physical state that makes an axisymmetric equilibrium problem fully specified. Ambix does not choose a free-boundary flux map, classify the resulting topology, fit magnetics inside a Grad–Shafranov iteration, or carry a second copy of Nova's field kernels.

Nova owns the deterministic physics operator. It takes conductor currents, machine geometry and the supplied flux-function state; constructs force-balanced current sources; solves the free-boundary fixed point; and reports topology plus force, field and current-conservation diagnostics. This moves the traditional equilibrium-regression boundary upstream: the regression is an Ambix state-estimation concern, while Nova's public solve contains no measurement design matrix and no least-squares profile update.

The same authority split covers transport, not only the Grad–Shafranov solve. Nova owns the deterministic transport forward solve as well: given the flux-surface-averaged geometry record, an initial profile state, actuator waveforms and a declared source and transport-model selection, Nova advances the 1D flux-surface state and returns conservation receipts (nova:flux-function-forward-transport). Transport fidelity — from the native current-diffusion rung to the embedded TORAX multi-channel step — is a declared model selection inside Nova's contract, never a silent approximation the consumer must reverse-engineer. Ambix's role is unchanged on both sides of the loop: it conditions what enters the physics forwards and corrects what leaves them, and a transport-evolved state enters this contract as a first-class causal producer.

  1. Transport-forward adapter. Convert Nova transport forward outputs — CurrentDiffusion today, the multi-channel ForwardTransport rungs as they land — and Ambix learned closure corrections into one causal FluxFunctionState. A projection onto a named representation is permitted only as deterministic state conversion; it must not use equilibrium measurements or re-fit a slice.
  2. Sequential-estimator adapter. Batch ensemble members without collapsing them to one mean solve. Preserve forecast, analysis and smoothing labels; only forecast/analysis states qualify for an online handoff.
  3. New-physics registry. Admit a term only with a declared force-balance model, independent inputs, units and a manufactured equilibrium. Rotation begins with an isothermal-surface toroidal-flow closure; generic source-basis terms stay diagnostics until physically identified.
  4. SOL extension. Represent toroidal SOL current through pressure and toroidal-field-function gradients on topology-qualified open regions. Field-aligned or poloidal current is a separate contract because it requires F(psi) and current closure at material boundaries, not merely a toroidal-current image outside the LCFS.
  5. Integral-constraint conditioner. Treat I_p, beta_p and l_i as observations of the supplied profile state. A one-amplitude shape family may use I_p as an explicit scalar closure. Two or more moment targets require the same number of physically named profile degrees of freedom plus an Ambix prior or covariance; that conditioning occurs before the Nova handoff and retains posterior provenance. Nova supplies the differentiable moment operator and its residual/Jacobian, but does not choose the correction.
  6. IMAS egress. Provide a lossless adapter to equilibrium profiles_1d where the selected Data Dictionary can express the state. Keep model-specific closure and uncertainty metadata in a versioned extension until a standard field exists; do not overload an unrelated IDS quantity.

Response kernels vs measurement models — produce, don't use

Diagnostic forward models split across the seam on one rule: Nova produces every deterministic response kernel and fits with none of them — coupling operators to sensors and grid points (the magnetics design matrix Nova builds but does not use), chord→ψN flux-surface sampling, line-integral synthetic diagnostics, and map-derivative operators such as Δ* extraction — while everything statistical about a signal stays in Ambix: noise and calibration nuisances, validity and phase policy, feature detectors, innovation construction and classification. Litmus: differentiable physics with a geometry argument is a Nova kernel; anything carrying a σ, a prior or a decision is an Ambix measurement model. Kinetic-profile diagnostics (Thomson) enter as pseudo-magnetics through exactly this split — the sampling kernel is Nova's, the isoflux claim and its confidence are Ambix's. §5 — Explicit exclusions

This plan does not choose a profile basis for learning, train the closure model, solve the Grad–Shafranov equation, or define a sheath/halo-current model. Profile conditioning against I_p or moment observations reuses the existing Ambix current-recovery and learned-closure authority; Nova's ForwardProfile supplies the deterministic observation operator and field, while a future open-field boundary-current model is required for material return currents.

§ Decisions

Which coordinate is canonical at the Ambix–Nova seam? Recommendation: normalised poloidal flux with the physical total-flux Jacobian carried explicitly, because it matches the free-boundary source evaluation while retaining Pa/Wb and T²/Wb derivatives.

Normalized poloidal flux is the stable producer-consumer coordinate, while the explicit total-flux Jacobian preserves physical derivative units and enables COCOS validation.

Does Ambix emit absolute physical source functions, or shapes plus a separate plasma-current target? Recommendation: absolute sources by default; allow a one-amplitude I_p closure only as a declared profile family with a target, tolerance, uniqueness receipt and provenance.

Transport and Ambix profiles are authoritative physical sources; target-current normalization is opt-in through a separately declared closure rather than implicit consumer scaling.

If beta_p or l_i must be matched rather than reported, where is the profile correction performed? Recommendation: condition the Ambix profile ensemble using Nova's differentiable moment map; do not add coefficient fitting to ForwardProfile .

Matching beta_p or l_i changes profile degrees of freedom and therefore belongs to Ambix inference with prior and uncertainty; Nova supplies the deterministic differentiable observation map.

When you say SOL current, is the first target toroidal current density from SOL pressure and F F' gradients, or also poloidal/field-aligned current into material surfaces? Recommendation: toroidal source-function current first; treat field-aligned current as a separately closed boundary-current problem.

Toroidal SOL current is representable by topology-qualified pressure and poloidal-current-function derivatives; field-aligned current additionally requires material return-current and sheath or halo closure.

What is the authoritative uncertainty payload? Recommendation: weighted ensemble members, because Nova can batch the nonlinear solves and preserve non-Gaussian topology changes that a mean/covariance pair cannot.

Weighted members preserve non-Gaussian uncertainty and member-specific topology changes that a mean and covariance summary can erase.
