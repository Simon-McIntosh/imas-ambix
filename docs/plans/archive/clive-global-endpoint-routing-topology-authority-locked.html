§2 · Why it was reopened

The choice was implemented by injecting launch-owned metadata into the serving process as AMBIX_VLLM_CATALOG_METADATA and having the engine echo it back through /v1/models. The engine contributed no knowledge: it was a courier for data the launcher already owned and already wrote to the serve's ServeRegistration at the same moment, from the same profile.

Because the echo depended on an ASGI middleware that only vLLM accepts, an engine without that channel could be healthy, serving and answering tool calls while every consumer refused it. The wording — in the global model response — was therefore load-bearing in a way its stated reason was not: the reason required only that the metadata be site-owned and never inferred client-side, which a registration written by the serve satisfies.

The superseding decision keeps the protection and drops the transport. See the live plan for the re-locked choice and its rationale.
