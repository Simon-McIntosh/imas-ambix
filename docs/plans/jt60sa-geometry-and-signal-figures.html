Seeing what the map binds

JT-60SA Geometry and Signal Figures

The JT-60SA machine map (jt60sa-machine-map) has converted the facility's SELENE input decks into an IMAS machine description for each operating phase, and cached the first EDDB pulses, yet nothing of it has been drawn. This plan draws it: the poloidal cross-section of each phase with every conductor and sensor, what changes between OP1 and OP2, and the cached coil, plasma-current and flux-loop signals. The figures are evidence the map's census and convention work will read, and they run in their own session, independent of the map's build.

§1 — Inputs, the style module and the palette

Every input is already on GPFS, so no node in this plan contacts the JT-60SA analysis server (no ssh jt-60sa, no EDDB call) and none writes under /work/projects/imas_gpu/jt60sa. The geometry is the converted description store under JT60SA_DESCRIPTION_DIR (imas_ambix/data/paths.py), one directory per phase map the JT-60SA catalogue declares (OP1 and OP2), one IMAS netCDF file per IDS (pf_active, pf_passive, magnetics, wall, tf) at DD 4.1.1, read through imas.DBEntry. Measured 2026-10-06: both phases carry twelve PF coils (CS1–CS4, EF1–EF6 and the two FPPC coils) with 40, 40, 40, 40, 16, 16, 16, 16, 16, 16, 6 and 6 elements; OP1's vessel is two resistivity blocks, loops VV1 (63 filaments) and VV2 (57), and OP2's is one loop VV1 (98); magnetics holds 17 pickup probes (MP1–MP17, with their poloidal angle) and 27 flux loops (FL1–FL27). The signals are the EDDB cache under JT60SA_ROOT, whose pulse layout, time-base sibling and unit attribute imas_ambix/data/eddb.py owns: 101154.zarr (MDAC 38, MMSYS 22 and PSRC 59 channels), 101173.zarr (MDAC 38, MMSYS 22) and 60033.zarr, the 2026 commissioning record C060033 (MMSYS 22, MDAC 4).

Reuse, from a search of imas_ambix/data/, imas_ambix/gs/ and scripts/ on 2026-10-06. Every root resolves through imas_ambix.data.paths (JT60SA_ROOT, JT60SA_DESCRIPTION_DIR), never a literal path in a script. A cached channel is read through the cache's own addressing in imas_ambix.data.eddb (channel_path, channel_time_path, UNIT_ATTR) rather than by re-deriving its layout; VirtualZarrView is not used because it reads through a compiled signal map and none is packaged for JT-60SA yet. The geometry is read from the store with imas.DBEntry, not through description_reader.read_geometry_table, the sole acquisition route, because these figures are evidence of what the converter wrote and that route hands back the geometry adapter's table, transformed before the figure would see it. The store is still addressed through the catalogue that owns it, imas_ambix/data/machine_maps/jt-60sa.json (landed 2026-10-06): the reader loads it with load_packaged_machine_map("jt-60sa"), takes the root from description_store_root_path(), and joins each phase map's name from catalog.maps, the static-over-map addressing transform_engine applies, so no script names a phase directory itself. The projection facade in imas_ambix/gs/ (MachineGeometryService.operator() returning OperatorGeometry, build_geometry_fields_from_table in geometry_export.py) and the MAST cross-section drawn by scripts/validate_mast_geometry.py from it sit downstream of the same route, through machine_selection into description_reader, and draw the adapted table, so a JT-60SA figure through that facade is a companion to these rather than a substitute for them. The figure scripts consume the store and nothing reads geometry back from them, so they open no second acquisition route.

Figures follow the workstation plot style: the installed data-ink sheet, a 14 in canvas at 100 dpi varying only in height, no gridlines or decorative shading, despined axes, direct labels in the series colour, and the explanation in the caption rather than a title. The repository has no style module yet, so this section adds one, imas_ambix/plot_style.py, that applies the sheet and holds the colour roles by name. It is the first and retires nothing: imas_ambix/ holds no plotting style code, and the scripts that style inline, including the two hand-rolled palettes in scripts/figures/wm_demonstration_figs.py and scripts/figures/playable_plasma_figs.py, stay as they are, since restyling them is outside this plan. Its public surface is the named roles, the function that applies the sheet and the direct-label helper §2 and §3 share: one role per component the figures draw (PF coils, OP1 vessel, OP2 vessel, wall, flux loops, pickup probes) and the neutrals for references. Because this is the repository's first colour assignment, the lead chooses it: the section renders two to four candidate palettes on the real OP1 and OP2 cross-section, each pair of colours that meet in a figure checked to a colour-blind ΔE of at least 8, and records the choice as a decision before §2 and §3 draw with it. The candidates are drawn by scripts/figures_jt60sa_geometry.py, whose one reader turns a phase's store into drawable families (coil elements, vessel filaments, wall outline, flux loops, probes) and whose one cross-section function draws them; §2 extends that script and calls the same reader rather than reading the store a second time.

Done-when: imas_ambix/plot_style.py exists and applies data-ink; two to four candidate cross-sections are written under docs/figures/jt60sa-palette/, each captioned with its role-to-colour table and its ΔE check; the node's evidence fragment shows them; and the landing records an open decision palette in this plan naming the candidates for the lead to choose.

§2 — Geometry: each phase, and what changes between them

Two figures, drawn by scripts/figures_jt60sa_geometry.py from the description store, read with imas.DBEntry as the reuse note in §1 explains, with the chosen palette. The first is the poloidal cross-section of OP1 and OP2 side by side on one shared R and Z scale: every PF coil element as its rectangle, every vessel filament, the wall and limiter outline, the flux loops as points and the pickup probes as short ticks along their measured poloidal angle, each coil and sensor family labelled directly. The second overlays the two phases' vessel and wall on one axis, the only components that change at the OP1 to OP2 boundary, so the change reads without comparing panels. Sensors EDDB acquires but no facility file positions (7 flux loops and 6 probes) are listed in the caption, not drawn.

Done-when: both figures are written as PNG and SVG under docs/figures/jt60sa-geometry/ and embedded with a project-absolute src and a caption in the node's evidence fragment, which the landing carries into this plan; a check in the script asserts the counts drawn equal the counts read from the store (268 coil elements, 120 and 98 vessel filaments, 27 flux loops, 17 probes) and prints them.

§3 — Signals: the cached pulses

Three figures, drawn by scripts/figures_jt60sa_signals.py from the cache, each channel read through eddb.channel_path and eddb.channel_time_path, with units taken from its eddb.UNIT_ATTR attribute and named on the axis. For E101154, the PF coil currents from MMSYS against time, and the plasma current from PSRC. Also for E101154, the flux loops as small multiples: each processed PSRC magFluxLp channel beside the raw MDAC magFlxLp channel of the same index, which is the raw-to-processed correspondence the map's census must settle, drawn rather than assumed. For C060033, the coil currents of the no-plasma commissioning record, the input the map's convention work uses. Where a channel's time base or unit differs from its neighbours, the figure says so rather than rescaling silently.

Done-when: the three figures are written as PNG and SVG under docs/figures/jt60sa-signals/ and embedded in the node's evidence fragment, which the landing carries into this plan, with a project-absolute src and a caption naming the shot, the channels and their units; the script reads only the cache and prints, per figure, the channel names and units it drew.
