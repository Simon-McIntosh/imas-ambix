Seeing what the map binds

JT-60SA Geometry and Signal Figures

The JT-60SA machine map (jt60sa-machine-map) has converted the facility's SELENE input decks into an IMAS machine description for each operating phase, and cached the first EDDB pulses, yet nothing of it has been drawn. This plan draws it: the poloidal cross-section of each phase with every conductor and sensor, what changes between OP1 and OP2, and the cached coil, plasma-current and flux-loop signals. The figures are evidence the map's census and convention work will read, and they run in their own session, independent of the map's build.

§1 — Inputs, the style module and the palette

Every input is already on GPFS, so no node in this plan contacts the JT-60SA analysis server (no ssh jt-60sa, no EDDB call) and none writes under /work/projects/imas_gpu/jt60sa. The geometry is the converted description store, /work/projects/imas_gpu/jt60sa/machine_description/OP1/ and OP2/, one IMAS netCDF file per IDS (pf_active, pf_passive, magnetics, wall, tf) at DD 4.1.1, read through imas.DBEntry. Measured 2026-10-06: both phases carry twelve PF coils (CS1–CS4, EF1–EF6 and the two FPPC coils) with 40, 40, 40, 40, 16, 16, 16, 16, 16, 16, 6 and 6 elements; OP1's vessel is two resistivity blocks, loops VV1 (63 filaments) and VV2 (57), and OP2's is one loop VV1 (98); magnetics holds 17 pickup probes (MP1–MP17, with their poloidal angle) and 27 flux loops (FL1–FL27). The signals are the EDDB cache under /work/projects/imas_gpu/jt60sa/, one {shot}.zarr per pulse laid out {category}/{dname} with a sibling {dname}_time array and the EDDB unit string as an attribute: 101154.zarr (MDAC 38, MMSYS 22 and PSRC 59 channels), 101173.zarr (MDAC 38, MMSYS 22) and 60033.zarr, the 2026 commissioning record C060033 (MMSYS 22, MDAC 4).

Figures follow the workstation plot style: the installed data-ink sheet, a 14 in canvas at 100 dpi varying only in height, no gridlines or decorative shading, despined axes, direct labels in the series colour, and the explanation in the caption rather than a title. The repository has no style module yet, so this section adds one, imas_ambix/plot_style.py, that applies the sheet and holds the colour roles by name: one role per component the figures draw (PF coils, OP1 vessel, OP2 vessel, wall, flux loops, pickup probes) and the neutrals for references. Because this is the repository's first colour assignment, the lead chooses it: the section renders two to four candidate palettes on the real OP1 and OP2 cross-section, each pair of colours that meet in a figure checked to a colour-blind ΔE of at least 8, and records the choice as a decision before §2 and §3 draw with it.

Done-when: imas_ambix/plot_style.py exists and applies data-ink; two to four candidate cross-sections are written under docs/figures/jt60sa-palette/, each captioned with its role-to-colour table and its ΔE check; and an open decision palette in this plan names the candidates for the lead to choose.

§2 — Geometry: each phase, and what changes between them

Two figures, drawn by scripts/figures_jt60sa_geometry.py from the description store with the chosen palette. The first is the poloidal cross-section of OP1 and OP2 side by side on one shared R and Z scale: every PF coil element as its rectangle, every vessel filament, the wall and limiter outline, the flux loops as points and the pickup probes as short ticks along their measured poloidal angle, each coil and sensor family labelled directly. The second overlays the two phases' vessel and wall on one axis, the only components that change at the OP1 to OP2 boundary, so the change reads without comparing panels. Sensors EDDB acquires but no facility file positions (7 flux loops and 6 probes) are listed in the caption, not drawn.

Done-when: both figures are written as PNG and SVG under docs/figures/jt60sa-geometry/ and embedded in this plan with a project-absolute src and a caption; a check in the script asserts the counts drawn equal the counts read from the store (268 coil elements, 120 and 98 vessel filaments, 27 flux loops, 17 probes) and prints them.

§3 — Signals: the cached pulses

Three figures, drawn by scripts/figures_jt60sa_signals.py from the cache, with units taken from each channel's EDDB unit attribute and named on the axis. For E101154, the PF coil currents from MMSYS against time, and the plasma current from PSRC. Also for E101154, the flux loops as small multiples: each processed PSRC magFluxLp channel beside the raw MDAC magFlxLp channel of the same index, which is the raw-to-processed correspondence the map's census must settle, drawn rather than assumed. For C060033, the coil currents of the no-plasma commissioning record, the input the map's convention work uses. Where a channel's time base or unit differs from its neighbours, the figure says so rather than rescaling silently.

Done-when: the three figures are written as PNG and SVG under docs/figures/jt60sa-signals/ and embedded in this plan with a project-absolute src and a caption naming the shot, the channels and their units; the script reads only the cache and prints, per figure, the channel names and units it drew.
