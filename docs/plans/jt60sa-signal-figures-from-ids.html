§4 — Redraw the flux loops from the differential entries

The machine-map decision jt60sa-differential-flux-loops landed its rules on 2026-10-08. The magnetics store now holds 53 loops: the 27 type-1 loops at indices 0–26 and 26 type-6 entries at indices 27–52. Each of loops 1–6 and 8–27 is routed to its type-6 entry, with indices_differential [7, L] and channel_factor −1. That stores the DD differential flux ψL − ψ7, the negative of the raw difference, so every differential loop changes sign relative to the run files §2 drew. Their type-1 entries carry geometry and no flux. FL7 stays at index 6, unchanged. jt60sa-pulse-ids §7 writes both pulses again under these rules.

Once that rewrite lands, this section re-runs scripts/figures_jt60sa_signals.py unchanged. extract_signal_traces skips any entry whose data is empty, so the same ("magnetics", "flux_loop/flux") call draws FL7 and then the 26 differential entries, each labelled with its IDS name. The lead ruled on 2026-10-07 that the redraw follows the IDS: the differential loops appear with the data dictionary's sign, the caption says so, and the script never re-signs data.

Done when the re-run, in place on the fleet allocation, rewrites both flux-loop SVGs with 27 panels: FL7 first, then the 26 differential entries in index order, each labelled with its IDS name.
