JT-60SA Signal Figures From IDS

The signal figures that jt60sa-geometry-and-signal-figures §3 landed on 2026-10-06 were not drawn from IDS. scripts/figures_jt60sa_signals.py built imas-ink TimeSeries from bare arrays: PF coil currents and Ip came from the signal map's VirtualZarrView, the flux loops were raw PSRC and MDAC EDDB channels read through read_channel, and the caller supplied every label and unit string. The lead ruled on 2026-10-07 that imas-ink draws only from IDS inputs, or from signals carrying standard-name metadata.

The pulse mint has since written E101154 as IDS, at /work/projects/imas_gpu/jt60sa/ids/101154/ (run r-20261006T164055420259): pf_active carries the ten coil currents CS1–EF6 and magnetics carries Ip. This plan redraws from those IDS through imas-ink's extract_signal_traces, added by the imas-ink plan ids-signal-traces, and withdraws everything that has no IDS behind it.

§2 — Flux loops and probes, drawn as measured

The run files /work/projects/imas_gpu/jt60sa/ids/101154_0.nc and 101031_0.nc carry all 27 flux loops (FL1–FL27) and all 17 poloidal-field probes in magnetics, each on its own time base (homogeneous time 0). Loop 7 is absolute. Loops 1–6 and 8–27 are measured as differences relative to loop 7, and the files store those differences as measured. The lead ruled on 2026-10-07 that this is how the signals are measured and that they are plotted as such. So the figures draw the stored values unchanged, and each flux-loop caption states that every loop except FL7 is a difference relative to FL7.

The section adds two more figure calls per pulse through the existing _pulse_figure: one with the pair ("magnetics", "flux_loop/flux"), one with ("magnetics", "b_field_pol_probe/field"). They write jt60sa-E101154-flux-loops.svg, jt60sa-E101154-pf-probes.svg and the same two for E101031 into docs/figures/jt60sa-signals/, one panel per loop or probe. Names and units come from the IDS through extract_signal_traces; the script passes no label, unit or array of its own, and does not rescale or re-sign a signal. The two coil-current SVGs stay byte-identical.

The machine-map decision jt60sa-differential-flux-loops will later move loops 1–6 and 8–27 to type-6 entries with indices_differential [7, L], storing the DD differential flux ψL − ψ7. That flips the sign of loops 12–27 relative to today's files and leaves their type-1 entries without flux. Its coordinator, imas-ambix-5b, reports on 2026-10-07 that the adapter, the store regeneration and the retarget have not started and are several hours away. When the pulses are written under those rules, the flux-loop figures are redrawn from the type-6 entries; 5b will signal.
