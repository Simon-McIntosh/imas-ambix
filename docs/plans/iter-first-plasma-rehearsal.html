ITER First-Plasma Rehearsal

Goal. Before ITER supplies a useful empirical corpus, place the validated machine-portable estimator on ITER geometry, feed proposed coil and actuator trajectories, and ask what it would forecast, reconstruct from commissioning-grade signals and learn from its first surprises. This is a rehearsal and uncertainty exercise, not a claim to know burning-plasma closures in advance.

Required products

  • Ensemble equilibria and flux maps for nominal, perturbed and counterfactual coil/current trajectories.
  • Forecast, causal analysis and fixed-lag/full-shot smoothed state products with unmistakable provenance labels.
  • Expected magnetics, flux-surface averages and available diagnostic signals; conditional camera streams as a by-product where an ITER observation geometry exists.
  • Innovation and OOD dashboards that distinguish transferable closure uncertainty from machine-description, calibration, missing-source and topology hypotheses.
  • A first-shot update protocol: how evidence changes the closure posterior without modifying conservation or force-balance laws.

Transfer gates

  1. Leave-one-machine-out tests on current machines demonstrate zero-shot and limited-adaptation skill separately.
  2. Dimensionless/invariant coordinates and diagnostic geometry are load-bearing under machine holdout.
  3. The Nova revision carries independent GHCR parity evidence and CPU/GPU agreement before its results enter Ambix.
  4. ITER scenarios remain finite, physically valid and uncertainty-aware under geometry and actuator perturbations.
  5. The direct learned generative challenger may propose or distil trajectories, but the Nova-led ensemble remains the reference until parity is earned.

H200 rehearsal workload

Use the eight H200s to run wide scenario ensembles, topology branches, complete-shot smoothers and camera/diagnostic decoding in parallel. Maintain CPU and one-H200 reference cases and report weak/strong scaling separately from scientific skill. The workload should exploit the server fully without coupling the deployed ITER estimator irreducibly to eight cards.
