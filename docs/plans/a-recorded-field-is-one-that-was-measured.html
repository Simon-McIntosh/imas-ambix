§1 — Why this is a new plan rather than a reopened one

These three items were carried as an open followup on a plan marked shipped at impl 1.0. Reckon excludes a completed plan from pending_work and from every open path, so the work was invisible to the roadmap and to anyone reading the sprint — it existed only for someone who opened the finished plan and read past its conclusion. That is the hidden-work trap, and the remedy is a live plan rather than a reopened record, so the shipped plan stays an accurate account of what it shipped.

The three share one property, which is why they belong together: in each case the record presents a field as measured when nothing measured it. A reader downstream cannot tell the difference, and the warehouse being built over these records will aggregate them without hesitating.

§2 — The contract the recorder no longer keeps

serving_receipts.py states that a probe which failed, is absent, or did not run contributes no key at all, and that a present key always means a measurement. The job table now breaks both sentences deliberately: it records a marker on a tick where squeue did not answer, because conflating a failed read with an absent source is exactly what hid that section never reading for the entire life of the feature. The behaviour is right and the contract is stale, which is the dangerous ordering — a reader who trusts the docstring will treat a marker as a measurement. Both sentences are quoted verbatim with line numbers in the probe-hostname manifest.

Done when. The stated contract matches what the recorder does, with the marker case named explicitly, and a test asserts the distinction a reader depends on: a failed read and an absent source produce different records, and neither is presented as a measurement.

§3 — A test that cannot fail

No recorded vLLM exposition exists anywhere — not in the tree, not in its history, not in the run records. The vLLM half of the family reader is therefore asserted only against a body synthesised in the test itself, so the test and the code agree because the same author wrote both. A namespace error or a label-shape change in that engine's real output cannot fail any assertion, and the failure would first appear as absent data in production.

This is the sharpest item of the three. A passing test over a synthetic fixture reports a protection it does not provide, and it is indistinguishable from a real one until the engine's output changes.

Done when. A recorded exposition from a real vLLM serve is committed under tests/data/ and the family reader is asserted against it. The control that proves the test can fail: a namespace or label-shape mutation of that recorded body must break the assertion.

§4 — A field that looks measured and is not

cards[].name returns None because the card model is never parsed from the probe output. It is small, and it is precisely the shape the telemetry work exists to stop: a present key carrying nothing, which a consumer renders as a blank rather than as a gap.

One measurement is worth carrying forward here rather than being rediscovered, because the obvious reading of it is wrong. The flat receipt columns were not dead: across twenty recorded files, 141,598 of 258,594 rows carry a flat prefix-cache hit rate, all of them from serves whose engine publishes the counters it is derived from. They are null only for SGLang. A coordinator sampling one file can conclude the column is unused and remove it.

Done when. The card model is parsed from the probe output, or the key is removed so nothing presents an unparsed field as present. The flat-column measurement is recorded in the evidence record so the column is not removed on a single-file sample.
