Open Decisions

Each decision below surfaces a choice that can be made now. Click a choice button to record your decision.

drop-charge-exchange Drop charge_exchange from v0 training corpus?

Audit on a 25-shot L2 sample found ~50% bit-pattern corruption in charge_exchange/t_i and charge_exchange/v_i, with values 10²⁶–10³⁸ (physical range: ≤ 30 keV / 10⁷ m/s). The diagnostic is only present in 37.9% of the 11,573 shots regardless. Source: plans/data-quality.md §10.4.

plasma-decoder-finetune Open-MAGVIT2 plasma-domain decoder fine-tune — when to trigger?

The current ImageNet-pretrained decoder produces MAE 324 (uint16) on MAST rbb shot 15085. Expected rFID on plasma imagery without fine-tune: 10–30. Decoder fine-tune cost: ~4–6 GPU-hours exclusive. Trigger gate (from plan): rFID > 5 on the 100-shot rbb benchmark. Source: plans/tokenizers.md §12.1.

patchtst-real-embedding PatchTST real embedding — defer to v1 vs land in v0?

Current state: identity passthrough (token ID 0, raw floats in metadata). The patch-projection matrix trains end-to-end inside the WHAM trunk. Real PatchTST would add ~1M trainable params with channel-independent self-attention over patches. Estimated effort: 1 Sonnet session. Source: plans/tokenizers.md §12.2.

equilibrium-2d-tokenizer Equilibrium 2-D tokenizer — which architecture?

Currently: equilibrium 2-D enters as a continuous cross-attention tensor (not tokenized). Two options to tokenize: Option A — reuse Open-MAGVIT2 at upsampled 256×256 (0.5 Sonnet sessions, no fine-tune). Option B — Cosmos-Tokenizer-DV at native 65×65 (NVIDIA OML weights, license check needed). Source: plans/tokenizers.md §12.3.

ir-camera-codebook IR camera codebook — share with visible or allocate separate?

Only 25 rir shots in FAIR-MAST (per data-acquisition.md §10.4). Current v0 default: share the Open-MAGVIT2 visible codebook. Decision gate: if IR MAE > 2× rbb MAE, allocate separate block. rFID unreliable with n=25; use MAE as primary metric. Source: plans/tokenizers.md §12.5.

slurm-training-reservation SLURM dedicated training reservation — file with SDCC or stay with exclusive-pause?

Current decision (2026-05-20): exclusive-pause mode — stop DeepSeek V4-Flash before training, restart after. Alternative: file a dedicated gpu_0003_grpA_train reservation (request body in plans/compute.md §3.2). Source: plans/compute.md §2–3.

smoke-training-timing 125M smoke training run — run now or wait for bulk encode?

Training loop FSDP scaffold is landed. Bulk-encode CLI produced 9,380 tokens for shot 15085 (CPU). A smoke run needs ~10 shots. The full GPU bulk-encode for ~3,000 rbb shots is pending the SLURM run. Source: plans/v0-runway.md §4.

demo-shots-selection Demo shots final selection

Current candidates: 30420 + 30421 (FAIR-MAST quickstart shots) + one M6-era TBD. Audit data now available to pick shots with confirmed clean data. Charge-exchange corruption affects ~50% of shots but shots 30420/30421 are M9-era — need to verify their CX status. Source: plans/demo.md §3.

camera-selection-v0 Camera selection for v0 training

Level-1 inventory: rbb = 9,527 shots (55.7%), rba = 6,155 shots (36.0%), rir = 25 shots (rare). rbb is the wide-angle midplane view; rba is the lower/divertor view; rir is infrared divertor. Adding more cameras → more training data per shot but adds IR codebook complexity. Source: plans/data-acquisition.md §11.2.
