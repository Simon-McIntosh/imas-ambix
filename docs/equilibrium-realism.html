§1 — Overview: why realism is the binding attack

The evidence that forced this plan. The plasma-screening-dynamics closure (S1b, 2026-07-18) was infrastructurally clean — flux ledger closing at 3×10⁻⁵, coupled plasma+vessel circuit, breakdown settle window — but its truth family is a plasma that cannot exist: at Ip = 134 kA (26% of flat-top) the truth LCFS spans R ≈ 0.27–0.62 m against Z ≈ −0.55 to +0.75 m — elongation κ ≈ 3.5 at breakdown scale, axis at R ≈ 0.45–0.5 m where the real MAST ramp axis sits near 0.9 m. A real startup is a small near-circular plasma (κ ~ 1.1–1.3) whose shaping is ramped with Ip and li under active vertical control; κ ≈ 3.5 at 130 kA has an n = 0 growth rate no vessel and controller holds. Every gate verdict in that plan — including the decisive negative — was measured on this family.

Premise status (§2.5, corrected 2026-07-19): the first workaround below — "no confined fixed point on real currents" — stands, with a refined mechanism. An initial §2.5 claim that the profile-free solve reversed it was walked back after a deeper test: warm-started at the reconstruction solution (R=0.82) with magnetics off, the free-boundary solve drifts back out to R=1.77, so the forward operator does NOT independently hold the physical equilibrium — it has a spurious outboard attractor. The physical equilibrium exists and is inboard (reality 0.9, reconstruction 0.82, rigid-ring 0.88 agree), but only the measurement-constrained reconstruction reaches it. This is not a sign/COCOS error (ruled out five ways — see the §2.5 card). What genuinely landed in §2.5 (eddy fold + one-interaction-matrix flux solve, clean winding audit, elongation-corrected identity band, verified sign chain) stands; the V2 forward-confinement gate does NOT pass and is re-scoped in §3 (v2-precondition-verdict = no-confined-fixed-point).

Mechanism corrected (§3, 2026-07-19): the §2.5 attribution — a "flux pocket at the in-vessel P4/P5/P6 coils" — is wrong. Instrumented across 36 slices, the forward axis is a genuine conductor-clear O-point at every slice (0/36 on a coil pocket; the coil O-points are excluded), the drift is purely radial (axis |Z| ≤ 0.37 cm — the n=0 vertical mode is stable, so the locked Z-controller is a no-op for V2-forward), and the forward current sits near the physical axis while the magnetic axis is Shafranov-shifted outboard by the profile peakedness — the radial interior-null, externally undetermined without magnetics (reconstruction, which passes) or a learned profile prior. V2 is met in reconstruction mode; V2-forward is the interior-null the learned operator resolves. The original motivation below is the standing record, not superseded; see the §3 record.

Why the chain produced it — three compounding workarounds (as understood at plan creation; the first is superseded per above):

  • On real MAST coil currents the forward operator has no confined fixed point (the outboard attractor, even warm-started at the EFIT axis) — so truths run on a manufactured field: build_confining_i_pf drives every P4/P5/P6 coil uniformly at −60 kA, a pattern no shot ever produced.
  • That fictitious pattern has an uncontrolled decay index — strongly elongating for a small inboard plasma with the in-vessel P6 coils close by — and the gate layered a fixed quadrupole knob on top. No decay-index, Shafranov vertical-field, or vertical-stability criterion exists anywhere in the repo.
  • The resulting n = 0 instability was silenced with a z_symmetric solve pin plus an ad-hoc up/down trim — the instability was the physics reporting that the scenario is unphysical, and the messenger was suppressed.

The unresolved tension this plan settles first. The vacuum-shot campaign validated coil→sensor predictions at 0.1–0.44σ (solenoid 8.25% scale found and fixed) — yet the same operator cannot confine a plasma under measured currents where a real plasma demonstrably sat. Both cannot be unqualified truths about "the coil model": either the error lives where sensors do not sample (the field at the plasma, not at the sensor ring), or the attractor is not a coil-model error at all and is solve-side (Picard basin, boundary-ψ read, profile family). The differential diagnosis is cheap and uses only physics we fully trust — radial force balance and the vacuum decay index.

End-goal framing (GenCast). GenCast is trustworthy because ERA5 is, and ERA5 is trustworthy because the assimilating model carries all confident atmospheric physics. Our spine is the assimilation engine that will produce reanalysis-grade equilibrium labels over the 12.6k-shot corpus; the learned operator is the GenCast trained on them. The spine is validated statically at flat-top (1.50 cm held-out LCFS) and unvalidated dynamically, because it cannot yet hold a confined equilibrium through a single real shot's coil program. Closing that is this plan; the representation questions (hollow flux-function profiles, screening columns) re-open only on truths that pass it.

Session protocol (lead directive, 2026-07-18). Every landed section MUST queue the next session's followup with a full ready-to-run prompt that carries pre-declared gates and tests — numeric thresholds, pass/fail rules, and stop conditions written down BEFORE the work runs, S1b-style. A followup prompt without its gates is a defect. Outcomes are recorded in this plan the same session the work lands.
