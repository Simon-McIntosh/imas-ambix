COCOS Convention Hardening

Pin the pipeline's working sign convention, and build one fail-loud COCOS-normalisation boundary at the IMAS ingestion seam — so mapping MAST (and later JET / ITER) data through IMAS realises the machine-agnostic North Star without injecting the silent sign hazard that naive mapping would. This plan is the direct action item from the MAST↔IMAS COCOS evaluation, which found the MAST-only pipeline sign-sound but the IMAS seam a latent North-Star risk.

§1 — Where we are: sound today, latent risk at the seam

The evaluation settled the lead's fear with evidence, and its verdict re-orders the priority here: the MAST-only pipeline is sign-sound and empirically verified — no current work is blocked — but there is exactly one real convention mismatch, and it is dormant only because MAST is read raw from FAIR-MAST zarr, not through IMAS.

  • Forward sign chain verified end-to-end. On a genuine pre-breakdown vacuum slice the known-coil prediction G_pf·I_pf correlates 0.83–0.87 with the raw amb magnetics, with a least-squares slope of 0.86–0.99 — sign and magnitude correct, which exercises the kA·turn→A conversion, μ₀, the filament Green's sign, and the B_R cosθ + B_Z sinθ projection together.
  • The one real mismatch: MAST is COCOS-3-like, IMAS is COCOS-11. All four standing-held-out shots have positive Ip (+640…+921 kA) yet ψaxis > ψboundary (axis is the flux maximum). COCOS-11 requires the axis to be the flux minimum for Ip > 0, so matches_cocos11 = False for every shot. Harmless for MAST-only work; live the instant a COCOS-11 magnetics IDS is ingested.
  • The boundary negative is NOT a sign flip. A ψ- or Z-flip would corrupt the axis-R skill too, but axis skill went positive (+0.019) in the same run — a sign fault cannot be that selective. The LCFS −6.26 is the representation artifact the equilibrium-topology-fidelity plan identifies, addressed there by thread 1, not here.
  • The unverified assumption. The IMAS magnetics reader (imas_ambix/gs/imas_geometry.py:74) assumes b_field_pol_probe.poloidal_angle follows MAST's magpr_ang convention into the same projection — documented, flagged, but never checked against real COCOS-11 data.

§2 — Declare and pin the working COCOS (regression test)

The pipeline's convention is currently implicit — correct, but written nowhere as a single checkable fact. Declare it explicitly (total poloidal flux Φ = 2πR·Aφ [Wb]; μ₀ explicit; ψaxis > ψboundary; Ip signed positive along +φ; B-probe projection B_R cosθ + B_Z sinθ) and lock it with a sign-regression test that re-runs the forward-coherence check (a vacuum slice: G_pf·I_pf vs raw amb, asserting positive correlation and near-unit LS slope). This makes any future silent sign regression a red test, not a slow-burning skill drop.

§3 — Verify the IMAS poloidal-angle assumption against a real COCOS-11 IDS

Resolve imas_geometry.py:74: obtain (or synthesise from the MAST geometry) a COCOS-11 magnetics IDS, read b_field_pol_probe.poloidal_angle through the IMAS reader, and confirm the resulting probe projection reproduces the same physical B_R cosθ + B_Z sinθ as the MAST magpr_ang path — or measure the exact offset/sign transform needed. Replace the assumption with a checked fact.

§4 — One fail-loud COCOS-normalisation boundary in the IMAS readers

Add a single, explicit COCOS-normalisation step at the IMAS ingestion boundary (ImasGeometryReader and any future IMAS signal reader): detect the stored COCOS from the IDS, and normalise into the pipeline's declared working convention (§2) — or raise if the stored COCOS is unknown / unhandled. Never rely on imas-python's silent auto-conversion. This is the machine-agnostic seam: it is what lets MAST, JET, and ITER data enter the same latent with one convention, and it is the reason mapping to IMAS is worth it rather than a hazard.

§5 — Cross-convention self-consistency gate

The end-to-end proof: take one MAST equilibrium, ingest it two ways — (a) raw from FAIR-MAST zarr (today's path), and (b) round-tripped through a synthetic COCOS-11 IMAS magnetics/pf_active IDS through the §4 normalisation boundary — and assert the reconstructed topology (axis, X-point set, LCFS radii) is identical to within numerical tolerance. A passing gate is the guarantee that the convention machinery is transparent: the model sees the same physics regardless of the ingestion convention.

§6 — Document the abs(Ip) plasma-on detector

Small but real: data.py:205 takes abs(ip). This is a plasma-on detector (thresholding the Rogowski magnitude), not a sign convention — the physics inverse keeps Ip signed (patch_inverse.py:204,209). Document it in place so it is not mistaken for a sign choice by a future reader.

§7 — Relationship to the other plans

This plan is decoupled from the boundary/interior work of equilibrium-topology-fidelity — that plan's negatives are representation/null-space, not sign, so the two proceed in parallel. This plan blocks machine-agnostic-latent-world-model only at the point where a second machine (JET/ITER) or an IMAS-native MAST re-ingest is wired in: at that seam §4's fail-loud normalisation and §5's self-consistency gate are prerequisites. Until then it is a queued, non-urgent hardening plan; the MAST-only programme runs unblocked.

§ Decisions

Which convention is the pipeline's canonical internal COCOS that §4 normalises INTO — the pipeline's current MAST-native (COCOS-3-like, total-Φ, axis=max) working convention, or IMAS-standard COCOS-11?

The MAST forward chain and the topology reads are verified in the current convention; making it canonical is zero-churn and confines all conversion to the one IMAS boundary (§4). Adopting COCOS-11 internally is cleaner against the DD but re-opens verified code. Recommend keeping the native working convention canonical and converting at the seam, unless a downstream IMAS-native consumer forces COCOS-11 internally.

§ Followups

Pin the working COCOS with a sign-regression test (§2), then verify the IMAS poloidal-angle assumption (§3)

Start with the zero-risk, high-value rungs: declare the pipeline's working convention as a single checkable fact and lock the verified forward-coherence result as a regression test (§2), then resolve the unverified imas_geometry.py:74 poloidal-angle assumption against a real/synthetic COCOS-11 magnetics IDS (§3). §4/§5 (the fail-loud normalisation boundary + cross-convention self-consistency gate) follow once a second-machine ingest is actually wired.
